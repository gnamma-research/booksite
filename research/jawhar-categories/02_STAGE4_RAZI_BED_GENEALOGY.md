## A. Witness, unit and order

The chapter sequence in the [Arabic digital witness](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/8217/56?lang=ar) is not a selection of aphorisms. It proceeds from intrinsic unity of composite quiddity (ch.5 p56), mental vs extramental composition (ch.6 pp56–58), taxonomy of composite conceptions (ch.7 pp59–61), application to substances and accidents (ch.8 pp61–62), and distinction of matter/genus/form/difference (ch.9 pp62–63), followed by a proof-qualification about genus and differentia (ch.10 pp63–65). The page boundary after p65 is not claimed to end the entire surrounding treatise.

- p55: preceding inquiry into parts, prior being/essential priority, and composition.
- p56: true unity of a composite requires an appropriate constitutive relation/dependence among parts; a person beside a stone is not one nature. **Objection:** medicinal compound appears an aggregate of independent ingredients. **Reply:** material ingredients act as the material part of the composite; a purported specific medicinal form and effects raise a distinct question. The scheme avoids dependence of each on the other in *the same respect* to block circularity.
- pp56–58: genus and difference may be distinct in understanding without distinct extramental beings. Example of sensible colour/blackness: “colour” and its differentiating content cannot be detached into two actually existing items. **Objection:** if difference causes genus, it must itself exist prior as producer. **Reply:** “مفيد الوجود هو الحق”, the giver of being is the Real; intellectual determination does not equal an extra existential prior cause. The first priority discussed is of quiddity-components, not necessarily independent existential priority.
- pp59–60: divides nominal/conceptual compounds by whether they include essential, causal, dependent, positive, negative, and/or relative constituents. A **gift**, **ring** named relative to its decorative end, **snub-nosed** thing considered through a material/formal explanation, **first** combining negative/positive, and others show this is a *semantic-metaphysical taxonomy*, not a count of objects additional to individual substrates.
- p61: classifies composites with all real constituents, all relational ones (e.g., **nearer**), or mixed real and relational ones. Explicit bed:
  > والسرير فإنه مركب من أجزاء خشبية وهي موجودات حقيقية ... فلا بد من وجود الترتيب ... فالترتيب أحد أجزاء السرير وهو أمر نسبي لا أنه ماهية مستقلة بنفسها
  Translation (provisional): “The bed is compounded of wooden components, which are real existents … but their obtaining is insufficient without the arrangement among them … the arrangement is one constituent of the bed, a relative matter rather than a quiddity subsisting in itself.” The text does **not** say arrangement is arbitrary or lacks a real basis; the denial concerns *an independent quiddity*. See [p61](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/8217/61?lang=ar).
- pp61–62: *jawhar* may have intellectual genus/difference without extramental separate grounds (separate intellects and rational souls according to the philosophical account); a human exemplifies actual body/soul grounding; a *quality* can have intellectual composition without actual detachable colour/differentia beings; physical house/body example refers to roof and walls without proving new substantial essence.
- pp62–63: **animal** as (i) *lā bi-sharṭ* considered without a restricting addition, (ii) *bi-sharṭ shayʾ* under positive determination, (iii) *bi-sharṭ lā* on condition of excluding; “animal” as material part is not predicable of the complete human, but as genus is. Rāzī emphasizes unity in external being with difference in what is intellectually distinguished: “ليس الحيوان موجودا والإنسان موجودا آخر بل الحيوان الموجود هو الإنسان بعينه”. This is an argument about categorical predication and unity—not that mind produces the existence of animals.
- pp63–65: **objection** that any sharing + difference proves composition. **Reply**: shared negation or merely external positive predicate does not establish distinct essential constituents; only common and distinctive intrinsic positive *muqawwimāt* justify specific genus/difference analysis. Even then, their distinct conceptual definition does not automatically entail two external entities.

## B. Argument and philosophical result (not a later AT comparison)

1. **Distinction of concepts** is consistent with a **single extra-mental actual thing**, even where the concept truly specifies its nature.
2. **True constituted unity** is stronger than adjacency or merely invented grouping. The necessary interdependence of appropriate principles is not alone a conclusive demonstration of a *new* natural substance.
3. A relative constituent (*amr nasabī*) can be indispensable in a **bed-concept** without independently inhering or subsisting as another *jawhar*. One must distinguish (a) truth of a description, (b) actual basis in parts/configuration, (c) extra substantial unity, (d) mental consideration and its object.
4. *Bi-sharṭ* vs *lā bi-sharṭ* changes the intelligible role of the same term (“animal” or “body”); do not construe this as two actual bodies.
5. A gift, ring, artifact, army, medicinal mixture, real human body, and cosmos cannot be assigned one kind of unity by a single *tarkīb ḥaqīqī* label.
