About these source pages

The Arabic is the two-volume Bidāya scan cited throughout the reader. The English pages reproduce Imran Ahsan Khan Nyazee’s The Distinguished Jurist’s Primer, from the supplied volumes. They are a published translation, distinct from the reader’s new working translations in the short source cards. Edition readings and notes may differ. “Full” refers to the complete question, chapter or book named above; this workspace does not reproduce the whole of Bidāya.
