Alchemical terms

These are qualified comparisons for effects of practising law, not names for legal inferences. Definitions and applications are preserved from the project’s operation register, Revision 13. Its historical vocabulary witnesses include Ruland’s uncorrected electronic text and the imported comparative register; these entries do not claim a new print collation.

Ablutio

Sense. washing through renewed infusions

Proposed application. A specified cleansing through practice; an inward effect requires an attributed interpretation and evidence of changed conduct. Bodily washing alone does not establish it.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-02, REP-03, REP-05

Solutio

Sense. loosening or resolving an existing cohesion

Proposed application. Possessiveness, self-preference or an obstructive dependency loosened through giving, restraint or an effective remedy. A legal dissolution alone is not inward release.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-09, REP-17, REP-18, REP-22, REP-51, REP-54, REP-55

Filtratio

Sense. a medium lets one fraction pass while retaining another

Proposed application. Unassigned in the current case pass. Sorting evidence or classifying a legal object is not the formative effect examined here; a distinct lived process would need to be established.

Digestio

Sense. measured treatment under suitable warmth that prepares material to exercise its power

Proposed application. Understood practice gradually assimilated into attention, judgment and conduct through time, reflection and correction. Duration or mechanical repetition alone is insufficient.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-07, REP-11, REP-28, REP-32, REP-38

Maturatio

Sense. bringing a crude or unready subject toward developed capacity

Proposed application. A specified human capacity developing through real opportunities to learn, choose and act. Majority, a status change or a declaration of competence is not the development itself.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-01, REP-43

Circulatio

Sense. a recurrent return within an apparatus, with rectification as an aim

Proposed application. Practice returning through experience and acknowledged correction to changed action. A remedy must actually be undertaken; a legal return, circular path or movement of money alone is insufficient.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-24

Cohobatio

Sense. returning withdrawn liquor to its body and repeating withdrawal

Proposed application. A restricted comparison for returning understood meaning to the same embodied practice and working it again. Repetition without renewed attention supplies no such effect.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-02, REP-06, REP-13

Imbibitio

Sense. repeated reception and incorporation of liquid into a body

Proposed application. Taught or recollected meaning becoming part of attention and conduct through repeated reception. Hearing words alone does not prove assimilation.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-02, REP-09, REP-23, REP-49, REP-56

Inceratio

Sense. gradual moistening toward a pliant, wax-like condition

Proposed application. Rigid self-insistence becoming more responsive to another person or a real circumstance through attentive conduct. The legal allowance of flexibility is an occasion, not the transformation.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies:

Cibatio

Sense. incorporation; feeding is a further usage in later symbolic literature

Proposed application. A possible comparison for sustaining a developing capacity through actual nourishment. Ruland gives the brief corporatio gloss; broader feeding imagery needs the additional witness recorded in the imported register. Unassigned in this case pass.

unassigned in this pass · Representative studies:

Fermentatio

Sense. an incorporated agent progressively assimilates a receiving mass

Proposed application. An exemplary practice changing others through identifiable teaching, imitation and uptake. Diffusion must be shown; legal publication alone is insufficient. Unassigned in this case pass.

unassigned in this pass · Representative studies:

Coagulatio

Sense. a tenuous or fluid condition becomes coherent and determinate

Proposed application. A dependable pattern of care, provision or cooperation taking shape through lived conduct. A contract or entitlement becoming legally determinate is not the effect named here.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-21, REP-31, REP-39

Fixatio

Sense. what would flee the fire is made able to remain under its action

Proposed application. Fidelity, self-command or a dependable shared practice becoming stable under temptation, scarcity or conflict. The survival or enforceability of a claim alone does not establish this change.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-06, REP-14, REP-15, REP-16, REP-20, REP-25, REP-29, REP-30, REP-31, REP-33, REP-35, REP-37, REP-40, REP-42, REP-47, REP-48, REP-50, REP-51, REP-58, REP-59, REP-62, REP-66, REP-68, REP-70, REP-71

Athanor

Sense. a furnace that sustains and regulates appropriate heat

Proposed application. The maintained conditions that permit formation: provision, protected time, instruction, care and usable remedies. This is an enabling image, not itself an operation or achieved virtue.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-07, REP-13, REP-58

Gradatio

Sense. improvement in determinate qualities without changing the species

Proposed application. Improvement in a specified competence through practice and correction. Better performance does not by itself establish moral virtue or spiritual realization.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-04, REP-19, REP-35, REP-43, REP-48, REP-72

Tinctura

Sense. a colouring or penetrating efficacy, qualified by the recipient's condition

Proposed application. An acknowledged meaning becoming effective across further conduct, such as humility entering treatment of others. Transfer beyond one practice requires actual exercise and evidence.

qualified formative comparison · Representative studies: REP-10, REP-11, REP-18, REP-21, REP-68

Distillatio

Sense. separation through vaporization, condensation and collection

Proposed application. Unassigned in the current case pass. A specific formative process with separation, return and retained result would need a warranted comparison; legal abstraction is not distillation.

unassigned in this pass · Representative studies:

Ablatio

Sense. Removal of an adherent substance by wiping, scraping or another instrument.

Proposed application. Giving up an acquired practice of evasion through concrete restitution and corrected conduct; not erasing another person's rights.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-16, REP-26, REP-33, REP-42, REP-47, REP-52, REP-68

Calcinatio corporum

Sense. Reduction of a body to calx through specified treatment; Ruland distinguishes several kinds of calcination.

Proposed application. A limited image for voluntary discipline dismantling a hardened habit. Punishment, pain and coercion are not thereby transformative, and no person is the dross to be removed.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-11

Ceratio

Sense. Softening what is hard and not readily fusible through repeated treatment, preparing it for penetration and union.

Proposed application. Rigidity of response becoming receptive through repeated patient correction. Overlaps with inceration; not an additional universal spiritual stage.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-05, REP-24, REP-45

Congelatio

Sense. Thickening or congealing, including the loss of fluidity exemplified by freezing.

Proposed application. A possible adverse effect: institutions or dispositions hardening so that they cease to respond to real need. Stability alone is not virtue.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-24, REP-59, REP-71

Exaltatio

Sense. Improvement or elevation of a substance's efficacy; Ruland also associates the term with sublimation, maturation and gradation.

Proposed application. A capacity becoming better in a specified respect. No automatic passage from legal compliance to spiritual rank is implied.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies:

Extractio

Sense. Drawing a selected component or power from a body through an appropriate added medium, with a residue left behind.

Proposed application. A specific exercise bringing a neglected capacity into effective use. This differs from making the whole person or household flourish; extracting a rule from a text is outside this comparison.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-06, REP-12, REP-13

Liquefactio probatoria

Sense. An assay by fusion that distinguishes constituents under treatment.

Proposed application. An expressly limited comparison for a person's fidelity tested in the actual pressures of obligation. It is not evidentiary adjudication, an ordeal, or permission to engineer suffering.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-01, REP-20, REP-22, REP-30, REP-32, REP-36, REP-49, REP-50, REP-62, REP-70, REP-72

Magisterium

Sense. A preparation retaining the subject's proper constituent parts while removing extraneous impurities; distinct from a selective extract.

Proposed application. An integrated pattern of living in which worship, livelihood, bodily need and others' claims remain together. It names an ideal of formation, not mastery over other people.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-03, REP-08, REP-12, REP-17, REP-18, REP-41, REP-53, REP-55, REP-65, REP-72

Putrefactio

Sense. Transformation through a specified warm, moist decomposition in one historical usage; other entries qualify its senses.

Proposed application. Unassigned: illness, bereavement, poverty and violence are not evidence of a necessary spiritual decomposition. A new application would need a more particular source and causal bridge.

unassigned · Representative studies:

Separatio per abscessum

Sense. Separation through constituents moving apart.

Proposed application. A limited image for the lived loosening of harmful entanglement when people actually gain room to act. The legal act of separation alone does not establish this effect.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-08, REP-14, REP-27, REP-28

Separatio per abstractionem

Sense. Separation by withdrawal through an instrument or operative means.

Proposed application. Practical removal of a means of domination or appropriation through an effective remedy. The comparison concerns changed access and conduct, not the court's inference.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-25, REP-44, REP-56, REP-57, REP-58, REP-60

Separatio per dilutionem

Sense. Separation through washing, suspension and settling, retaining a distinction before remixing.

Proposed application. A restricted image for disentangling a shared practice while preserving useful relations. Mere distribution of shares or semantic classification is not the operation.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-41, REP-53

Sublimare

Sense. Ruland's German explanation includes becoming better or more excellent, not only physical ascent.

Proposed application. An explicit qualitative improvement must be named: for example, power exercised with restraint. Do not infer vertical realization from an upward image.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-15, REP-63

Temperamentum

Sense. A nature's action tempered by admitting another.

Proposed application. One legitimate tendency moderated by another: zeal by care, generosity by provision, autonomy by responsibility. This is not a fixed moral typing by bodily humours.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-04, REP-07, REP-12, REP-17, REP-19, REP-23, REP-26, REP-27, REP-29, REP-32, REP-34, REP-36, REP-39, REP-40, REP-44, REP-46, REP-52, REP-54, REP-55, REP-60, REP-61, REP-63, REP-64, REP-66, REP-67, REP-69

Balneum Mariae

Sense. A vessel heated indirectly through a surrounding water bath.

Proposed application. An enabling comparison for gentle, regulated conditions of instruction or recovery. Distinguish the environment that permits change from the change achieved.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-05, REP-43

Compositio

Sense. A bringing together of diverse things; Ruland distinguishes mixing and conglutination within it.

Proposed application. A limited comparison for distinct contributions sustained in actual cooperation. The requirement to preserve persons and their claims comes from the ethical argument, not from a chemical mixture.

qualified comparison; see case for actual application · Representative studies: REP-10, REP-23, REP-34, REP-37, REP-38, REP-39, REP-45, REP-46, REP-54, REP-64
