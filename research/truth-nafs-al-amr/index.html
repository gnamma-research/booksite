One important false attribution

The epistle often printed as a separate work of al-Sayyid al-Sharīf al-Jurjānī on nafs al-amr is an independent copy of a passage from Shams al-Dīn al-Samarqandī's al-Maʿārif fī sharḥ al-Ṣaḥāʾif, as shown by Maşuk Aktaş (2022). Historical catalogue attribution and critical attribution must be recorded separately. Authentic Jurjānī positions require his other writings.

Translation status

Ṭūsī's short Ithbāt is translated in the existing bilingual dossier. The Kīshī, Dawwānī, Jīlānī, Ardabīlī, Harawī, Jandī and Kashmīrī texts are identified but not yet fully translated.
