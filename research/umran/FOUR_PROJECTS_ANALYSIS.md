# Two analytical passes: the four great projects of ʿUmrān

**Research note · 15 November 2026 · Design status: editorial synthesis, not a juristic classification**

## Pass 1 — Begin with ends rather than sectors

The former twelve-programme catalogue put quite different things on one plane: human formation, scholarly texts, governance, technology, material services, and the methods used to assess them. The classical practical-philosophy division of **self, household, city** (e.g., Ṭūsī, *Akhlāq-i Nāṣirī*) reminds us that the human and collective orders relate to each other. Al-Fārābī further argues that a political association should create the conditions for true felicity, not merely provide goods. Ibn Khaldūn's discussions of the transmission of crafts and acquired *malakāt* show why reproducing knowledge deserves a clear civilizational project of its own.

A working four-part division therefore emerges:

1. **Form Persons & Households** — worship, moral practice, care, family and formation within work.
2. **Renew Knowledge & Education** — scholarship, texts, teachers, curricula and intellectually competent engagement with contemporary questions.
3. **Order Just Institutions** — law, governance, accountable authority and institutional means.
4. **Sustain Livelihood & Life** — crafts, essential services, resources, housing, health and resilience.

This division **does not** turn those four categories into canonical `maqāṣid`. The human end of nearness to God is higher than the social taxonomy, and spiritual worth is not inferred from occupational visibility.

## Pass 2 — Test the boundaries and map the smaller work

The second pass asks whether every existing programme has one comprehensible principal home, without denying its other relationships. It maps exactly **12 subordinate programmes** and **14 existing communal functions**, each with one navigational parent in `major-projects.json`.

| Great project | Programmes included | Some need functions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form Persons & Households | Spiritual/ethical formation; household and care | Dependant care, funerals, household sufficiency |
| Renew Knowledge & Education | Scholarly succession; critical texts; curricula; contemporary intellectual inquiry; civilizational learning | Teaching, curricular competence |
| Order Just Institutions | Living jurisprudence; institutional integrity; technology and human agency | Community institutions, accountable AI |
| Sustain Livelihood & Life | Crafts and livelihoods; resources and resilience | Clinical care, water, food, work skills, mental health, relief and shelter |

### Why not create a fifth project for technology, risk or careers?

These are **cross-cutting problems and instruments**, not another ultimate end. AI safety can concern public authority (**project 3**), intellectual formation (**project 2**), or bodily security and infrastructure (**project 4**). Education also spans every project, while its specifically scholarly work is principally under **project 2**. Career matching belongs to the practical layer downstream of all four; it is not a separate civilizational end.

A legal obligation, an observed shortage, and an individual's professional fitness likewise require **different judgments**: jurisprudence, evidence and vocation. Neither a useful campaign nor a flagship programme automatically creates a duty to change careers.

### Stress tests

- A care worker's daily activity can fulfil serious responsibilities within **project 1** without being spiritually subordinate to prestigious research.
- A digital critical edition or integrated curriculum belongs primarily to **project 2**. It must still be tested for scholarly quality and pedagogical value.
- A project to govern AI deployment is examined under **project 3**, while AI evaluation in education and physical infrastructure links to projects 2 and 4.
- A municipal sanitation service belongs to **project 4**, though its governance and labour ethics also touch projects 3 and 1.
- New charities, research institutes and universities are **possible means**, not independent success measures. Improving an existing institution can be preferable.

## Editorial requirements

The main site presents **four projects → twelve subordinate programmes**. Detailed source and programme dossiers are accessible from the Research section. A communal function remains marked with its provisional legal classification and **unknown** actual sufficiency unless independently reviewed. No career, task, research grant or job title is assigned a measure of spiritual merit.
