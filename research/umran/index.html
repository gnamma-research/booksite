Your path

From discernment to concrete action

Read the practical guide →

Understand your responsibilities

Begin with the obligations already present in your life: worship, care, livelihood, household and existing commitments.

Develop the capacity to contribute

Consider aptitudes, acquired skills, good habits and what you might become capable of through formation and experience.

Investigate a real need

Ask whether a required communal good is adequately provided, at the relevant level and by the appropriate means.

Choose a proportionate route

Practise, teach, fund, coordinate or build institutions, without treating any profession as a measure of spiritual worth.

Research meets practice

Follow the evidence, not just the job title

Read institutional studies from Malaysia alongside actual community fundraising initiatives. A need must be established independently of the way it is advertised or funded.
