The four great projects · ʿUmrān

Four projects. One human purpose.

Form people. Renew knowledge. Establish just institutions. Sustain life. Smaller programmes, community needs and careers belong inside these larger undertakings.

Nearness to God remains the human end; the four projects describe fields of collective action, not spiritual ranks or new canonical maqāṣid.

Maqāṣid, farḍ kifāya and personal vocation are different layers of judgment; they are not competing fifth, sixth or seventh “great projects.”

Where does AI safety belong?

Primarily under just institutions, because technical systems alter authority and social responsibility. It also connects to knowledge and education and the protection of life and services. It is an important possible duty, but not a separate ultimate purpose.

Why four? The two rounds of analysis

First pass — organise by positive ends. Classical practical philosophy distinguishes the formation of persons, households and cities; the tradition of learning and acquired malakāt warrants particular visibility as a civilizational project. These distinctions supply four working fields rather than twelve unrelated activities.

Second pass — test boundaries. Every existing programme receives exactly one primary home. Technology, research, finance, public policy and education can serve several fields and retain cross-links. Maqāṣid evaluates the ends and means; communal sufficiency diagnoses actual provision; personal vocation matches capacities and responsibilities. None is itself a fifth sector.

For the supporting discussions see Ṭūsī on ethics, household and polity, al-Fārābī on co-operation for human felicity, and al-Shāṭibī's theory of legal purposes.
