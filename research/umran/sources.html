Research basis · 13 November 2026

Sources, methods & unresolved questions

This is a research programme, not a fatwa service. Draft classifications should be traceable to texts and revisable when evidence or conditions change.

Current status

The first edition defines a method and offers eight illustrative need profiles. It does not certify shortages, assign personal legal duties or maintain a live jobs feed. Juristic generalisations require comparison with original passages and disagreement across schools; empirical conclusions require dated evidence for the population under study.

Research principles

  1. Distinguish the duty from its means. A necessary good may be supplied through several occupations or institutions.
  2. Separate law from empirical observation. A known collective duty may be adequately supplied; an apparent shortage may not yet have an established legal classification.
  3. Report disagreement. Do not convert a contested interpretation into an asserted consensus.
  4. Do not infer personal obligation from aptitude. Personal circumstances need qualified juristic judgment.
  5. Preserve scale and temporality. Evidence about a city cannot automatically establish a need worldwide.

Primary and institutional sources

  1. Al-Ghazālī, Iḥyāʾ ʿUlūm al-Dīn, Book of Knowledge and Book of Earning and Livelihood. Necessary sciences, crafts and occupations. Arabic text (digital edition).
  2. Al-Nawawī, al-Majmūʿ, introductory discussion of knowledge and communal duties. Records important disagreement about crafts. Arabic text.
  3. Al-Shāṭibī, al-Muwāfaqāt, discussion of farḍ kifāya. Collective versus particular responsibility, qualification and enabling competent persons. Arabic text.
  4. Ibn Taymiyya, Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā, vol. 29, discussion of necessary industries. Need, alternative provision and remunerated work. Arabic text.
  5. International Islamic Fiqh Academy, Resolution 164 (18/2), 2007. Human resources, useful knowledge, skills, curricula and ʿumrān. Resolution.
  6. Naṣīr al-Dīn al-Ṭūsī, Akhlāq-i Nāṣirī. Formation of the person, household and city. Source passage verification remains to be completed for this site.
  7. Shāh Walī Allāh, Ḥujjat Allāh al-Bāligha, treatment of irtifāqāt. Occupational cooperation and social development; original passages remain to be reviewed for quotation.

Malaysian and related contemporary studies

The annotated Malaysia reading room documents scope, method and limits for each source. Especially relevant are International Islamic Fiqh Academy Resolution 164 (Putrajaya, 2007); Mohd Sani Badron (IKIM, 2009); Mukhlas Nugraha (TAFHIM, 2017); Febrian and Hidayatullah (TAFHIM, 2023); Eletrebi and colleagues (IIUM, 2023); the IIUM Bustan ʿIlm study (2024); Abd Wakil and colleagues on mosque economic aid (2024); MASA@Sungai Nibong (2025); and Abdul Muzil Abd Rahim on ḥadd al-kifāyah (UKM, 2025).

Terminology: ḥadd al-kifāyah in zakat eligibility and household poverty assessment must not be collapsed into farḍ kifāya, the juridical category of collective obligation. Likewise, not all sources described here are Malaysian-authored: the 2007 resolution is international, and several articles were published by Malaysian journals with international contributors.

Community projects as research leads

The project observatory annotates nine LaunchGood campaigns by intervention, institutional function, required capacities and further evidence needed. The campaign itself is an organiser's statement, not an independent need assessment. LaunchGood's review guidance also advises independent donor judgment. We neither recommend donating nor certify current campaign status.

Questions left open

  • The precise relation between al-Ghazālī's discussions in the Iḥyāʾ and legal writings concerning the formal obligation of crafts.
  • A comprehensive four-madhhab comparison of the definition, discharge and assignment of communal obligations.
  • Who counts as the relevant community for international technological and ecological risks.
  • How to measure the sufficiency of complex institutions whose outputs are qualitative.
  • The distinct roles of inclination, capacity, training, heredity and social circumstance in Guénon, Coomaraswamy and Islamic accounts of vocational differentiation.
Editorial distinction.

Each need profile carries a provisional normative classification and a separate actual-provision status. None is a determination of a binding personal obligation.
