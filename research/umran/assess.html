Sufficiency assessment studio · browser-local

Describe a need. Test the evidence. Do not guess the verdict.

Use this worksheet to record a proposed communal function, the people concerned, the required standard, existing provision and possible contributors. It creates a structured research memo—not an automated fatwa or a declaration of inadequate provision.

A research instrument, not a comprehensive framework.

The assessment studio examines supply and sufficiency after the human end, maqāṣid and any positive programmes of renewal have been clarified. Completing an evidence form cannot establish what an entire civilization ought to become or what occupation gives a person nearness to God.

An auditable method

What must be known before saying “the community needs more”?

Every entry should identify the same population and period, or explicitly state why the sources differ. Record why the function is important independently of a particular project asking for support.

Information you enter is not sent to Gnamma or stored by this site. You can export your own draft as JSON or copy the research memo.

1. The required function and the relevant community

A field or job title cannot substitute for a precisely defined good. Specify the people, place and period before interpreting any indicator.

2. Normative basis

Explain the source of the duty and its level of certainty. A project may serve a good without becoming personally obligatory for its workers.

3. Standard of adequate provision

Do not divide an official output statistic by an unrelated population statistic. An official government target may be valuable evidence without itself being a legal minimum.

4. Present need and present provision

Ideally establish both independently, with matching definitions, dated sources and a clear unit of analysis.

5. Comparison, alternatives and capacity

A shortage may involve access, skill, distribution, quality or governance rather than the number of employees. Consider institutions already serving the population.

Safeguard: A completed worksheet only means that the investigator has supplied a draft description. It does not independently validate any source, establish that the community is deficient, or issue a binding ruling for any individual. Avoid entering identifying information about vulnerable beneficiaries.

Evidence-readiness summary





What follows from an assessment?
A completed audit might show a documented shortage against a well-founded standard, or merely that no adequate evidence has been collected. Even in the first case, a decision about what particular individuals should do requires separate consideration of qualifications, other duties, effectiveness, cost and lawful means.
Move between the worked evidence studies, underlying communal functions and occupational profiles. The system should help researchers identify who might contribute; it should not pressure a person to abandon a suitable livelihood.
