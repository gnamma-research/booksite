Needs of ʿumrān · working catalogue

What must be adequately provided?

Begin with a function that people depend upon, not with a fashionable job title. These eight dossiers are hypotheses for further juridical and empirical research—not claims that any particular location has a shortage.

Knowledge & formation

Religious teaching and scholarly succession

Established duty in necessary formsActual sufficiency: not assessed

Provide reliable basic religious instruction and sustain qualified future teachers and jurists.

Possible scope: Local · regional · transnational

Possible contributions

Teacher · Scholar and mentor · Translator · Administrator · Endowment organiser

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Access to reliable instruction
  • Competence and supervision of teachers
  • Advanced training and scholarly succession

Research starting points: Al-Ghazālī, Iḥyāʾ; Al-Nawawī, al-Majmūʿ; IIFA Resolution 164. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Knowledge & formation

Curricula for contemporary intellectual discourse

Context-dependent derivationActual sufficiency: not assessed

Develop the intellectual competence needed to understand and respond responsibly to contemporary scientific, philosophical and technical debates.

Possible scope: Regional · transnational

Possible contributions

Curriculum developer · Researcher · Teacher · Translator · Educational evaluator

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Specify missing learner competencies
  • Assess existing teaching and curricula
  • Evaluate actual learning and teacher formation

Research starting points: Al-Nawawī, Rawḍat al-Ṭālibīn; IIFA Resolution 164. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Care & health

Necessary medical care

Strong classical foundation; details disputedActual sufficiency: not assessed

Ensure clinically significant medical needs can be addressed through competent, accessible treatment.

Possible scope: Local · city · regional

Possible contributions

Clinician · Clinical educator · Health IT specialist · Hospital administrator · Medical logistics

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Relevant clinical needs and waiting periods
  • Qualified treatment capacity and access
  • Quality and continuity of care

Research starting points: Al-Ghazālī, Iḥyāʾ; Al-Nawawī, al-Majmūʿ. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Infrastructure

Safe water, sanitation and infrastructure

Context-dependent obligationActual sufficiency: not assessed

Sustain indispensable water, waste handling, roads and civil infrastructure where reliable provision is required.

Possible scope: Neighbourhood · city · region

Possible contributions

Sanitation worker · Engineer · Technician · Municipal administrator · Infrastructure planner

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Service safety and reliability
  • Waste collection and pollution indicators
  • Maintenance capacity and interruption risk

Research starting points: Classical discussions of necessary crafts and utilities; Contextual assessment required. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Livelihood

Secure food access

Context-dependent obligationActual sufficiency: not assessed

Ensure reliable, affordable access to essential food without presuming that local production is always necessary where trade suffices.

Possible scope: Household · city · nation · transnational

Possible contributions

Farmer · Trader · Logistics specialist · Community organiser · Supply analyst

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Household food security and affordability
  • Stability of supply and alternatives
  • Access under disruption

Research starting points: Al-Ghazālī, Iḥyāʾ; Ibn Taymiyya, Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Technology

Safety and accountable governance of advanced AI

Plausible but disputed derivationActual sufficiency: not assessed

Investigate credible grave harms from advanced AI to life, justice, institutions and human moral agency, and identify effective safeguards.

Possible scope: National · transnational · humanity-wide

Possible contributions

AI evaluation specialist · Security researcher · Governance analyst · Independent auditor · Educator and institution-builder

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • A specified risk mechanism and its evidence
  • Independent evaluation of proposed safeguards
  • Existing institutional response and remaining gaps

Research starting points: General duties to prevent grave harms; 80,000 Hours AI problem profile (empirical argument). See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Care & health

Care for dependent and vulnerable people

Mixed personal and collective obligationsActual sufficiency: not assessed

Prevent neglect and grave harm to vulnerable dependants through family, community and appropriate institutional arrangements.

Possible scope: Household · neighbourhood · city

Possible contributions

Family caregiver · Care worker · Social worker · Respite organiser · Institutional coordinator

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Existing personal obligations identified
  • Care needs and safety
  • Access to respite and adequate support

Research starting points: Classical treatment of family and communal duties; Contextual legal review required. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Religious practice

Funeral rites and burial provision

Established classical communal dutyActual sufficiency: not assessed

Ensure competent and accessible performance of obligatory funeral rites and burial arrangements.

Possible scope: Local · regional

Possible contributions

Community volunteer · Burial coordinator · Funeral practitioner · Administrator

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Qualified practitioners and access
  • Appropriate facilities and coordination

Research starting points: Al-Nawawī, al-Majmūʿ; Classical funeral law. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Important distinction

“Established” refers to a preliminary legal classification of a function, not to a finding of a present deficit. Every profile has an unknown sufficiency status until dated evidence for a defined community is reviewed.
