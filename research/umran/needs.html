Needs of ʿumrān · working catalogue

What must be adequately provided?

Begin with a function that people depend upon, not with a fashionable job title. These fourteen dossiers are hypotheses for further juridical and empirical research—not claims that any particular location has a shortage.

Knowledge & formation

Religious teaching and scholarly succession

Established duty in necessary formsActual sufficiency: not assessed

Provide reliable basic religious instruction and sustain qualified future teachers and jurists.

Possible scope: Local · regional · transnational

Possible contributions

Teacher · Scholar and mentor · Translator · Administrator · Endowment organiser

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Access to reliable instruction
  • Competence and supervision of teachers
  • Advanced training and scholarly succession

Research starting points: Al-Ghazālī, Iḥyāʾ; Al-Nawawī, al-Majmūʿ; IIFA Resolution 164. See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Community project leads: education fund · southgate (organiser claims, not shortage verification)

Evidence audit: Bangi education organiser claim (research stage only)

Livelihood

Secure food access

Context-dependent obligationActual sufficiency: not assessed

Ensure reliable, affordable access to essential food without presuming that local production is always necessary where trade suffices.

Possible scope: Household · city · nation · transnational

Possible contributions

Farmer · Trader · Logistics specialist · Community organiser · Supply analyst

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Household food security and affordability
  • Stability of supply and alternatives
  • Access under disruption

Technology

Safety and accountable governance of advanced AI

Plausible but disputed derivationActual sufficiency: not assessed

Investigate credible grave harms from advanced AI to life, justice, institutions and human moral agency, and identify effective safeguards.

Possible scope: National · transnational · humanity-wide

Possible contributions

AI evaluation specialist · Security researcher · Governance analyst · Independent auditor · Educator and institution-builder

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • A specified risk mechanism and its evidence
  • Independent evaluation of proposed safeguards
  • Existing institutional response and remaining gaps

Research starting points: General duties to prevent grave harms; 80,000 Hours AI problem profile (empirical argument). See source and method notes. Scope and personal obligations have not been adjudicated.

Household & welfare

Adequate provision for vulnerable households

Overlapping duties and administrative standardsActual sufficiency: not assessed

Investigate whether households can access necessary material, educational, health and social provisions, including properly administered assistance where warranted.

Possible scope: Household · city · state

Possible contributions

Social worker · Zakat administrator · Household assessor · Waqf manager · Community researcher

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Define household costs and access locally
  • Distinguish zakat eligibility thresholds from collective legal duties
  • Assess continuity, dignity, accessibility and longer-term support

Research starting points: Abdul Muzil Abd Rahim, UKM (2025), ḥadd al-kifāyah; MASA@Sungai Nibong study (2025). See Malaysia research and source notes. The obligation and actual provision remain unadjudicated.

Community examples: tanzania skills · tanzania care · orphan education. Example campaigns do not establish shortages.

Evidence audit: Penang household audit (research stage only)

Livelihood

Vocational capability and pathways into livelihood

Potential means; not automatically a separate farḍ kifāyaActual sufficiency: not assessed

Prepare people for competent, lawful and sustainable livelihood when training and access to work are genuinely necessary to remedy dependence or exclusion.

Possible scope: Local · regional

Possible contributions

Vocational teacher · Apprenticeship mentor · Small business adviser · Programme evaluator · Employer

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Identify viable work and market demand
  • Evaluate training quality and earnings after completion
  • Check household needs, safety and alternatives

Research starting points: MASA@Sungai Nibong (2025); Malaysian research on social entrepreneurship and human capital. See Malaysia research and source notes. The obligation and actual provision remain unadjudicated.

Community examples: tanzania skills. Example campaigns do not establish shortages.

Evidence audit: Penang household audit (research stage only)

Institutions

Sustainable community and mosque institutions

Potential enabling means; function-specific dutyActual sufficiency: not assessed

Examine the spaces, governance, qualified personnel, finances and services needed for community learning, care, worship and social cooperation.

Possible scope: Neighbourhood · city · regional networks

Possible contributions

Community trustee · Facilities manager · Imam and teacher · Financial controller · Institutional organiser

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Separate building, maintenance and service functions
  • Analyse real usage and accessible alternatives
  • Evaluate governance, financial sustainability and benefit

Research starting points: Abd Wakil et al., Journal of Fatwa Management and Research (2024); International Islamic Fiqh Academy Resolution 164 (2007). See Malaysia research and source notes. The obligation and actual provision remain unadjudicated.

Community examples: southgate. Example campaigns do not establish shortages.

Accessible mental-health and psychosocial care

Emergency & protection

Emergency relief and displacement support

Contextual responsibilities to avert grave harmActual sufficiency: not assessed

Secure food, safe water, health and other essential support during acute displacement or disaster, including coordination and continuity of supply.

Possible scope: Local · regional · transnational

Possible contributions

Humanitarian coordinator · Relief logistics worker · Health and WASH practitioner · Interpreter · Field administrator

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Verify the acute humanitarian situation independently
  • Map relief provision, gaps and competing authorities
  • Examine logistics, safety, local participation and sustainability

Research starting points: Juristic duties concerning rescue and prevention of grave harm (further verification); Organiser reports and independent humanitarian data needed. See Malaysia research and source notes. The obligation and actual provision remain unadjudicated.

Community examples: sudan basic · refugee support. Example campaigns do not establish shortages.

Emergency & protection

Safe temporary shelter and stabilisation

Context-dependent collective and institutional provisionActual sufficiency: not assessed

Investigate provision of safe shelter, protection and basic services for displaced persons, with attention to lawful responsibility and the conditions for lasting stabilisation.

Possible scope: City · region · transnational

Possible contributions

Shelter coordinator · Civil engineer · Social worker · Local housing administrator · Protection specialist

What would evidence of adequate provision require?
  • Document the affected population and emergency housing supply
  • Assess safety, family integrity, health and access
  • Verify agencies' responsibilities and alternatives

Research starting points: General duties concerning protection and grave harm (juristic assessment required); Independent displacement and housing data required. See Malaysia research and source notes. The obligation and actual provision remain unadjudicated.

Community examples: refugee support. Example campaigns do not establish shortages.

Important distinction

“Established” refers to a preliminary legal classification of a function, not to a finding of a present deficit. Every profile has an unknown sufficiency status until dated evidence for a defined community is reviewed.
