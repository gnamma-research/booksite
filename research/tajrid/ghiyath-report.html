Ghiyāth al-Dīn Manṣūr: critical witness update, 31 October 2026

The present update adds one bounded Arabic–English extract, C31G02, from a visually checked catalogue quotation of the work’s ending, and corrects the witness identity in C31G01. It does not supply a complete manuscript or an original sixth-stage philosophical argument. The evidence now includes the complete catalogue, an ownership/title thumbnail, one interior manuscript preview spread, and an independently checked scholarly manuscript register.

Witness and title identity

The correct principal locator is Ragıp Paşa 809, not Hamidiye 809. The catalogue’s own title page reads Fahras al-makhṭūṭāt al-ʿArabiyya wa-l-Turkiyya wa-l-Fārisiyya fī Maktabat Rāghib Bāshā, volume 6. Its entry 1042 occupies printed 381–382, also PDF pages 381–382. `al-raqm al-Ḥamīdī` is an inventory label within that catalogue; expanding it into the name of a different library collection is an error. The small ownership image independently shows `RAGIP P. K.No 809`. Catalogue, pp. 381–382.

The entry’s title is تجريد الغواشي على الطبقات الجلالية على الصدرية, Tajrīd al-ghawāshī ʿalā al-ṭabaqāt al-Jalāliyya ʿalā al-Ṣadriyya. Its first footnote also gives Muḥākamāt ʿalā ḥawāshī Sharḥ al-Tajrīd. These are catalogue-associated names for this witness; the complete original title/opening leaf has not been read. The printed extent is clearly 254 leaves, with 27 lines per page. The web-search OCR’s 245 is a digit reversal. The catalogue continuation names the Ragıp Paşa endowment and CD number 51917. This metadata should guide acquisition, not be used to imply that all 254 leaves have been obtained.

Günaydın’s register identifies Ghiyāth’s al-Muḥākamāt at Ragıp Paşa 809, fols. 1b–254b, and H. Hüsnü Paşa 79, fols. 89–145. His discussion also questions a rigid alternating chronology: Ṣadr’s second gloss continued to be revised after Dawwānī’s last, and the surviving jadīd/ajadd identifications are disputed. These are modern manuscript-research conclusions; the relevant original witnesses were not collated here. Günaydın 2016, pp. 248–249, 266.

For the map, retain `ghiyath_dashtaki_gloss`, add the full Tajrīd al-ghawāshī title as a catalogue-attested alias, and connect it to both the Ṣadrian and Jalālian dossier. Do not create a duplicate solely because one catalogue uses Muḥākamāt. The familiar three Dawwānī glosses, two Ṣadr glosses, and Ghiyāth contribution remain a useful dossier arrangement; their arrows must not claim a securely verified six-step chronology.

New extract: C31G02

Source: catalogue p. 382, entry 1042, reported excipit; selected text from khāmisuhā through ḥattā yaṣduqa al-qaḍiyya. The catalogue—not the original terminal folio—is the immediate witness. The manuscript folio and speaker have not been established.

> خامسها: أن يكون مناط صدق القضية الإنصاف بالمحكوم به وح (وحينئذ) إن كان الإنصاف بالعنوان في نفس الأمر كانت القضية صادقة من غير اعتبار تعليق كقولنا: كل كاتب متحرك بالأصابع ما دام كاتباً، أما إذا لم يكن الإنصاف بالعنوان بحسب نفس الأمر، بل بحسب الفرض فقط فلا بد من اعتبار التعلق بذلك الوصف حتى يصدق القضية

The fifth [case]: the ground of a proposition’s truth is [the subject’s] qualification by what is predicated of it. Then, if qualification under the subject-description obtains in the fact of the matter (nafs al-amr), the proposition is true without considering a conditional dependence, as when we say: “Every writer moves the fingers for as long as he is writing.” But if qualification under that description does not obtain in the fact of the matter, and obtains only by supposition, dependence on that description must be taken into account for the proposition to be true.

Apparatus and limits. The printed catalogue repeatedly has الإنصاف, literally fairness, where the logical context calls for الاتصاف, qualification. The Arabic above preserves the print; the translation explicitly adopts the latter as a conjectural normalization. This is not a manuscript variant verified by collation. The abbreviation `وح` and catalogue expansion `(وحينئذ)` are retained. The text’s shift from `تعليق` to `التعلق` is also retained.

The untranslated remainder of the catalogue’s excipit reads:

> إذ لم يعتبروا حكم ثبوت المحمول للموضوع بحسب الواقع، لا على تقدير الإنصاف بالعنوان لم يصدق قطعاً كما في المعدوم المطلق، فإن الحكم بامتناع الحكم عليه إذعاني.

The connection beginning `إذ لم يعتبروا حكم` is syntactically uncertain in this print. It must not silently become an invented fluent sentence. The final example names “the absolutely nonexistent” and an assentive judgment about the impossibility of judging it. Restoring the argument requires the original terminal folio and preceding context. The present translation therefore stops before that uncertain connective, and is marked selected, not complete.

The excerpt connects usefully to debates 08 (absent and negative objects) and 09 (nafs al-amr and objective truth): it distinguishes a subject-description that obtains in the fact of the matter from one introduced by supposition. It does not establish Ghiyāth’s theory of the Necessary Existent or of unity of existence. The word “fifth” belongs to a missing preceding division. Its referent must remain open.

Speaker layers

C31G01’s catalogue quotation identifies the son as “Ghiyāth, known as Manṣūr.” Later on the same page the catalogue reports a new first-person speaker, “Ṣadr al-Ḥusaynī al-Shīrāzī,” beginning an account of his own notes on the New Commentary. This visible shift prevents attribution of every subsequent first-person sentence to the son. The catalogue’s excipit omits the local quotation markers; C31G02 therefore names the attributed work, while leaving its individual speaker unresolved. Polemical descriptions of al-Jalāl are an authorial stance within the reported text, not a neutral finding about Dawwānī’s truthfulness.

Acquired previews and access boundary

- `ragip809-title-thumbnail.jpg`: 268 × 401 pixels, a title/ownership spread. Readable institutional stamp supports the Ragıp 809 identification; most handwritten title matter is too small for a secure complete transcription.

- `ragip809-preview-spread.jpg`: 2487 × 2245 pixels, a dense interior spread. The left upper corner displays ٧. This could be pagination or foliation; without adjacent images it is not promoted to a certain folio locator. It is not the opening and is not the complete manuscript. The source’s own public Drive metadata identifies it as a screenshot JPEG.

The preview is preserved as direct photographic evidence, but no uncertain handwriting or unresolved speaker has been promoted into a publication-ready doctrinal translation. Its association with the title thumbnail relies on the public listing. A continuous sequence would be needed to secure that identification fully. Public listing.
