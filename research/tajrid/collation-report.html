Second critical collation: continuous witnesses and corrections

31 October 2026. Eight new records bring the cumulative register to 224; the thirty debates and all 837 inherited Tusi units remain. Twenty-six continuation records have been added to the inherited 198. Counts measure source records, not completely collated works.

Iṣfahānī and Jurjānī: persistence

C31I03 transcribes and translates Iṣfahānī §77, printed II p.225 (PDF227). C31I04 supplies the complete §78 objection and reply across printed226–227 (PDF228–229). C31J02 gives Jurjānī77.1 including the temporal-origination alternative, his sustained contingency reply, the sun analogy and critique of the builder analogy. C31J03 and C31J04 translate the objection/reply portion of78.1 and complete78.2.

The former snippets WIT11–13 remain in the archive and reader with links to the directly inspected continuation. The crucial qualification is that making a thing persist is not producing an externally existent relational property. Jurjānī calls the qualification and its duration iʿtibārī while affirming their dependence on the agent. Do not collapse this distinction into either an independently produced entity called persistence or a denial of continued dependence.

The editions place Iṣfahānī’s main text above a distinct Jurjānī gloss. Neither author’s words are silently assigned to the other. Arabic transcription retains lexical wording, normalizes punctuation, and omits editor footnote markers. This is edition-level checking, not a fresh collation of the manuscripts behind the edition.

Source: Tasdīd, volume II.

Qūshjī: the reported interpretation and three objections

C31Q04–05 cover printed173–174, from وقد قيل في شرحه through لغوا محضا. The impersonally reported interpretation precedes أقول: فيه نظر, the return to Qūshjī’s own voice. WIT14 is retained as the mediated witness through Lāhījī.

Against the inherited mediated transcription, the direct edition reads المتقابلين rather than المقابلين, سلب للوجود rather than سلب الموجود, and لغوا محضا rather than لغوا. The second objection is substantially fuller; وقد before يعقلان معا in the inherited third objection is absent here. These are readings of this edition compared with the preserved transcription. Lāhījī’s relevant page has not been newly facsimile-collated in this second pass, so no claim of an authorial alteration or manuscript stemma follows.

Source: Qūshjī, volume I.
