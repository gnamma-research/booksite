The Avicennian Summa · Critical translations · Working edition, 30 October 2026

From Ibn Sīnā to the late Peripatetics

An integrated, evidence-graded edition of arguments on essence, existence, causation, substance, cognition, motion, divine agency and cosmic unity. The chapters reconstruct the disagreements between Ibn Sīnā, Rāzī, Ṭūsī, Lāhījī, Ṣadrā and Narāqī, alongside original Thomistic witnesses.

What is and isn't completed

These chapters include multiple bounded translated arguments and directly identified sources, including a complete Lāhījī existence argument, a detailed jaʿl objection–reply sequence, a primary Ibn Sīnā–Aquinas confrontation on natural generation, and Narāqī's critique of existential monism. They are not an exhaustive critical edition of the twenty-domain tradition: some arguments are excerpted, reported quotations await independent collation, and manuscript witness comparison remains outstanding.

Twenty-domain evidence audit

The ten chapters below are the translated backbone of the larger system. See the twenty-domain systematic findings and evidence audit for what each domain now establishes and what remains uncollated. Editorial method

Every primary passage has a link and locator; the category of evidence is stated. Quoted or paraphrased discussions by other philosophers in a commentary are separated from the author's response, and printed variant readings are not misrepresented as new manuscript collations. The GitHub reading links render the full scholarly Markdown; each chapter also has an offline Markdown source link for the downloadable Atlas.
