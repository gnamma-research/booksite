# Mature Avicennism and Aristotelian–Thomistic metaphysics
## Research programme 1: four causes, Giver of Forms, and natural agency
**Status:** source-indexed comparative research plan and preliminary analytical dossier, 30 October 2026. This is not a completed textual collation. The source locators are starting points; page-precise Arabic quotations and variant readings remain to be checked before publication.

### Governing question

Does later Avicennian philosophy preserve Ibn Sīnā's assignment of substantial-form bestowal to the Active Intellect, or reinterpret natural agents as genuinely producing substantial forms? This question is independent of the common affirmation of continuous existential dependence.

### Six questions in their logical order

1. **Four causes and the substance they explain.** Define matter, substantial form, efficient agency and end in Ibn Sīnā's *Shifāʾ* (Physics and Metaphysics); distinguish *form as an intrinsic constituent* from *the source from which form is received*.
2. **Natural preparation and the Giver of Forms.** Identify exactly what a natural generator does (*iʿdād*, disposing matter) and what the Active Intellect does (*ifāḍat al-ṣuwar*); do not infer that the natural generator has no causal efficacy.
3. **The unity of the composite.** Ask whether natural form is intrinsic, how the composite persists and how individuation and matter interact; distinguish the efficient cause of generation from the sustaining source of existence.
4. **Post-classical objections.** Compare Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī's objections, Ṭūsī's response, and later Tajrīd/Mawāqif debates on substance, form, generation and celestial/intellectual causes.
5. **Ṣadrā and the later Peripatetic response.** Separate substantial motion, primacy/gradation of existence, and the status of separate intellects. Translate Lāhījī, Narāqī, Khwānsārī and Jilwa before assigning each a position on the Giver of Forms.
6. **Aquinas and mature Thomism.** Determine precisely whether proximate natural agents educe forms from matter and how God's universal causation concurs without replacing them. Test whether the difference from Avicenna is an additional intermediary, a difference in agency, or a deeper disagreement about being.

### Primary-source acquisition order

**A. Ibn Sīnā**: *al-Shifāʾ*, *al-Samāʿ al-ṭabīʿī* on the principles of natural substances, *al-Kawn wa-l-fasād* on generation and corruption, *al-Ilāhiyyāt* II.4, IV.2, VI.1–2, IX.5; *al-Ishārāt*, natural philosophy and metaphysics. Extract complete argument units and identify the precise Active Intellect passages, not merely general summaries.

**B. The Rāzī–Ṭūsī axis**: Rāzī's *Sharḥ al-Ishārāt* and *al-Mabāḥith al-mashriqiyya*, Ṭūsī's *Ḥall mushkilāt al-Ishārāt*, and Quṭb al-Dīn al-Rāzī's *al-Muḥākamāt*. Classify objections as (i) to hylomorphism, (ii) to the separate intellect, (iii) to causal necessitation, (iv) to proof of generation.

**C. Philosophical kalām**: Ṭūsī's *Tajrīd*, al-Iṣfahānī's and al-Qūshjī's commentaries, al-Jurjānī's *Sharḥ al-Mawāqif*, al-Taftāzānī's *Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid*, Lāhījī's *Shawāriq*. Record the specific doctrinal and demonstrative changes rather than assuming school continuity.

**D. Post-Ṣadrā**: Ṣadrā's *Asfār* and *Taʿlīqāt al-Shifāʾ*, Khwānsārī's glosses, Narāqī's *Sharḥ al-Ilāhiyyāt* and *Jāmiʿ al-afkār*, Jilwa's Shifāʾ/Ishārāt/Asfār annotations, and Khayrābādī's *al-Hadiyya al-saʿīdiyya*. Treat the incomplete commentaries as incomplete. The 1904 Khayrābādī printing is catalogued at https://ddl.ae/book/95961; inspect the pages before quoting it.

**E. Aquinas**: *De principiis naturae*, *De ente et essentia*, *De potentia* q.3, *Summa theologiae* I qq.44–45, 104–105, and relevant *In Physicorum* and *De generatione* discussions. For each causal issue separate natural generation, creation ex nihilo, conservation, instrumental causation and finality.

### First argument: why the two accounts cannot yet be declared equivalent

- **Shared:** A generated thing has intrinsic form, and contingent existence requires an ultimate ground.
- **Disputed:** Does the proximate generator truly bring about the substantial form, or does it merely prepare the matter for a separate intellect to bestow that form?
- **Not settled by conservation:** The proposition that God sustains every creature does not specify the causal source of substantial form in ordinary generation.
- **Necessary proof:** Find late Peripatetic statements explicitly affirming or denying the Active Intellect's form-bestowing function; compare the causal vocabulary (*iʿdād*, *ifāḍa*, *ījād*, *tawlīd*, *jaʿl*) against Aquinas's *educere formam de potentia materiae*.
- **Possible outcomes:** (a) unchanged Avicennian hierarchy; (b) stronger natural causality within the hierarchy; (c) rejection/replacement of the hierarchy; (d) several incompatible late Avicennian systems. The evidence must choose, not an imposed narrative.

### Explicitly connected future chapters

1. **Natural generation → substantial motion:** if a form is received, does the substrate remain numerically identical during change? Lāhījī's objection to Ṣadrā becomes central.
2. **Form and composite → existence/quiddity:** is the cause of form also the cause of particular existence? Compare Lāhījī's *Shawāriq* I:159 with Narāqī's *Jāmiʿ* I:38–42.
3. **Causality → regress:** what kinds of dependent causal orders need a first source? Do not conflate an infinite temporal sequence with an essentially ordered hierarchy.
4. **Plurality → waḥdat al-wujūd:** distinguish shared meaning of existence, gradation, and the numerical/metaphysical unity thesis.
5. **Soul and intellect → cognition:** the Active Intellect's role in human abstraction may differ from its role in substantial generation; prove or deny the connection explicitly.

### Editorial completion criteria

A topic is **source-complete** only when the full Arabic/Persian passages for each principal author, edition/page locators, textual variants, and all objection–reply sequences are included. A topic is **analytically complete** only when doctrinal agreement and disagreement are separately adjudicated and the AT comparison is grounded in exact Thomistic loci. At present this dossier is a research architecture and preliminary analysis, **not** a finished critical translation or collation.
