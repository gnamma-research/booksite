**31 October 2026 · cumulative research handoff**

## 1. Separate the two projects by historical object, not arbitrary filenames

**Tajrīd Sourcebook (connected, independent project):** `research/tajrid/`, [public reader](https://gnamma-research.github.io/booksite/research/tajrid/). Its [current crosswalk](../../../tajrid/bookmap-crosswalk.json) contains **224 source records**, **837 inherited Ṭūsī text units**, **47 named/contextual works**. These are heterogeneous record statuses, not 224 fully checked primary texts. Tajrīd owns direct matn and lemma-level collation: Ṭūsī → Ḥillī/Iṣfahānī/Jurjānī → Qūshjī → Dawwānī–Dashtakī/Khafrī → Lāhījī/Yazdī and related gloss networks. Preserve the important correction that *al-Sayyid al-Sanad* ordinarily refers to al-Sayyid al-Sharīf al-Jurjānī rather than Ṣadr al-Dīn al-Dashtakī.

**Late Avicennism After the Asfār (this project):** the **new and changed philosophical positions in independent later works**, especially after the Ṣadrian synthesis, and the effects of the earlier commentary tradition in Persian, Ottoman and Indian discussions of reality, causation, natural form, cognition, divine will and practical science. Shared terminology is no longer permission to attach an unrelated paragraph to a heading.

[Machine-readable exact overlap](../../../avicennism/TAJRID_DEDUP_AND_POST_ASFAR_SCOPE.json) documents a crucial correction: exact *Shawāriq* pp.90–91 already appear as Tajrīd **NEW05/NEW06**, and *Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid* p.79 as **SN01/SN03**. These are shared passages; do not independently count them as new witnesses. Astarābādī *Barāhīn* II:96–99 here differs from Tajrīd's **volume I** records COLL26–28/EXT14. Composite Jurjānī editions have not yet been proven identical by page.

**New book-level distinction:** Lāhījī's Persian *Gawhar-i murād* and *Sarmāya-i īmān* are **different authored works** from his Arabic *Shawāriq* gloss on *Tajrīd*. Reconstruct them as such.

### The argument must be read in this actual order

**What is created? (I–II)** → **How is procession interpreted? (III)** → **How can plurality proceed from the One? (IV)** → **What accounts for elemental differences and preparation? (V)** → **Which source bestows forms and souls? (VI)** → **Why does an event occur at this time? (VII)** → **How is divine willing to be conceived? (VIII)**.

This makes it possible to test whether Lāhījī actually changed specific Avicennian commitments instead of relying on modern labels.

**Textual caveat:** the unit is a page-ordered, detailed **working English rendering** of identified textual arguments with short Persian source anchors, not word-for-word verified complete dual-language transcription; printed A/B/C variants remain editor-reported. Citation of Avicenna/Bahmanyār, Rāzī, Ṭūsī, Suhrawardī or unnamed Sufis inside Lāhījī is **reported speech**, not independent direct collation of those authors.

## 3. Distinctive post-Asfār questions now have separate dossiers

[Machine-readable 12 dossiers](../../../avicennism/POST_ASFAR_DISTINCTIVE_DOSSIERS.json) and the reader's **What changed after Asfār?** route distinguish (1) made quiddity vs particular existence, (2) unity grounded in dependence, (3) variable number and functions of intellects, (4) natural form bestowal, (5) causal time specificity, (6) divine riḍā and voluntariness, (7) second intentions, (8) substantial motion and its undercollated critics, (9) Ottoman natural philosophy, (10) Indian teaching and physical change, (11) Qajar debate, and (12) knowledge as being.

**Primary-evidenced positions:** Lāhījī *Gawhar* I–VIII and Narāqī's two separate works yield original textual arguments, not simply secondary literature. Contrast the **Gawhar II actual-quiddity-as-made thesis** with **Narāqī's *al-Lamaʿāt* p.72,p.82 existence-first idiom** without assuming direct historical polemic.

**Not yet directly established:** full Ottoman Abharī–Maybudī–Lārī primary chain, Qajar Jilwa/Sabzawārī/Zunūzī direct passages, original Khayrābādī al-Hadiyya, full Asfār comparative counterpart, and the original Qūshjī/Khafrī/Siyālkūtī witness layers. Those remain bibliographical leads or mediated testimony and are not presented as complete philosophical conclusions.

## 5. What is complete and what cannot be claimed

**Completed:** source-graded eight-section *Gawhar* Book III working edition (32 consecutive pages), separate later Persian studies, cross-project source ownership, historical gaps, proper argument linkage, portable reader changes, and source-method validation once CI passes.

**Not complete:** complete **late Avicennism** across centuries; full original-language sentence-by-sentence collation; all *Asfār* comparative originals; whole volumes of Mawāqif, Shawāriq, Qūshjī, Khafrī and later Indian/Ottoman manuscripts. Do not mark bibliographical titles or a loose single line as translated chapters. No promise of exact percentage of all tradition without a defensible corpus boundary.
