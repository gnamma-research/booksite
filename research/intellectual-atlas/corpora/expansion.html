How to read the catalogue

These are research leads, not a definitive or exhaustive inventory of the four madhhabs or the Arabic linguistic traditions. The raw OpenITI extraction uses work-level deduplication and inherited metadata tags. Historical grammar-lineage labels and balāgha pathways are optional, overlapping search routes. The manuscript shortlist is drawn only from publicly visible bibliographic tables, with each original catalogue record linked. School assignments inferred from the author rather than the individual book are explicitly weaker.

Do not take a book's presence under a school as evidence of the school's settled doctrine; do not turn a shared text-title keyword into an assertion of commentary or influence. Multi-volume completeness, manuscripts, physical holdings, printed editions, translations and passage alignment still require examination.
