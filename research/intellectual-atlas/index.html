About this research · PDFs & data

Scope and method

The register brings together 331 works, work-series and contextual entries, with 312 individually qualified textual relationships. It traces ancient and late antique antecedents, translation networks, and Avicennan, Illuminationist, Akbarian, Sunni and Imami traditions into their later continuations. The historical display includes 45 political periods and 36 events.

Empire durations sit above the same calendar axis as the works. Event lines extend through the text field; arrows between works identify commentary, criticism, citation, transmission or another specified relationship. Select a work, event, period or arrow to inspect its dates, evidence and sources.

Coverage is selective and the atlas remains open to correction. A latest possible date is not a composition year; many records retain upper-bound marks. Colors identify reception families rather than exclusive doctrinal identities. Chronological overlap does not establish influence or patronage. The Evidence & audit view retains the critical revisions and disputed connections.

The 84-page reference register covers the earlier revision-4 corpus; ten additional sourced ancient bridge entries appear in the live web edition. The 204-page illustrated atlas and the 15-page historical overview preserve earlier diagrams and should be read with the new material. None of these reproduces complete third-party source books or manuscript scans. Original date wording and qualifications remain available in each record.

New research branch: تهذيب المنطق والكلام (1387). The 26 new records distinguish the extensive logic-commentary families (Dawwānī, Yazdī, Lāhījī, al-Khubayṣī and their successors) from later commentaries specifically on the kalām section. All manuscript and publication bounds remain qualified. Read the critical genealogy and evidence.
