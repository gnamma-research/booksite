A different way to read history

In Matn mode, follow the historical narrative without references interrupting it. In Matn + Sharḥ, the commentary gives the Qurʾānic and ḥadīth sources, historical reconstruction, relationships between institutions and later spiritual interpretations. Seven figures show the geography, chronology, social arrangements and intellectual transmission.

The migrants reached Yathrib with their religious fellowship intact but without much of the property, kin protection or livelihood that had supported them in Makkah. Their hosts cultivated dates, lived among fortified neighbourhoods and remained bound by obligations to their own families and allies. Editorial status

This is a working scholarly synthesis, not an edition claiming comprehensive collation of Sīrah manuscripts. The Sunni narrative provides the organizing framework while Shiʿi perspectives are presented at consequential historical points. The source dossiers document selected primary comparisons and indicate where evidence remains incomplete.
