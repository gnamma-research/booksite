## Governing distinction

The vertical order explains comprehensiveness, mediation and the ordering of meaning. The reported act realizes guidance among determinate persons and circumstances. The normative order includes the truth and scope of legal and practical determinations. The historical order involves real causes—language, households, resources, offices, schools, statutes and technologies. None of these levels may be substituted for another. The matn tells the story, the sharḥ develops the intelligible relations, and exact textual verification remains accountable to primary witnesses.

## Overall acceptance and remaining evidentiary controls

- The canonical text has **46** matns and **46** shurūḥ, distributed across **seven books**; paragraphs are addressed in the static reader and reflected in the complete versioned manuscript.
- The integrated work advances beyond a generic claim of comprehensiveness through the crying child, the Ghāmidī woman, al-Ḥudaybiyyah, Khaybar, institutional fiqh, spiritual inheritance, the family waqf and other specific scenes.
- The public reader’s status notices, progress messages, experimental-reader links and editorial dossier cross-references are removed. This audit and dated prior versions remain in the editorial directory.
- **No claim of full primary manuscript collation**: critical reading must check early narrative variants, translations of later metaphysical texts, legal recensions, regional chronicles and archives. The table identifies especially consequential witness controls.
- Full migration of remaining in-prose scholarly attributions into footnotes, without losing necessary distinctions between historical actors and later interpreters, is **not certified complete**. The experimental source-footnote presentation in `read-preview-1.9.0.html` supplies a reference model; do not replace the current full 46-chapter reader with its abbreviated three-chapter content.
- Specialist review should test the distinctions among principial claims, legal normativity and empirical historical causes, without confusing a compelling interpretation with documentary proof.
