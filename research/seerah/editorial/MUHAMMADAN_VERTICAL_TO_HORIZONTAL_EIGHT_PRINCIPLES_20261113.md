# From the Muḥammadan Reality to the particular encounter
## Eight generative principles for the historical Sīrah and the study of ʿumrān

*Research companion · 13 November 2026. Interpretive synthesis. Primary text reports and the source-critical status of later metaphysical passages must be distinguished. This companion sits outside continuous matn and sharḥ.*

### The question of derivation

The Muḥammadan Reality, in the metaphysical account used by this work, is a principial reality through which differentiated perfections are intelligible as one ordered disclosure. The historical Prophet ﷺ is the final bearer of revelation and the concrete human exemplar in whom knowledge, character, worship, judgment and service are enacted together. The relations among these levels can illuminate history if their *kinds of derivation* are kept distinct:

- **Ontological / principial:** the interpretation of the comprehensive Name, the levels of manifestation and the perfected human as the gathering of differentiated meanings.
- **Formal / exemplary:** a living act embodies multiple rightful relations in one occasion. The act has an intelligible unity in its real agent and circumstances.
- **Normative / disciplinary:** Qurʾān, Sunnah, juristic method, transmitted reports and practical learning yield binding rules, valid qualifications, character formation and teachable capacities.
- **Historical / efficient:** households, scholars, social conditions, contracts, administrators, trade, state power, ecological constraints and material technologies account for the formation of concrete institutions.

The vertical order supplies an orientation and an account of the originating completeness; the horizontal order has its own demonstrable causes. A sound explanation crosses between them through reported acts and historically documented transmissions.

### 1. Barzakh: differentiated relations joined in one situation

The *barzakh* joins distinct orders while maintaining their specificity. The human being is receptive to intelligible meaning and to the sensible world through body, soul and heart. Prophetic conduct can consequently have spiritual, legal, household and political significance within one real occasion.

**Horizontal implication:** a judicial case involving a penitent mother concerns repentance, public proof, punishment, pregnancy, care of a child and communal speech. The report supplies the encounter; legal schools determine distinct juridical questions; character formation concerns the judgment and dispositions of real people. A barzakhic reading explains the meaningful conjunction without converting each aspect into the same discipline.

### 2. The two eyes: universal orientation and accurate knowledge of the particular

The comprehensive station gathers an orientation toward God and the actuality of the person encountered. A partial standpoint can be perfectly true under a determinate aspect. The mature agent has the further task of seeing how particular truths belong within an ordered whole.

**Horizontal implication:** when the Prophet ﷺ shortens prayer upon hearing a crying child, the intention to worship, the child's need and the mother's distress are held together. Ritual norms determine permissible action; accurate perception determines what this congregation needs. A subsequent imam requires both juridical competence and attention to the persons for whom he acts.

### 3. The return: spiritual ascent made effective through bodily practice

The Miʿrāj exemplifies a movement from higher disclosure back to the conditions of earthly life. Its narrated result includes the five daily prayers through which bodies, time, place, households and congregations are recurrently ordered toward God. The ascent culminates in a responsible return.

**Horizontal implication:** the prayer teaches orientation to the divine across a lived day; formation of a community requires instruction, physical spaces, accessible times and knowledgeable leadership. Those arrangements develop institutionally through actual teachers and builders.

### 4. Form and meaning: revelation embodied rather than merely repeated

The world may be contemplated as a manifest Book; the Qurʾān is the revealed verbal address; the Prophet's speech, actions, qualities and character are its enactment within a human life. This threefold relation is an interpretive account of correspondence across forms of disclosure, with the Qurʾān retaining its unique revelatory authority.

**Horizontal implication:** the later schools interpret the reported words and actions through specialized practices. The living formation of a student also demands imitation, correction, understanding and practical judgment. A correct formulation remains valuable because it truthfully bears a normative aspect of the original.

### 5. Real formal unity: the whole acts through differentiated faculties

The comprehensive person is a real unity of intellect, imagination, appetite, will, heart and embodied capacity. The relation resembles the unity of a living organism more than the aggregation of disconnected techniques.

**Horizontal implication:** a political office can coordinate law, revenue and protection while remaining answerable to their several proper ends. The jurist's competence, the administrator's skill and the ethical formation of each can be coordinated in a legitimate public order. Institutional specialization becomes a useful mode of transmission when integrated through truthful governance.

### 6. Takhalluq and tahaqquq: from repeated exercise to realized presence

Acquiring forms of good conduct through repeated action (*takhalluq*) develops habits and stable capacities (*malakāt*). More comprehensive realization (*taḥaqquq*) concerns a depth of spiritual understanding and presence. The different achievements are joined through living practice while retaining distinct meanings.

**Horizontal implication:** a teacher who repeatedly corrects recitation or conduct makes knowledge transmissible as a skill; a household that practices mercy and responsibility provides the setting in which virtues become operative. Spiritual companionship may cultivate further modes of attention, whose specific claims belong to a distinct spiritual inquiry.

### 7. Inheritance through works, states and knowledge

Prophetic inheritance includes reported words, practical works, spiritually formative states and different kinds of knowledge. Their transmission requires different kinds of evidence and human preparation. The final commission remains unique; later people participate in its guidance through specified responsibilities and degrees of realization.

**Horizontal implication:** hadith criticism determines transmission; fiqh and uṣūl determine legal consequences; moral formation concerns dispositions; public institutions provide the setting for recurring duties. None of these activities can be eliminated from the study of an Islamic civilization. Each has a positive field of truth and competence.

### 8. The three scales of practical wisdom and the open future of ʿumrān

Human action operates through the formation of the person (*tahdhīb al-akhlāq*), the management of family and immediate community (*tadbīr al-manzil*) and the ordering of public life (*al-siyāsa al-madaniyya*). Their relations are simultaneous. Fiqh articulates normative determinations within each; practical competence addresses the actual means available and the effects of decisions.

**Horizontal implication:** a landowner asks about an asset, acquires an enduring charitable form for its use, and becomes a precedent through which jurists develop the endowment. Subsequent historical actors create deeds, courts and organizations. A family endowment brought to the Privy Council in 1894 reveals the changing institutions that determine such obligations. This chain is provable only at the points for which reports, books and documents survive.

### A comparative explanatory matrix

| Principial category | Embodied question | Normative / formative distinction | Historical evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barzakh | Which distinct claims meet here? | Public judgment, household responsibility, spiritual intention | Reports of the encounter and its participants |
| Two eyes | How are enduring ends determined in this case? | Valid legal form, awareness of actual persons | Variant reports, juristic analysis and case detail |
| Return | How does spiritual disclosure become temporal practice? | Worship, habituation, teaching, leadership | Transmission, congregations, buildings and learning |
| Form and meaning | How does a revealed sign become lived understanding? | Recitation, understanding, action, character | Reports, teaching lineages and texts |
| Formal unity | What coordinates specialized excellences? | Justice, craft, expertise and intention | Institutions, office-holders, material settings |
| Takhalluq / tahaqquq | How is knowledge incorporated into persons? | Habit, acquired virtue, received states | Education, companions, first-person testimonies |
| Triple inheritance | Which element of guidance is being carried? | Narration, law, ethical formation and spiritual realization | Scholarly chains, practices, textual institutions |
| Three practical orders | Whose rights and responsibilities are engaged? | Ethics, household care, public policy and fiqh | Contracts, cases, revenue, state decisions |

### Limits of what can be claimed

1. The metaphysical account gives a coherent interpretation of why the prophetic acts are intelligible as integrated manifestations. Its universal and ontological claims are philosophical–theological; historical research does not prove them by collecting enough political examples.
2. A report establishes observable action and reported speech within the limits of transmission. The historian must resist inventing an unreported inner state to make every action fit an image of spiritual perfection.
3. A rule's legitimacy is argued through legal evidence and methodology. Spiritual description does not annul a binding norm.
4. A named later institution has ascertainable contingent causes. Similarity to an earlier prophetic case can identify a generative orientation; historical transmission requires further documentary proof.
