## Source-critical controls

The Nūḥ narrative retains the Ark's construction before the flood and does not impute intentions beyond Qurʾān 11:37–47. The original Christian court's protection of the migrants is distinguished from the Negus's later standing in al-Bukhārī 3877 and 1333. The founding of Deoband is variously dated 1866 in its own history and 1867 by Barbara Metcalf and SherAli Tareen; the date remains contested pending direct calendar and institutional collation. Aligarh's 1875 school and 1877 college are distinguished in the institution's published history. The Bengal material distinguishes the 1459 inscription associated with Khān Jahān from retrospective local stories. The modern chapter distinguishes political transitions and an *ongoing* digital legal-text research project from completed scholarly infrastructure.
