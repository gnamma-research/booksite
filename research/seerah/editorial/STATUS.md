# Sīrah — research release status

Source: *Sīrah and the Making of Islamic Civilization*, source-critical integrated study edition 1.4, with a direct-prose editorial revision 1.4.1, 46 matns and 46 shurūḥ. The stylistic changes did not involve new source collation.

**Public label:** Research edition · under review. Not a definitive critical edition or manuscript collation of the early Sīrah texts.

The 46 narrative passages form the matn; the 46 matching commentaries contain references, historical argument and later spiritual interpretations. Nine critical dossiers belong to the research apparatus, not to a third continuous commentary layer.

Limitations: primary Arabic witnesses have not been collated comprehensively, not all proposed historical transmissions are documentarily established, and comparative metaphysical readings remain interpretive. The Sunni narrative forms the organizing framework while Shiʿi perspectives are represented at relevant historical points.

Figures in this section use **schematic and interpretive** visualizations, never purporting to be exact seventh-century route reconstructions. Indicative geographic positions must not be interpreted as excavation-quality sites.

Revised source files are in `data/`, with the complete original 1.4 manuscript preserved at `editorial/source-edition-1.4.md`. Future revisions must modify the structured source and regenerate views, not manually alter the generated reader alone.
