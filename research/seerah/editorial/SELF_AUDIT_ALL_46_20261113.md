# Independent Audit · Sīrah research edition 1.8.0
## Each of 46 matns and shurūḥ assessed against the original metaphysical model

**Date:** 13 November 2026  
**Editorial finding:** The 1.8.0 restoration recovered significant metaphysical content in the opening but retained bibliographic and school labels within the continuous text. The 46-chapter argument has still to be systematically regenerated from the original model. The numerical audit below locates stylistic symptoms; the substantive editorial decisions are separately authored for each chapter.

### Binding rules for the next edition

1. The matn speaks in its own voice. It presents revealed narratives, metaphysical principles and historical actions without names of scholarly interpreters, their books, or schools used as labels of authority.
2. The sharḥ supplies explanatory argument, precise distinctions and evidential reasoning. Its prose stays focused on the matter itself. Names of commentators, historians and works, citations, variants, methodological qualifications and bibliographical data belong in **numbered sharḥ footnotes**.
3. Named people whose actions *constitute the history*—prophets, companions, rulers and concrete historical actors—may appear as subjects of the narration; this is distinct from bibliographic credit or "according to" attributions.
4. Practical philosophy has three dimensions: formation of the person (*tahdhīb al-akhlāq*, *tadbīr al-nafs*), order of household and near community (*tadbīr al-manzil*), and public and political order (*tadbīr al-madīna* / *al-siyāsa al-madaniyya*). They are concurrent perspectives on lived action, not a rigid ladder in which every encounter has exactly one of each.
5. Fiqh and uṣūl specify normative determinations, criteria and methods throughout these practical domains. The revealed guidance provides obligations and limits; ethical and spiritual wisdom shapes the discernment required as circumstances differ. The precise institutional forms of government, finance, administration and education emerge through additional practical knowledge and valid interpretive methods.
6. The original metaphysical account of the divine Names, the Fall and Iblīs's restricted vision has editorial priority. Recover the earlier extended research on self-confirming devotion, *wahm*, providence and repentance. The Muhammadan Reality supplies the comprehensive prophetic principle and appears through the historical Prophet's uniquely integrated actions. Later sciences are genuine differentiated inheritances and each historical institution has ascertainable causes.
7. The matn and sharḥ explain positively. Negation is retained only where it proves a real difference or guards against a definite error. Decorative "not this but that" formulas are deleted.
8. Revision is judged by explanatory steps: metaphysical ground → situated person and act → ethical / household / political order → normative extraction → pathways of formation and transmission → particular, evidenced historical *ʿumrān*. Each step requires its proper kind of evidence. Do not assert a causal historical genealogy merely from metaphysical analogy.

**Statistics from the current structured text (pattern-based, approximate):** 9791 matn words and 27128 sharḥ words. 82 selected negative constructions in matns and 391 in shurūḥ. These are counts of word patterns, not a judgment that every negative construction is wrong.

### Priority and quality gates

**P0 — structurally decisive (6):** 1, 11, 13, 28, 33, 44. These govern metaphysical beginning, everyday acts, juristic derivation, realized inheritance and the major public-law historical proof.

**P1 — substantial recomposition (19):** 2, 7, 8, 10, 12, 16, 17, 19, 20, 23, 24, 27, 29, 30, 31, 32, 43, 45, 46. These carry the missing links and intermediate social mechanisms.

**P2 — targeted integration (21):** 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 14, 15, 18, 21, 22, 25, 26, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42. These retain useful factual sequences while acquiring a generating question and human-level mechanism.

**Editorial verdict:** Mechanical source/reader parity and the presence of metaphysical vocabulary are inadequate criteria for completeness. Many later matns are only ~150–200 words and their shurūḥ only ~200–500; these passages often describe rather than explain. The opening and Chapter 13 contain substantial insights but require clearer hierarchy, stronger authentic episode handling and footnote attribution. The title and arrangement of the 46 chapters can survive; their explanatory joins require systematic revision.

**Source controls:** Sīrah 1.4.1, the earlier detailed *Matn 1* working research with 23 notes, the separate ʿAyn al-Quḍāt / *Ṭawāsīn* discussions, the Akbarian methodological research, the 1.6 source-critical dossiers and 1.7 historical microcases. The original source-critical statuses remain open, especially independent Arabic collation of interpretive passages. This audit documents failures and defines the next version; it does not claim that all 46 are corrected.
