# The Tajrīd Debates — editorial and source status

**Working sourcebook, 29 October 2026. Not a definitive critical edition.**

## Scope
The quoted comparative research reader organizes **18 research questions** and **57 source selections**: 15 inherited passages from Fascicle I, 20 from the Akbarī–Ishrāqī fascicle, five from Hāshim al-Ṭihrānī’s *Tawḍīḥ al-murād*, and 17 further short Sunni and Illuminationist excerpts. A complete documentary appendix preserves the 40 inherited selections.

The record distinguishes direct *Tajrīd* commentaries, supercommentaries, parallel primary texts, and comparative secondary literature. **An author’s exposition of another school must not be treated as his own endorsement.** Source URLs and locators are included at extract level; some locators still require checking against independent printed editions or manuscript witnesses.

## Outstanding primary-source work
- Transcribe, collate and translate **all three direct Jurjānī *Tajrīd* mystical glosses** printed by Nicholas Heer and check their context in al-Iṣfahānī.
- Translate al-Lāhījī's complete **Ishāra ʿirfāniyya**, including objections and replies.
- Reconstruct the prolonged **Dawwānī–Dashtakī** exchange as a direct dialogue, preserving commentator and supercommentator voices.
- Expand **Quṭb al-Dīn al-Shīrāzī** from his own *Sharḥ Ḥikmat al-Ishrāq*, *Durrat al-Tāj*, and glosses on *Ḥikmat al-ʿAyn*; distinguish his interventions from borrowed exposition.
- Expand **Hāshim al-Ṭihrānī** beyond the five translated selections (including the proof of the truthful and prophecy).
- Complete topic-by-topic coverage from al-Ḥillī, al-Iṣfahānī, al-Jurjānī, al-Qūshjī, al-Lāhījī, Sunni *Mawāqif*/*Maqāṣid* traditions, and later glossators.

## Method and caution
Do not infer doctrinal consensus from an agreement in vocabulary. Distinguish *quwwa* as potentiality/faculty from *qudra* as power, *wujūb* from epistemic *ḍarūrī*, and Akbarī fixed entities from Muʿtazilī establishment of the nonexistent. Identify the original Arabic speaker and the editorial layer in every passage. The Aquinas/Aertsen paragraphs are comparative analyses, not quotations from *Tajrīd* commentators.

Work from the supplied original edition and translated source registers; undertake manuscript-level collation and independent philological proofreading before calling any portion definitive.
