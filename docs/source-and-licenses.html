Source accountability

Sources & editorial method

Each book has a separate evidence register. The collection's Arabic textual witnesses, new English translations, and modern scholarship do not all carry the same licensing or evidentiary status.

OpenITI Arabic text

The Arabic witness of al-Ghazālī's al-Mustaṣfā comes from OpenITI, version 0505Ghazali.Mustasfa.Shamela0005459-ara1. It has been adapted for page-by-page browser navigation under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. OpenITI's documentation explains the licence. Attribution and source references must be retained.

English translations

The English text is Gnamma's working translation from the Arabic, not a reproduction of Hammad's 1987 English dissertation. The Hammad scans are not uploaded publicly. The new edition has selected bilingual passages, not a complete English translation.

Technical vocabulary

Context-sensitive legal and logical terms are interpreted with help from scholarly research including Hallaq's A History of Islamic Legal Theories and Opwis's work on al-Ghazālī's syllogistic logic. These studies are cited as scholarship, not as interchangeable primary Arabic readings.
