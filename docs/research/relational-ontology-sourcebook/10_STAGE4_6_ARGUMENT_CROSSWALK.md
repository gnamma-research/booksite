## 2. The ontology/semantics distinctions emerging (analytical apparatus, NOT claims attributed wholesale to a single author)

A. **Actual constituents:** Are there existing wood pieces, soldiers, human participants? This can be true for aggregates.

B. **Configuration/determination:** Is there a determinate order, proportion, purpose, power, or causal disposition? It may be actual and effective without being a further substance.

C. **Unity:** Is the resultant unity real and substantial, real but non-substantial, or merely considered? *Maqāṣid* I106 and *Mawāqif* III59–60 question automatic substantial unity for artifice/army.

D. **Inherence:** Is there a numerically identical dependent feature established **in** a preconstituted subject? Does it mediate through another accident? This is distinct from being necessary as an object's constituent.

E. **Formal relative reference:** Is the object/quality understood *toward* another term? Direction of a relative determination should not be confused with a shared numerically identical accident in two termini.

F. **Cognition:** What occurs in an actual knower as psychic *ṣūra/kayfiyya*, and what is the known object/predicate's objective correctness? Quṭb's argument and al-Dasūqī's independent theological *iʿtibārī* cannot be conflated.

G. **Truth-ground:** A genuinely correct consideration is not an additional **existing object merely because it is true**. The positive ground may be actual beings, their dispositions, absence of an appropriate power, a nature's possibility, or a broader metaphysical explanation. The explanatory role of a distinct higher realm is a further theory.

## 3. Spiker audit implications

**Strongly supported narrower thesis:** “Some relationally specified objects, classifications and collective descriptions may be objectively true without corresponding to an independently subsisting extra natural substance.” This can be supported internally by Rāzī I61, Maqāṣid I106/I172, Mawāqif III60 and al-Dasūqī 129–130 (with author-specific qualifications).

**Unresolved broader thesis:** “All actual relations *qua relations* are necessarily only universal mind-dependent predicates, never individuated real accidents.” *Tasdīd* raises precise objections against a **shared** accident, not a complete demonstration of this universal thesis. Spiker SP12 stays inferential; do not manufacture agreement from heterogeneous texts.

**Independent metaphysical conclusion:** “Therefore all objective relational order or every universal/privation is causally effused from a separate prior exemplary realm.” None of the cited bed, genus, two-subject accident, or *iʿtibārī* distinctions alone entails it. A complete argument should state further principles of Divine Knowledge, exemplary causality, possibility, participation and alternative truthmaker accounts. Spiker SP17 remains a substantive open philosophical thesis.
