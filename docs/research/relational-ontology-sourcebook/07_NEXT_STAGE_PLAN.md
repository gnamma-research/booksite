## A. The next source tasks, in order

### Task A1 — al-Kātibī / Mubārakshāh / al-Jurjānī, Sharḥ Ḥikmat al-ʿAyn

Cited by Spiker note 132 p38, 133 pp43–44, note 136 p41, and note 144 p48. **This is the highest priority missing source** because its reported argument explains precisely how real wooden pieces plus relational *tartīb* can make a bed and what *laysa yastaqill bi-l-maʿqūliyya* means in the local commentary.

Available witness records:
- Library of Congress digital manuscript (multiple layers): https://www.loc.gov/resource/amed.2017454638/
- Searchable Arabic transcription (uncertain OCR): https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-hikmat-al-ayn/37
- Older Kazan printed commentary, 1321/1322 AH, appears in independent catalogue; establish exact edition.

**What to do**: 
1. Locate passage first by the distinctive Arabic phrases **al-qurb wa-l-ziyāda**, **al-sarīr**, **al-ajzāʾ al-khashabiyya**, **al-tartīb al-makhṣūṣ**, **lā yastaqill bi-l-maʿqūliyya** rather than page numbers alone.
2. Determine base matn al-Kātibī, commentary Mubārakshāh, and gloss al-Jurjānī (and whether later marginalia are by others).
3. Read backward to the first definition and forward through all objections, replies and conclusions, perhaps longer than Spiker's pp38–48.
4. Transcribe Arabic from legible source images when available; preserve source spelling, note alternative readings without silently normalizing.
5. Produce continuous English translation with apparatus and formal argument tree; add [08_DOSSIER_HIKMAT_AL_AYN.md] when a witness has been read.
6. Update historical lexicon headwords *jawhar, iʿtibār, khārij, qiyām, tartīb, hayʾa, ṣūra, taqarrur*, especially any explicit definitions.
7. Update SP-03, SP-04, SP-07–09 as textual evidence warrants, without assigning an author to a gloss not identified by manuscript layout.

Exit condition: full argument-bounded source verified against legible facsimile or high-quality scholarly transcription, matn/sharḥ/ḥāshiya attributions established, relevant Spiker note-by-note verdicts marked with confidence and limitations.
