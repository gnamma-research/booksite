# Hasan Spiker — claim-level critical audit v1

**Date:** 31 October 2026.
**Scope:** Things as They Are (2021), §2.3.2 printed pp67–81, numbered notes 127–165 and conclusion pp156–157. This is NOT a global evaluation of the book or a finished verification. It is a rigorously qualified first map. Each item records its evidential level from the ORIGINAL ARABIC CONTEXT where read, or says explicitly that original is unverified.

## Status legend

E = explicit in verified paginated Arabic HTML (but not yet collated to facsimile).
Q = explicitly qualified by original contextual argument.
R = position reported/quoted as a view, but not unambiguously adopted by the principal commentator.
I = interpretive addition or consequential premise supplied by Spiker.
C = broader context materially challenges an unqualified formulation; NOT a verdict that the original is 'false'.
U = original Arabic source witness not yet verified.

Use e.g. E+Q for claim fact supported but inference too broad. Distinguish factual evidence status from our philosophical evaluation.

## 04. Additional original-source conclusions found in wider context (not direct Spiker quotation)

S-EX1. **Maqāṣid I105:** The logical genus/difference can differ in the mind without corresponding two actual external components, even where matter and form are separately discussed. Implicates SP-03/04.

S-EX2. **Maqāṣid I106:** New *ṣūra jawhariyya* versus only accidental *mizāj* for medicines receives *fīhi taraddud* rather than a decisive ruling. Implicates SP-08/09/11.

S-EX3. **Maqāṣid I172:** 'form' of sword, bed, house can be an *accidental hayʾa* in matter. Implicates SP-08/09/11/20.

S-EX4. **Mawāqif III38:** 'khārijan ay ḥissan' and later alternative against dhihn. Implicates ALL assumptions concerning 'extramental'.

S-EX5. **Mawāqif III54:** non-inherence ≠ non-dependence, and bed constitution introduced under supposition *ʿalā taqdīr*. Implicates SP-03/09.

S-EX6. **Mawāqif III57–58:** inter-part need may be necessary but is not sufficient for one natural substance; collective form overgenerates. Implicates SP-03/05/09.

S-EX7. **Mawāqif III60:** the army's aggregate exists 'without doubt', but its additional unity is iʿtibārī. Implicates SP-05/06.

S-EX8. **Jurjānī Taʿrīfāt vs reported Bannānī:** strict mentally existing iʿtibārī vs two-sense account. Historical question, not instant 'contradiction'. Implicates SP-10.
