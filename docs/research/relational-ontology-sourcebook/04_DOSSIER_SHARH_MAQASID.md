# Source Dossier 01 — al-Taftāzānī, Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid I, 99–108; 171–173

**Working critical reconstruction v1 · 31 October 2026**

Source: Saʿd al-Dīn al-Taftāzānī, Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid, 1981-based paginated Arabic HTML as reproduced at usul.ai and almntqim.com. This dossier is **argument-bounded analysis of source Arabic actually read**, NOT a verified edition of continuous pages. The online text must be compared against an original paginated printed copy. Matn lemmas introduced by **qāla** and their prose discussion must be distinguished. Exact transcription/reproduction of full page is not yet licensed or collated; short source expressions and long contextual summaries below.

**Spiker anchors:** Things as They Are, §2.3.2 pp69–72, notes 129, 131, 135. The further, indispensable p171–173 passage is NOT indicated in those notes.

## Argument map — why pp. 99–108 form one unit

A. Consider a nature in itself and under added conditions; what distinguishes *mahiya* and logical consideration?  
B. What does it mean that a quiddity is simple or composite, and in what sense can a whole and part be identified?  
C. Does logical composition show real constituent parts? Are genus/differentia distinct in actual individuation?  
D. Is an 'army' or 'ten' a *real* whole or only an *iʿtibārī* unity? How does a genuine composite differ?  
E. Are all cases of mixed composition the same (wood/bed, medicine, hylomorphic body), or does a new substantial form need proof?  
F. When an actual quiddity exists, must the agent 'make' the quiddity, its existence, its existence-as-participated, or the conjunction of parts? This question supplies a causal continuation to the parts debate, not a detached topic.

## Page-by-page contextual synopsis

### I pp. 99–102 — preliminaries, simplicity and definition

The text considers quiddity under different intellectual respects and the kind of complexity required for definability through genus and differentia. Simplicity can mean not actually having parts, or it can mean simplicity relative to a particular complex whole. The mere sharing of an accidental predicate with another quiddity does not show that both are really composite. The overall argumentative target is not just the existence of actual parts but the criterion by which an apparent plurality within a definition warrants an ontology of distinct principles.

In Spiker §2.3.2, human/parrot illustrate a genus and differentiating characteristic. Footnote 129 points to Maqāṣid I102–103. His move from necessary essential features to actual real constituents needs this larger logical-ontological argument, not an isolated definition.

### I p. 103 — two different senses of simple and composite

The text explicitly distinguishes **ḥaqīqī** and **iḍāfī** simplicity/composition. Here *iḍāfī* means a thing taken **relative to a part** or as part of a wider whole; a substance can be simple in one respect and composite in another. It is not (at this locus) the assertion that the composition consists of a number of purely relational constituents. The whole/part comparison depends on whether the relation to the part is being considered. There is argument about universal inclusion and relative logical extension.

Source https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-maqasid/103

**Terminological warning:** Spiker later uses *iḍāfī* (p. 72) for a constituent whose very meaning is reference. The word appears in a different classification, not a stable ontology of all things called 'relative'.

### I p. 104 — real composition and precedence of parts

The discussion asks whether the parts are in some sense prior to a genuine composite, and why arbitrarily placing a stone next to a human does not create a further single essence. Merely adding one thing to another is not enough; the underlying parts must be united under the appropriate essential, material/formal analysis. Distinguish *part-before-whole* in understanding from material temporal priority. The author stresses that the priority of a part as constitutive does not mean the complete individual existed first or its form was added as a second separate substance.

The necessary/sufficient logic must be extracted precisely: dependence or part-priority may be necessary for certain real composites, while another independent condition may establish substantial unity.

Source https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-maqasid/104 (site intermittently inaccessible; check independent HTML witness or print).

### I p. 105 — genus and differentia are not two external things

**Decisive excerpt:** 'بل التحقيق أن ليس في الخارج إلا الأشخاص وإنما الجنس والفصل والنوع صور متمايزة عند العقل'. Close translation: rather, in external actuality there are only individuals, while genus, differentia and species are forms distinguished in the intellect.

But the surrounding paragraph is more nuanced. It responds to an objection that the blackness example is a *simple* species, while an animal seemingly has bodily substrate plus animal soul. The reply distinguishes **jism taken as matter** from **jism taken as genus**: as material principle, it can be actually distinguished from a material/formal principle; as genus, it is an essential predicable and not a separate externally differentiated individual object.

The text explicitly allows that logical essentially distinct elements can, in some cases, have externally distinct *principles* (body and animal soul; body and intellectual soul) and in other cases not (blackness as color-plus-visual contraction, surface as quantity differentiated by divisibility). It does NOT infer matter/form from every genus/differentia analysis.

**Additional detail:** the text says genus is indeterminate and the differentia determines its intelligible specification, without producing the genus anew as an independent thing in external reality. This is a distinction between causal/formal specification and psychological emergence of concepts.

Source https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-maqasid/105

**Audit implication:** Spiker n134 (Tasdīd) must be checked, but Maqāṣid itself warns against a blanket genus≈matter and difference≈form identity.

### I p. 106 — the army, mixtures, mixed composition and the cited proposition

The page begins in the continuation of matter/form specificity and turns to *iʿtibārī* composite: several things are regarded as one, e.g. ten from units and an army from individual people. The collective form of such a compound is **maḥḍ iʿtibār al-ʿaql**, with no independent **taḥaqquq fī al-khārij** as a new additional form, since the soldiers themselves exist.

**Critical qualification in same paragraph:** for medicinal compounds such as *tiryāq* and *sakanjabīn*, does there occur a *ṣūra jawhariyya* responsible for the distinctive effects, or only a *mizāj makhṣūṣ* classifiable among accidents? The passage says **fīhi taraddud** — there is hesitation. It likewise hesitates about whether a genuine substantial composite might involve substance plus accident. Therefore the author is NOT asserting that any evident new function shows a further substantial form.

The following taxonomy divides parts into overlapping/real contrasted, being-parts vs negative/privative parts, and, in another respect, **ḥaqīqiyya** (human soul/body), **iḍāfiyya** (al-aqrab through nearness + degree/increase), or **mumtazija** (bed through wood + *tartīb nisabī*). This last iḍāfī is DIFFERENT from p103's relative whole/part composition.

Short cited phrases:
- 'الصورة الاجتماعية في المركبات الاعتبارية محض اعتبار العقل لا تحقق لها في الخارج'
- 'فهل يحدث صورة جوهرية ... أو هو مجرد المزاج المخصوص ... ففيه تردد'
- 'أو إضافية كما في الأقرب من القرب وزيادته'
- 'أو ممتزجة بعضها حقيقي وبعضها إضافي كما في السرير من الأجزاء الخشبية والترتيب النسبي'

Source https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-maqasid/106

**Interpretive caution:** a bed classified as a *mixed* quiddity by conceptual constituents is not therefore automatically a new *natural* substantial form. Do not take *ḥaqīqī* 'real part' as the whole's substantial unity.

### I p. 107 — causality of quiddity: is the nature made?

The work continues into disagreement about *jaʿl māhiyyat al-mumkin*. It contrasts the mutakallimūn's language of quiddity being made by an agent and philosophers' or Muʿtazilīs' distinguishing a quiddity from the actualization of it. There are four possible candidate effects: the quiddity, existence, essence's association with existence, and conjunction of parts. This is not an irrelevant digression: if one claims that a bed acquires a form from a prior intelligible order, the account must specify **what is made by its cause**.

An explicit reply distinguishes the quiddity's need for another from its actual need for a causal producer, and warns against the equivocation “collection of establishments” versus “establishment of a collection” that arises through mental regard. The complete objection/reply must be retained.

Source https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-maqasid/107

### I p. 108 — composite nature, actual individuation, and two senses of being made

The text analyzes whether an individual *shakhs* rather than a universal *māhiyya* is made; then distinguishes *iḥtiyāj ilā al-fāʿil* (need for a producing cause) from *iḥtiyāj ilā al-ghayr* (need for another thing, including intrinsic parts). The former may pertain to the actual identity of a possible created individual (*huwiyya*), while the latter belongs to the composition of a *māhiyya* even where one brackets existence. This means *necessary to be composite* is not the same proposition as *caused to exist by an agent*.

The text distinguishes *māhiyya min ḥaythu hiya* from a nature considered with a further condition, and discusses whether creator produces the intrinsic nature or the actual individual.

Source https://usul.ai/ar/t/sharh-maqasid/108

### I pp. 171–173 — required cross-reference: natural vs artificial forms and ends

The later treatment discusses kinds of matter/form and why the term *ṣūra* does not always name a natural *substantial* form. With artifacts such as a **sword, bed and house**, the form in a significant accepted sense is a **hayʾa ʿaraḍiyya** (accidental configuration) associated with metal/wood. This makes the bed example of p106 insufficient to conclude that the bed has a genuinely new natural substance and a fresh substantial act of being. The context also continues to natural kinds that allegedly receive specific form, and to how artificial form is *a specific ordering* of something still retaining a natural substrate.

The subsequent page treats intentional and causal finality, what ends exist only as intellect's objects versus what occurs outside and the tradition's disagreements about created secondary causality. These issues must be kept for the later challenge to Spiker's slide from purposive ordering to higher exemplar.

## Internal logical reconstruction, NOT an AT comparison

1. Definitions of genus/differentia do not always mark actual constituent principles.
2. Being an aggregate is compatible with actual existence of the aggregated individuals without an additional real substantial form.
3. Artificial objects can possess a real particular arrangement but it remains unsettled, in this source, whether that entails a further substantial species.
4. Some mixtures genuinely produce distinctive effects; the nature of what accounts for these effects (specific substantial form or accidental temperament) is contested.
5. Essential dependence among compositional parts is not equivalent to efficient causal production of the quiddity; the difference matters if a later argument appeals to divine effusion of a form.
6. The source alone does NOT prove that relational concepts must have their foundation in a third eternal exemplar realm. That is a subsequent hypothesis requiring a separate argument.

## Claims affected

Spiker SP-03/04 on genus/real parts: source partially supports and qualifies. SP-05 on army: source explicit for no new substantial unity, but avoid assertion none of its actual positions is real. SP-07 on bed: source describes it as mixed parts, but later discussion admits an accidental artifact form; so bed-as-natural-substance is unsupported without further argument. SP-13 on extra-personal nafs al-amr: this text supplies objectivity problem but not an independent proof of prior exemplar realm.
