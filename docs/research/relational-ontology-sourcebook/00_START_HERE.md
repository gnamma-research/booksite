## Major actual findings (not merely a plan)

- Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid I, 103 distinguishes real versus **relative** (*iḍāfī*) composition; I, 104–106 moves to real versus **perspectival** (*iʿtibārī*) composition. These are different divisions.
- I, 105 explicitly distinguishes genus and differentia as intellectual parts even when a real composite also possesses material/formal principles.
- I, 106 grants that a collective form of the army is merely a mental consideration, **but explicitly hesitates** whether some physical mixtures have a new substantial form or merely an accidental temperament.
- I, 172 describes bed, sword and house forms as an accidental **hayʾa** in their materials on a significant reading. This is a crucial contextual qualification of the bed example.
- Mawāqif III, 38 glosses **khārijan** as **ḥissan** (sensibly) while a later gloss discusses the contrast to **dhihn** (mind). Meaning is context-specific.
- Mawāqif III, 54 interprets **qāʾim bi-nafsihi** as not requiring a **maḥall** (inherence locus) rather than causal self-sufficiency; **iḥtiyāj** is broader than **qiyām/ḥulūl**.
- Mawāqif III, 57–60 discusses the army and medicinal compound as objections to simplistic definitions of real unity. Dependence is not automatically sufficient for substantial unity.
- III, 60 says the army as **majmūʿ al-āḥād** is **mawjūd bilā shubha** (exists without doubt) while its unity is *iʿtibārī*. This requires qualification of any sentence saying 'no army exists in al-khārij'.
- III, 59 reports an attempted substance-from-substance-plus-accident explanation using the bed and tests its difficulties. Reported view does not equal all commentators' final view.
- Tahānawī, Kashshāf I, 657–658 distinguishes *jawhar* as indivisible atom in some usage from broader substance, *ʿayn* and philosopher's not-in-a-subject. No univocal Arabic→Latin equivalent.
- Jurjānī, Taʿrīfāt p. 16 has a strict definition of iʿtibārī as existing only in a considering intellect; Spiker's Bannānī gives two meanings, one objectively established. They must be historically reconciled, not assumed identical.

## Mandatory working discipline

Each claim and term must identify AUTHOR, WORK, EDITION, MATN/SHARH/HASHIYA, PAGE OR LEAF, TOPICAL QUESTION, ARGUMENT UNIT, EXACT SOURCE WORDING, TRANSLATION, AND LEVEL OF VERIFICATION. The HTML of Mawāqif explicitly contains more than one author's voice; site metadata alone does not attribute a passage.

**Evidence levels:** E = source explicit; Q = source qualifies; R = view reported, not necessarily endorsed; I = interpretive inference or addition; C = contextual challenge; U = unverified witness. These do not automatically designate a verdict on the whole argument.

**Never equate:** non-subsistence with unreality, iʿtibārī with false, jawhar with atom, khārijī with visually observable, formal unity with any well-functioning organized system, a true relational claim with a distinct relation-entity, or source reference with proof of Spiker's further exemplarist conclusions.
