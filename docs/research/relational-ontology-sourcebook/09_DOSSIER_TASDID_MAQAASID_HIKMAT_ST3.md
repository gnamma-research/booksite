The *Hikmat* pp38–48 and *Tasdīd* I406–407 print concordance remains open.

**Fivefold jawhar:** soul, intellect, body, matter and substantial form belong to a philosophically conditional, disputed division, not one empirically proven extension of 'substances'. The theologians' spatial jawhar/body/atom taxonomy is different. Matter/form and accidental subject/quality involve different senses of dependence and inherence; *maḥall* is broader than *mawḍūʿ*.

**Tasdīd decisive argument:** separated logical genus and differentia are not themselves two numerically distinct extramental beings. They may or may not have actually separate external source-principles. The commentary says “al-jins ashbah bi-l-mādda ... wa-l-faṣl ashbah bi-l-ṣūra”, and explicitly rejects making specific difference the cause of the generic nature outside the intellect. This qualification limits any universal Spiker inference that genus/fasl correspond to physically differentiated matter/form. Its root lemma about *jins* being like *mādda* must be distinguished from the *aqūlu* explication.

**Additional *iʿtibārī* distinction:** *Tasdīd* distinguishes hypothetical and genuine considerations; whether this is equivalent to Bannānī's later two senses remains unverified.
