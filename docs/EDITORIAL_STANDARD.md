# Editorial standard for every Gnamma text

**Stage A — bibliographic witness.** Assign URI, edition, witness and page concordances. Distinguish modern digital transcriptions from print collations.

**Stage B — translated passages.** Preserve full Arabic source spans for each English unit. Distinguish consecutive paragraphs from selections, overlaps, lacunae, and non-equivalent summaries. Avoid unsourced modernization.

**Stage C — technical vocabulary.** Contextual sense in the specific author first; then the historical dictionary witnesses in Arabic with independent English translations, then secondary academic discussion. Distinguish e.g. syllogistic from legal qiyās; juridical ʿilla from natural causation; ḥukm from a mere state of affairs.

**Stage D — cross-work concordance.** Record quoted primary passages with work/edition/page; label direct authorial reference, verified textual parallel, conceptual comparison or research lead. No fabricated links.

**Stage E — navigation and accessibility.** Stable anchor for each passage; mobile Arabic shaping and bidirectional script handling; explicit back-to-text control preserving exact reading position; genuine search; no dead link disguised as publication.

**Stage F — editorial review.** Independent review of primary text, translation, annotations, typographic proof and PDF. Only then may an edition advance from selected to complete or definitive.

Per-work status belongs within that work. Catalogue status can never outrun the narrower local status.
