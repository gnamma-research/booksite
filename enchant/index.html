FROM AN ENCOUNTER TO AN INQUIRY

What the glasses would do

01 · SENSE

See or hear

With an intentional activation, camera input recognizes the passage or object in view; optional audio capture requires consent.

02 · RETRIEVE

Find the sources

Locate related primary texts, translations, commentary and evidence. Preserve the source and edition behind each suggestion.

03 · INVESTIGATE

Think in space

Work with a knowledge layer that stays in the scholar's view. Compare passages, question apparent similarities and revise links.

04 · REMEMBER

Retain the inquiry

Preserve an evidence-linked note and unresolved questions that remain accessible across books, dialogue and places.

WHO IS IT FOR?

For researchers who work across worlds of text.

ENCHANT is conceived for multilingual scholars, editors, translators and advanced students who move between printed passages, editions, languages and intellectual traditions. Its aim is sustained attention—not instant certainty.

WHY NOT SIMPLY USE CHATGPT?

The environment of inquiry.

A conversational model can answer comparative questions. ENCHANT would keep the sources and research structure situated with what is being read or encountered, allow relationships to be revised, and preserve the result beyond a single answer.

THE VISUAL STUDY

SCHOLARSHIP FIRST

Not every resemblance is a relationship.

The system would distinguish verified quotation, attested historical reception, provisional analogy and unresolved scholarly interpretation. A source-aware interface must preserve uncertainty and empower researchers to correct its suggestions.
