Technical notes and limitations

PDF comments may appear as pop-up annotations or icons depending on the PDF application. Apple Files, browsers and Acrobat differ. The appendix is ordinary printable PDF pages, so observations remain readable on paper.

PDF comment locations are deliberately conservative: an annotation is pinned to the verified numbered section's page, not to guessed word coordinates. Bookmarks and notes outside the book text are preserved in the appendix. Keep your independent Reading Desk JSON backup for future editing or transfer.

Personalized PDF generation uses a locally executed, version-pinned PDF-processing library delivered over HTTPS. The original PDF and your notes are processed on your device. The site does not send personal note content to a server. The unchanged PDF option does not need this library.
