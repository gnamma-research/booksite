## Editorial standard
The corrected reader starts from the complete 258-section, 416-passage bilingual reader. All 40 earlier audit items have been *addressed*, not all solved by a newly verified Arabic manuscript: robust source-supported corrections were made in the reading text; interpretive reconstructions are bounded visibly; places where no decisive witness exists retain the transmitted text with a prominent explanation. An additional issue, DAR-041, records the omitted §2 clause recovered from the reported Escorial witness. The four manuscript-source categories must not be conflated: (1) reported Escorial witness variant, (2) Bou Akl editorial conjecture, (3) parallel with al-Ghazālī or other sources, and (4) our contextual interpretation. Independent manuscript collation has **not** been undertaken.
