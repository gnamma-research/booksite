SOURCES & COVERAGE

A working translation

The five newly completed sūrahs are al-Qadr, al-Humazah, al-Tīn, al-Zalzalah, and al-Qāriʿah. All earlier translated material is included. Each author’s arguments, reports, poems, readings, and grammatical discussions are retained. Ṭībī’s repeated Kashshāf base is not duplicated as new commentary.

The Arabic basis is the Dār al-Kitāb al-ʿArabī Kashshāf and the Dubai 2013 Futūḥ al-Ghayb, with edition-specific locators in each chapter. The Turkish Kashshāf has been used for comparison, not as the source of Ṭībī’s additions. Sources and uncertainties accompany the text.

These are complete first drafts for the chapters designated complete, not a final critical edition. The new batch has received one Turkish comparison and selective Arabic scan checks. Reports are translated with their transmitted attributions. Unresolved readings remain visible in the notes.

Al-Fātiḥa: Kashshāf is complete; Ṭībī is translated only through I:686. I:687–770 remains outstanding. The other 95 sūrahs and general book material are outside this reader’s completion claims.

The reader presents the English translations. Arabic chapter names identify the sūrahs; a full aligned Arabic edition is not claimed.
