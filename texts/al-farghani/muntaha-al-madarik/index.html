Saʿīd al-Dīn al-Farghānī

The four foundations and concluding preface to Farghānī’s commentary on Ibn al-Fāriḍ’s Tāʾiyya, presented as a book to read rather than a scholarly research dossier.

◉
Complete English working translation of the introduction

All 128 consecutive Beirut printed page positions (pp. 18–145), including the concluding preface. This is a working translation, not yet a fully proofread or manuscript-established critical edition. The subsequent verse-by-verse commentary is outside this scope.

About this edition · source and verification status

Every page of the introduction has a working English translation. An Arabic digital transcription exists across the same page range, but its online text has not been exhaustively verified against independent print or manuscript witnesses. The parallel reader aligns by printed page, not clause by clause. Modern editorial notes have been separated where identified.

There are 559 selected source-comparison records of mixed evidentiary grade and 15 bounded comparisons with an 1876 printing. These figures measure textual review, not how much of the introduction has been translated.
