### CR005-001 · B2007 p37 · retain

**Arabic selected span:** فكان المريد والإرادة داخلا في العلم ومنتشئا منه

**Reviewed English:** Thus the Willing and willing are included in knowledge and arise from it.

**Editorial note:** The order describes the derivation of divine-name meanings, not successive temporal events.

**Source:** https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/37?lang=ar. **Witness grade:** B2007 selected printed-digital reading only.

### CR005-002 · B2007 p37 · annotate
