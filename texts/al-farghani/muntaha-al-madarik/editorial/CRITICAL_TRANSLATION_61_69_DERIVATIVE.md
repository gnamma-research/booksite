## Critical findings

1. **al-Ghanī (p64) changes classification with sense:** possessing bestowable richness places it under al-Jawād; absolute lack of need is an essential negative name, a genuinely multi-perspectival taxonomy.
2. **al-Ghaffār / al-Ghafūr (p62):** distinguish generalization (`تعميما`) from multiplicity (`تعديدا`); do not reduce both to repetitive forgiveness.
3. **al-Fattāḥ (p63):** opening is an ordered emergence, from intelligible differentiation through mineral/vegetal/animal human development to the 'birth of the heart'. The word `مشيمة` needs lexical-anatomical scrutiny in early-modern Arabic before translating it as modern placenta.
4. **al-Rabb (pp65–67):** particular Lordship through the name where a person's existence first becomes determined is subordinate to general Lordship; not an assertion of independent divine entities.
5. **`الخلق الجديد` (p66):** precise statement of renewal at instants, important together with p73; compare against Ṣadrā without conflating the two.
6. **`مفيض/مفاض` (p68):** descent has agentive bestowal and recipient attribution; the ascent reverses determinations and returns them to principle.
7. **`لو` (pp68–69):** Farghānī explicitly raises an objection about the particle and appeals to Ibn Fāris for alternative force. This linguistic attribution and hadith about inner and outer readings need independent source vetting.
