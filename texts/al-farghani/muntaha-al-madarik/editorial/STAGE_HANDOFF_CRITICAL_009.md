## Source-critical advances

1. **B2007 p38 `القابل` vs expected `القائل`:** three authorial cross references at p52 `وكذا القائل والقوي`, p55 `وأما القائل`, and p56 `اسم القادر والمريد والقائل والعالم والحيّ والجواد والمقسط` strongly imply the seventh-name item should be Speaker, not Receptive. This is an INTERNAL evidentiary argument only, not manuscript collation. Do not silently change p38.
2. **B2007 p48 `بسبحاب`:** probable transcription/typographical error in a passage on *taḥaqquq*. Keep diplomatic reading; prospective `بسحاب` demands photographed page comparison. The three levels `تعلّق`, `تخلّق`, `تحقّق` now have selected checks in both p47 and p48.
3. **B2007 p49:** `صورة (اسمي الواحد والأحد) لا حقيقتهما` differentiates outward form from first-rank reality. The status of the Living/All-merciful reverses depending on the metaphysical *iʿtibār* rather than an historical order of becoming.
4. **B2007 pp51–56:** Thinghood is specified through `مشيئة`; the family relation of the divine names varies with ontological rank, action, and semantic consideration; `الخَلْق` explicitly has three lexical senses, and the seven principal divine names are written out at p56.
5. **B2007 p57:** creativity without precedent is explicitly **without an external precedent outside divine knowledge**; the Creator, Originator, and Fashioner are distinguished by forms.
6. **B2007 p58:** `ويشبه أن يكون` marks a **tentative interpretation** of a `qadam` report; worldly mediation of causes differs from eschatological direct disclosure.
7. **B2007 pp59–60:** divine kingship includes inward realities; the Re-Originator returns creatures through ranks corresponding to descent; `المميت` has layered spiritual and literal senses.
