**Total:** five bounded textual comparisons across I1876 printed pp5,6,7,18 (PDF scans 9,10,11,22): **three differences; two agreements; zero verified manuscript variants**.

**Newly established p34 lemma:** B2007 `الحضرة العملية` differs from I1876 `الحضرة العمائية`; the latter is adopted for the working edited text and the English “Cloud-like Presence”, supported by B2007 p36 internal same-referent parallel. This is a print-to-print reading, not a manuscript variant.
