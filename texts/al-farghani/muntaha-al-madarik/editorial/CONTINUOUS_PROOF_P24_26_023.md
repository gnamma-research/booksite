# Farghānī · Stage XXIII continuous Arabic–English proofreading, Beirut pp.24–26

**Scope:** Authorial Arabic introduction, Foundation I, archived modern edited-digital B2007 page positions only. This follows preserved Stage XXII pp18–23 and does not duplicate their clauses. All authorial Arabic is captured as contiguous original-text slices; modern publisher explanatory notes were already separated from `reading_ar`. B2007 physical print/manuscripts have **not** been newly inspected.

## Beirut B2007 p.24

Digitized source: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/24?lang=ar

### FCP-24-01

**Arabic**  معاني الشأن وهو الأمر ،

**English**  ... of the meanings of *shaʾn*, namely 'affair' or 'matter'.

*Editorial review:* Completes p23 ending «وصفة بقائها وثباتها اعتبارا لبعض». Together: attributes of the subsistence and stability of some meanings of 'affair'. The p23–24 join is one authorial syntactic unit, not two unrelated entries.

### FCP-24-02

**Arabic**  وعبّر بعضهم عن بعضها بالأحوال الذاتية ،

**English**  Some have designated certain of these 'essential states' (*aḥwāl dhātiyya*).

*Editorial review:* Some speakers are reported; Farghānī does not here make 'states' substantial hypostases.

### FCP-24-03

**Arabic**  وهي التي تظهر حين تبدو متحوّلة من صورة إلى صورة معنوية أو حسّية ؛ كالقبض والبسط ، والرضا والغضب ، وجميع الأعراض التي لا تدوم ، وكالخواطر والصور والهيئات المثالية والحسّية دنيا وبرزخا وآخرة

**English**  These are displayed when they appear transformed from one intelligible or sensible form to another: contraction and expansion, satisfaction and anger, and all the accidents that do not endure; likewise thoughts, images and imaginal or sensible configurations in this world, the intermediate world and the hereafter.

*Editorial review:* «أعراض لا تدوم» explicitly names nonenduring accidents; avoid making the ontological transition a change of God's unconditioned Essence.

### FCP-24-04

**Arabic**  تذكر تحوّل الحق تعالى وتقدّس من صورة لم يعرفها قوم في الآخرة إلى صورة يعرفونه بها ، كما ورد في الخبر الصحيح « 1 » ،

**English**  Recall the report deemed sound by Farghānī in which the Real—exalted and holy—is encountered in the Hereafter in a form that a group does not recognize, and then in a form by which they recognize Him.

*Editorial review:* Author says «الخبر الصحيح»; the hadith concerning divine recognition after resurrection should be analyzed as transmitted, without equating an imaginal form with God's Essence. Modern source footnote on this page is excluded.

### FCP-24-05

**Arabic**  واللّه المرشد .

**English**  God is the One who guides.

*Editorial review:* Short authorial devotional phrase.

### FCP-24-06

**Arabic**  فصل اعلم أن أوّل اعتبار وتعيّن تعيّن من الغيب هذه الوحدة التي انتشأت منها الأحدية والواحدية ،

**English**  **Chapter.** Know that the first consideration and determination to arise from the Unseen is this unity from which *aḥadiyya* and *wāḥidiyya* have arisen.

*Editorial review:* The printed authorial «فصل» initiates the chapter; not a modern editor's chapter title.

### FCP-24-07

**Arabic**  فظلّت برزخا جامعا بينهما ؛

**English**  It is therefore an intermediary (*barzakh*) embracing them both,

*Editorial review:* The intermediary encompasses two *considerations* of one unity, not two independent principles.

### FCP-24-08

**Arabic**  كحقيقة المحبة المنتشئة منها المحبّية والمحبوبية وكونها جامعة بينهما ورافعة بينهما موحّدة إياهما ،

**English**  like the reality of love, from which the states of being lover and beloved arise, while it unites them and removes what separates them.

*Editorial review:* «رافعـة بينهما» means removing the interval or division; the analogy of love is not a divine emotional need.

### FCP-24-09

**Arabic**  أو قل هي بحكم اقتضاء الباطن الحقيقي المكنى « بكنت كنزا مخفيّا » « 2 » ، الظهور المعبّر عنه بأن أعرف

**English**  Or say: it follows from the true inwardness indicated by 'I was a hidden treasure' that there should be the manifestation signified by 'that I might be known'.

*Editorial review:* Preserve both allusive sayings as Farghānī's conceptual source. Priority is of rank, not chronological change.

### FCP-24-10

**Arabic**  وهذا الاعتبار والتعيّن الأول الذي هو عين الذات بحكم البرزخية والجمعية المذكورة هو عين قابلية الذات لبطونها وغيبها وانتفاء الاعتبارات عنها وحكم أزليّتها ،

**English**  This first consideration and determination, identical with the Essence under the aforementioned intermediate and comprehensive aspect, is the Essence's very receptivity to its hiddenness, its Unseen, the exclusion of considerations from it and the judgment of its pre-eternity,

*Editorial review:* The language of receptivity is analogical and under an explicit consideration; do not posit physical passivity or addition to the divine Essence.

### FCP-24-11

**Arabic**  ولظهورها أيضا وظهور ما تضمّنت من الاعتبارات المثبتة حكم أبديتها لنفسها إجمالا ، ثم تفصيلا ،

**English**  and likewise to its manifestation and that of the affirmative considerations it contains, entailing everlastingness for itself, first comprehensively and then distinctly.

*Editorial review:* The pair of inwardness/manifestation maps onto aḥadiyya/wāḥidiyya without collapsing the two.

### FCP-24-12

**Arabic**  فكانت هذه القابلية أصل كل قابلية وفاعلية أيضا ،

**English**  This receptivity is the origin of every receptivity, and of every activity too.

*Editorial review:* «قابلية وفاعلية» here are reciprocal relational capacities, not created substances.

### FCP-24-13

**Arabic**  فهي مع أنها عين الذات كانت كالمتحدثة مع نفسها في نفسها ، والمخبرة لها بما هي عليه من اقتضاء ظهورها وظهور اعتبارات واحديتها والكمال الذاتي

**English**  Though identical with the Essence, it is likened to an inward speaker speaking to itself within itself, informing itself of what it is through its demand for its own manifestation and the manifestation of its considerations of oneness, together with essential perfection—

*Editorial review:* «كالمتحدثة» is an explicit analogy: as if speaking within itself; this unit ends mid-phrase at the printed page boundary.

## Beirut B2007 p.25

Digitized source: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/25?lang=ar

### FCP-25-01

**Arabic**  والأسمائي المتعلّق بذلك الظهور

**English**  —and the name-related perfection attendant upon that manifestation,

*Editorial review:* Continuation of p24 authorial phrase «والكمال الذاتي»; do not start a new doctrine.

### FCP-25-02

**Arabic**  حديثا وإخبارا نزيها بحرف نزيه وحداني هو عين الذات متضمّن جميع المعاني كلّياتها وجزئياتها ،

**English**  in a speaking and informing transcendent of ordinary articulation, by a transcendent, unitary 'letter' identical with the Essence and embracing all meanings, universal and particular,

*Editorial review:* «حرف نزيه وحداني» is the author's transcendent analogical term, not an ordinary printed letter or a physical sound. The full grammatical relation of «حديثا وإخبارا نزيها» remains interpretative.

### FCP-25-03

**Arabic**  والألفاظ والكلم القولي والفعلي الدالّة عليها في سابع رتب أبطن .

**English**  and the expressions and words, spoken or enacted, that signify them at the seventh and most inward rank of speech.

*Editorial review:* «الكلم القولي والفعلي» are verbal and active words in Akbarian usage, not merely utterances.

### FCP-25-04

**Arabic**  الكلام المشار إليه في قوله صلى اللّه عليه وسلم : « إن للقرآن ظهرا وبطنّا ، وفي بطنه بطن إلى سبعة أبطن » « 1 » ،

**English**  This is the speech alluded to in the saying attributed to the Prophet: 'The Qurʾān has an outward and an inward; within that inward an inward, down to seven inward depths.'

*Editorial review:* The saying of seven Qurʾānic inward depths is not authenticated merely by its presence here. The modern edition's footnote reportedly could not locate this exact wording.

### FCP-25-05

**Arabic**  كما يتحدّث أحدنا في نفسه بلا واسطة حرف وصوت ظاهر ،

**English**  An analogy is our speaking inwardly to ourselves without a manifest letter or audible sound.

*Editorial review:* «كما» marks the human inward-speaking analogy, not physiological speech attributed to God.

### FCP-25-06

**Arabic**  وكان في تلك القابلية أيضا قابلية ميل الذات بالسماع والإصغاء إلى ذلك الحديث هي أثر تلك المحبّة في الحقيقة ،

**English**  In this same receptivity there is the Essence's receptivity to inclination through hearing and attentive listening to that inward speech; this inclination is, in reality, an effect of original love.

*Editorial review:* «قابلية» and «ميل الذات» in this context are rank-relative language; do not imply a need or acquired psychological disposition in the absolute Essence.

### FCP-25-07

**Arabic**  فمالت بالسماع في سابع أبطن الميل بالسماع والإصغاء ،

**English**  So it inclined to listening at the seventh and most inward rank of inclination, hearing and attentiveness.

*Editorial review:* Seventh depth introduced before the subsequent human seven-depth exposition.

### FCP-25-08

**Arabic**  وفي تلك القابلية أيضا قابلية ملاحظة الذات نور جمالها الذي هو عين واحديتها وظهورها وظهور اعتباراتها وملاحظة الكمال المتعلق بذلك بحسب ذلك الميل والسماع والإصغاء ،

**English**  In the same receptivity is the capacity to behold the light of its beauty, which is identical with its oneness, its manifestation and the manifestation of its considerations, and to attend to the perfection related to this manifestation in accordance with the inclination and hearing.

*Editorial review:* «نور جمالها» is its own beauty; sight here is contemplative, not retinal.

### FCP-25-09

**Arabic**  فلاحظ في سابع رتب أبطن الملاحظة ،

**English**  Its contemplation thus occurred in the seventh and most inward rank of contemplation.

*Editorial review:* Repetition of the seventh inward rank is authorial.

### FCP-25-10

**Arabic**  وفيها أيضا قابلية التأثر بذلك الحديث ليميط خمار غلبة حكم جلال الغيب والبطون عن الجمال والظهور المكني عن تلك الإماطة بتغليب الرحمة وسبقها على أنها غايات حكم الغضب عارجا لا نازلا وأبطنها الذي هو اللاظهور المتضمّن معنى الردى والعدم ،

**English**  It also admits an influence from this inward speech, removing the veil whereby the majesty of the Unseen and inwardness predominates over beauty and manifestation. Farghānī calls that removal the predominance and priority of mercy over the outermost reach of the judgment of anger, understood as ascending, not descending, and over its deepest aspect—nonmanifestation, associated with destruction and absence.

*Editorial review:* The source says «على أنها غايات حكم الغضب عارجا لا نازلا»; the English is deliberately provisional for this difficult syntax. It is a short photographed 1876 print agreement (I1876-L08), but not a manuscript-resolved reading.

### FCP-25-11

**Arabic**  فإن الظهور واللاظهور كان في حكم اقتضاء هذه القابلية الأولية على السواء ،

**English**  For appearance and nonappearance were equally possible under the exigency of this first receptivity.

*Editorial review:* «على السواء» modifies manifestation and its nonmanifestation under the first qualification, not a division of the Essence.

### FCP-25-12

**Arabic**  فغلب أثر هذا الحديث والمحبّة والميل الذاتي حكم الظهور على اللاظهور وحكم بسبقه عليه ،

**English**  But the effect of inward discourse, love and essential inclination caused the judgment of manifestation to prevail over nonmanifestation and to have priority over it.

*Editorial review:* Priority here remains ontological/rank-priority, not historical sequence within God.

### FCP-25-13

**Arabic**  فتجلّى الذات الأقدس على نفسها بحكم ذلك الأثر والتغليب والسبق وظهر لنفسها في نفسها ، أعني في عين ذلك التعيّن والقابلية الأولية ،

**English**  The most holy Essence thus disclosed itself to itself according to that influence and priority, and manifested to itself within itself—that is, precisely within the first determination and receptivity.

*Editorial review:* Self-disclosure is stated as «تجلّى الذات الأقدس على نفسها»; reflexive construction central.

### FCP-25-14

**Arabic**  فوجدت الذات نفسها بهذا التجلّي الذي هو عين العلم والشهود بحكم مرآته المذكورة التي هي عينه الحاملة له المتضمّنة ذلك الحديث والميل والملاحظة ،

**English**  Through that self-disclosure, which is identical with knowing and witnessing, the Essence found itself by virtue of the aforementioned mirror, a mirror identical with it that bears the self-disclosure and contains this discourse, inclination and contemplation.

*Editorial review:* «فوجدت الذات نفسها» joins wujūd/recognition and witnessing; mirror not a new divine being.

### FCP-25-15

**Arabic**  والتأثير بذلك الحديث في نفسه التي هي عين التجلّي والقابلية الأولية منصبغ بحكم اعتبار معنى هو باطن صفة القول وصفة السمع وصفة البصر وصفة القدرة في سابع رتب أبطن كل واحد منها ، وهي رتبة هذا التعين الأول المذكور ،

**English**  The effect of this inward discourse in itself, identical with the first self-disclosure and receptivity, bears a qualification of a meaning that is the inner reality of speech, hearing, sight and power at the seventh inward degree of each—the degree of the first determination.

*Editorial review:* Attributes are present here as undifferentiated meanings or considerations, not already independently distinct qualities.

### FCP-25-16

**Arabic**  فإن القول عبارة عن نفس منبعث من باطن المتنفّس متضمّن معنى يطلب المتنفّس ظهوره ، فيتعيّن ذلك النفس في مراتب المخارج ،

**English**  For speech is a breath issuing from the inwardness of the speaker, bearing a meaning the speaker seeks to manifest. The breath is then determined at successive articulatory outlets.

*Editorial review:* «نفس» is breath; avoid an implication of bodily respiration in the unconditioned divine reality.

### FCP-25-17

**Arabic**  وكذلك المحبة الأصلية التي هي عين التعين الأول والقابلية متضمّنة معنى الكمال المتعلق بالظهور مقتض للتجلي

**English**  Likewise, original love—identical with the first determination and receptivity—contains a meaning of perfection linked to manifestation that requires the first self-disclosure—

*Editorial review:* Continues on p26 «الأول الذي هو مثل النفس المتعين ...». The translation remains open at the page boundary.

## Beirut B2007 p.26

Digitized source: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/26?lang=ar

### FCP-26-01

**Arabic**  الأول الذي هو مثل النفس المتعين في التعين الأول المنبعث من باطن الغيب المطلق ،

**English**  —the first self-disclosure, like the breath determined at the first determination and proceeding from the inwardness of the undelimited Unseen.

*Editorial review:* Continuation of p25; retain double meaning of nafas (breath), no physical divine breathing.

### FCP-26-02

**Arabic**  والسمع هو قبول ذلك المعنى ظاهرا أو باطنا ،

**English**  'Hearing' is the reception of that meaning outwardly or inwardly.

*Editorial review:* Passive/active distinction remains qualified by rank.

### FCP-26-03

**Arabic**  والبصر هو قبول ظهوره بما هو ظاهر من القابل منه

**English**  'Sight' is receptive apprehension of its manifestation through what appears from its recipient.

*Editorial review:* Source «بما هو ظاهر من القابل منه» syntax somewhat compressed; the English is provisional.

### FCP-26-04

**Arabic**  والقوة تمكن مما يطلب ظهوره ،

**English**  Power is the capacity to enable what is sought to appear.

*Editorial review:* «القوة» is capacity, not a created instrumental force in the Essence.

### FCP-26-05

**Arabic**  وهذا المعنى هو الذي أراد به الشيخ ابن الفارض رحمة اللّه عليه في قصيدته بقوله :

**English**  This, according to Farghānī, is the meaning intended by Shaykh Ibn al-Fāriḍ, may God have mercy on him, in the following verses:

*Editorial review:* The quotation is from Ibn al-Fāriḍ's poetic text within Farghānī's introduction; the separate lineation requires checking against a reliable poetry edition, but extending into the later verse-by-verse commentary is excluded.

### FCP-26-06

**Arabic**  فلفظ وكلي بي لسان محدث * ولحظ وكلي فيّ عين لعبرة وسمع وكلي بالنّدا أسمع النداء * وكلّي فيّ ردّ الرّدى يد قوة معاني صفات ما ورى اللّبس أثبتت * وأسماء ذات ما روى الحسّ بثّت

**English**  'My utterance: wholly I am within myself a tongue that speaks. My gaze: wholly I am an eye for contemplation. My hearing: wholly I hear the call when called. My whole self, warding away destruction, is the hand of power. Meanings of attributes were established beyond the veil of clothing; the names of the Essence disseminated what sense related.' [Provisional verse rendering; Arabic hemistich boundaries and verbal nuances remain to be verified against a separate poetic witness.]

*Editorial review:* Three paired hemistichs in the digitized text are grouped into one unit to preserve source verbatim. Interpretation of «ورى اللّبس», «ما روى الحس» is not independently established by a poetic witness.

### FCP-26-07

**Arabic**  يعني : أثبتت تلك المعاني في الرتبة الأولى التي هي وراء رتبة لبسها من كونها شؤونا ذاتية بأحكام الكثرة والتميّز

**English**  That is: these meanings were established at the first rank, which is beyond the rank of their being clothed, when they were essential affairs without judgments of multiplicity and distinction.

*Editorial review:* «شؤون ذاتية» appear as later clearly differentiated attributes by rank, not a temporal emergence of faculties in the Essence.

### FCP-26-08

**Arabic**  حتى صارت عين تلك المعاني بحكم أحكام اللّبس بأحكام الكثرة والتميّز صفات متغايرة في الرتبة الثانية بحسبها وحكمها ؛ كصفة السمع والبصر والقدرة وصفة الكلام ونحو ذلك ،

**English**  When clothed in judgments of multiplicity and differentiation, these same meanings became distinct attributes in the second rank, in conformity with its ruling, such as hearing, sight, power and speech.

*Editorial review:* Plurality under the second rank, not under the absolute first.

### FCP-26-09

**Arabic**  بعد أن لم تكن في تلك الرتبة الأولى صفات ، بل اعتبارات غير متميّزة ولا متغايرة فيها بحسبها وحكمها ،

**English**  Previously, at the first rank, they were not distinct attributes, but considerations that were neither differentiated nor separate from one another.

*Editorial review:* Explicit first-rank ontological caveat.

### FCP-26-10

**Arabic**  وهذا اللفظ الذي كل الذات لسان محدث نفسه به ، واللحظ الذي كلّه عين لا حظة به ، ليعبر من الباطن إلى الظاهر ، والسمع الذي كل الذات سامع به حديثه ذلك ، واليد التي كل الذات فاعل بها في ردّها اللاظهور المكنى عنه بالردى بملابسة الخفاء والبطون إلى الظهور والوجود ،

**English**  Thus the utterance by which the entire Essence is a tongue inwardly speaking to itself; the gaze in which all of it is the eye that beholds, enabling a passage from inward to outward; the hearing by which all of it hears its own discourse; and the hand by which all of it acts to repel nonappearance, figuratively called destruction because of hiddenness, and to bring about appearance and existence—

*Editorial review:* Author's long enumeration is kept as a single syntax-bearing unit without dropping speech, sight, hearing or hand of power.

### FCP-26-11

**Arabic**  كل ذلك معاني صفة السمع والبصر والقدرة والكلام الثابتة متميّزة في الرتبة الثانية الألوهية متغايرة ،

**English**  all these are meanings of hearing, sight, power and speech, which are established as differentiated attributes in the second rank of divinity.

*Editorial review:* Second-rank differentiated meanings.

### FCP-26-12

**Arabic**  وهذه المعاني التي هي باطن هذه الصفات كانت غير متغايرة في الرتبة الأولى التي هي سابع أبطنها بالنسبة إلى غاية ظهورها ،

**English**  These meanings, being the inward reality of those attributes, are not differentiated at the first rank, the seventh inward degree relative to the extremity of their outward manifestation.

*Editorial review:* Do not confuse 'seventh depth' with a sevenfold numerical partition of God's Essence.

### FCP-26-13

**Arabic**  فإن غاية ظهورها أن تظهر بصورة اللفظ الجاري على لسان الإنسان ونظره بعينه ، وسماعه بأذنه وعمله بيده المضافة كلّها إلى صورة بدنه .

**English**  The furthest outward display is speech from the human tongue, seeing with the eye, hearing with the ear and acting by the hand, all attributed to the bodily form.

*Editorial review:* Human bodily analogues belong to the furthest outward mode.

### FCP-26-14

**Arabic**  وباطنها الأول : أن تكون هذه الصفات مضافة إلى نفسه ، لكن من حيث لم تتميّز عن نفوس سائر الحيوانات إلا بظاهر العقل المعيشي المقيّد بأمور دنيوية ، بحيث يكون نطقه وسماعه ونظره وفعله مقصودة على ما يتعلق بأمر الدنيا غير متعدّ إلى أمر أخروي هو المقصد منه ؛

**English**  **First inward degree:** their attribution to the soul, yet insofar as its distinction from animal souls is only the outward face of an intellect bound to worldly livelihood. Speech, hearing, sight and action are thus devoted to this life, not reaching the affairs of the next life, which are their intended end.

*Editorial review:* «باطنها الأول» marks first degree inside the human form; the worldly-only intellect's limit is the hereafter, not the absence of cognition.

### FCP-26-15

**Arabic**  كما أخبر اللّه تعالى عن هذا الوصف في قوله عزّ وجلّ : يَعْلَمُونَ ظاهِراً مِنَ الْحَياةِ الدُّنْيا وَهُمْ عَنِ الْآخِرَةِ هُمْ غافِلُونَ ( 7 ) [ الرّوم : الآية 7 ] .

**English**  This is the condition described by God in Qurʾān 30:7: 'They know an outward aspect of the life of this world, while they are heedless of the Hereafter.'

*Editorial review:* «(7)» is the Qurʾānic verse number, explicitly NOT a modern editor's note. Do not strip it from Arabic. The English reader may cite 30:7.
