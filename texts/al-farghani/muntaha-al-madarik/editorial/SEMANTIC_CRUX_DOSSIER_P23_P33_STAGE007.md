# Farghānī — Disclosed knowledge and "in aid of this": grammatical crux dossier

**9 November 2026 · B2007 pp.23 and 33 · Stage VII.** This is philological **interpretation**, not independent manuscript reconstruction. Source comparison: B2007 printed pp23,33 with photographed I1876 pp8–9 (scans12–13), p16 (scan20). The historical printing has 1) `سوى أنه ربما يكون` at B2007's `إلّا أنه ربما يكون`, and 2) `في عون هذا` agreeing with Beirut.

## A. B2007 p.23: qualification of cognitive access is crucial

**Immediate argument:** Farghānī has reached a stated limit of the *shuhūdāt*, *idrākāt*, and *mukāshafāt* of even perfected knowers, then introduces an exceptive qualification (`سوى / إلا`). The scope of the following difficult statement thus matters as much as its verb.

**Bounded source segments:**
- Exceptive qualification: I1876 `سوى أنه ربما يكون`; B2007 `إلّا أنه ربما يكون`.
- Delimitation of domain: `في الحضرة الغيبية الأزلية`, things or states `لم تتعيّن بعد لا في الرتبة الثانية ولا في اللوح المحفوظ`.
- Result: `فلم تعلم ولم يحكم بشيء منها إلا بعد تعيّنها`.

**Grammatical questions:**
1. The nonvocalized `تعلم` is compatible with a passive *tuʿlam* (the states "were not known") rather than a verb attributing not-knowing directly to the divine Essence. Its subject is the preceding hidden `أمور` and `أحوال`, not a newly introduced divine subject. The reading must be noted **as an interpretation**, not silently vocalized into a definitive manuscript text.
2. The operative condition is the states' being determined in named ranks. The sentence may specify **the cognizability of those affairs in the orders of disclosure under discussion**, not knowledge of the undelimited Essence. That is supported by the preceding limitation of realized witnessing, but Farghānī's language is strong; one must not make the problem disappear by translating an unrestricted “God always knows every differentiated state” as if it were written.
3. `ربما` explicitly makes the case a qualified possibility, not an assertion that actual eternal hidden events come into being at a specified time.

**Provisional translation option (not adopted as a settled lemma):** “Yet perhaps in the pre-eternal unseen Presence, with regard to these universals and particulars, there are inner affairs and concealed states not yet determined either in the second rank or the Preserved Tablet; consequently no determinate knowledge or judgment of them obtains *at the ranks under discussion* until their determination and progressive occurrence in existential stations.” The italicized scope words are **interpretive supplementation**, not an Arabic phrase in the cited sentence. A strictly more literal English should keep the stronger “they were not known and no judgment was made concerning any of them”.

**Critical decision:** Retain authorial ambiguity; document passive/active potential in grammatical apparatus. Independent manuscripts may preserve helpful context or variant vocalisation but cannot be presumed to resolve a genuinely philosophical commitment.

## B. B2007 p.33: `في عون هذا` belongs to the transition between disclosures

**Immediate sequence:** a first self-disclosure whose orientation is toward differentiated name-perfection lacks a suitable recipient, returns to its principle, and the effect of primordial love makes manifestation predominate over concealment. Then comes:
`فعاد ذلك التجلّي ظاهرا معيّنا في عون هذا بقوّة المحبّة الأصليّة اللازمة له والباطنة فيه ... تعيّنا قابلا لتحقيق مطلبه الغائي`.

**Secure readings:** `في عون هذا` in both photographed I1876 p16 and printed-digital B2007 p33. It is not legitimate to substitute `في عين هذا` on grounds of metaphysical preference. The manuscript archetype has not been collated, and agreement of two prints is not proof of authorial origin.

**Syntactic possibilities that require adjudication, not silent translation:**
- `في عون هذا` may function as an adverbial “in support of this [aim/process]”, with the deictic `هذا` referring to the recently specified goal of name-related perfection, or to the predominance of manifestation. The following `بقوّة المحبّة الأصليّة` provides the stated enabling principle.
- `تعيّنا قابلا لتحقيق مطلبه الغائي` may attach to the renewed *tajallī* or a related determination, with `القابل الثاني` then identifying its receptive rank. A printed comma cannot decide the syntax.

**Provisional literal translation:** “That self-disclosure reappeared, manifest and determinate, in aid of this [aim], through the force of primordial love inherent and hidden in it ... with a determination receptive to the fulfilment of its ultimate goal, which is the perfection through the names.” Square brackets are explanatory and **not authorial text**. The existing broad draft remains usable but not definitively adjudicated; the final edition should distinguish an optional interpretive reading from the literal Arabic.

## C. Why this matters to the project

The same term—knowledge, existence, will, receptive determination—operates at different **ontological or phenomenological registers**: the undelimited Essence under negation of considerations; first rank and comprehensive unity; second rank and articulation of names; actual cosmic stations; realized human unveiling and return. The p23 epistemic crux is an especially sharp case of an overbroad translation turning a ranked cognitive claim into an unqualified claim about divine ignorance. The p33 crux links metaphysical manifestation to a relational (not temporally prior) orientation of primordial love.
