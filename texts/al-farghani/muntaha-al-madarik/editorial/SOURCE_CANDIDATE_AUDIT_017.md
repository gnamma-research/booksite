## Disqualified as independent B2007 evidence
Some external third-party pages describe `https://archive.org/download/lis_q115/lisanarb.com_q1508.pdf` as the 2007 Farghānī edition. Direct PDF access redirected to an unavailable mirror; **the actual PDF pages were NOT viewed**. However the Internet Archive's accessible associated OCR transcript at https://archive.org/stream/lis_q115/lisanarb.com_q1508_djvu.txt repeatedly labels book leaves **شرح داود بن محمود القيصري**, i.e. the separate commentary on the Tāʾiyya attributed to Dāwūd al-Qayṣarī, not Farghānī's *Muntahā* (OCR transcript around its lines 6430–6435; also later repeated headings). The OCR's printed bibliographic data appear incompatible with the target ISBN, but OCR is imperfect and cannot by itself prove the entire PDF's identity. **Provisional determination: strong mismatch; disqualify the URL for comparison until actual cover/title images are independently verified.** Do not cite its OCR as Farghānī's 2007 printed text, and do not present it as the source of any corrected textual locus.

A different archive link from https://www.nafahat-tarik.com/2015/03/sufism_11.html points to `https://archive.org/download/lis02029/01.pdf` and may be more promising, but fetching returned HTTP 403 and no page images were inspected. **Its title and image contents remain UNVERIFIED.**
