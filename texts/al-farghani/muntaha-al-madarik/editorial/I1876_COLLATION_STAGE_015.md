Each record preserves scan identity and derivative SHA-256. This is **NOT** a manuscript collation, and comparison with B2007 is to its **digitized/retyped text** unless specifically stated.

## 1. Corrected status of the name: p38 B2007 ↔ I1876 p20, scan24

**Observed B2007 digital:** `( والقابل ) يجمعها`

**Read in I1876 printed p20:** `والقابل يجمعها`

The disputed noun **القابل** is attested in the historical photograph as well as the modern edited digital. The previously conjectured **القائل** is not independently witnessed in this locus. Retain **القابل**, with an explicit note that `القائل` is an original name in other, distinct passages (p52, 55, 56, 72, 89, 106). A receptive *qābil* could be intended here as distinct from the speaking *qāʾil*, especially within the Breath's encompassing determinations; no emendation is now justified merely from other contexts. Any conclusion about original authorial intention requires handwritten witnesses.

## 2. Actual consequential textual difference: p48 B2007 ↔ I1876 p28, scan32

**Observed B2007 digitized Arabic:** `وبالاستتار بسبحاب أعيانها وأسرارها وأنوارها`

**Directly read older printed photo:** `وبالاستتار بسبحات أعيانها وأسرارها وأنوارها`

Provisional edited reading: **بسبحات**, the plural of *subḥa* ('radiance, splendour, effulgence'); **not** `بسحاب` ('clouds'), which was a previous uninformed conjecture. Revised English: **'and by becoming concealed within the splendours of their realities, mysteries, and lights.'** Editorial uncertainty concerns whether B2007 physical ink has the anomalous form or the online transcription introduced it; compare actual B2007 printed leaf before saying two physical printings differ. Neither is a verified manuscript variant. The earlier reader's 'clouds' translation is explicitly superseded.

## 3. An independently checked whole conceptual clause: p61 B2007 ↔ I1876 p38, scan42

Bounded photographed passage: `الإيثار يكون بالوجود أولا ثم بالبقاء ثانيا ثم بنفخ الروح والحياة ثالثا`. The earlier secondary transcription's same **substantive threefold order** is confirmed. The derivative text prefixes `فذلك` whereas the old photo prefixes `وذلك`; only the core enumerative clause is counted as photographic agreement, while the connective remains a separate lower-grade discrepancy that needs B2007 photo.

## 4. The two emphatic divine names: p62 B2007 ↔ I1876 p39, scan43

Photo reads `بصيغة المبالغة تعميما وهو الاسم الغفار وتعديدا وهو الاسم الغفور`, confirming that **الغفار** is marked by *taʿmīm* (scope/generality) while **الغفور** is marked by *taʿdīd* (multiplicity of instances). The earlier amended English was thus justified within this actual historical print. Modern version still a secondary retranscription at p62.

## 5. p52 (I1876 p32 scan36) — observation NOT counted

A low-resolution printed page seems to show `وتفرع (اسم الحكم) منه` where the B2007 online text reads `تفرع (سم الحكيم) منه`. The presence of the yāʾ that distinguishes *al-Ḥakam* from *al-Ḥakīm* is not confidently resolved at the available 760px resolution. **No new verified variant is counted for this observation**; obtain 2200px IIIF original, actual B2007 printed page and early manuscripts before any lemma decision.
