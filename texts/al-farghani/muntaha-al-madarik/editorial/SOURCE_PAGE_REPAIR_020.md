## Newly checked p.138 (CR011-044)
«ويسمّى ذلك مكاشفة لانكشاف حقيقة كل واحد منهما بحكمه ووصفه على الآخر» — “This is called unveiling (*mukāshafa*), since the reality of each is disclosed to the other in accordance with its ruling and attribute.” Located exactly in the digital modern edited Arabic at B2007 p.138; no independent printed-paper or manuscript collation. This preserves independently selected source coverage of p.138 after its two mislocated source records were corrected to p.137.

Farghānī's p.138 sequence distinguishes **mukāshafa**, **mushāhada**, and **muʿāyana** through increasingly direct reciprocal disclosure. **Textual crux deliberately unresolved:** B2007 digitized «فيتولّد من مشمّة الروح عند ذلك ولد قلب» is rendered “From the womb of the spirit ...” in the 128-page working English. The form «مشمّة» does not by itself authenticate “womb”; inspect the physical I1876 counterpart and manuscripts before normalizing or revising this sentence. Preserve uncertainty in this apparatus, not in uninterrupted reader prose.
