# Farghānī · Stage XIII · Foundation III, B2007 pp79–101

**9 November 2026.** 74 bounded Arabic–English comparative checks touching 23 page labels, completing the page-label coverage of the Third Foundation. **This is a secondary, derivative Arabic retranscription, NOT an original photographed B2007/1876 printing or independent manuscript, and individual source pages are not line-by-line proofread.** Cumulative reader before this stage had 394 selected records (283 modern edited-digitized + 111 derivative); this stage raises lower-grade derived checks by 74.

## Key interpretive issues

1. **p79–80: two levels of differentiation.** In the first rank differentiation is `نسبي`, not `حقيقي`; in the second it becomes true differentiation; the account of the Breath must keep `مفيض` (bestowing) and `مفاض` (bestowed) distinct.
2. **p81–82: prime matter, principles and geometric model.** `الهباء` is associated with indivisible parts and four qualities (heat/cold/moisture/dryness), then with the composite Nature. Distinguish these qualities from four elements and from modern atomic theory. Dimensions are explained via three different judgments.
3. **p83–85: Throne and Footstool.** `اثنتي عشرة قسمة مفروضة لا محسوسة` means twelve *conceptual*, not perceived, divisions; the geometric and astronomical descriptions are period cosmological metaphors or models, not modern empirical findings. The Footstool is related to three gardens and the visible/sensory realm.
4. **p86–87: imperative/prohibition and cosmology.** Command preserves unity in descent and prohibition orients return, expressed by the 'two feet' metaphor. The 'joined' material `رتق` and `العنصر الأعظم` are part of the author’s reading of Q21:30.
5. **p89–90: seven names and causal efficacy.** The Moon is assigned `الاسم القائل`, further internal support for correcting p38 `القابل`. More importantly, created causes and astral configurations are **معدّات** (preparatory conditions), while creative efficacy belongs to the name-realities. Occasional unmmediated divine action is contrasted with the regular mediated order. Farghānī also describes the earth as spherical in rational apprehension but flat to ordinary visual apprehension.
6. **p91–93: matter, time and polarity.** The rank of sensation is associated with time; the second determination has the knowing/receptive and bestowing/activity poles, with the human intermediate rank connecting them. Three generated kingdoms are mineral, plant and animal.
7. **p94–96: vegetal, animal and Adamic nature.** Vegetal capacities include digestion, growth, generation and retention; the human combines the three lower balance levels and is completed by spirit. The author distinguishes a whole's excellence as a collective whole from the perfecting of its individual parts.
8. **p97–101: angels' learning.** An especially contestable interpretation lists eighteen alleged defects of the angels in the Adamic narrative; distinguish authorial spiritual exegesis from theological consensus and from the Qurʾān's direct wording. Crucially p98–99 explains knowing all divine names versus unknowability of the Divine Essence as such, and repeats `أسماء الأسماء`. On p101 he explicitly rejects the philosophical view that angels, spirits and intellects lack growth, arguing that they can undergo `الترقّي والزيادة`.
