## What «continuous» and «verified» mean here

The Arabic text is the online modern edited B2007 **digitization**, whose `reading_ar` preserves certain embedded modern in-text numbered footnote cues. Unit fields are exact contiguous substrings of that archived digital field, **including any such cues**; English does not treat the marker numbers as authorial words. The modern editor's actual footnote prose is excluded, as in the existing reading desk. All 76 clauses have a fresh continuous-order English rendering and a linguistic/critical editorial note. These are **not independently photographed physical Beirut 2007 pages, full readings of I1876 pages, or medieval manuscript checks**.

## Corrections carried into both English reading modes and the working English Markdown

1. **B2007 p19, «الكمّل في عرض التعيّن والتجلّي الأول»:** prior reader “participate in the display” asserted a specific action not demanded by the Arabic. The revised reader retains *within the scope* of the first determination and self-disclosure; the exact cross-page syntax (beginning at the end of p18) is shown in the optional full clause record. No claim is made that this resolves the semantic range of `ʿarḍ` from an original manuscript.
2. **B2007 p22, «كبعلبك ومعديكرب»:** the author himself supplies *Baʿlabakk* and *Maʿdīkarib*, rather than unspecified “certain other compound names.” The names are now visible in the uninterrupted text, without an editorial footnote.
3. **B2007 p22, «نظرنا ... ألفينا»:** these are **first-person plurals** (“we considered ... we found”), not a hypothetical conditional. The main English text now follows the literal authorial syntax. Stage XXI's already corrected selected CR002-023 remains intact.

All three changes were applied identically in `continuous-translation.html`, `parallel-introduction.html`, and `WORKING_TRANSLATION_18_145.md`. The unchanged surrounding 122 English page positions and the digitized Arabic master are protected from overwriting.

## Specific difficult readings and non-resolutions

- **B2007 p18 «وبطون المشتمل»** is present in the *photographed 1876 printing* and modern digitization; its syntax is awkward. The English construal is provisional. Do not claim a manuscript variant or silently emend to a smoother Arabic phrase.
- **B2007 p19 «المطلب الغائبّ» / I1876 printed p6 «المطلب الغائي»:** older PRINT variant already photographed, and working English follows the purposive sense. Both readings remain source-graded. **B2007 p19 «عمليّا» / I1876 p6 «علميّا»** likewise remains documented; the knowledge/existence pair in English follows photographed older PRINT provisionally. These remain print-level controls, not an established archetype.
- **B2007 p19 «المقدّر والمقدّر»:** same unvowelled spelling admits active/passive interpretation (determiner and determined). No unwarranted vowel-restoration or manuscript claim.
- **B2007 p20 «ربّ زدني فيك تحيّرا»** and **B2007 p18 “hidden treasure”** are reproduced as Farghānī's own attributed reports; the editor does **not** validate them as prophetic ḥadīth. See al-ʿAjlūnī, *Kashf al-khafāʾ* (also documented by later commentators) and discussion in [hadith critical literature](https://islamicportal.co.uk/hadith-query-i-was-a-hidden-treasure-and-i-wished-to-be-known/); and the discussion of [«زدني فيك تحيّرا»](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/مجموع_الفتاوى/المجلد_الحادي_عشر/سئل_هل_قال_النبي_صلى_الله_عليه_وسلم_زدني_فيك_تحيرا). Maintain the separation between Farghānī's use of a report and authentication.
- **B2007 p21 «من حيث واحديتها»** is independently corroborated in a selected photographed I1876 print locus; critical to contrast *wāḥidiyya* with *aḥadiyya*. Avoid conflating these or projecting chronological succession onto the Essence.
- **B2007 p23 «فلم تعلم ولم يحكم بشيء منها إلّا بعد تعيّنها»:** preserves the tension concerning possibilities that have not yet received differentiated rank-determination. Do not silently substitute a dogmatic harmonization or claim that the author necessarily denies divine omniscience.
- **B2007 p23 «ليت رب محمد لم يخلق محمدا»:** Farghānī attributes this saying to the Prophet; its hadith authenticity was **not established** in this pass. The nearby «أحاديث صحاح» reflects Farghānī's assessment of his cited reports, not independent grading of every report.
- **B2007 pp23–24:** p23 terminates `اعتبارا لبعض` and p24 begins `معاني الشأن وهو الأمر`. The reviewed clause record preserves the open syntactic boundary; the next proofreading pass must treat the joined sentence rather than artificially complete p23.
- **B2007 p87 «أوجبت محضة»** and **p138 «مشمّة الروح»** remain unresolved as in Stage XXI. Do not change either in the Arabic or English until controlling historical print/manuscript evidence permits.
