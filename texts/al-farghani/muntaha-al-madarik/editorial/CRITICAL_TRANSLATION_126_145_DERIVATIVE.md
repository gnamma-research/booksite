# Farghānī · Critical Pass XIV-B · B2007 pp126–145

**Source grade:** derivative secondary retranscription of modern Beirut edited Arabic, NOT an independently inspected edition or any handwritten witness. 43 bounded comparisons from Foundation IV pp126–143 and the *supplement to the preface*, pp144–145. Not a continuous critically transcribed Arabic text. Existing working English p18–145 remains separate.

## Central editorial conclusions

1. **pp126–134:** Three linked journeys or domains — outward Islam, inward faith and excellence or witnessing — accompanied by repentance, renunciation, scrupulousness, discipline, entrustment, patience, gratitude, contentment, certainty, poverty and the birth of the heart. Distinguish Farghānī's structured spiritual account from universal juridical definitions or modern therapeutic claims.
2. **pp135–143:** Inner states of love, contraction/expansion, stabilization, and ultimate comprehensive realization. The account of two aspects of existence, *bestowing* and *bestowed*, is reiterated. A fivefold taxonomy of love occurs on p142. A 'vision of action' on p143 introduces Ibn al-Fāriḍ's poem; do not mistake it for all levels of vision.
3. **pp144–145 (supplement):** The preface explicitly gives four ranks of proximity: love, unification, knowledge, and realization (*taḥqīq*), and explains the bond of love through acts, attributes, and Essence. This is the most compact systematic recap of the preceding introduction.
