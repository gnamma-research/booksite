# Farghānī · Historical-print source collation, Stage VII

**Date:** 9 November 2026. *Muntahā al-madārik*, the four introductory uṣūl. **Two new photographs inspected at the loci below; modern B2007 readings are separated from the 1876 printed facsimile.** These are not medieval manuscript variants.

## New finding 1 · The modal reservation, B2007 printed p. 23

**B2007:** `إلّا أنه ربما يكون` · **I1876 printed p. 8 / PDF scan 12:** `سوى أنه ربما يكون`.

The older print uses `سوى` where the Beirut digital text reads `إلّا`. Both introduce an exception/qualification; neither should be pressed into evidence of a new metaphysical thesis. **Adopt `سوى أنه ربما يكون` provisionally** in the source-corrected Arabic reading layer, retaining `إلّا أنه ربما يكون` as the Beirut reading. The substantive point is the modal reservation `ربما` and the author's discussion of possible hidden affairs not yet determined in the explicitly stated ranks. Do **not** erase that reservation by explaining it away as temporal divine learning, and do not smooth the text without observing the manuscripts.

## New finding 2 · The difficult instrument phrase, B2007 printed p. 33

**B2007:** `في عون هذا` · **I1876 printed p. 16 / PDF scan 20:** `في عون هذا`.

The high-resolution historic printed image supports the word `عون`, not the conjecture `عين`. **Retain `في عون هذا` in the diplomatic and provisional edited text.** The syntax and referent of “this assistance” remain uncertain; historical print agreement is not a grammatical interpretation and does not certify an authorial manuscript form. The existing English bracket flag should be maintained pending philological reconstruction with earlier codices and neighbouring clauses. Do not invent an unattested easy reading.

## Additional control · water and vessel, B2007 p.31

The Beirut digital display merges words as `لون الماءلون إنائه`. The original 1876 p.14 (scan 18) reads `لون الماء لون إنائه`. This is a **word-spacing/typography correction**, not a substantive variant between copy traditions, and is recorded under typographic controls rather than added to the ten substantive comparisons.
