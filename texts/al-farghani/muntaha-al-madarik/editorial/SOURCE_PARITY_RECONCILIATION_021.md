**17 November 2026 · B2007 digitized Arabic only.** This report establishes where **previously selected excerpt strings** occur in the digitized copy-text. It does **not** prove that the English translation is complete and literally checked clause by clause, does not inspect the physical Beirut volume, and does not establish any medieval manuscript readings.

- **20** selected Arabic excerpt quotations were made literal to the currently archived B2007 digital authorial text (previous abbreviated or reconstructed quotation preserved in each record under `source_excerpt_before_reconciliation`); **six** corresponding short English excerpts were also revised where their sense or syntax required.

- **Six** excerpts deliberately use textual ellipses between source clauses that occur in the correct order on the same page; these are **abridged selections, not contiguous verbatim quotations**.
- **One** prior flag proves already exact under conservative normalization and needs no textual rewrite.
- All **37/37 previously flagged quote locations** have now been classified and reconciled. The 560 selected record IDs, their grade labels (284 modern edited-digital, 276 derivative), 128 introductory page labels, and 15 inherited historic-print-photo readings are conserved; **no** physical-print or manuscript collation has been added.

## Source repairs with substantive implications
**B2007 p.22 · CR002-023:** digitized **«نظرنا ... ألفينا»** is first-person plural (“we considered ... we found”), not the prior selected **«نظرا»**; the corresponding English excerpt has been recast to reflect that syntax. The critical dataset retains the former wording.

**B2007 p.84 · CR009-019:** an earlier stitched phrase referring to the Footstool has been replaced by its actual statement concerning the name *al-Bāriʾ*, a circular form and the receptive appearance of intelligible, spiritual and sensible forms. This is now a continuous source excerpt rather than a conflated quotation.

**Beirut printed-page continuities:** `CR004-026` pp.32–33 (receptive locus), `CR009-008` pp.80–81 (*habāʾ*), `CR009-054` pp.94–95 (creatures moving on their bellies), and `CR009-066` pp.98–99 (words as names of names) are not misassigned one-page quotations: the sentences literally run across printed-page breaks.

**Remaining Arabic/English crux not falsely “resolved”:** B2007 digital p.138 `مشمّة الروح` vs working English “womb of the spirit,” and p.87 `أوجبت محضة` with a modern editor's warning. Their digitized *location* can be verified while their linguistic/historical sense remains open. An independent printed or manuscript witness, not a guessed correction, is required.
