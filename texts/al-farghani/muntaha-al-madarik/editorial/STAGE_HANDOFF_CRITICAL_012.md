## Stage X — pp61–69 (end Foundation I)
[Full Arabic–English checked selected record](CRITICAL_TRANSLATION_61_69_DERIVATIVE.md) · [data](../data/critical-pass-007-p61-69.js). **57** records: retain 36, annotation 12, revision 8, unresolved crux 1.

- p61: Seven modes of generosity, with the first gift of existence distinguished from sustenance and character.
- p62: `الاسم الغفار` as `تعميما` (generality) must not collapse into `الاسم الغفور` as `تعديدا` (multiplicity).
- p63: `الفتح` names an articulated process from concealed intelligibles through bodily life into spiritual rebirth; `مشيمة` should not automatically be translated as modern 'placenta' without lexicographic and contextual investigation.
- p64: `الغنى` falls under `الجواد` when it means possessions and bestowed sufficiency, but as `عدم الاحتياج` belongs to negative essential names. This is explicit **classification by iʿtibār**, not a global immutable parent-tree.
- pp65–67: Proper Lordship (`ربه الخاص`) through the differentiating divine name resides within universally divine Lordship; p66 speaks of continual `الخلق الجديد` with every instant.
- pp68–69: Distinguish **bestowing** existence (`مفيض`) from **bestowed** existence (`مفاض`) and track the exoteric/esoteric conditional argument about `لو`; independently verify attribution to Ibn Fāris and reported ḥadīth. End of first uṣūl at p69.

## Stage XI — pp70–78 (whole Foundation II)
[Full Arabic–English checked selected record](CRITICAL_TRANSLATION_70_78_DERIVATIVE.md) · [data](../data/critical-pass-008-p70-78.js). **54** records: retain 39, annotation 11, revision 3, unresolved crux 1.

- pp70–71: Original love (`المحبة الأصلية`) explains non-temporal activity of names through the cosmos. There are causally differentiated roles, not successive temporal actors inside the Divine Essence.
- **p72: `الاسم القائل` (Speaker) is explicitly contrasted with `عين القابل` (receptive entity) in a single account of the creative decree.** This joins direct modern Arabic p52, p55, p56 corroboration of `القائل` and makes a p38 digital `القابل` error very plausible. This is **INTERNAL** contextual evidence, not an independent manuscript variant or a photographed I1876 reading. Keep p38 unaltered pending witnesses.
- **p73: `وذلك القبض مثل رجوع الوجود كل آن إلى أصله` and `لكونه عرضا على الحقيقة وقيام بدل مثله مكانه في الخلق الجديد`.** The syntactic referent of `لكونه` and ontology of `عرض` must be examined against full context and independent witnesses. Do NOT directly equate renewed replacement of created determination with Ṣadrā's continuous substantial motion. The water/reflection illustration explicitly fits only in some respects (`مطابق من بعض الوجوه لا كلّها`).
- pp74–75: The Supreme Pen, First Intellect, and Muhammadan Spirit are related under different respects; the Preserved Tablet carries six interconnected accounts, not six created substances.
- **p76: `ففي إيجاد الأرواح كان القول ركنا ، والقدرة تتمّة له وشرطا وفي الأجسام بالعكس`**: speech is principal in the origination of spirits but power in that of bodies. A fixed one-direction parent map of the seven names is inadequate.
- pp77–78: Names of four angels express life, knowledge, will, and power; 'wings' as knowing, acting, instructing and helping are a spiritual interpretation, not a historical manuscript variant or independent authentication of reports.
