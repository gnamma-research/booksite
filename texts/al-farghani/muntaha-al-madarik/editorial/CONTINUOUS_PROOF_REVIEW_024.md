## Source-specific decisions and controls

### B2007 p29 · «المذكور» vs «المذكورة»
The digitized 2007 text has «والتعيّن الأول المذكورة وقلبه المطهّر». The **photographed Istanbul 1876 PRINT** (I1876-L06) reads «والتعيّن الأول المذكور وقلبه المطهّر», agreeing with the masculine agreement demanded by `التعيّن`. The English proof uses 'the aforesaid first determination' provisionally. Both printed-era textual witnesses remain separate; the digital Arabic is **not silently emended**, and this is **not a manuscript variant**. Inspect further original witnesses before constituting an authorial text.

The p29 claim concerning the first creation of the Prophet's light «أوّل ما خلق اللّه تعالى نوري» is an authorial *attributed saying*. Its appearance in Farghānī is not an independent authentication of it as a Prophetic ḥadīth. The terms *qāb qawsayn*, *aw adnā* and Aḥmadian Reality are read as Farghānī's symbolic structure, not as an empirical measurement of God's nearness.

### B2007 pp30–31 · Essential and name-related perfection
Essential perfection is without differentiating conditions, whereas name-related perfection appears through conditioned manifestations and the all-comprehensive Perfect Human. The definitions of knowledge, *wujūd*, light and witnessing are distinct. On p31 Farghānī invokes a **grammatical analogy** of knowing one object at the first rank and two at the second; this does not impose a universal grammar theory. The digital «لون الماءلون إنائه» appears to have joined two words, but no uninspected print emendation is entered in the source.

### B2007 pp32–33 · Receptivity and relative privation
The two modalities of second-rank manifestation distinguish the Real's divine names and the appearance of the cosmos. Intelligible loci are described as relative privations; do not translate the claim as an absolute denial of all created actuality. The first determination cannot receive differentiated name-related perfection under the pure-unicity consideration, hence the turn toward the second receptive determination. The p32–33 cross-page phrase is preserved intact. **I1876-L10**, photographed 1876 printed «في عون هذا», agrees with the awkward digitized reading; the English is provisional rather than an invented correction.

### B2007 p34 · Cloud-like Presence
The digital 2007 transcription reads «الحضرة العملية»; the photographed older 1876 **printing** (I1876-L05) reads **«الحضرة العمائية»**. The English scholarly proof and already corrected clean reader provisionally favour the latter, preserving the later digital lemma separately. B2007 p36 itself has «الحضرة العمائية» in the same conceptual setting; this is useful internal comparison, **not** authentication of either printed text from a manuscript. Other labels (Divinity, Presence of Inscription, Eternal Knowledge, Perfected Humanity, Possibility) are different considerations of the second determination, not separate divine substances.

### B2007 pp35–36 · Names, human intermediary, and life
The list of divine negative names is retained in full, including *al-azalī*, *al-ghanī*, *al-fard*, *al-witr*, *al-quddūs*, *al-salām*, *al-subbūḥ* and *al-mutaʿālī*. The human *barzakh* mediates the judgments of unity and multiplicity. The account of life/knowledge is hierarchical: the Living comprehends appropriate perfections, while knowledge discriminates known things in the second rank. Will begins at the end of p36 and continues in **p37**; do not complete the syntax without reading p37.
