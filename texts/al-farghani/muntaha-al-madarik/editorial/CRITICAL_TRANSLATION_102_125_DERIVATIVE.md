# Farghānī · Critical Pass XIV-A · Foundation IV pp102–125

**Source grade:** secondary online retranscription of the modern Beirut printed Arabic. **48** selected bounded Arabic-to-English checks on every printed-page label 102–125. They are not complete pages and not historical manuscript or 1876 print collation. Full continuous English draft is already present for pp102–125; do not count this as another complete translation.

**Key results:** distinction between essential perfection and name-related perfection (pp102–105); the seven names, Adam, revealed law and the scripture as measure of human perfection (pp106–111); wilāya, nubuwwa and risāla (pp112–117); the Qurʾān’s hermeneutical comprehensiveness (p118); precise renewed-creation definition (p119); spiritual obstacles and therapeutic treatment by sharia and an experienced guide (pp120–122); barzakh, fitra, human composite and excess/deficiency (pp123–125).
