# Farghānī · continuous bilingual clause proof II: B2007 pp.21–23

**Source and standing:** complete contiguous coverage of authorial B2007 online-digitized Arabic pp21–23, separate from the earlier 560 selected excerpts. ## B2007 p.21

[Source Arabic](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/21?lang=ar) · 15 reviewed clauses, no source gaps

### FCP-21-01

**Arabic**  المتجلّي له من باب « كنت سمعه وبصره ، فبي يسمع وبي يبصر » « 1 » ،

**Reviewed English**  ... the servant to whom He discloses Himself, according to the words 'I became his hearing and sight; through Me he hears and through Me he sees.'

*Critical note:* Continuation of the same p20 sentence; preserve the servant's role in the n awāfil path (the Real as means of servant's apprehension).

### FCP-21-02

**Arabic**  وفي السير المحبوبي وقرب الفرائض وتأخّر السلوك عن الجذبة وتقدم البقاء الأصلي على الفناء حيث يتبيّن أن العبد المتجلّى له آلة لإدراك الحق المتجلّي من باب « فإن اللّه تعالى قال على لسان عبده : سمع اللّه تعالى لمن حمده » « 2 » ،

**Reviewed English**  In the journey of being beloved and nearness through obligatory acts, by contrast, disciplined wayfaring follows attraction, and original abiding precedes annihilation. Then it is disclosed that the servant who receives the self-disclosure is an instrument through which the self-disclosing Real apprehends, as in the words, 'God has said upon His servant's tongue: God hears the one who praises Him.'

*Critical note:* Do not collapse the n awāfil/obligatory-acts polarity: f arāʾiḍ path reverses which term is described as 'instrument'. The prayer formula سمع الله لمن حمده is an authorial analogy, not an argument for identity of worshipper and God.

### FCP-21-03

**Arabic**  وعند انتهاء السيرين والجمع بين الحكمين ابتداء وانتهاء حيث يصح أن كل واحد منهما مدركا وآلة على التعاقب أو معا في حالة واحدة من باب وَما رَمَيْتَ إِذْ رَمَيْتَ وَلكِنَّ اللَّهَ رَمى [ الأنفال : الآية 17 ] ،

**Reviewed English**  When the two journeys end and their two judgments are united from beginning to end, each [the Real and the servant] may be apprehender or instrument, alternately or simultaneously in a single state, as in the Qur'ānic words 'You did not throw when you threw, but God threw' (8:17).

*Critical note:* Q 8:17 anchors a simultaneous/alternating two-sided attribution, neither sheer monism nor autonomous causation.

### FCP-21-04

**Arabic**  فعلى كل حال يكون ذلك العلم والشهود والتجلّي والإدراك من حيث تعينه ومشيئته وعلمه المقدّس بذاته الأقدس تعالى وتقدّس من حيث واحديتها ، لا من حيث إطلاقها وأحديتها ،

**Reviewed English**  In any case, knowledge, witnessing, self-disclosure and apprehension of this kind pertain to His determination, His will, and His holy knowledge of His most holy Essence, under the consideration of oneness (wāḥidiyya), not under that of non-delimitation and unicity (aḥadiyya).

*Critical note:* Important exact B2007 qualifier «من حيث واحديتها» independently corroborated in I1876 p7; distinguish Essence's aḥadiyya from determinate wāḥidiyya.

### FCP-21-05

**Arabic**  فإن إدراكه وشهوده من حيث تعيّنه وعلمه غير ممنوع ، بل موكول إلى مشيئته تعالى وتقدّس كما قال عزّ من قائل : وَلا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِما شاءَ [ البقرة : الآية 255 ] ،

**Reviewed English**  His apprehension and witnessing in respect of determination and knowledge are not prohibited; rather they are dependent upon His will, as in His words: 'They encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills' (Qurʾān 2:255).

*Critical note:* Author explicitly makes knowledge of the qualified determination possible by divine permission. Do not overgeneralize essential unknowability.

### FCP-21-06

**Arabic**  ثم إن وحدته التي انتشئت منها الأحديّة « 3 » والواحدية هي عين ذاته لا صفة زائدة ولا نعت زائد عليها ،

**Reviewed English**  His unity, from which unicity (aḥadiyya) and oneness (wāḥidiyya) arise, is identical with His Essence, neither an added attribute nor a further qualification.

*Critical note:* These are two considerations of one essential unity, not two divine substances.

### FCP-21-07

**Arabic**  لكن للوحدة اعتباران أصليّان أوليّان ،

**Reviewed English**  But unity admits two primordial and primary considerations.

*Critical note:* The grammatical pair iʿtibārāt is philosophical 'considerations', not ontologically independent properties.

### FCP-21-08

**Arabic**  أحدهما : سقوط الاعتبارات عنها بالكلية ، وسمّي الذات أحدا بهذا الاعتبار ومتعلقة بطون الذات وإطلاقها وأزليّتها ،

**Reviewed English**  In the first, all considerations whatsoever fall away from it. Under this consideration the Essence is called aḥad (One-in-unicity); this consideration concerns the inwardness, non-delimitation and pre-eternity of the Essence.

*Critical note:* «سقوط الاعتبارات» indicates the absence of delimiting considerations at the level named aḥad.

### FCP-21-09

**Arabic**  وعلى هذا يكون نسبة الاسم الأحد إلى السلب أحقّ من نسبته إلى الثبوت والإيجاب .

**Reviewed English**  In this respect the name aḥad is more properly referred to negation than to positive affirmation.

*Critical note:* Negation is predicated of the designation aḥad, not of divine actuality itself.

### FCP-21-10

**Arabic**  والاعتبار الثاني : ثبوت الاعتبارات الغير المتناهية لها مع اندراجها فيها في أول رتبة الذات ،

**Reviewed English**  The second consideration is that infinitely many considerations are established for it, though they remain included within it at the first rank of the Essence.

*Critical note:* «ثبوت» is establishment of relations in unity, not independent external realization.

### FCP-21-11

**Arabic**  وتحقّق أكثر تفصيل تعيّناتها في ثاني الرتبة الآتي بيان كليهما عن قريب إن شاء اللّه تعالى ؛ كالنصفية والثلثية والربعية الثابتة والمندرجة في هذا الواحد المشهود ههنا ، التي ينتشىء منها الأعداد في المراتب الوجودية ، وينتشىء من تلك الاعتبارات أعيان الاثنين والثلاثة والأربعة فيها ،

**Reviewed English**  The greater differentiation of their determinations becomes actual at the second rank, and both ranks will be explained soon, God willing. The analogy is with the relations of being a half, third or quarter, established in and included within the numerical one whose effects we observe: numbers arise through those relations in the orders of being, and with them the determinate twos, threes and fours.

*Critical note:* Keep analogy of number limited: the one is not divided into physical parts when it is half of two.

### FCP-21-12

**Arabic**  كما يقال : الواحد نصف الاثنين ، وثلث الثلاثة ، وربع الأربعة وهلمّ جرّا .

**Reviewed English**  As one says: one is half of two, a third of three, a quarter of four, and so on.

*Critical note:* These are examples of logical relations, not a claim that God is a numeral.

### FCP-21-13

**Arabic**  والذات بهذا الاعتبار يسمّى واحدا اسما ثبوتيّا لا سلبيّا ،

**Reviewed English**  Under this consideration the Essence is called wāḥid, a name of positive affirmation rather than negation.

*Critical note:* Positive title wāḥid is not coextensive with the negatively characterized aḥad.

### FCP-21-14

**Arabic**  ولم تصح إضافة هذه الاعتبارات المذكورة إلى الاسم الأحد لا حقيقة ولا وضعا لغويّا ،

**Reviewed English**  The considerations just described cannot be assigned to the name aḥad, neither in reality nor according to linguistic convention.

*Critical note:* Technical/linguistic limitation stated explicitly.

### FCP-21-15

**Arabic**  فحقائق هذه

**Reviewed English**  The realities of these ...

*Critical note:* Printed p21 ends mid-noun phrase and p22 begins «الاعتبارات»; do not invent a complete standalone sentence.

## B2007 p.22

[Source Arabic](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/22?lang=ar) · 14 reviewed clauses, no source gaps

### FCP-22-01

**Arabic**  الاعتبارات في أول رتبة الذات ثابتة للاسم الواحد الحقيقي ، ومندرجة فيه ثبوتا حقيقيّا واندراجا أصليّا ؛

**Reviewed English**  ... considerations at the first rank of the Essence are established under the name of the truly One, and are truly established and originally contained in it.

*Critical note:* Continuation of p21 «فحقائق هذه»; no newly introduced subject.

### FCP-22-02

**Arabic**  كما أن أمثلتها وصورها ثابتة لهذا الواحد المشهود أثره لنا ههنا ، ومندرجة فيه غير قادحة في الرتبتين في صرافة هذا الواحد ، ولا منافية لها ،

**Reviewed English**  Just as the examples and images of these considerations are established and included within the numerical one whose effect we witness here, without, in either rank, impairing or contradicting the purity of that one.

*Critical note:* The analogy is logical relation-without-partition, not numerical identity of Essence with created number.

### FCP-22-03

**Arabic**  ومتعلق هذا الاعتبار الواحدي ظهور الذات ووجودها وأبديّتها ؛ كما أن متعلق الاعتبار الأحدي بطونها وأزليّتها ،

**Reviewed English**  What pertains to the consideration of wāḥidiyya is the Essence's manifestation, existence and everlastingness, just as what pertains to the consideration of aḥadiyya is its inwardness and pre-eternity.

*Critical note:* Pre-eternity (azaliyya) and everlastingness (abadiyya) are presented under different considerations, not two temporal segments of God.

### FCP-22-04

**Arabic**  ولا مغايرة ولا غيرية بين هذين الاعتبارين ، ولا بين كل اعتبار واعتبار من اعتبارات الواحدية في أول رتبة الذات الآتي بيانه ؛

**Reviewed English**  These two considerations are not different or other than one another; neither is any one consideration of oneness different from another at the first rank of the Essence, to be explained.

*Critical note:* Multiplicity of considerations is not multiplicity of essences.

### FCP-22-05

**Arabic**  لأن المغايرة والغيرية من أحكام الكثرة والمنافاة الثابتة بينها وبين الوحدة ، ولا كثرة ثمة تنافي الوحدة ،

**Reviewed English**  Otherness and difference are consequences of multiplicity and of its opposition to unity; no such multiplicity at that rank opposes unity.

*Critical note:* «المغايرة والغيرية» preserve distinguishable but related terms.

### FCP-22-06

**Arabic**  بل تلك الوحدة هي المنشئة لكل وحدة وكثرة مفهومة ههنا متغايرة عندنا ، ولا مغايرة ولا غيرية ولا امتياز كما قلنا آنفا في أول رتبة الذات الأقدس ،

**Reviewed English**  Rather, that unity is the source of every unity and multiplicity which appears mutually differentiated to our understanding. As stated, otherness, difference and differentiation do not obtain at the first rank of the most holy Essence.

*Critical note:* Earlier synthesis correctly denies opposition at rank one; this does not erase later degrees of differentiated realities.

### FCP-22-07

**Arabic**  ولا يفهم ارتفاع هذه المغايرة في تلك الحضرة إلّا من ارتفع حالا ومقاما عن التقيّد بالمراتب المقيّدة بأحكام الكثرة والمقيّدة لأربابها بها ،

**Reviewed English**  Only someone who has risen, in spiritual state and station, beyond restriction by ranks governed by multiplicity, which bind their possessors to those judgments, can understand how this otherness is absent in that Presence.

*Critical note:* Refers to transformation of the knower, not suspension of distinctions in ordinary law or empirical cognition.

### FCP-22-08

**Arabic**  ولارتفاع حكم هذه المغايرة في الرتبة الأولى حكم بها بعض أكابر المحقّقين من أهل اللّه بأن الواحد الأحد اسم واحد مركّب ؛ كبعلبك ومعديكرب .

**Reviewed English**  Because difference is suspended at this first rank, some great realized knowers among the people of God regard al-wāḥid al-aḥad as a single compound name, just as Baʿlabakk and Maʿdīkarib are compound proper names.

*Critical note:* Author supplies the actual compound-name examples Baʿlabakk and Maʿdīkarib; generic 'certain proper names' may be expanded in the reader without technical intrusion.

### FCP-22-09

**Arabic**  ثم اعلم أن هذه الاعتبارات الثابتة للواحد ثمة ، والمندرجة فيه في أول رتبة الذات المحكوم عليها فيها بأنها عين الذات ، بحيث إن كل ما يضاف إلى الذات يضاف إلى كلّ واحد منها من الجمعية والاشتمال على الكلّ في هذه الرتبة الأولى بحسبها

**Reviewed English**  Know further that those considerations established for the One and included within it at the first rank are there judged to be identical with the Essence. Accordingly, whatever is attributed to the Essence in respect of comprehensiveness and containing the whole is attributed to each of them at that rank.

*Critical note:* «محكوم عليها فيها بأنها عين الذات» is predication at a particular rank; avoid asserting independent eternal essences.

### FCP-22-10

**Arabic**  نظرنا من حيث مظاهرها وصورها وآثارها إليها ألفينا بعضها كلّيات وأصولا أول بمثابة أجناس عالية هي مسمّى ألفاظ دالّة على حقائق أسماء الذات ومفهوماتها التي منها مفاتح الغيب ، والواحد الأحد وباطن اسم اللّه ، وباطن اسم الرحمن الرحيم ، ومفهوم جمع أسماء الضمائر ،

**Reviewed English**  When we consider them with regard to their manifestations, forms and effects, we find some to be universals and first principles, comparable to the highest genera. They are what the expressions denoting the realities and intelligible contents of the essential names signify: among them the Keys of the Unseen, al-wāḥid al-aḥad, the inward aspect of the name Allāh, the inward aspects of al-Raḥmān and al-Raḥīm, and the collective sense of the pronominal names.

*Critical note:* Stage XXI selected excerpt CR002-023 already corrected the Arabic «نظرنا ... ألفينا» (we considered, we found). Continuous English should reflect the first-person authorial grammar too.

### FCP-22-11

**Arabic**  ووجدنا بعض تلك الاعتبارات المندرجة في الواحدية من حيث النظر في مظاهرها وآثارها بمثابة أجناس تالية وأنواع مندرجة فيها وهلمّ جرّا إلى أشخاص هاوية إلى الدّركات الجزئية جدّا التي بها يحكم على الحضرة الأبدية بعدم التناهي كثرة وتعيّنات وتقيّدات ،

**Reviewed English**  Others of these considerations included in oneness, viewed through their manifestations and effects, resemble subordinate genera and species contained within them, down to individual things descending to the most narrowly particular degrees. By these, the everlasting Presence is spoken of as having infinitely many determinations, multiplicities and delimitations.

*Critical note:* Particularization ends in very low ranks, not in metaphysically autonomous individuals.

### FCP-22-12

**Arabic**  والكل ثابتة بصورها المعنوية في الرتبة الثانية بعضها بطريق التشخّص ، وبعضها بطريق الاندراج في الأصناف والأنواع .

**Reviewed English**  All remain established in intelligible forms at the second rank: some through individuation and some through inclusion in species and kinds.

*Critical note:* Established intelligible forms are not identical with physical external existence.

### FCP-22-13

**Arabic**  نعم ، وقد تعيّنت وانضبطت جملة منها في اللّوح المحفوظ ، وتصوّرت صورا وجودية روحانية فيه منتهيا ظهورها بانتهاء يوم القيامة مجملا ومفصّلا ،

**Reviewed English**  Many have further been determined and registered in the Preserved Tablet, taking on spiritual existential forms there. Their manifestation, general and detailed, extends to the end of the Day of Resurrection.

*Critical note:* «نعم وقد» introduces a qualification; do not identify Preserved Tablet with the earlier first rank.

### FCP-22-14

**Arabic**  ويتفصّل منه في المراتب الوجودية مجملا في العرش ومفصّلا في الكرسي دفعة واحدة ، ثم يتفصّل منهما في الأركان والسماوات على التعاقب إلى أنهى مراتب

**Reviewed English**  They are differentiated from the Tablet in the existential degrees, collectively in the Throne and distinctly in the Footstool at once, and thereafter in successive stages in the elements and heavens, down to the furthest degrees ...

*Critical note:* Sentence runs through next printed page, «الكون مثالا وحسّا». Do not silently complete the cosmic descent here.

### FCP-23-01

**Arabic**  الكون مثالا وحسّا ، وفي المواطن دنيا وبرزخا وآخرة ،

**Reviewed English**  ... of cosmic existence in imaginal and sensible modes, and of the abodes of this world, the intermediate state, and the world to come.

*Critical note:* Completes p22's gradual cosmological descent; 'world' is not just physical/empirical reality.

### FCP-23-02

**Arabic**  وينتهي إلى ههنا جميع الشهودات والإدراكات والمكاشفات الحاصلة للكمّل والأولياء فالأدنى منهم وما فيهم أدنى ، والأعلى في حكم هذا الانتهاء سواء ،

**Reviewed English**  Here is the limit of all the acts of witnessing, apprehension and unveiling attained by the perfected and the friends of God, whether lowest or highest among them; in regard to reaching this limit, both are alike.

*Critical note:* «الأدنى ... والأعلى» are alike only in the asserted terminus of disclosed ranks, not in all attainable degrees.

### FCP-23-03

**Arabic**  إلّا أنه ربما يكون في الحضرة الغيبية الأزليّة نحو هذه الكلّيات والجزئيّات أمور باطنة وأحوال مستورة لم تتعيّن بعد لا في الرتبة الثانية ولا في اللّوح المحفوظ ،

**Reviewed English**  Yet in the pre-eternal unseen Presence there may be concealed matters and hidden states of this kind among the universals and particulars that have not yet been determined in the second rank or the Preserved Tablet.

*Critical note:* Preserve modal qualification «ربما» and the exact level at which determination has not occurred.

### FCP-23-04

**Arabic**  فلم تعلم ولم يحكم بشيء منها إلّا بعد تعيّنها ووقوعها في المراتب الوجودية بالتدريج مع الآنات ،

**Reviewed English**  These have not been known or judged until they become determined and occur gradually in the ranks of existence, moment after moment.

*Critical note:* The text literally says «فلم تعلم ولم يحكم ... إلا بعد تعينها»; do not rewrite away the apparent tension with classical accounts of divine omniscience. Distinguish determinate knowability at ranks from God's essential knowing in later analysis, not in translation.

### FCP-23-05

**Arabic**  وهي أبطن بطون أحكام الغيب الذاتي المغلوب والمسبوق بالرحمة الذاتية ،

**Reviewed English**  They belong to the innermost hiddenness of the rulings of the essential Unseen, over which the essential mercy predominates and has precedence.

*Critical note:* «الرحمة الذاتية» means essential mercy here; the apparent priority of divine mercy is not described as an event in time.

### FCP-23-06

**Arabic**  فلهذا أوجبت الرهبة والخشية المشار إليها في قوله تعالى : قُلْ ما كُنْتُ بِدْعاً مِنَ الرُّسُلِ وَما أَدْرِي ما يُفْعَلُ بِي وَلا بِكُمْ [ الأحقاف : الآية 9 ] ،

**Reviewed English**  This produces the fearful awe indicated by the Qurʾānic words: 'Say: I am no unprecedented messenger, and I do not know what will be done with me or with you' (Q 46:9).

*Critical note:* Q 46:9; do not replace 'I do not know' with theological paraphrase.

### FCP-23-07

**Arabic**  ومن وجه أيضا يشير إليها قوله صلّى اللّه عليه وسلّم شفقا منها : « ليت ربّ محمد لم يخلق محمدا » « 1 » ،

**Reviewed English**  One aspect of this is also, in his fearful concern, alluded to by the saying attributed to the Prophet: 'Would that the Lord of Muḥammad had not created Muḥammad.'

*Critical note:* Attribution of «ليت رب محمد ...» is Farghānī's, not a verified prophetic report. Authentication requires a separate sourced takhrīj.

### FCP-23-08

**Arabic**  مع أنه كان من حيث ما تعيّن من حاله صلّى اللّه عليه وسلّم في الحضرة العلمية واللّوح المحفوظ على بصيرة من ربّه

**Reviewed English**  Yet insofar as his state was determined in the Presence of knowledge and the Preserved Tablet, he was granted insight by his Lord.

*Critical note:* The Prophet's qualified insight is distinguished from absolute, unlimited foresight.

### FCP-23-09

**Arabic**  حتى أنه كان يقول اعتمادا عليها ونظرا إليها : « آدم ومن دونه تحت لوائي يوم القيامة ولا فخر » « 2 » ، « ولو كان موسى حيّا لما وسعه إلّا اتّباعي » « 3 » ،

**Reviewed English**  Hence, relying upon that determined state and considering it, he would say: 'Adam and those below him are under my banner on the Day of Resurrection, without boasting'; and 'If Moses were alive, he would have no course but to follow me.'

*Critical note:* Preserve exact wording as Farghānī quotes it; prophetic-hadith grading varies by report and isnād, and this clause is not independently authenticated here.

### FCP-23-10

**Arabic**  وقوله : « أنا أول من يقرع باب الجنّة وأول من يفتح باب الشفاعة » « 4 » ونحو ذلك من الإشارات في أحاديث صحاح ،

**Reviewed English**  And: 'I shall be the first to knock at the gate of Paradise and the first to open the gate of intercession', together with similar indications in reports that Farghānī describes as sound.

*Critical note:* The author says «أحاديث صحاح», which reports his assessment; avoid making an editor's blanket authentication.

### FCP-23-11

**Arabic**  وعلى الحقيقة كثيرا ما يجرّه الدعاء والسؤال من الأمور والأحكام الغيبية إنما يكون من تلك الأمور والأحوال ،

**Reviewed English**  In truth, many things drawn forth from unseen matters and judgments by prayer and supplication arise from those hidden matters and states.

*Critical note:* Supplication does not physically produce an eternal change in divine knowledge; preserve his contrast between unmanifest contingencies and established allotments.

### FCP-23-12

**Arabic**  فإن ما عداها لا يتحصّل إلّا المكتوب الثابت المقسوم في الحضرة العلمية لا يزيد ولا ينقص ،

**Reviewed English**  As for what lies outside them, nothing can come to be except what is written, established and allotted in the Presence of knowledge; this neither increases nor decreases.

*Critical note:* «لا يزيد ولا ينقص» explicitly qualifies this class of established allotments, not the entire prior class.

### FCP-23-13

**Arabic**  ثم إنه قد عبّر بعض العلماء باللّه الواقفين على حضراته الكلية عن بعض اعتبارات الواحدية المندرجة فيها في الرتبة الأولى بالشؤون الذاتية ،

**Reviewed English**  Some knowers of God who have stood in His universal Presences have called certain considerations of wāḥidiyya, included at the first rank, 'essential affairs' (shuʾūn dhātiyya).

*Critical note:* These terms are the author's reported technical terminology for certain relations, not independent hypostases.

### FCP-23-14

**Arabic**  وهي التي تظهر في الرتبة الثانية وما تحتها من المراتب بصور الحقائق المتبوعة وبعض صفاتها التابعة التي لها الثبات ، نحو الحياة والعلم

**Reviewed English**  At the second rank and below they appear as forms of leading realities and of certain stable subordinate attributes, such as life and knowledge.

*Critical note:* «الحقائق المتبوعة» are leading/primary realities; «الصفات التابعة» subordinate stable properties.

### FCP-23-15

**Arabic**  وبصور أمور كائنة ثابتة نحو الذوات والجواهر ،

**Reviewed English**  They also appear as forms of enduring existent things, such as essences and substances.

*Critical note:* Do not flatten ذوات and جواهر to the same English technical term without note.
