## Results that replace superseded Stage XIV inferences

### A. Beirut B2007 p38 ↔ 1876 original printed p20, image scan24 (I1876-L11): printed agreement

**Modern edited digital:** `( والقابل ) يجمعها`  
**I1876 photographic PRINT:** `والقابل يجمعها`

The disputed word **القابل**, *al-qābil* (“receptive/receptacle”), appears unambiguously in both the photographed 1876 printing and the B2007 online transcription. Earlier speculation that the passage **must** instead read **القائل**, *al-qāʾil* (“Speaker”), based on the seven-name structure and appearances of `اسم القائل` on pp52,55,56,72,89 and 106 is **not supported by the two checked printed sources**. Preserve **القابل**; explain receptive aspect versus speaking name as a provisional semantic distinction. Do not claim the author's archetypal reading has been determined: actual handwritten sources may differ. *Exact official source image:* https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco000264/24/full/2200,/0/default.jpg.

### B. B2007 p48 ↔ I1876 original printed p28, image scan32 (I1876-L12): real substantive reading difference

**Modern edited digital:** `وبالاستتار بسبحاب أعيانها وأسرارها وأنوارها`  
**I1876 photographic PRINT:** `وبالاستتار بسبحات أعيانها وأسرارها وأنوارها`

Provisional edited Arabic: **بسبحات**, plural of `سُبْحَة` (“splendour, radiance, effulgence”). Earlier conjecture `بسحاب` (“clouds”) was **not** witnessed and is **superseded**. The English now reads: **“…and through becoming concealed within the splendours (*subuḥāt*) of their realities, mysteries, and lights.”** This correction is actually made both in the 128-page working English translation and in bilingual machine-readable CR006-002, which preserves the modern digital `ar` and new `edited_ar`. Stage XV's apparatus gives exact scanned page. Since the physical B2007 page has not been inspected, label this an **older printed versus later digital text difference**, rather than conclusively an error on B2007's paper. *Official source image:* https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco000264/32/full/2200,/0/default.jpg.

### C. B2007 p61 ↔ I1876 original printed p38, image scan42 (I1876-L13): substantive clause agreement

The older print photo directly verifies the three initial modes of generosity: **الإيثار يكون بالوجود أولا ثم بالبقاء ثانيا ثم بنفخ الروح والحياة ثالثا** (granting existence, continuance, then life/spirit). The previous DERIVATIVE modern forum uses initial connective `فذلك` whereas the photographed older print appears `وذلك`; the verified **agreement is confined to the shared core enumerative clause**, not the connective. This does **not** turn all of B2007 p61 into a primary-edition line-check. *Official source image:* https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco000264/42/full/2200,/0/default.jpg.

### D. B2007 p62 ↔ I1876 original printed p39, image scan43 (I1876-L14): bounded agreement

The historical photo independently corroborates **تعميما ... الاسم الغفار** as a generality/extent and **تعديدا ... الاسم الغفور** as multiplicity of instances, materially supporting the distinction already drawn in the English correction. Again the B2007 modern source at p62 was a **secondary forum retranscription**, not a new independently photographed printing. *Official source image:* https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco000264/43/full/2200,/0/default.jpg.

### E. Unverified watch-list B2007 p52 ↔ I1876 original printed p32, scan36 (NOT counted as historical print reading)

The scanned low-resolution text appears perhaps `اسم الحكم` rather than the modern digital excerpt `سم الحكيم`. This may distinguish the substantively different names **al-Ḥakam** (Judge) and **al-Ḥakīm** (Wise), but the yāʾ and surrounding marks are not adequately legible in the 760px archive derivation. It is filed solely as `I1876-OBS15-PENDING`, **no new variant and no emendation**. Obtain original-resolution 2200px IIIF image, independent B2007 paper photo, and actual handwritten witnesses first. *Official image to inspect:* https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco000264/36/full/2200,/0/default.jpg.
