## Stage IX key collation implication

The B2007 p38 online printed reading `القابل` is now challenged by **three independent internal authorial contexts within the same B2007 digital witness**—pp52,55,56—which use `القائل`; p56 moreover explicitly enumerates the *seven* original names. This is compelling internal evidence for a possible p38 corruption but does **not** count as an I1876 difference or manuscript variant. Preserve p38 B2007 lemma in diplomatic records and propose `القائل` in a clearly marked conjectural edited-text field only after additional witness checks.
