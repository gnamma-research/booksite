## Second source-location check

The first externally indexed Lisanarb PDF `lis_q115/lisanarb.com_q1508.pdf` was physically inspected and rejected: its cover is a different commentary on Ibn al-Fāriḍ's Tāʾiyya, and the PDF has 223 pages, incompatible with the cited first volume of Farghānī's 2007 496-page Beirut edition. It is **not** a B2007 witness and must never enter the apparatus.

An independent bibliography ([Nafahāt al-Ṭarīq](https://www.nafahat-tarik.com/2015/03/sufism_11.html)) links the intended first volume to `https://archive.org/download/lis02029/01.pdf`. Verify the cover/colophon and page numbering in this newly proposed candidate. If not the correct edition, reject it explicitly. Never assume the actual physical 2007 reading without a photographed source.
