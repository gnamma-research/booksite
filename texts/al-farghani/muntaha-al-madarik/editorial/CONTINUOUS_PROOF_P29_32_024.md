# Farghānī · Stage XXIV contiguous Arabic–English proof: B2007 pp. 29–32

**Scope and witness:** source-order bilingual checking of the four-foundation introductory text only, following the verified Stage XXIII p28 frontier. The archived digital B2007 `reading_ar` is preserved verbatim in exact offsets, not an independently photographed physical Beirut 2007 printed page or a manuscript. Reader source remains separately preserved. English clauses are reviewed working translations, with uncertainties in the scholarly notes.

## B2007 printed p. 29
[Digitized Arabic witness](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/29?lang=ar) · 17 continuous reviewed units

### FCP-29-01

**Arabic**  وتعيّن وباطن كل حقيقة إلهية وكونيّة ،

**Reviewed English**  —and determination, and the inward reality of every divine and cosmic reality.

*Scholarly note:* Sentence completes p28 «أصلا لكل اعتبار»; do not treat the initial conjunction as a new independent sentence.

### FCP-29-02

**Arabic**  وأصله الذي انتشىء منه وهو سار بكليته فيه بحيث يكون في الإلهيّة إلهيّا ، وفي الكونية كونيّا ،

**Reviewed English**  It is the source from which each such reality arises, permeating it in its entirety, so that it is divine in the divine order and cosmic in the cosmic order.

*Scholarly note:* Qualify *ilāhī* / *kawnī* as relative to respective determinations, not two different ultimate realities.

### FCP-29-03

**Arabic**  والكل مظاهره وصور تفصيله

**Reviewed English**  All are its manifestations and the forms in which its details unfold.

*Scholarly note:* General predication of manifestations, not a second independent hypostasis.

### FCP-29-04

**Arabic**  وسمّاه بعضهم البرزخ الأكبر الجامع لجميع البرازخ وأصلها الساري فيها ،

**Reviewed English**  Some have named it the Supreme Intermediary (*al-barzakh al-akbar*), comprehending all intermediary states and serving as their permeating source.

*Scholarly note:* Author's reported designation; barzakh is a mediating relation.

### FCP-29-05

**Arabic**  وكنى عنه الشرع بمقام أو أدنى ،

**Reviewed English**  The religious language alludes to it as the station 'or nearer' (*aw adnā*).

*Scholarly note:* Alludes to Q 53:9, not a literal historical measurement of transcendence.

### FCP-29-06

**Arabic**  فإنه باطن مقام قاب قوسين ، أي قرب قوسي الوحدة والكثرة ، أو قل القابلية والفاعلية ، أو قل قوسي الوجوب والإمكان ،

**Reviewed English**  It is inward to the station 'the distance of two bows' (*qāb qawsayn*): the nearness of the two bows of unity and multiplicity, or of receptivity and activity, or of necessity and possibility.

*Scholarly note:* Three paired descriptions refer to relational contrasts, without numerical parts in the Essence.

### FCP-29-07

**Arabic**  وجمعهما وجعلهما دائرة واحدة متّصلة لكن مع أثر ما خفي من التميّز والتكثر بينهما ،

**Reviewed English**  It joins the two as one continuous circle, yet with a faint trace of distinction and plurality between them.

*Scholarly note:* The circular figure is a metaphysical analogy, not a diagram of space.

### FCP-29-08

**Arabic**  وباطن هذا المقام هو مقام أو أدنى من قرب القوسين المذكورين لم يدع أثر التميّز والتكثّر في دائرة الجمعية بين حكم الأحدية والواحدية أصلا ،

**Reviewed English**  The inward reality of that station, 'or nearer' than the two bows, leaves no trace of differentiation or multiplicity in the comprehensive circle between the rulings of unicity (*aḥadiyya*) and oneness (*wāḥidiyya*).

*Scholarly note:* Distinguish the two considered ranks and the absence of even relational difference at *aw adnā*.

### FCP-29-09

**Arabic**  وكنى عنه بعضهم بالحقيقة الأحمدية الثابتة في حاق الوسطية والبرزخية والعدالة بحيث لم يغلب عليه حكم اسم أو صفة أصلا ،

**Reviewed English**  Some designate this the Aḥmadian Reality, established at the very centre of equilibrium and intermediacy, so that the ruling of no single name or attribute prevails over it.

*Scholarly note:* The distinct Aḥmadian terminology must not be conflated with the physical human body.

### FCP-29-10

**Arabic**  وهذا التجلّي الأول المتعين بهذا التعين الأول وفيه هو نوره المظهر لرتبته والمقدّر ظهوره في هذه الحقيقة البرزخية ،

**Reviewed English**  The first self-disclosure, determined in and by this first determination, is his light, manifesting the rank of that reality and destined to appear in this intermediate reality.

*Scholarly note:* Pronoun and attribution in the Aḥmadian/Muḥammadan sequence remain context-bound; source alone does not establish authenticity of later sayings.

### FCP-29-11

**Arabic**  والتعيّن الأول المذكورة وقلبه المطهّر التقي النقي صورة هذا التعين والبرزخية ،

**Reviewed English**  The aforesaid first determination, and his purified, God-fearing and immaculate heart, are the form of this determination and intermediacy.

*Scholarly note:* **I1876-L06:** modern B2007 digital «والتعيّن الأول المذكورة» differs from photographed Istanbul I1876 PRINT «والتعيّن الأول المذكور». Retain digital Arabic diplomatically; the English uses the grammatical masculine sense provisionally. PRINT comparison, not manuscript evidence.

### FCP-29-12

**Arabic**  ولكن صورة جمعية معنوية ومزاجه الأشرف العنصري الحاصل في أعلى درجات الاعتدال صورته ومظهره الجسمانية ،

**Reviewed English**  It is a comprehensive intelligible form, however, while his noblest elemental temperament at the highest level of equilibrium is its bodily image and manifestation.

*Scholarly note:* Maintain Farghānī's distinction between intelligible form (heart) and corporeal temperament.

### FCP-29-13

**Arabic**  فكان ذلك التجلّي الأول النوري - كما قلنا - هو نوره أولا صلى اللّه عليه وسلم ، حتى أشار إليه بقوله : « أوّل ما خلق اللّه تعالى نوري ، واللّه يقول الحقّ وهو يهدي السبيل » « 1 » .

**Reviewed English**  That first luminous self-disclosure is thus, as stated, his light in the first instance—God bless him and grant him peace. He alludes to it with the reported saying 'The first thing God created was my light'; God speaks the truth and guides to the path.

*Scholarly note:* «أوّل ما خلق اللّه تعالى نوري» is reproduced as Farghānī's attributed saying, **not** certified as a ḥadīth.

### FCP-29-14

**Arabic**  فصل ثم إن هذا التجلّي الأول المذكور كما قلنا يتضمّن الكمال الذي حقيقته حصول ما ينبغي على نحو ما ينبغي ،

**Reviewed English**  **Chapter.** The first self-disclosure contains perfection, whose reality is that what ought to occur comes about as it ought to occur.

*Scholarly note:* Chapter *faṣl* is authorial, with a two-division definition of perfection.

### FCP-29-15

**Arabic**  وذلك قسمان : ذاتي مجمل وحداني تحقّقه يكون في هذه المرتبة الأولى ، وفي مبدأ المرتبة الثانية تتعيّن الحياة واسم الحيّ على ما سنذكره إن قدّر اللّه وشاءه ،

**Reviewed English**  Perfection falls into two kinds. The first is essential, comprehensive, unitary perfection realized at the first rank. At the beginning of the second rank, life and the name the Living are determined, as will be explained if God wills.

*Scholarly note:* Do not present determinations of names as temporal changes in the unconditioned Essence.

### FCP-29-16

**Arabic**  ويلازمه الغنى الذاتي الذي معناه شهود الذات وإدراكه نفسه من حيث واحديته بجميع اعتباراتها وشؤونها وأحوالها وبجميع صورها ومظاهرها المعنوية والروحانية والمثالية والحسّية متبوعاتها وتوابعها المندرجة بعضها في البعض والمتعيّنة والمتفرّعة بعضها من بعض أصلا وفرعا ، جنسا ونوعا وشخصا وبدءا وعودا ، نزولا وعروجا ، دنيا وبرزخا وآخرة ،

**Reviewed English**  Essential self-sufficiency accompanies it: the Essence witnesses and apprehends itself under the consideration of *wāḥidiyya*, together with all its considerations, affairs and states, all their intelligible, spiritual, imaginal and sensible forms and manifestations, and principal and subordinate realities included within or derived from one another—roots and branches, genus, species and individual, beginning and return, descent and ascent, this world, the intermediary world and the hereafter.

*Scholarly note:* All lists belong to essential self-sufficiency; no item is omitted in the English.

### FCP-29-17

**Arabic**  كما تثبت وتشاهد وتدرك في مراتب الكون لأربابها دفعة ، أو متعاقبا من كونها أغيارا بالنسبة

**Reviewed English**  The possessors of those forms find them established, witnessed and apprehended in the cosmic ranks, simultaneously or successively, as things different from one another relative—

*Scholarly note:* Page 29 ends within a comparison. Join p30's «إلى شهود الأغيار...» before assessing the whole sentence.

## B2007 printed p. 30
[Digitized Arabic witness](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/30?lang=ar) · 16 continuous reviewed units

### FCP-30-01

**Arabic**  إلى شهود الأغيار في مراتبهم وتقيّدهم بها ، ومن كون الكلّ عينا واحدا بالنسبة إلى شهود الحقّ الواحد الأحد فيما ينسب إليه من المراتب شهود مفصّل في مجمل دفعة واحدة مثل ما يشاهد المكاشف في النواة الواحدة نخيلا وثمارا لا تعدّ ولا تحصى .

**Reviewed English**  —in relation to their witnessing of other things at their own ranks and their restriction by those ranks. By contrast, relative to the One Real's witnessing of all the ranks attributed to Him, they are one reality: a detailed witnessing within a comprehensive whole at once, like the knower by unveiling who sees innumerable palms and fruits within one date-stone.

*Scholarly note:* Completes p29's sentence and contains the date-stone analogy without a temporal division of eternal knowledge.

### FCP-30-02

**Arabic**  وأمّا القسم الثاني من الكمال ، فهو الكمال الأسمائي المفصّل الساري في تفاصيل الأسماء والحقائق ،

**Reviewed English**  The second kind of perfection is differentiated name-related perfection, permeating the distinct names and realities.

*Scholarly note:* Keep *kamāl dhātī* distinct from *kamāl asmāʾī*.

### FCP-30-03

**Arabic**  وهو ما يظهر للذات من حيث تفصيل اعتباراتها وحقائق تميّزاتها مضافة إلى المراتب ،

**Reviewed English**  It appears to the Essence through the differentiation of its considerations and their distinctive realities as related to particular ranks.

*Scholarly note:* The qualified mode 'as attributed to particular ranks' is essential.

### FCP-30-04

**Arabic**  وذلك يكون بشرط شيء ، بل أشياء ، من تعيّن وغير وغيرية بالنسبة ، ومظهرا واجتماع وتركيب معنوي أو صوري إلى غير ذلك ؛ كما أن تحقّق الكمال الذاتي يكون بلا شرط شيء أصلا ،

**Reviewed English**  Its realization requires something—rather, several conditions: determination, an other and relative otherness, a manifesting locus, conjunction and intelligible or formal composition, and the like. Essential perfection, by contrast, is realized without the condition of anything.

*Scholarly note:* Multiple conditions differentiate the name-related order from absolute essential perfection.

### FCP-30-05

**Arabic**  فحقيقة الكمال الأسمائي ظهور الذات لنفسها من حيث كلّيتها وجمعيتها شؤونها واعتباراتها ومظاهرها مفصّلا أو مجملا بعد التفصيل من كونها أغيارا ،

**Reviewed English**  Name-related perfection is the Essence's manifestation to itself in the totality of its affairs, considerations and loci, articulated in detail or grasped comprehensively after differentiation, in their aspect as mutually 'other'.

*Scholarly note:* «مفصّلا أو مجملا بعد التفصيل» means detail or comprehensiveness **after** differentiation.

### FCP-30-06

**Arabic**  لكن بشرط أن يكون ذلك الشهود من حيث مظهر شأن كلّي جامع جميع أفرادها بالفعل وهو الإنسان الكامل الحقيقي ووجدانها نفسها من حيث ذلك المظهر الكامل ،

**Reviewed English**  The witnessing must, however, be through the manifestation of a universal affair that comprehends all its individuals in actuality: the true Perfect Human. Through this perfect locus the Essence finds itself.

*Scholarly note:* Original text names the *insān kāmil ḥaqīqī* as the fully encompassing locus; not the unconditioned Essence itself.

### FCP-30-07

**Arabic**  وظهورها أيضا لنفسها من حيث كل فرد متميّز من أفراد مظاهر تلك الشؤون وظهور كل فرد ووجدانه أيضا لنفسه ولمثله من كونهما مسمّى بالأغيار مقيّدا بالمراتب ،

**Reviewed English**  Likewise it appears to itself through each distinct individual manifesting those affairs, and each individual appears to and finds itself and another like itself, as things called 'others' and delimited by their ranks.

*Scholarly note:* The experience of separate manifestations belongs to determinate ranks.

### FCP-30-08

**Arabic**  واعتبار العلم الذي حقيقته ظهور عين العين بحيث يكون أثر الظاهر حاصلا فيمن ظهر له من حيث الظهور فقط ،

**Reviewed English**  Consider knowledge: its reality is the very reality of a thing becoming manifest, so that the effect of what is manifest occurs in the one to whom it is manifest, in respect of manifestation alone.

*Scholarly note:* «ظهور عين العين» leaves the reflexive repetition; translation as 'the very reality' is provisional but preserves the epistemic point.

### FCP-30-09

**Arabic**  واعتبار الوجود الذي حقيقته ما به وجدان العين نفسه في نفسه أو في غيره ، أو غيره في غيره من محل ومرتبة ونحوهما ،

**Reviewed English**  Consider existence (*wujūd*): its reality is that by which an individual reality finds itself in itself or in another, or finds another in another, in a locus, rank, or the like.

*Scholarly note:* «الوجود ... الوجدان» must preserve finding/presence and not translate every instance indiscriminately as abstract Being.

### FCP-30-10

**Arabic**  واعتبار النور الذي هو الكاشف للمستور

**Reviewed English**  Light is what discloses something concealed.

*Scholarly note:* Short distinct technical predicate.

### FCP-30-11

**Arabic**  واعتبار الشهود الذي هو الحضور مع المشهود

**Reviewed English**  Witnessing is presence with what is witnessed.

*Scholarly note:* «الحضور مع المشهود» indicates presence with the witnessed.

### FCP-30-12

**Arabic**  كان من حكم ذلك التجلّي الأول وما يتضمّنه من الاعتبارات من كونه واحدا بلا مغايرة بينه وبينها وبين كل منها من حيث الكمال الذاتي ولا تميّز حقيقي إلّا تميّزا خفيّا نسبيّا عند ظهور هذا الكمال باسم الحيّ لا غير .

**Reviewed English**  These considerations arise by the ruling of the first self-disclosure and its included considerations, inasmuch as it is one without real difference among them under the aspect of essential perfection. A hidden, relational distinction emerges only when this perfection appears by the name the Living.

*Scholarly note:* Do not infer multiple first-rank essences from distinct names arising later.

### FCP-30-13

**Arabic**  وأمّا من حيث الكمال الأسمائي المتعلق بسائر الأسماء الكلّي الأصلي والفرعي الجزئي ، فمن شرطه التميّز والمظهر والمرتبة والغيرية بالنسبة أو بالحقيقة ،

**Reviewed English**  But name-related perfection, concerning the remaining universal, principal and subordinate particular names, requires distinctness, a locus of manifestation, a rank, and relative or real otherness.

*Scholarly note:* Relational and real otherness must remain distinct.

### FCP-30-14

**Arabic**  فالمغايرة بين جميع ما ذكرنا ثابتة لازمة ، وذلك بحكم المحل وحسب اقتضائه لا بحسب أعيان هذه الاعتبارات ،

**Reviewed English**  Differentiation is therefore real at the relevant level because of the locus and its exigency, not because the considerations have separate essential realities.

*Scholarly note:* «بحكم المحل» rank-relative, not absolute division of God.

### FCP-30-15

**Arabic**  فافهم ذلك فإن المحل له أثر لازم وحكم جازم فيما قام به وثبت فيه سواء كان المحل صوريّا كالظروف ، أو معنويّا كالمراتب ،

**Reviewed English**  Understand: a locus exerts a necessary effect and ruling upon what subsists within it, whether a formal locus such as a vessel or a meaning-based locus such as a rank.

*Scholarly note:* Formal and intelligible loci are compared without identifying the two ontologically.

### FCP-30-16

**Arabic**  فإن الظرف يحكم في المظروف بأن يتبعه في اللّون أو الهيئة مدوّرا أو مربّعا أو غير

**Reviewed English**  A vessel makes what it contains follow its colour or shape, round, square or otherwise—

*Scholarly note:* Joined to p31's analogy of water and its vessel.

## B2007 printed p. 31
[Digitized Arabic witness](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/31?lang=ar) · 21 continuous reviewed units

### FCP-31-01

**Arabic**  ذلك تذكر قولهم لون الماءلون إنائه ،

**Reviewed English**  —as illustrated by the saying that water takes on the colour of its vessel.

*Scholarly note:* The digital has a close-spaced «الماءلون»; preserve sense and mark potential tokenization issue, not an independent textual variant.

### FCP-31-02

**Arabic**  وكذا المراتب لها الحكم في أهلها بالاستتباع ،

**Reviewed English**  Likewise, ranks impose their own rulings upon those that occupy them.

*Scholarly note:* Ranks are determinants of appearance, not autonomous divine substances.

### FCP-31-03

**Arabic**  فإن مرتبة الحسّ تحكم بأنه لا يظهر فيها شيء إلّا محسوسا ، ومرتبة الروح والمثال كذلك ،

**Reviewed English**  At the rank of sense, nothing appears except sensibly, and similarly spirit and imaginal form appear according to their proper modes.

*Scholarly note:* Spirit, imaginal and sensory each have characteristic modes.

### FCP-31-04

**Arabic**  ومراتب الكون لا يظهر فيها إلّا الخلق ، ومراتب الحق لا يظهر فيها إلّا الحق ،

**Reviewed English**  In cosmic ranks only the created appears; in the ranks of the Real only the Real appears.

*Scholarly note:* Phrase about 'the ranks of the Real' belongs to author's system of manifestation.

### FCP-31-05

**Arabic**  تدبّر تعرف سرّا لا يعرف ولا يشهد ولا يرى الحقّ إلا هو أو به ،

**Reviewed English**  Reflect, and learn a secret: none knows, witnesses or sees the Real except the Real Himself or through Him.

*Scholarly note:* «لا يعرف ولا يشهد ولا يرى الحقّ إلا هو أو به» qualified theological doctrine.

### FCP-31-06

**Arabic**  فاستحضر تفز بأسرار جمّة .

**Reviewed English**  Keep this in view and you will attain many mysteries.

*Scholarly note:* Authorial imperative, not an additional content claim.

### FCP-31-07

**Arabic**  منها : أن العلم بحسب المرتبة الأولى والتعيّن الأول هو ظهور عين الذات لنفسه باندراج اعتبارات الواحدية فيها مع تحقّقها ،

**Reviewed English**  One implication: at the first rank and first determination, knowledge is the reality of the Essence appearing to itself, with the considerations of oneness included and established within it.

*Scholarly note:* The grammatically first-rank 'one object' analogy is explicitly developed below.

### FCP-31-08

**Arabic**  فكان متعلّقا بمعلوم واحد ،

**Reviewed English**  Knowledge then relates to one known thing.

*Scholarly note:* One known thing is the Essence's own reality within this considered rank.

### FCP-31-09

**Arabic**  فكان لفظه حينئذ متعدّيا بحسب هذه المرتبة الأولى ، بمفعول واحد فإنه علم فيها ذاته فقط .

**Reviewed English**  Accordingly, the word denoting 'to know' governs a single object at that rank, for the Essence knows only itself there.

*Scholarly note:* «متعدّيا ... بمفعول واحد» is a grammatical analogy, not a general claim about all Arabic verbs for knowing.

### FCP-31-10

**Arabic**  وأما بحسب المرتبة الثانية ، فهو ظهور الذات لنفسها بشؤونها من حيث مظاهر تلك الشؤون المسمّاة صفات وحقائق فيها ،

**Reviewed English**  At the second rank knowledge is the Essence appearing to itself together with its affairs, considered through their loci of manifestation, called attributes and realities at this rank.

*Scholarly note:* Second-rank differentiation is explicitly through loci of affairs.

### FCP-31-11

**Arabic**  فكان لفظ العلم بهذا الحكم متعدّيا بالمفعولين ، فإنه يظهر لنفسه ذا حياة ، وذا علم ، وذا إرادة ، وذا قدرة ، وذا كلام ، وذا جود ، وذا عدل ؛

**Reviewed English**  Here the word denoting knowledge governs two objects: it appears to itself as possessed of life, knowledge, will, power, speech, generosity and justice.

*Scholarly note:* «ذا حياة، وذا علم...» is a predicate of manifested qualifications, not substantial addition to Essence.

### FCP-31-12

**Arabic**  فكان للعلم بحسب انتهاء المرتبة الثانية وحكم معلوماته فيها كثرة حقيقية ووحدة نسبية مجموعية .

**Reviewed English**  At the completion of the second rank, knowledge, in virtue of the things known there, has real plurality and a collective, relative unity.

*Scholarly note:* Real pluralities of the known things are level-dependent.

### FCP-31-13

**Arabic**  ومنها : أن الوجود من حيث المرتبة الأولى ما به وجدان الذات نفسها في نفسها باندراج اعتبارات الواحدية فيها وجدان مجمل مندرج فيه تفصيله محكوم عليه بنفي الكثرة والمغايرة والغيرية والتميّز .

**Reviewed English**  Another implication: at the first rank, existence is that through which the Essence finds itself in itself while the considerations of oneness are included in a comprehensive finding with details contained within it, without plurality, difference, otherness or distinction.

*Scholarly note:* *Wujūd* and *wijdān* are closely paired: existence as that by which a reality finds itself.

### FCP-31-14

**Arabic**  وأما من حيث المرتبة الثانية ، فهو على نوعين ،

**Reviewed English**  At the second rank, however, existence is considered under two kinds.

*Scholarly note:* Keep two modalities of the second rank distinct.

### FCP-31-15

**Arabic**  أحدهما : من حيث ما هو منها مجلى الظهور للحق ،

**Reviewed English**  The first is the second rank as a locus in which the Real is manifested.

*Scholarly note:* The Real's outward self-disclosure, contrasted with the cosmic outward order on p32.

### FCP-31-16

**Arabic**  وهو - أعني الوجود - من هذه الحيثيّة ما به وجدان الذات عينها من حيث ظهوره وظهور صورته التي هي مسمّاه بظاهر اسم الرحمن ، وظهور صور تعيّناته المسمّاة أسماء إلهيّة مع وحدة عينه وصحة إضافة الكثرة النسبية إليه ،

**Reviewed English**  From this standpoint, existence is that through which the Essence finds its own reality as manifested, along with its outward form—named the outer aspect of the name the All-Merciful—and the forms of its determinations, called divine names. Its underlying reality is one, even though relational multiplicity may be attributed to it.
