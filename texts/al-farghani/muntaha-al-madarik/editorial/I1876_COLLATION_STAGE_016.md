**9 November 2026.** The previous Stage XV photo register documented 14 bounded photographic I1876 phrase comparisons; its low-resolution pending p52 note has now been resolved by a **newly retrieved original-resolution 2200 × 3370 pixel** scan 36 (= historical printed p32), permanently archived in the GNAMMA repository.

## The verified locus

| Witness/source | Printed location | Reading | Source grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I1876** (photographed original print) | I1876 printed p32, IIIF canvas/scan 36 | **تفرّع ( اسم الحكم ) منه** | Verified visual reading from full-resolution original **printing** |
| **B2007 digitized** (online transcription of 2007 edition) | Beirut printed p52 | **تفرّع ( سم الحكيم ) منه** | Verified *digital text*, not the physically inspected 2007 printed page |

**Difference:** historical print **الحكم** (*al-Ḥakam*, “the Judge”) versus modern digitized **الحكيم** (*al-Ḥakīm*, “the Wise”); the latter also loses initial alif in `اسم`. This is a **substantive lexical/theological difference**, not only a broken ligature. The 1876 page is visibly unambiguous at 2200 pixels, superseding the old 760px unresolved note `I1876-OBS15-PENDING`.

**Provisional edited text:** `تفرّع ( اسم الحكم ) منه`. **Corrected English:** “From it branches the name **the Judge** (*al-Ḥakam*).” The original digital lemma is retained in the apparatus as found. **Do not claim** the true B2007 printed leaf contains the same anomaly until a photo of the physical 2007 page is independently inspected.

## Context and interpretation

The earlier digitized text explains a name arising from **the Knowing** (*al-ʿAlīm*) under an aspect involving the effective judgment or determination of the manifestation of what is known. The I1876 **al-Ḥakam** therefore has plausible immediate semantic support in `أحكام`; yet this contextual fit does not independently prove an archetype. At B2007 p59 Farghānī also presents **al-Ḥakam** among branches of **al-Malik** under the aspect of adjudicating between litigants. These distinct placements are not automatically inconsistent: the names can be connected to several roots under different `iʿtibārāt` (considerations). Do **not** automatically change p59 or infer a universal, single-parent hierarchy.

The prior working English at p52 erroneously followed the modern digitized **Wise** (*al-Ḥakīm*) for this clause; it is now corrected to **Judge** (*al-Ḥakam*) with an explicit source note. The p52 bilingual machine-readable locus CR006-015 now preserves the original B2007-digital `ar`, plus `edited_ar` and a scan/photo link.
