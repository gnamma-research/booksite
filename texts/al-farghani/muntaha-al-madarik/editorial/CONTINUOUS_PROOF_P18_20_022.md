# Farghānī · continuous bilingual clause proof I: B2007 pp.18–20

**Source:** modern online edited Beirut B2007 digitized authorial reading, archived in `data/intro-arabic-b2007-digital.json`. This is a literal **continuous clause-coverage proofreading of three printed page positions**, not independent full printed-page collation or manuscript-based editing. Full Arabic source remains unchanged; each unit is an exact contiguous substring of the archived Arabic `reading_ar` and the units concatenate back to it byte-for-byte. Modern editor footnotes are excluded.

**Prior work preserved:** 128-page English working translation, 560 selected bilingual excerpts, Stage XXI source-parity audit, and historic I1876 photo evidence. This new aligned proof is distinct from the earlier selected-clause audits. Text within a page may begin/end mid-sentence. Proposed fluent English below is a direct editorial review, not an independent historical reading.

## B2007 printed p.18
[Digital Arabic source](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/18?lang=ar) · 9 contiguous reviewed clauses · no unexamined intervening authorial text

### FCP-18-01

**Arabic**  الأصل الأول في ذكر رتب الذات وتعيّن الأسماء منها والصفات وذكر كيفية انتشاء الفروع من تلك الأسماء والصفات من أصولها مع تعرّض لشرح الأسماء التسعة والتسعين ، وذكر انتشاء باقي الأسماء منها دقيقها وجليلها

**Reviewed English**  First foundation. On the ranks of the Essence, the determination of the names and attributes from it, how branches of the names and attributes arise from their roots, the ninety-nine names, and the arising of the other names from them, whether subtle or exalted.

*Critical note:* Authorial first-foundation heading, not an editorial title; two other foundations and the closing preface remain outside this first proof segment.

### FCP-18-02

**Arabic**  بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمنِ الرَّحِيمِ

**Reviewed English**  In the name of God, the All-Merciful, the Ever-Merciful.

*Critical note:* Basmala is in the digital source, not a modern preface.

### FCP-18-03

**Arabic**  نقل عن رسول اللّه صلّى اللّه عليه وسلّم أنه قال : « قال اللّه عزّ وجلّ : كنت كنزا مخفيّا فأحببت أن أعرف ، فخلقت الخلق لأعرف » « 1 » .

**Reviewed English**  It is transmitted from the Messenger of God—may God bless him and grant him peace—that he reported God, mighty and exalted, as saying: 'I was a hidden treasure and loved to be known; so I created creatures that I might be known.'

*Critical note:* Farghānī attributes the hidden-treasure saying to the Prophet. This preserves his own framing but is not an authentication. Historical hadith criticism does not establish an isnād for the saying.

### FCP-18-04

**Arabic**  اعلم أيّدك اللّه بالفهم الصحيح أنه قد كنى في هذا الحديث الإلهي عن كنه الغيب وإطلاق الذات الأقدس وباطن الهوية الأزلية بالكنز المخفي ،

**Reviewed English**  Know—may God assist you with sound understanding—that in this divine saying 'hidden treasure' stands figuratively for the innermost reality of the Unseen, the non-delimitation of the most holy Essence, and the inward aspect of pre-eternal divine Identity.

*Critical note:* كنه, إطلاق and باطن are respectively inward reality, non-delimitation and inward aspect; avoid treating all three as undifferentiated 'hiddenness'.

### FCP-18-05

**Arabic**  وذلك لملابسة أن الكنز عبارة عن عين مغيب مكنون وسرّ مخزون مشتمل على جواهر عظيمة الجدوى مرغوب فيها ما بين أنفس تصان ويضنّ ويستأثر بها وبين نفيس ، ربما يقع فيه التسامح والتبدّل خصوصا أو عموما ،

**Reviewed English**  The image is used because a treasure is a concealed entity and safeguarded secret containing jewels of great worth. Some are so choice that their possessor guards and reserves them; others are precious but may be permitted to pass into exchange, whether privately or publicly.

*Critical note:* Preserve the scale of الأنفس versus النفيس; the 'exchange' motif is an analogy, not trade in divine realities.

### FCP-18-06

**Arabic**  ومهما أريد إظهاره يعرض أولا عرضا مجملا بحيث يبين الكنز ويظهر لواجده في محله ، ثم يعرض ثانيا عرضا يميّز فيه بين الأنفس والنفيس ، وينقل منه عن محله لحكمة يراها الواجد من إظهار كماله الذي يتضمّن غناءه وإظهار التجمّل والجود ونحو ذلك ،

**Reviewed English**  When the treasure is to be disclosed, it is displayed first collectively, so that it appears as a treasure to the finder in its place; next it is presented in a differentiated way, distinguishing the choicest from the precious, and some is moved from its place according to the wisdom of its finder, thereby displaying a perfection that includes wealth, beauty, and generosity.

*Critical note:* First collective exposure, then differentiated disclosure; do not replace succession of rank with temporal change in the Divine.

### FCP-18-07

**Arabic**  وكذلك كنه الجناب الأقدس تعالى وتقدّس وله المثل الأعلى في السماوات والأرض وهو العزيز الحكيم ، بلا نسبة تشبيه وتشكيل وتمثيل إليه تعالى عن ذلك علوّا كبيرا ،

**Reviewed English**  Likewise the innermost reality of the most holy divine Presence—sublime and sacred, and God has the supreme similitude in the heavens and earth; He is the Mighty, the Wise—is to be spoken of without likening Him to creatures, attributing a form to Him, or making any representation of Him.

*Critical note:* Literal language of المثل الأعلى and three explicit disavowals of comparison. These are authorial qualifications, not secondary editorial footnotes.

### FCP-18-08

**Arabic**  فإنه الغيب المكنون والسرّ المصون الذي هو أبطن كل باطن ، وبطون المشتمل على جواهر أسماء الذات التي هي أنفس نفائس الأسماء التي منها ما يستأثر في مكنون الغيب عنده ،

**Reviewed English**  For it is the guarded Unseen and preserved secret, more inward than every inwardness, and an inwardness comprising the jewels of the names of the Essence, the choicest of precious names.

*Critical note:* «وبطون المشتمل» is awkward in both digitized B2007 and photographed I1876 printed p5 (I1876-L01). 'An inwardness comprising' is a provisional construal, not a textual emendation.

### FCP-18-09

**Arabic**  فلا يعلمها إلّا هو ومن ارتفعت بينونته ورفعت كينونته ممن هو أكمل جميع

**Reviewed English**  Of these He reserves some in the hidden mystery with Him. None knows them save He, and one whose otherness and separate self-subsistence have been lifted away, among the most perfect of all ...

*Critical note:* Sentence continues on printed Beirut p19 with «الكمّل في عرض التعيّن والتجلّي الأول». The translation must not invent a completed syntactic ending before the page break.

## B2007 printed p.19
[Digital Arabic source](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/19?lang=ar) · 14 contiguous reviewed clauses · no unexamined intervening authorial text

### FCP-19-01

**Arabic**  الكمّل في عرض التعيّن والتجلّي الأول ، لتحقيق الكمال الذاتي الأشرف المعني بقوله : « فأحببت أن أعرف » « 1 » ،

**Reviewed English**  ... the perfected, within the breadth or scope of the first determination and first self-disclosure, for the realization of the noblest essential perfection signified by the words 'I loved to be known.'

*Critical note:* «في عرض» is retained as 'within the breadth/scope' rather than the earlier potentially misleading claim that perfected persons 'participate in the display'; syntax follows p18 without a new sentence.

### FCP-19-02

**Arabic**  وهو المتضمّن أيضا نفائس درر أسماء الصفات التي يسمح بتعريفها لكل من يصلح لتشريفها من كل فرد متخلّق ، أو كامل متحقّق ،

**Reviewed English**  It [the treasure] also contains the precious pearls of the names of the attributes. He allows them to be made known to each person suited to be honoured by that disclosure, whether one who has acquired their qualities or one fully realized in them.

*Critical note:* Preserve attribution of qualifying spiritual capacities, not a universal disclosure of all attributive names.

### FCP-19-03

**Arabic**  والمحتوي أيضا على لآلىء أسماء الأفعال العامّ نفعها وأثرها ، والمستفيض حكمها وخبرها في جميع المراتب الكونيّة التي هي محال تمام الكمال الأسمائي والمطلب الغائبّ المعني بقوله : « فخلقت الخلق لأعرف » « 1 » .

**Reviewed English**  It further includes the pearls of the names of acts, whose benefits and effects are general and whose judgments and reports flow into every cosmic rank. Those ranks are the loci in which the full perfection of the names and the ultimate object expressed by 'I created creatures that I might be known' are realized.

*Critical note:* Older I1876 PRINT p6 reads «المطلب الغائي» (ultimate/purposive goal), while modern digitized B2007 has «المطلب الغائبّ». The translation follows the photographed older PRINT provisionally; both readings preserved in apparatus, no manuscript claim.

### FCP-19-04

**Arabic**  فقوله : « كنت » يخبر عن تعيّن مسبوق بالإطلاق ،

**Reviewed English**  The words 'I was' tell of determination preceded by non-delimitation.

*Critical note:* «كنت» signals self-reference in a qualified determination, not a temporal change in the Essence.

### FCP-19-05

**Arabic**  فإن مفهوم تاء المتكلّم عن نفسه يؤذن بتعيّنه في نفسه وظهوره حالة تكلّمه تعيّنا وظهورا .

**Reviewed English**  For the first-person marker in the speaker's reference to himself implies determination within himself and appearance at the moment of self-reference.

*Critical note:* «حالة تكلّمه» is within the analogy of linguistic self-reference and must not assert a temporal divine utterance.

### FCP-19-06

**Arabic**  أما قوله : « كنزا مخفيّا » ينبئ عن غيبه وإطلاقه بحيث لا يدخل تحت حكم معيّن يحكم عليه من حيث هذا الغيب والإطلاق ، ولا تحت إحاطة به علما من حيث تعيّناته أصلا ،

**Reviewed English**  By contrast, 'a hidden treasure' indicates His unseen and non-delimited character: in this respect no specified judgment can be imposed upon Him, nor does knowledge derived from His determinations encompass Him.

*Critical note:* The names and modes of knowledge are not the same consideration as the non-delimited Essence.

### FCP-19-07

**Arabic**  فإنّ مطلق الخفاء يحكم بذلك كلّه ،

**Reviewed English**  For absolute hiddenness entails all of this.

*Critical note:* No metaphysical 'nothingness' is predicated of the Essence.

### FCP-19-08

**Arabic**  وحيث كان الخفاء وقع خبرا « لكنت » عرف سبق الإطلاق والغيب على ذلك التعيّن ، ولكن سبقا رتبيّا لا زمانيّا ، فإنه صحّ أنه ليس عند اللّه صباح ولا مساء .

**Reviewed English**  Because hiddenness is predicated of 'I was', non-delimitation and the Unseen are understood to precede that determination; but the priority is one of rank, not of time. For it is true that with God there is neither morning nor evening.

*Critical note:* «سبقا رتبيّا لا زمانيّا» is an explicit authorial constraint; preserve it exactly.

### FCP-19-09

**Arabic**  وقوله : « فأحببت » يخبر عن ميل أصلي هو وصلة بين الخفاء والظهور المذكور ، وما ثمّ إلّا التعيّن الذي هو عين الوحدة ، فكان عينه .

**Reviewed English**  The words 'and so I loved' announce an original inclination which joins the hiddenness and manifestation mentioned; since there is there nothing beyond the determination which is itself unity, this inclination is identical with it.

*Critical note:* «الميل الأصلي» is an inclination connecting hiddenness and disclosure; do not imply physical movement or acquired want.

### FCP-19-10

**Arabic**  وقوله : « أن أعرف » يشير إلى ظهور ذلك الخافي ،

**Reviewed English**  The phrase 'that I might be known' points to the disclosure of what was hidden.

*Critical note:* Do not translate this as created need for recognition.

### FCP-19-11

**Arabic**  وقوله : « فخلقت الخلق لأعرف » يعرف أن تقدير المقدّرات علما ووجودا يعقب ذلك الميل ويترتّب على متعلّقه الذي هو الظهور الحاصل بذلك التعيّن ليتحقّق كمال ذلك الظهور مفصّلا متميّزا ويشمل حكمه وأثره المقدّر والمقدّر تعقّبا ذاتيّا لا زمانيّا ، بل عمليّا ووجوديّا أيضا بالنسبة إلى المقدّر العالم الأزلي وعلمه الذاتي والصفاتي الأبدي ، فإن علمه عين وجوده ، فافهم تقرّ بالكبريت الأحمر والكنز الأكبر إن شاء اللّه تعالى .

**Reviewed English**  The phrase 'so I created creatures that I might be known' indicates that determinations, in knowledge and existence, follow that inclination and are ordered to its object, namely disclosure through that determination. This allows the perfection of manifestation to be realized distinctly and in detail and to extend its effects to determiner and determined, in a succession of essence or rank, not time—indeed in knowledge and existence with respect to the pre-eternal Knower and His essential and attributive knowledge, for His knowledge is His very existence. Understand, and God willing you will gain the red sulphur and the greatest treasure.

*Critical note:* Two observed textual controls: historical 1876 PRINT reads «علميّا ووجوديّا» for modern digital «عمليّا ووجوديّا» and supports 'in knowledge and existence'; the repeated unvowelled «المقدّر والمقدّر» admits active/passive grammatical construal (determiner/determined), which needs better source control. Neither is claimed as a medieval MS variant.

### FCP-19-12

**Arabic**  ثم اعلم أن كنه الذات الأقدس وغيب الهوية والإطلاق والأزلية المندرج فيه حكم الأبدية لا يشهد ولا يفهم ولا يعلم ولا يدرك من حيث التعيّن أصلا ، ولا يدخل تحت حكم متعيّن البتّة .

**Reviewed English**  Know further that the innermost reality of the most holy Essence, the Unseen of Identity, non-delimitation and pre-eternity—which contains within it the import of everlastingness—cannot be witnessed, conceived, known or apprehended with respect to determination; nor can any determined judgment apply to it whatever.

*Critical note:* «الإطلاق» means the lack of delimitation; this does not nullify possibility of qualified knowledge described next.

### FCP-19-13

**Arabic**  نعم اللّهمّ إلا حكما سلبيّا بأنها لا تعلم ،

**Reviewed English**  One can except only the negative judgment that it cannot be known.

*Critical note:* Only a negative judgment is asserted, not a positive comprehending cognition of Essence.

### FCP-19-14

**Arabic**  وإنما توجه وتأتى هذا الحكم السلبي أيضا بأحد وجهين ؛ أحدهما : إخبار إلهي عن ذلك

**Reviewed English**  Even that negative judgment is reached in one of two ways. The first is divine notification of that fact ...

*Critical note:* The first way is elaborated at the start of p20. Maintain page-break continuity, no editorial completion.

## B2007 printed p.20
[Digital Arabic source](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9606/20?lang=ar) · 8 contiguous reviewed clauses · no unexamined intervening authorial text

### FCP-20-01

**Arabic**  بقوله تعالى : وَلا يُحِيطُونَ بِهِ عِلْماً [ طه : الآية 110 ] ، وبقوله : « كنت كنزا مخفيّا » ونحو ذلك .

**Reviewed English**  ... in His saying: 'They do not encompass Him in knowledge' (Qurʾān 20:110), and in the words 'I was a hidden treasure', and similar expressions.

*Critical note:* The Qurʾānic reference is Q 20:110; the 'hidden treasure' saying's poor transmission must not be conflated with Qurʾānic authority.

### FCP-20-02

**Arabic**  والوجه الثاني : انقطاع أكمل الإدراكات والعلوم وأوسع الشهودات والفهوم إليه واعترافه بالعجز والقصور والحيرة فيه ، وبأن ما وراء غاية مدركه ونهاية مشهده بحار يحار في تيّارها فهم كل غائص واقف ، ويتيه في تيه كنهها وغور أسرارها علم كل خائض عارف ، هي المقصد الأعلى والمطلب الأولى ؛

**Reviewed English**  The second way is that the utmost reaches of apprehension, knowledge, witnessing and understanding fall short of Him and confess inability, limitation and bewilderment. Beyond the furthest reach of what they apprehend and witness lie seas whose currents bewilder even the understanding of a deep diver and trackless wastes whose depths and hidden secrets bewilder every knowledgeable seeker. These are the highest goal and the most worthy object of seeking.

*Critical note:* Rank of intellect/witnessing is a confessed limit; do not read author as simply denying all qualified experiential knowledge.

### FCP-20-03

**Arabic**  كما نصّ صلّى اللّه تعالى عليه وسلم بقوله في مناجاته : « لا أحصى ثناء عليك » « 1 » ،

**Reviewed English**  As the Prophet—may God bless him and grant him peace—said in intimate supplication: 'I cannot enumerate praise of You.'

*Critical note:* «لا أحصى ثناء عليك» is a transmitted prophetic supplication. Retain authorial quotation and separate authentication from translation.

### FCP-20-04

**Arabic**  لا أبلغ كل ما فيك ،

**Reviewed English**  That is: 'I cannot attain all that belongs to You.'

*Critical note:* «لا أبلغ كل ما فيك» is Farghānī's interpretative paraphrase, NOT a clause in the prophetic supplication.

### FCP-20-05

**Arabic**  وبقوله أيضا في أثناء دعائه : « أو استأثرت به في مكنون الغيب عندك » « 2 » ،

**Reviewed English**  And in another passage of his supplication: 'Or [a name] that You have reserved for Yourself in the concealed Unseen with You.'

*Critical note:* «أو استأثرت به في مكنون الغيب عندك» appears in the supplication concerning divine names; '[a name]' is a contextual English addition, not present as a new separate Arabic noun in this clause.

### FCP-20-06

**Arabic**  وبقوله : « ربّ زدني فيك تحيّرا » « 3 » ، وأمثال ذلك .

**Reviewed English**  And in the words 'My Lord, increase me in bewilderment concerning You', and similar sayings.

*Critical note:* «رب زدني فيك تحيّرا» is found in Farghānī's text but has been judged not an established prophetic hadith by hadith critics, e.g. Ibn Taymiyya in his discussion of the saying. The edition must distinguish authorial citation from authentication.

### FCP-20-07

**Arabic**  ومهما علم أو شوهد أو أدرك شيء منها عند تجلّيها الظاهري أو الباطني أو الجمعي في السير الحبي وقرب النوافل ، وتقدم السلوك على الجذبة وسبق الفناء « 4 » على البقاء « 5 »

**Reviewed English**  Whenever something of these hidden realities becomes known, witnessed or apprehended through their outward, inward, or comprehensive self-disclosure in the journey of love and proximity through supererogatory works, where discipline precedes attraction and annihilation precedes subsistence ...

*Critical note:* «السير الحبي وقرب النوافل» and 'discipline before attraction; annihilation before subsistence' mark one path in contrast to the following p21 beloved-path.

### FCP-20-08

**Arabic**  حيث يكون ويظهر لدى الفتح أن الحقّ المتجلّي آلة لإدراك العبد

**Reviewed English**  ... it becomes apparent at the opening that the self-disclosing Real is the means by which the servant to whom He discloses Himself apprehends ...

*Critical note:* Sentence continues at p21 «المتجلّي له من باب كنت سمعه وبصره». Do not read the p20 fragment as a complete claim independent of p21.
