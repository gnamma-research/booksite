## Important readings

- B2007 p38 reads **القابل**; p37 discusses **القول** as a principal sequence-member, motivating but **not proving** proposed **القائل**. Historical print + manuscript comparison is compulsory before adoption.
- B2007 p40: **لم تبرح منها** and **لم يظهر بالوجود العيني إلا لوازمها وأحكامها وعوارضها** distinguish abiding fixed entities within the second rank from manifestations of their concomitants and effects.
- B2007 p41: **كان في عماء ما فوقه هواء وما تحته هواء** admits significant manuscript/transmission/vocalization complications in the **hadith tradition**. This is not yet a Farghānī-textual variant; preserve source wording and avoid unargued 'no air' or 'air' as definitive.
- B2007 p43: **five universal ranks**, two divine and three cosmic, **plus a sixth comprehensive rank**.
- B2007 p44: **أسماء الأسماء** means the mutable language-dependent words are names *of* the realities of the names, not those realities themselves.
- B2007 pp47–48: **taʿalluq / takhalluq / taḥaqquq** triad; p47 selected spans checked, p48 needs retrieval and verification.
- B2007 p50: **الحق المخلوق به كل شيء** requires 'the Real by which everything is created', not 'the created Real'.
