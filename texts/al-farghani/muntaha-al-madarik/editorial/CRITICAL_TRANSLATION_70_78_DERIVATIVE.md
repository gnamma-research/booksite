# Farghānī · Stage XI · Foundation II, B2007 printed pp70–78

**9 November 2026.** 54 newly checked selected Arabic–English clauses on nine printed-page labels, from a DERIVATIVE online transcription of modern edited Arabic. This work does not establish the text against historical or manuscript images. Earlier 283 modern digital checks plus Stage X 57 derivative-source checks remain preserved separately by provenance.

## Major findings

- **p70–72 — Original love and creative activity:** the seven names have role-differentiated, non-temporal participation in the creation narrative.
- **p72 — Critical internal corroboration:** the SAME authorial passage names `اسم القائل` (Speaker) and `عين القابل` (receiving reality) as distinct. This sharply strengthens the conjectural p38 `القائل`, but it is NOT a manuscript witness. Keep p38 `القابل` in diplomatic record until photographed copies are compared.
- **p73 — Continuous creation:** `رجوع الوجود كل آن إلى أصله` and `لكونه عرضا على الحقيقة وقيام بدل مثله مكانه في الخلق الجديد`. This is substantially different from reading the text as a straightforward Ṣadrian doctrine of one substantially moving subject. The referent of `عرضا` and what exactly is substituted require manuscript-aware contextual exegesis.
- **p73 — Limits of analogy:** `هذه تمثيل مطابق من بعض الوجوه لا كلّها` explicitly limits the water/ray/wall image; preserve this caveat.
- **p74–75 — Pen and Tablet:** the First Intellect, Supreme Pen and Muhammadan Spirit are distinct aspects of one reality under specified considerations. A corresponding analysis of the Tablet has six semantic directions; do not literalize the analogy.
- **p76 — Cosmological reversal:** `ففي إيجاد الأرواح كان القول ركنا ... وفي الأجسام بالعكس` makes speech principal in spirits and power principal in bodies; this rules out a simple context-free name dependency hierarchy.
- **p77–78 — Angels and wings:** Isrāfīl, Jibrīl, Mīkāʾīl and ʿAzrāʾīl map onto life/knowledge/will/power. Wings are explained as knowledge, action, teaching and helping other beings under divine permission. Separate tafsīr from hadith grading.
