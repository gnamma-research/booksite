Mandatory procedure for every stage

  1. Recover: Read the prior full archive, stage manifest, and source. Retain everything already translated.
  2. Translate: Work from the exact next source passage. Record every new page and skipped or corrupt clause.
  3. Consolidate: Preserve one cumulative master plus all original stage files, Arabic source excerpts, notes, and the variant history.
  4. Archive: Publish a new self-contained complete stage handoff and machine-readable manifest with source, SHA, counts, quality grades, and exact continuation pointer.
  5. Publish and verify: Update GNAMMA's text catalogue, reader, edition status and this handoff archive. Check both repository commits and Pages deployment separately.
