Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd

قواعد التوحيد

The ancestor's concise treatise by Afḍal al-Dīn Abū Ḥāmid Muḥammad al-Iṣfahānī (Turka), recovered from quotations in Ṣāʾin al-Dīn Ibn Turka's Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid. Only the ancestor's matn is translated here; the later sharḥ is used to interpret and identify it, not translated as though it were the matn.

Editorial status: provisional, not a completed critical edition. Six source-located matn passages are currently transcribed and independently translated. The source has not been collated against the three independently catalogued manuscripts or checked page-by-page against a physical facsimile. Chapter labels and clause IDs are editorial.
