# Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd — editorial status and source register

**Gnamma Research · Matn-first translation · 2 November 2026**

## Scope and state

- **Author of the matn:** Afḍal al-Dīn Abū Ḥāmid Muḥammad al-Iṣfahānī, known as Turka.
- **Commentator:** Ṣāʾin al-Dīn ʿAlī ibn Turka al-Iṣfahānī, *Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid* / *al-Tamhīd fī sharḥ Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*.
- **Completed in this first working release:** Six verified quotation loci from the original matn, printed Āshtiyānī pp. **48–60**, with six new English translations and interpretative and variant notes.
- **Incomplete:** This is the opening sequence **only** and is not the full matn. The gaps between quotation loci contain Ibn Turka's commentary. Remaining matn must be extracted throughout the entire sharḥ in source order.
- **Critical-edition status:** Source-located working text, **not an established critical edition**. No independent manuscript has yet been collated; digital transcription has not been proofed against an image of the printed exemplar. The later sharḥ has **not** been translated.
- **Policy:** Translate and collate the complete ancestor's matn first. Only after that reaches a credible critical-edition standard begin a **separate** sharḥ translation.

## Matn/commentary boundary rule

The commentary identifies the ancestor's wording by **قال** followed by a quoted passage, then switches to Ibn Turka's discussion by **أقول**. Only the material between those signals is included in the matn dataset; quotations from other works occurring *inside* Ibn Turka's commentary (such as the ancestor's *al-Iʿtimād*) are **not** imported as passages of *Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*. Medieval layout and modern punctuation must be verified against printed facsimiles; tags are the starting criterion, not an infallible substitute for witness collation.

This edition uses stable editorial loci QW-001 onward. Original text has no such numbers or newly supplied English headings. No compression or reordering of the matn may be made when the rest of the text is extracted.

## Checklist before claiming 'critical edition'

1. Recover **every** ancestor quotation across the full *Tamhīd*, with continuous order, exact published pagination, and unquoted source gaps flagged.
2. Read at least two independently printed Arabic editions (Āshtiyānī and Ḥasan-zādeh Āmulī) against their page images, including typesetting, punctuation, and footnotes.
3. Obtain facsimiles of at least two independent manuscript witnesses; verify actual matn boundaries and collate substantive variants.
4. Identify which variants belong to the ancestor, Ibn Turka, later glosses, or modern editors; keep conjectures distinct from attested readings.
5. Provide translation notes for every difficult lemma, especially the contrast between Avicennian, post-Avicennian and Akbarian usages.
6. Conduct a full clause-completeness audit, Arabic/English alignment, and locus citation audit.
7. **Only then** initiate a separate, full-length translation of Ibn Turka's *sharḥ* with independent clause identifiers linked back to QW loci.
