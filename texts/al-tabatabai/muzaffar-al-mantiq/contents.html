al-Manṭiq: six books and their reading coverage

The six main books of Muḥammad Riḍā al-Muẓaffar’s original logic text are mapped to their source. This is an expanding topic outline, not an exhaustive transcription of every heading. The bilingual reader currently contains 62 selected Arabic–English working passages: eleven from the introduction, eleven from Book I, seventeen from Book II, fourteen selected from Book III, and nine selected from Book IV, reaching the quantification of conditional propositions (source printed p.137). The Book II section M-K-14 is explicitly a normalized explanatory transcription, not a diplomatic copy. The remainder of Book IV, Books V–VI, and omissions in Books I–III remain absent.

The original Arabic source comprises 455 pages in the Dār al-Taʿāruf 1402 AH witness. Only directly checked page locators are linked; other topic headings have no guessed page numbers.
