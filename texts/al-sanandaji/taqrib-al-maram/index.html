    Taqrīb al-Marām

    تقريب المرام في شرح تهذيب الكلام

    ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Sanandajī

    A separate English translation of Sanandajī’s extensive explanation of the Tahdhīb. The first image-checked excerpts are linked to their short matn counterparts. Quoted lemmata, Sanandajī’s own discussion and the later marginal apparatus are distinguished in the notes.

    Editorial status: initial direct-page collation with six selected passages; a continuous translation of both volumes has not yet been completed. The source-graded text is publicly readable as work in progress; it is not presented as a finished or manuscript-critical edition. Selected explanatory notes distinguish Sanandajī's voice from al-Taftāzānī's matn and the additional marginal glosses.

    Citation policy: cite the indicated primary printed pages and independent witnesses, not the working OCR locator as if it were a verified printed page. Footnotes identify their own authorship where known. This reader will retain stable passage IDs as critical collation advances. Editorial status & source witnesses.
