    Taqrīb al-Marām

    تقريب المرام في شرح تهذيب الكلام

    ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Sanandajī

    A source-facing, independent reader for ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Sanandajī’s Taqrīb al-Marām and Muḥammad Wasīm’s Ḥāshiyat al-Muḥākamāt. The running commentary, separately quoted lemmata, independently attributed marginal notes, and marginalia of uncertain authorship have distinct controls. The companion Tahdhīb matn remains a separate project until independent critical review permits a three-layer alignment.

    Editorial status, 2 Nov 2026: Existing checked selections and provisional page-level English: I:8–90 (83 of 632 scanned pages represented, about 13.1% as a page-coverage proxy). Many are abbreviated interpretive drafts, not full translations; 0 pages critically complete. One Wasīm II:117 fragment is source-image located and independently attributed; its critical collation is not yet definitive. Source labels are not a claim of authorial verification.
