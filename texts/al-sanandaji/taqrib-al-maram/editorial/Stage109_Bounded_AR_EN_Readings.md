## I045.upper.02 — I:45, physical PDF 58 · If particular existence is its establishment, the accidental-predicate account fails
**Selected Arabic:**

> … لزم من القول بعروض الكون عليه اتحاد العارض والمعروض، إذ الاختلاف حينئذ إلا بالإطلاق والتقييد، وذلك لا يصحح العروض. وإن كان أمرا وراء الثبوت لم يصح جعل الكون بمعنى الثبوت عليه، وهو منه يعلم ضعف قولهم (كالنور على الأنوار) …

**Literal English:**

> … the claim that being inheres in it would entail the identity of the accident and the substrate. Their difference would then be merely that between unrestricted and restricted descriptions; this does not establish real inherence. If [particular existence] is something beyond establishment, it is likewise incorrect to apply “being” in the sense of establishment to it. This exposes the weakness of their analogy “as light [is predicated] of lights” …

**Boundary and open proof:** Selected continuous upper words AFTER I045.upper.01 «أن الوجود الخاص لكل شيء إن كان عبارة عن ثبوته»; ends before printed I046 upper light-of-sun/lamp comparison; omitted connective and some sentence words are left visibly untranscribed. Recheck compressed conditional after «إذ الاختلاف» and original parentheses; independently join I045/I046 without repeating I045.upper.01 or I046.upper.01; no full-page certification.

## I046.lower.01 — I:46, physical PDF 59 · Existence as relational meaning versus the principle by which a thing is realised
**Selected Arabic:**

> … فيمكن جعل الخلاف في الوجود لفظيا، فافهم. فالقائلون بزيادة الوجود أرادوا منه المعنى النسبي، والقائلون بكونه نفس الماهية أرادوا به مبدأ الآثار، لكن يلزم منه الاشتراك اللفظي بين هذين المعنيين، وإن كان كل منهما مشتركا معنويا في أفراده. […] والذي يظهر لنا ويجمع به جوانب الكلام هو أن مرادهم من الوجود المطلق الذي اختلف فيه أنه هل هو زائد على الماهية أو عينها وهل هو مشترك معنوي أو لفظي هو الوجود بمعنى ما به تحقق الشيء لا نفس التحقق أي الكون المصدري. […]

**Literal English:**

> … The dispute concerning existence could then be regarded as verbal. Understand this: those who say existence is additional intend the relational meaning, whereas those who say it is identical with quiddity intend the principle from which effects proceed. Yet this entails verbal homonymy between those two senses, even though each sense may be shared univocally among its own instances. […] What seems to us to bring together the sides of the discussion is that the unrestricted “existence” disputed as additional to quiddity or identical with it, and as shared in meaning or merely in name, is existence in the sense of *that by which a thing becomes actual*, not the occurrence of actuality itself, namely “being” as a verbal noun. […]

**Boundary and open proof:** Upper part of continuous lower printed I:46 before «قوله فيعارض النور المطلق»; the opening and intervening argument are omitted from this bounded selection. Independently collate omitted lower clauses, the elliptical expression «وتجمع الحمل» near opening, and the words of the reported reconciliation; preserve the individual writer’s anonymity and the printed lower/ruled distinction.
