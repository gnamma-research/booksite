## Specific philosophical argument added

The upper text on I:16 distinguishes nonfirm assent, firm assent false to reality, firm true but unstable assent, and firm true stable assent. The lower note on I:6 clarifies why calling this the kalām 'division' of a book containing logic and kalām is not a claim about a metaphysical division of science. The ruled I:17 note explains that the soul's movement between intelligible objects is a *simile*, not literal spatial motion.
