# I:2 — signed editorial stream between the two prefaces (source-facing, provisional)

Source: first numbered body page in Būlāq Volume I, printed I:2 (physical combined PDF page 15).

The printed page has three different text streams:
(1) upper Sanandajī invocation; (2) a separate ruled intervention signed by the printed editor; (3) lower Wasīm preface independently identified by the writer on I:3.

**Arabic transcript attempt of the previously omitted middle stream** (uncertain wording is bracketed, not silently supplied):

> لما كان هذا الكتاب مشتملاً على أهم المطالب الدينية وأعظم المقاصد اليقينية، بدأ رحمه الله بالبسملة اقتداءً بقوله عليه السلام: «كل أمر ذي بال لا يبدأ فيه ...» وإشارة إلى أن مثل هذا الأمر لا يتم إلا بالاستعانة به تعالى وحمده. وهذه البسملة للشارح، وبسملة المصنف هكذا: «بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم وبه نستعين في التتميم». وحذفناها استغناءً عنها. ⟦تلي ذلك جملة مطبوعة غير محرَّرة الضبط هنا، في بيان موضع البسملة في القسم الأول والإشارة إلى أهمية هذا القسم⟧. الفقير إليه فرج الله زكي الكردستاني المرواني الأزهري.

**English working translation:**

Because the book embraces important religious questions and the weightiest objects of certainty, the commentator begins with the basmala, following the Prophetic saying about every matter of consequence that does not begin with it. This also points to seeking God's aid and praising Him in an undertaking of this kind. That invocation belongs to the commentator. The author of the underlying text begins: “In the name of God, the All-Merciful, the Compassionate; in Him we seek help to complete [the work].” The editor omits that repetition. [The subsequent explanation of its placement is not securely deciphered.] The note is signed by Faraj Allāh Zakī al-Kurdistānī al-Marwānī al-Azharī.

**Editorial policy:** The added record must be represented as `ruled` / signed print-editor commentary, NOT Sanandajī's own sharḥ and NOT Wasīm's authorial preface.
