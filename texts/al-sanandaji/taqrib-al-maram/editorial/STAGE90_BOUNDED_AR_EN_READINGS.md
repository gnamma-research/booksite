## I014.lower.01 — Beginning of the long lower gloss on knowledge as an end
**Printed p. I:14, source combined PDF p. 27; lower register.**

**Arabic (visibly bounded):**

العلم غاية لنفسه بمعنى أنه مقصود في ذاته وليس آلة لعلم آخر […] الإتيان بفاء التفريع صريح في أن المراد بما ذكر هو الأمور التي غايتها الفوز لا الفوز نفسه.

**English:**

Knowledge is an end in itself: it is sought for its own sake, and is not an instrument for some other discipline. […] The use of the consequential particle *fāʾ* makes clear that the earlier expression concerns matters whose end is attaining success, not success itself.

**Editorial boundary:** First three lines of the lower stream; two source stretches with an intervening omission. The term الفوز requires surrounding context before fixing its referent.

**English:**

## I014.lower.03 — The particular act of knowing and knowledge in general
**Printed p. I:14, source combined PDF p. 27; lower register.**

**Arabic (visibly bounded):**

والثاني علم كل أحد بوجوده ضروري، أي حاصل بلا نظر، وهذا علم خاص مسبوق بمطلق العلم لتركيبه منه ومن الخصوصية. […]

**English:**

Second: each person’s knowledge that he exists is necessary—that is, attained without reflection. This is a particular act of knowledge, which presupposes knowledge in general, because it comprises that general notion and a particular qualification. […]

**Editorial boundary:** Lower final portion; the ensuing objection and response are not yet translated.

## I015.lower.02 — The objection from the presence of knowledge to conception of knowledge
**Printed p. I:15, source combined PDF p. 28; lower register.**

**Arabic (visibly bounded):**

فإذا كان حصول العلم ووجوده بديهيًّا كان تصوره بديهيًّا وهو المطلوب. […]

**English:**

If the occurrence and existence of knowledge are self-evident, then its conception would likewise be self-evident—and that is precisely what was to be established. […]

**Editorial boundary:** Middle lower portion; this is the conclusion of an objection, not an endorsement by the editor. The response remains untranslated.

## I022.ruled.01 — Short ruled explanation of intellectual self-evidence
**Printed p. I:22, source combined PDF p. 35; ruled register.**

**Arabic (visibly bounded):**

(قوله والجواب أن البديهة) أي بداهة العقل لا الحس حتى يرد أنه إثبات حكم الحس بنفسه. (قوله إلى غير ذلك) أي من القطعيات العادية، اه منه.

**English:**

On “The answer is that the self-evidence”: this means the intellect’s self-evidence, not that of sense-perception; otherwise the objection would arise that a judgment of the senses was being established by the senses themselves. On “and other such things”: that is, habitual or customary certainties. [End of the cited gloss.]

**Editorial boundary:** The complete short ruled printed passage has been provisionally transcribed in one connected unit; it still lacks a second independent proof.

## I023.lower.02 — Discursive inference and preliminary conception
**Printed p. I:23, source combined PDF p. 36; lower register.**

ويمكن أن يقال إن الأنظار المنتهية إلى هذه القضية هي الأنظار التصديقية، لأن التصورية إنما تنتهي إلى التصور البديهي. […]

**English:**

**Editorial boundary:** Lower middle: selected contrast of inferential assent with conception; rest of lower argument remains.

## I024.ruled.02 — The Muʿtazilite account of mediated generation
**Printed p. I:24, source combined PDF p. 37; ruled register.**

**Arabic (visibly bounded):**

(قال رحمه الله أو التوليد) هذا مذهب المعتزلة، وهو مبني على أصلهم من أن أفعالنا الاختيارية صادرة عنا إما مباشرة إن لم يكن صدورها عنا بتوسط فعل آخر منا، وإما توليدًا إن كان بتوسط فعل آخر. […]

**English:**

On his saying “or by generation”: this is the Muʿtazilite position, resting on their principle that our voluntary acts issue from us either directly, when they are not brought about through another act of ours, or by generation, when they arise through the mediation of another act. […]

**Editorial boundary:** Beginning of second note in the middle ruled stream; subsequent application to knowledge following inquiry is left open.

## I024.lower.01 — Customary conjunction and its possible interruption
**Printed p. I:24, source combined PDF p. 37; lower register.**

**Arabic (visibly bounded):**

(قوله بطريق العادة) أي تكرر ذلك دائمًا مع جواز أن يتخلف خرقًا للعادة. […]

**English:**

On “by way of custom”: that is, the occurrence recurs habitually, while it remains possible that it fail to occur through a breach of the customary order. […]

**Editorial boundary:** First opening phrase of lower paragraph, not the complete argument comparing custom with generation.
