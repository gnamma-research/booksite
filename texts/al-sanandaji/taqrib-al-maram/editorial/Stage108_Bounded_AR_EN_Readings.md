## I044.upper.02 — Existence shared by gradation: mental addition, existence in cause and effect

**Arabic source-bounded excerpt:**

> وذلك لأن زيادة الوجود على الماهية وقيامه بها إنما هو بحسب العقل دون الخارج، وليس كقيام البياض بالجسم. […] وهو مفهوم (الكون) أي الثبوت، وهو الوجود المشترك (المقول على الوجودات الخاصة) (بالتشكيك)، لأنه في العلة أقدم منه في المعلول، وفي الجوهر أولى منه في العرض، وفي القار كالعلم أشد منه في غير القار كالحركة. […]

**Corresponding English:**

> This is because existence being additional to quiddity and subsisting in it is only according to the intellect, not in external reality; it is not like whiteness inhering in a body. […] The concept of “being” (kawn), that is, establishment, is the existence shared [in meaning], “predicated of the particular existences by gradation”: it is prior in the cause to its effect, more properly predicated of substance than of accident, and more intense in what abides, such as knowledge, than in what does not abide, such as motion. […]

**Source boundaries:** Upper printed register I:44, beginning AFTER the existing I044.upper.01 disjunction «... فدور أو تسلسل». The printed upper register ends with «... مقولة بالتشكيك على» and joins I045 upper beginning «أفرادها». The above selections omit both intervening commentary and the I045 continuation, which are NOT retranslated.

**Unresolved:** Independently transcribe the parentheses adjoining «مشارك» and «في عارض», settle whether «القار»/«غير القار» refers to abiding categories exactly as parsed, then proof all omitted upper lines and the I044/I045 join without overriding I045.upper.01.

## I045.lower.01 — Shared concept of existence does not entail coincidence of its particulars

**Arabic source-bounded excerpt:**

> (قوله جاز كون واحد من الوجودات الخاصة إلخ) وما ثبت من اشتراك الكل في مفهوم الوجود لا يقتضي تصادق أفراده. […] وكون الوجود المطلق صادقا على الوجودات الخاصة المختلفة الحقائق صدق العرضي اللازم على معروضاته الملزومة. […]

**Corresponding English:**

> On his words, “it is possible that one of the particular existences ...”: the fact established—that they all participate in the concept of existence—does not require its instances to coincide. […] The predication of unrestricted existence of particular existences differing in their realities [is considered] like an inseparable accidental predicate being truly predicated of the subjects to which it necessarily attaches. […]

**Source boundaries:** First inherited editorial subdivision of the continuous printed lower register on I:45; the word «لا» before «يقتضي» and the relative grammar were checked against the original scan. Not a separate original ruled note. Some intervening analogies and qualifications remain omitted.

## I045.lower.02 — Predicating “existent” differs from treating “existence” as one real universal

**Arabic source-bounded excerpt:**

> […] فلا يكون الوجود المطلق مشتركا معنويا بالنسبة إليها، بل المشترك بينها هو الموجود لا الوجود، كما أن المشترك بين أنواع الحيوانات هو الماشي لا المشي. […] وإنما يصح على المشي بالنسبة إلى حصصه لا على المشي بالنسبة إلى الأفراد التي تحته، كما هو ظاهر على أولي الأبصار. […]

**Corresponding English:**

> […] Hence unrestricted existence would not be shared in meaning among them in that way; rather, what is shared among them is “the existent,” not “existence,” just as what is shared among kinds of animals is “the walking thing,” not “walking.” […] [That analogy] applies to walking in relation to its particular portions, not to walking [predicated] of the individuals falling under it, as is apparent to those of insight. […]

**Source boundaries:** Second inherited editorial subdivision within the same unruled lower page I:45; individual sentences continue to I:46. Distinguishes «الماشي» (that which walks) from «المشي» (walking): do not silently substitute the earlier provisional generic “animal” interpretation. The attached quoted lines are partial.
