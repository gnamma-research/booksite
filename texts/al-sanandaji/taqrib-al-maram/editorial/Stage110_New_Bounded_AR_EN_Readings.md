## I047.lower.01 — printed I:47, physical PDF 60
**Arabic selected text**

ما صرح به القاضي في شرح الأصول من أن أكثر القائلين بأن المعدوم ليس بشيء يقولون بزيادة الوجود على الماهية، فراجع وتدبر. (قوله فإن ذلك باطل إلخ) لما سبق من أن تغاير مفهوم الوجود والماهية ضروري، لكن إذا كان معنى التحقق والكون المصدري لا أعني ما به التحقق، فتدبر. (قوله لا تحقق للوجود في الخارج مطلقا إلخ) إنما يتم لو أريد من الوجود المعنى المصدري الاعتباري …

**Aligned English**

What the Qāḍī stated explicitly in his commentary on the Uṣūl—that most of those who hold that a nonexistent thing is not a thing also hold that existence is additional to quiddity—requires reconsideration; examine it carefully. On the words “this is invalid”: the difference between the concept of existence and the concept of quiddity is necessary, but [the argument holds] if existence means realization or being in the abstract, not that by which realization is obtained. Consider this. On the statement “existence has no realization in the external world whatsoever”: this holds only if one intends the abstract, considered sense of existence …

**Boundary and limitations:** First selected passages in the continuous lower commentary on printed I:47, physically PDF60. The uncertain qualifier after the first gloss, all intervening transitions, and the rest of the lower page are OPEN. This is not a whole lower note.
**Still unproven:** Verify the exact Qāḍī title/wording and the subtle use of تحقّق versus ما به التحقّق independently; keep all authorial attributions to the marginal voice unassigned.

## I047.lower.02 — printed I:47, physical PDF 60
**Arabic selected text**

… فالتحقق هو الموجود، لا هو والوجود بتحققين، ولا بتحقق واحد. … إن المقصود من كون وجود الشيء نفسه هو أنه إذا عرض الوجود على الماهية حصل هناك موجود واحد بوجود واحد، لا موجودان بوجودين. … ضرورة أن الوجود بالمعنى المصدري أمر اعتباري لا وجود له … ثم هناك أمران أحدهما الماهية المعروضة والآخر هو الوجود العارض … ضرورة امتناع حمل أحدهما على …

**Aligned English**

… What is realized is the existent, not it together with existence by two realizations—or by one realization shared by two [separately existing things]. … The intended meaning of saying that a thing’s existence is itself would be that, when existence is predicated of quiddity, a single existent results with a single existence, not two existents with two existences. … For existence taken as an abstract verbal notion is a considered matter and has no independent existence of its own. … Yet the objector then speaks of two items: the quiddity as substrate and existence as the predicate or accident. … For it is impossible to predicate one of the two of … [the sentence continues on I:48].

**Boundary and limitations:** Selected noncontiguous segments of I:47 lower commentary, with meaningful omissions preserved. I047.lower.01 source content is NOT duplicated. End printed I:47 cuts a sentence at «على» before I048 continuation; the actual I048.lower material must be checked next, not silently inserted here.
