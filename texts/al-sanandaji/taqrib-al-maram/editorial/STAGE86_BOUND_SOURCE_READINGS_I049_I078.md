# Taqrīb al-Marām · Volume I · Stage 86 bounded bilingual source readings

**Copytext:** Printed Būlāq 1318 AH, Volume I, p.49–72 and 74–78. **Scope:** 45 selected bilingual passages on 29 body leaves. No full-page, all-register, or independent second proof is claimed. The source scan is image-only and remains authoritative.

**Editorial method:** Source-facing short spans are entered rather than paraphrastic overviews. Where ellipses occur, they mark *genuinely omitted printed text*. The older provisional English summaries remain in `stage86_prior_english_analytic_draft` and are not displayed as literal translations. Upper and ruled commentary voices are distinguished. The text has not been manuscript-collated.
