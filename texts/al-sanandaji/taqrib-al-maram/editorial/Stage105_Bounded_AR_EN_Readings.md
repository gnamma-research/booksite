## I042.lower.01
**Arabic (selected; explicit ellipses are omissions):**
حاكمة بوجوب تقدمها عليه بالوجود، فإن العقل ما لم يلاحظ كون الشيء موجودا امتنع أن يلاحظه مبدأ الوجود ومفيدا له، خلاف القابل للوجود، فإنه لا بد أن يلاحظه العقل غير معتبر فيه الوجود لئلا يلزم حصول الحاصل، […] ثم أقول قد سبق آنفا ما حاصله أن الماهية على التقدير المذكور تصير علة اعتبارية لا خارجية، فلا يلزم تقدمها بالوجود مطلقا على الوجود، فافهم. […]

**Literal English of selected Arabic:**
[The preceding I:41 sentence continues:] “…judges that it must precede it in existence.” For, unless the intellect takes something to exist, it cannot regard that thing as the principle or giver of existence. The situation is different with what receives existence: the intellect must consider it without already regarding existence as realised in it, lest this entail the acquisition of what is already acquired. […] I add: what was said earlier amounts to this—that, on the proposed account, quiddity becomes a cause by consideration (iʿtibārī), not an externally existing cause. It therefore does not follow that it must unconditionally precede existence through an existence of its own. Understand. […]

**Coverage:** Opening lower-register argument at printed I:42; translated selected continuous portions and intentionally omitted a difficult intervening qualification concerning nonexistence.

**Open proof obligation:** Check the full construction following «ولا العدم» for its intended scope; the end of the earlier I:41 line, as well as the remaining intervening clauses, needs independent second proof. Lower voice unassigned.

## I042.lower.02
**Arabic (selected; explicit ellipses are omissions):**
(قوله لكن الوجود عند القائلين بزيادته إلخ) أقول قد تقرر في المباحث السابقة أن النزاع إنما هو في الوجود الحقيقي الذي هو مبدأ الانتزاع، وإلا فلا خلاف في زيادة الوجود الانتزاعي على الماهيات، صرح بذلك المحققون. […] أن مقصود المتكلمين من زيادة الوجود على الماهية مطلقا ذهنا هو تغايرهما بحسب المفهوم لا الهوية […] فالتحقيق هو أن مراد الحكماء من كون الوجود في الواجب عين الماهية هو أنه لا ماهية للواجب غير الوجود، بل حقيقته هو الوجود فقط، أعني الوجود الخاص المجرد عن الماهية […] وإما أن يريدوا الاتحاد في المفهوم أيضا، فهو باطل لما سبق آنفا. […]

**Literal English of selected Arabic:**
On his words “but existence, according to those who hold that it is additional,” and so forth: I say that the preceding discussions have established that the disagreement concerns real existence, which is the ground from which [the concept of] existence is abstracted. Otherwise there is no disagreement that existence as abstracted is additional to quiddities; the critical authorities expressly state this. […] What the theologians mean by the addition of existence to quiddity, considered generally in the mind, is their difference in concept, not necessarily a difference of external identity. […] The more exact interpretation of the philosophers’ assertion that the Necessary’s existence is identical with its quiddity is that there is no quiddity of the Necessary other than existence itself; its reality is existence alone—namely, particular existence considered apart from a quiddity. […] If instead they intended identity even at the level of concept, that would be false for the reasons already given. […]

**Coverage:** Distinct lower note introduced by «(قوله لكن الوجود عند القائلين بزيادته إلخ)»; three separated source passages concerning real/extracted existence, mental concept vs external identity, and the Necessary. Ellipses leave multiple substantive arguments untranslated.

**Open proof obligation:** Several transitions (especially the exact subject of the “additional” distinction, the human analogy, and the final adjudication) remain untranscribed. The final printed paragraph must be source-aligned against I:43, not presumed complete. Lower voice unassigned.
