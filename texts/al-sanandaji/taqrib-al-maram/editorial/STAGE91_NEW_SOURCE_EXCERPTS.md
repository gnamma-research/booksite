## I030.ruled.01 · printed Volume I p. 30 (ruled)

**Printed location:** middle ruled register, short note on fear as a ground for obligation; facsimile crop `zoom/I030_ruled.png`, combined PDF physical page 43.

**Arabic:**

> (قوله خوف العقاب) يعني أن الخوف من العقاب على ترك معرفة الله ضرر ... وبوجود المعرفة ينتفي ذلك الخوف فكانت واجبة

**English:**

> On “fear of punishment”: fear of punishment for failing to know God is a harm. […] When that knowledge is attained, the fear is removed; hence such knowledge is obligatory. […]

**Unfinished:** Middle source phrase deliberately omitted from this first literal pass; ellipsis is not missing print.
