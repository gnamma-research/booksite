## New argumentative locus

I:221–226 debates apprehension (*idrāk*), knowledge by presence (*ʿilm ḥuḍūrī*), the difference between a mental cognitive form and its extramental object, and the rival account of knowledge as mere relation (*iḍāfa*). I:227–230 classifies apprehension into *iḥsās*, *takhayyul*, *tawahhum* and *taʿaqqul*, then treats assent, certainty and ignorance. I:231–233 debates necessary/acquired knowledge and the individuation of created cognition by what is known. I:234–240 concerns the soul and heart as loci of knowledge, then will, aversion, power and the debate over responsibility for impossible acts.

### P1.228: Sensation, imagination, estimation and intellection distinguished

**Translation:** Among them is apprehension: the awareness and manifestation of a thing to the intellect.

### L.I.223.02: The definition of knowledge by presence (I:223 / PDF 236)

> ويقال له العلم الحضوري لأن المدرك حاضر بنفسه لا بصورته عند المدرك

### L.I.223.03: The soul knows itself without a distinct representative image (I:223 / PDF 236)

> فإن النفس مدركة لها ولصفاتها بلا واسطة صورة مغايرة للصورة الخارجية

**Translation:** For the soul apprehends itself and its attributes without the mediation of an image different from the external [thing].

### L.I.223.04: The objection concerning a thing's awareness of itself (I:223 / PDF 236)

> واعترض بأن الحضور يقتضي شيئين ضرورة ويمتنع علم الشيء بنفسه

**Translation:** An objection is raised that presence necessarily implies two things and that a thing's knowing itself is impossible.

### L.I.224.05: Mental presence is not ordinary accidental inherence (I:224 / PDF 237)

> ليس حصولها في الذهن كحصول العرض في المحل

**Translation:** Its occurrence in the mind is not like the inherence of an accident in a substratum.

### L.I.226.06: The account of cognition as a mere relation (I:226 / PDF 239)

> ومن أنكر الوجود العقلي جعل الإدراك مجرد إضافة بين العالم والمعلوم

### L.I.227.07: The four modes of apprehension (I:227 / PDF 240)

> وأنواع الإدراك أربعة أحدها إحساس

### L.I.229.09: Stable assent in accordance with reality (I:229 / PDF 242)

### L.I.230.10: Simple and compound ignorance (I:230 / PDF 243)
