# Taqrīb al-Marām + Ḥāshiyat al-Muḥākamāt — Stage 12 (2 November 2026)

## Volume II, printed pages 61–100 | Source-image first reading and selected translation

**Primary witness:** user-supplied two-volume combined Cairo facsimile `taqribmaram.pdf`, printed vol. II:61–100 = physical PDF images **356–395**. All 40 source images were directly inspected at their original locations; selected arguments were enlarged for reading. The scan is raster-only, and no OCR string has been treated as an authoritative Arabic source.

**Source levels must remain independent.** The larger running register contains Sanandajī’s sharḥ together with parenthesized lemmata quoted from al-Taftāzānī; the smaller register contains Wasīm’s *Ḥāshiyat al-Muḥākamāt* and annotations by other scholars, not yet all individually attributed. The Sanandajī–Wasīm workstream is independent of the Tahdhīb matn project in the other chat.

**Critical limitation:** **None of the following 40 printed pages has received a full literal translation of both registers.** The English arguments in Part A are source-oriented *interpretive first readings*, and the short Arabic phrases are not entire-page transcriptions. Part B alone comprises short directly translated source excerpts. The separate direct lower-register fragments in Part C have **unverified individual authorship** and must not be cited as positively Wasīm’s.

### Major source boundaries and intellectual issues

- **II:61–63:** vegetative/generative faculty (*al-quwwa al-muwallida*), formation of the embryo, whether several named faculties are truly different or differentiated by function.
- **II:64–73:** powers peculiar to animals—movement, external senses, theories of sight, common sense, imagination, estimation, recollection and the composing faculty.
- **II:74:** **مقالة في المجردات وفيها بحثان** — the printed treatise-section on immaterial beings, with its first investigation on the soul.
- **II:75–94:** the rational soul versus bodily spirit, universals and particulars, whether intellectual content is divisible, and the argument for an immaterial bearer of intellection.
- **II:95:** **البحث الثاني من المقالة في العقل** — the second investigation, discussing the philosophers’ proposed separate intellects.
- **II:96–100:** the first cause, causal intermediaries, separate intellects and objections to the necessity of emanation. Distinguish reported philosophical hypotheses from Sanandajī’s judgments.
