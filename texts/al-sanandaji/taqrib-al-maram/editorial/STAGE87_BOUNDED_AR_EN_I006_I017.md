# Sanandajī, *Taqrīb al-Marām* · Volume I: selected critical rereadings (Stage 87)

**Copytext:** Būlāq 1318 AH first-volume facsimile (combined PDF physical p = printed page +13). These are **bounded source-confirmed passages**. Bracketed cautions mark omissions, not textual variants; no all-register page is now complete. The original 1,401 source records and earlier working English are retained in the cumulative dataset.

## Printed I:6 · I006.upper.01 · upper

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

(هذا) أي ما سيتلى عليك (قسم الكلام من) الكتاب المسمى (بالتهذيب وعلى الستة وقع التبويب) لأن ما ذكر فيه إما أن يجب تقدمه في علم الكلام وهو الباب الأول في المقدمة أولاً، وحينئذ إما أن يبحث فيه عما يختص بواحد من الأقسام.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

“This”—that is, what is about to be set before you—“is the kalām division of” the book called “al-Tahdhīb; and the arrangement into six chapters has been made.” For what is mentioned therein must either precede [the discussion] in the science of kalām—this is Chapter One, the introduction—or else it treats something peculiar to one of the divisions. [The classification continues on the next printed page.]

**Scope / source note:** Direct reading of the three upper lines, physical PDF p.19; the join to I:7 is not a separate new work. A connective near the end remains a first-pass reading; recheck before full-page acceptance.

## Printed I:6 · I006.ruled.01 · ruled

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

(قوله أي ما سيتلى عليك) يشير إلى أن (هذا) عبارة من الألفاظ من حيث دلالتها على المعاني. اه فرج الله زكي. (قوله المسمى بالتهذيب) أي في عرف المحصلين. اه منه.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

On his words “that is, what will be recited to you”: he indicates that “this” belongs to expressions considered insofar as they signify meanings. [Signed:] Faraj Allāh Zakī. On “called al-Tahdhīb”: that is, by the usage of those who study the work. [By the same writer.] [A longer subsequent ruled discussion of the title remains untranslated here.]

**Scope / source note:** Two directly legible entries at top of ruled middle register, I:6. Not attributed to Wasīm; the subsequent longer note is not covered.

## Printed I:6 · I006.lower.01 · lower

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

قال (هذا قسم الكلام من التهذيب) أقول لما ذكر من الديباجة أن هذا غاية تهذيب الكلام في تحرير المنطق والكلام علم أن كتابه قسمان: قسم في تحرير المنطق وقسم في تحرير الكلام، ثم لما قال الأول في المنطق كان الأوفق هنا أن يقول الثاني في الكلام، إلا أنه لما صرح بأولية ما في المنطق استغنى عن التصريح بثانوية ما في الكلام، فعدل إلى هذا تفنناً، فيكون الحاصل أن هذا هو القسم الآخر من الكلام المهذب الكائن في تحرير علم الكلام، فالظاهر أن إضافة القسم إلى الكلام إضافة الدال إلى المدلول.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

He said, “This is the kalām division of the Tahdhīb.” I say: since the preface mentioned that the work seeks the utmost refinement of discourse in treating logic and kalām, one knows that his book has two divisions: one devoted to the treatment of logic and one devoted to the treatment of kalām. When he had spoken of the first as concerning logic, the most fitting expression here would have been “the second, concerning kalām.” But having explicitly stated the priority of the logic section, he had no need to state explicitly that the kalām section came second. He varied his expression and used “this” instead. The result is that this is the other division of the refined discourse, the one concerned with the science of kalām. Accordingly, the relation of “division” to “kalām” is evidently the relation of the signifying expression to what it signifies. [The lower argument continues and is not exhausted by this excerpt.]

**Scope / source note:** Visually read continuous opening stretch of the lower commentary, Būlāq I:6. This is a selected span, not the entire lower annotation. No identified author in the cropped span.

## Printed I:7 · I007.upper.01 · upper

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

⟦الوصل من I:6⟧ وهو الباب الثاني في الأمور العامة، أو ما يختص بالممكن الذي لا يقوم بنفسه بل بغيره وهو الباب الثالث في الأعراض، أو بالممكن الذي يقوم بنفسه وهو الباب الرابع في الجواهر، وأما الواجب تعالى فإما باعتبار إرساله الرسل وبعثه الأنبياء وهو الباب السادس في السمعيات، أو لا باعتباره وهو الباب الخامس في الإلهيات. الباب الأول في المقدمة. منها ما يراد تقديمه في كل علم كمعرفة حده وموضوعه وغايته، ومنها ما صدروا به علم ⟦يتصل بـ I:8: الكلام خاصة⟧.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

[Continuing the classification from I:6:] This is Chapter Two, on general matters; or [one treats] what pertains to a contingent thing which does not subsist by itself but in another—Chapter Three, on accidents; or what pertains to a contingent thing that subsists by itself—Chapter Four, on substances. As for the Exalted Necessary One, He may be considered with regard to His sending messengers and raising up prophets—Chapter Six, on matters known through transmitted revelation—or apart from that consideration—Chapter Five, on divine matters. Chapter One: Introduction. Of [the preliminary matters], some are to be presented in every science, such as knowledge of its definition, subject and purpose; others are matters with which they introduced the science of ... [continued on I:8: kalām in particular].

**Scope / source note:** The visually distinct chapter heading and the sentence printed immediately beneath it are part of the main upper sequence. The older I007.ruled.01 explanatory paraphrase is not a separate ruled source annotation.

## Printed I:16 · I016.upper.01 · upper

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

بالدليل الثاني، وطريق معرفته عنده القسمة والمثال، أما القسمة فنقول: الاعتقاد إما جازم أو غير جازم، والجازم إما مطابق للواقع أو غير مطابق، والمطابق إما ثابت أو غير ثابت.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

[Continuing from] the second proof: according to him, the way of making knowledge known is division and example. As for division, we say: assent is either firm or not firm; firm assent either accords with reality or does not; and what accords with reality is either stable or unstable. [The reason this classifies rather than strictly defines knowledge is discussed further on the printed page.]

**Scope / source note:** Continuous first four upper lines on p.I:16 (physical PDF 29). The initial words continue an argument begun on I:15.

## Printed I:16 · I016.upper.02 · upper

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

وأما المثال فكأن يقال: العلم إدراك البصيرة الشبيه بإدراك الباصرة، أو يقال هو كاعتقادنا أن الواحد نصف الاثنين.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

As for illustration, one might say: knowledge is the perception of inner sight, resembling perception by the seeing eye; or that it is like our belief that one is half of two. [The following argument concerning genus and essential differentia has not yet been transcribed in this record.]

**Scope / source note:** Continuous example from the third and fourth upper lines, I:16.

## Printed I:16 · I016.ruled.01 · ruled

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

(قوله وأما المثال) المثال يطلق على المشابه وعلى الجزئي، فالأول للأول والثاني للثاني. اه منه.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

On “as for the example”: the word “example” is used both for what resembles [something] and for a particular instance. The first [kind of example] pertains to the former, the second to the latter. [A short interposed gloss, with its own ending “from him.”]

**Scope / source note:** Direct reading of the short middle-ruled entry on I:16, separate from both the upper discourse and the lower commentary.

## Printed I:17 · I017.ruled.01 · ruled

**Arabic (visually checked selection):**

(قوله حركة النفس) إطلاق الحركة على الانتقالات الواقعة للنفس من معقول إلى آخر دفعة إنما هو على سبيل التشبيه لعدم وجود المسافة اللازمة في الحركة. اه منه.

**English (selected literal rendering):**

On “the movement of the soul”: calling the soul’s transitions from one intelligible object to another “movement” is only by way of likeness, because the distance required for [literal spatial] movement does not exist here. [End of the short ruled annotation.]

**Scope / source note:** Directly read two-line ruled annotation in I:17; it concerns metaphorical intellectual motion, not physical locomotion.

## Corrected layer assignment: I007.ruled.01

The independent-ruled-note entry was actually an *editorial paraphrase* of a printed heading and the following **upper** line. It remains in the archival source registry but is hidden from normal reading and text search; that main line is explicitly preserved in `I007.upper.01`. There is no justification for presenting it as an independently printed voice.
