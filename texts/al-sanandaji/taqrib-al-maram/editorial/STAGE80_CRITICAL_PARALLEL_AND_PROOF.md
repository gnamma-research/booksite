## I:73, marginal-only page: source transcription repairs

- `I073.lower.01`: previous draft read `اعتبر ذلك الذي خارجا عنه غير داخل فيه`. The Būlāq image reads `اعتبر ذلك الذي قارنها خارجا عنه غير داخل فيه`. Restored omitted **قارنها**. The sense: when something accompanies a quiddity considered *bi-sharṭ lā*, the concomitant is treated as outside that quiddity, not included in it.
- `I073.lower.02`: previous `لو قارنها شيء لا يشترط دخوله`, source revised to `لو قارنها شيء لم يشترط دخوله` (past negative **لم**).
- `I073.lower.02`: congested print `⟦للمقولية؟⟧` now recorded as probable `⟦للمقولية⟧` **in editorial brackets**, on the support of Būlāq glyphs and an exact parallel in *Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid* I:100: `مبهما محتملا للمقولية`. This parallel is not another witness of *Taqrīb*.
- `I073.lower.04`: the second occurrence of `⟦للمقولية؟⟧` is **still uncertain**; a probable parallel is recorded in the separate apparatus, not substituted into the printed text.

Parallel citation: https://almntqim.com/٤٥٠١_شرح-المقاصد-في-علم-الكلام-التفتازاني-ج-١/١٠٠_الصفحة
Also compare *Sharḥ al-Ishārāt wa-l-Tanbīhāt*, quoted treatment of quiddity considered `bi-sharṭ lā` and `lā bi-sharṭ`: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/8239/80?lang=ar.

## I:222, quotation with an apparent omitted term

`I222.lower.03`, Būlāq: `فيصح جعل العلم تارة نفس الصورة وتارة انتهى`, with second term apparently unprinted. *Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid* I:227 has `فبهذا الاعتبار يصح جعل العلم تارة نفس الصورة وتارة حصولها`.

Editorial treatment: **preserve Būlāq Arabic**, but propose **[حصولها]** only in the apparatus and bracketed English, i.e., the form *itself* versus *its occurrence*. Do **not** claim this is an actual variant reading in another *Taqrīb* manuscript.

Parallel citation: https://almntqim.com/٤٥٠١_شرح-المقاصد-في-علم-الكلام-التفتازاني-ج-١/٢٢٧_الصفحة

## I:88 and I:112

- `I088.lower.01` is retained from the previous corrected reading `لأن عدمه ضروري` (*because its nonexistence is necessary*), **not** `لا أن عدمه ضروري`. A mistaken negation inverts the logic of general possibility.
- I:112 contains only the continuous lower marginal register, with three existing long Arabic–English records. The source was visually re-examined for the arguments over production versus intending to produce and essential versus temporal priority. Some close printed glyphs remain uncertain: no independent complete-page approval is asserted.
