## 1. `I025.upper.02` · printed I:25 · upper commentary

**Arabic (a contiguous clause following the separate objection `I025.upper.01`):**

> وأجيب بأن الفعل الآخر هنا إفادة العلم وهي فعل. (أو) بطريق (الوجوب) بناء على أن فيضان الحوادث من المبدأ الفياض عند الاستعداد التام للقابل واجب.

**English:**

> The answer is that the other act here is the imparting of knowledge, which is an act. “Or” [knowledge follows] by way of “necessity”, on the assumption that the effusion of originated things from the effusive principle is necessary when the recipient is fully prepared.

The two parenthesized words are embedded lemmata of al-Taftāzānī, not separate lower commentary. The printed objection “knowledge is not of the category of acting” belongs to the already established `I025.upper.01`; the explanatory lower gloss belongs to `I025.lower.02`, which **has not been changed**.

## 2. `I026.lower.02` · printed I:26 · lower-register marginal gloss (voice unassigned)

The passage begins immediately after the *existing* `I026.lower.01` sentence ending with **كمذهب المعتزلة**. The following transcription does not repeat that text. Ellipses mark uncertain connectors and passages left untranscribed rather than silently emending them.

**Arabic (extended selected source reading):**

> لكن صرح بالوجوب فلا يحمل الاستعقاب العادي فيرجع إلى المذهب الأول، بل المراد الاستعقاب العقلي بمعنى أن من علم النظر يمتنع أن لا يعلم النتيجة كما في جميع اللوازم والملزومات. […]
>
> فإنه حينئذ يجوز أن لا يقع بأن لا تتعلق به قدرة الله واختياره فيكون المذهب الأول بعينه. […]
>
> ولا ينافي هذا الوجوب كون العلم أثر القادر المختار وجائز الفعل والترك لكن بمعنى أن لا يخلقه ولا ملزومه لا بأن يخلق الملزوم ولا يخلقه كالمذهب الأول. لأن جواز الترك أعم من أن يكون بوسط أو لا بوسط فلا يمتنع أن يكون مشروطا بارتفاع مانع هو أيضا مقدور كالمتولدات عند المعتزلة القائلة بكونها بقدرة العبد. […]
>
> وإنما المنافي لجواز الترك الوجوب بمعنى امتناع الانفكاك عن المؤثر مطلقا، فلزوم العلم للنظر عقلي على هذا المذهب وإن لم يناف جواز الترك كاللزوم بين سائر الممكنات، وعادي على المذهب الأول بالمعنى الذي مر آنفا.

**English (matched to these source spans):**

> But he expressly asserted necessity. Therefore the ensuing relation must not be understood as merely customary succession, for that would take us back to the first position. Rather, what is meant is rational entailment: a person who grasps the inquiry cannot fail to know the conclusion, as in all relations of necessary consequence and antecedent. […]
>
> For in that case it may fail to occur if God’s power and choice do not attach to it, and the first position would return unchanged. [The precise preceding qualification has not been established.] […]
>
> This necessity does not contradict knowledge being the effect of an agent possessed of power and choice, whose acting and refraining are possible. But refraining here means not creating either the knowledge or that which entails it—not creating the entailing antecedent while failing to create the knowledge, as on the first view. For the possibility of refraining is broader than cases occurring with or without mediation, so it may be conditional upon the removal of an impediment that itself falls within power, as with generated effects according to the Muʿtazilites who say that they occur through the servant’s power. […]
>
> Only necessity understood as absolute inseparability from the producer conflicts with the possibility of refraining. Hence knowledge’s entailment by inquiry is rational on this position, even though it does not contradict the possibility of refraining, as with relations among other contingent things; on the first view the entailment is customary, in the sense just explained.

### Proof and attribution cautions

- The words immediately before **فإنه حينئذ** appear to concern necessity “by choice,” but whether the text negates this interpretation cannot yet be established with confidence. Do **not** silently supply a negative. The reader and this file therefore mark the connective `[…]` and refrain from claiming to have translated it.
- The lower-register scholium is not securely attributed to one author. Its reference to al-Rāzī and Ḥujjat al-Islām earlier in the note must not be treated as authorship.
- The meaning of **جواز الترك** shifts with the comparison: not producing an antecedent at all is different from producing the antecedent and withholding its rational consequent. This distinction is explicit in the verified clauses; the exact punctuation is editorial.
