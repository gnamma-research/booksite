# Sanandajī — Volume I source corrections: I:222 and I:73

**Primary scanned witness:** Būlāq 1318 AH combined PDF SHA256 `4addd660de68794f9d64e062e3b25a2782497b3342eb4580218c3e07e8d12134`.

## I:222, lower marginal text: restored omitted noun

Older transcription: `افترقوا فرقتين يقال لهم أهل الحقيقة والأخرى أهل الشبح`

**Printed source:** `افترقوا فرقتين فرقة يقال لهم أهل الحقيقة والأخرى أهل الشبح`

The second **فرقة** is present in the original image and was omitted in the earlier typed record `I222.lower.01`. Correct English: *They divided into two groups: one called the proponents of reality, and the other the proponents of the representational likeness.* This is a source-image correction, not a variant from a second witness.

## I:73, lower marginal text: continuous selected close reading

Arabic from a continuous passage:
> وأما الشيخ الرئيس فقد يستفاد من كلامه المنقول اعتبارات غير ما اشتهر، وهي أن الماهية المأخوذة بشرط لا شيء هي ما يتصور معناها وحده، بحيث لو قارنها شيء اعتبر ذلك الذي قارنها غير داخل فيه، فلا يكون ذلك المعنى مقولا على المجموع المركب حال المقارنة، بل جزءا منه ومادته.

Close translation:
> Yet from the Chief Master's transmitted words one may derive ways of considering quiddity other than those that have become customary. In this usage, a quiddity taken on condition that nothing accompany it is its meaning conceived by itself alone: if something were to accompany it, the accompanying thing would be considered not to enter into it. Accordingly, when that accompaniment is present, the meaning would not be predicable of the resulting composite as a whole; rather, it would be a constituent part of it and its matter.

This distinguishes quiddity as a **part** of a composite from a universal **predicable of the whole**. It is *one selected passage* from an entirely marginal-only printed page, not a complete line-by-line transcription of I:73 or manuscript collation.

These changes must not be used as evidence that the volume is definitive, or that all printed marginalia are assigned to Muḥammad Wasīm.
