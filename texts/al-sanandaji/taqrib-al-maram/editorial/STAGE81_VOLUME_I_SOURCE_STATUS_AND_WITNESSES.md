The updated structured working corpus has **1,401 unique source-linked records** (Stage80 1,400 + one newly inventoried signed middle register on I:2), with **1,063** nonempty typed-Arabic *working drafts* and **338** anchor-only entries. The two I:2 preface records were additionally expanded with continuous Arabic attempts. These counts do not measure word completeness.

Three already typed source leaves — I:88 (upper + lower), I:112 (marginal continuation only), I:253 (one upper line + lower) — received another full-image, enlarged-strip **same-assistant** reread, their cross-page joins recorded, and remaining ambiguous words left provisional. This is **not independent critical certification**. **0/279** body leaves independently certified all-register complete; **0** manuscript-collated leaves. Most long ḥāshiya prose still needs literal full-line proof, and I:2 still contains a marked lacuna.

## New manuscript lead: Zheen Centre

The Zheen Centre for Documentation and Studies, Sulaymaniyah, catalogues *Taqrīb al-Marām* by ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Muhājir al-Mardūkhī, **171 folios**, copyist **ʿAbd al-Karīm**, **1298 AH**, 21 × 15 cm. It appears in the centre’s physical holdings catalogue (#12 under kalām) and electronically photographed manuscripts catalogue (#26). The centre says its manuscripts have been scanned.

**No images have been obtained and no variants have been collated.** Its potential precedence over the Būlāq print and possible scope must be tested by actual folio images, incipit/excipit and marginal-layer provenance. The 47-folio Azhar manuscript and incomplete Bayāra 137-page catalogue lead likewise remain uncollated.

## New I:2 discovery

The original I:2 contains an **upper Sanandajī invocation**, an **intervening separately ruled signed editorial comment** identifying the printed invocations, and a **lower Wasīm authorial preface** independently identified at I:3. Earlier data had only the upper and Wasīm records; Stage81 adds the middle as `I002.ruled.publisher.01`. The signature names Faraj Allāh Zakī al-Kurdistānī al-Marwānī al-Azharī. The latter part of his text and one ornate upper invocation remain uncertain: we mark them as printed lacunae, not guessed words. The Wasīm preface was already preserved in `stage51-wasim-preface.js` and must not be reattributed to the anonymous lower glosses.
