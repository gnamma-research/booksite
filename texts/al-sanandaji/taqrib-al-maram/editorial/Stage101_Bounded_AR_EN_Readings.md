## `I033.lower.01` · printed I:33, physical PDF page 46 · lower commentary

**Arabic, source-selected (not full transcription):**

> النقل […] لكن ينبغي أن يعلم أن ذلك إنما هو فيما يقصد منه حصول القطع، وأما ما يقصد به مجرد إفادة الظن فيكفي فيه خبر واحد أو جماعة يظن المستدل صدقه. بل لو جعل العلم الحاصل بالتواتر نظريًّا واستدلاليًّا كما هو عند بعض لم يتوقف النقل القطعي فيه أيضًا على نحو إثبات الصانع وبعثة الأنبياء المتوقف على العقل، فتدبر. (قوله من الواجب والجوهر والعرض إلخ) أشار به إلى أن مرادهم بالموجودات ههنا أقسام الموجود لا أفراده التي لا سبيل للعقل إلى […]

**English, corresponding source selection:**

> “... transmission.” […] But it should be known that this is so when what is sought is the attainment of certainty. As for what is intended merely to yield probability, the report of one person—or of a group whom the person reasoning considers truthful—can suffice. Indeed, even were the knowledge attained through widespread transmission (tawātur) regarded as discursive and inferential, as some hold, the definitiveness of transmission in this case would not depend on proofs of the Creator and the sending of prophets that themselves depend on reason. Reflect. On “the Necessary, substance and accident, etc.”: he indicates that by “existents” they mean here the divisions of what exists, not its individual instances, which reason has no way to […].

**Textual boundary and omissions:** At the I:32–33 boundary, the already transcribed I032.lower.02 ends «في العقل دون […]». This record alone begins with «النقل» and does not duplicate the prior words. The immediately following connective is not fully certain. Later source text discriminates probative certainty from mere probability, then begins the lower note on the general categories of existents. The end «لا سبيل للعقل إلى» is left open for I:34, which already has partial Arabic and must undergo clause-level proof; it is NOT silently included in this record.

**Still unresolved:** Uncertain connective after «النقل»; qualifications around tawātur; downstream transition and end of sentence on I:34; full Arabic and independent translation proof of all printed lower lines.

## `I036.lower.01` · printed I:36, physical PDF page 49 · lower commentary

**Arabic, source-selected (not full transcription):**

> موجود ضروري، وهذا تصور وجود خاص فبداهته تستلزم بداهة العام. لكن أظهر من هذا الاستدلال لسلامته من المصادرة والتكلف للجواب عنها، ولعدم ورود ما سبق من الفرق بين حصول الشيء وتصوره؛ لأن ذلك إنما يقدح فيما لو جعل استدلالًا على بداهة تصور العلم كما سبق، لا فيما إذا جعل استدلالًا على بداهة تصور الوجود كما هنا، ضرورة أن حصول العلم بالوجود عين تصور الوجود وإن كان غير تصور العلم. […]

**English, corresponding source selection:**

> “[That something is] existent” is self-evident; this is a conception of a particular existence, and its self-evidence entails the self-evidence of the general [conception]. But [the argument under discussion] is clearer than this inference, because it is free from begging the question and the strained attempt to answer that charge. Nor does the earlier distinction between something's occurring and conceiving it apply: that distinction would count against using the argument to establish the self-evidence of the conception of knowledge, as before, but not against using it to establish the self-evidence of conceiving existence, as here. For acquiring knowledge of existence is precisely conceiving existence, although it is not the same as conceiving knowledge. […]

**Textual boundary and omissions:** The opening «موجود ضروري» continues a preceding discussion across the I:35–36 boundary: no claim is made to reconstruct or retranslate I035.lower.02. The argument distinguishes gaining knowledge of existence from forming a conception of knowledge. Some qualifications after the quoted selected span are omitted pending proof. The separately ruled note I036.ruled.01 is left unchanged.

**Still unresolved:** Exact scope of comparative «أظهر» and intervening qualifications; boundary with next quoted lemma; all remaining lower clauses and independent second proof.

## `I036.lower.02` · printed I:36, physical PDF page 49 · lower commentary

**Arabic, source-selected (not full transcription):**

> (قوله حاصل لمن لا يتصور منه كسب كالصبيان إلخ) أشار بهذا إلى الاستدلال على بداهة التصديق بقولنا الشيء إما موجود أو معدوم، بأنه لو لم يكن بديهيًّا بل كان كسبيًّا لما حصل ممن لا يتأتى منه الكسب، واللازم باطل فكذا الملزوم. وهذا مبني على جعل التصديق ببداهة هذا التصديق كسبيًّا مفتقرًا إلى النظر كما جعل التصديق ببداهة تصور الوجود كسبيًّا، واستدل عليه بقوله فإن قولنا الشيء إما موجود إلخ، فتدبر فإنه دقيق. […]

**English, corresponding source selection:**

> On “[the knowledge] occurs in those for whom acquired reasoning cannot be conceived, such as children, etc.”: this points to an argument for the self-evidence of assent to our proposition “A thing is either existent or nonexistent.” If that assent were not self-evident but acquired, it would not occur in someone incapable of acquiring it; the consequence is false, so the antecedent is false likewise. This assumes that assent to the *self-evidence of that assent* is acquired and requires inquiry, just as assent to the self-evidence of conceiving existence was treated as acquired; the argument appealed to the words “A thing is either existent or nonexistent,” and so forth. Reflect: the point is subtle. […]

**Textual boundary and omissions:** Begins with an explicit lower-register lemma «قوله حاصل لمن لا يتصور منه كسب كالصبيان». Not the upper text or the separate ruled gloss. The selected argument distinguishes the basic assent itself from a reflective assent that it is self-evident; the dense conclusion about demonstration versus indication and «الحد والرسم الحقيقيين» is left untranslated pending independent source comparison rather than turned into a synthetic paraphrase. The old Stage75 multi-page English header is preserved privately in the prior-overview field, not shown as reader text.

**Still unresolved:** Read and translate concluding clauses after «فتدبر فإنه دقيق»; possible terminological contrast استدلال/تنبيه; verify last line and its relation to I:37.
