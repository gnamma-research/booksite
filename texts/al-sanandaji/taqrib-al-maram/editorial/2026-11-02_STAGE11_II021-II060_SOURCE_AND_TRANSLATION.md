# Taqrīb al-Marām & Ḥāshiyat al-Muḥākamāt · Stage 11

## Original volume II, printed pages 21–60

**Source:** the previously supplied 632-image combined facsimile `taqribmaram.pdf`. The first forty pages in this instalment are printed **II:21–60** = physical PDF **316–355**; all were visually inspected, and the exact original pages are preserved in the attached facsimile PDF.

### Source-author and translation limits

The upper register is Sanandajī's commentary with al-Taftāzānī's embedded quoted lemmata, and the lower register contains Wasīm's *Ḥāshiyat al-Muḥākamāt* **plus other named or unattributed printed marginal contributions**. Type position alone does not establish the author of a given note. In this instalment **17 selected short upper-register source phrases** were translated directly, as was **one lower-register fragment with author UNASSIGNED**. The broader page-specific English paragraphs are **argument summaries**, not complete direct translations of original Arabic. The printed marginal register is largely untranslated; none of these pages is certified complete.

**Primary philosophical sequence:** The continuation of atomism and bodily shape, the new `فصل في أحكام الأجسام` on II:21, proofs and objections concerning the origination of the world (roughly II:31–38), the new `فصل في تقسيم الجسم وبيان أقسامه` at II:39, the divisions of celestial and elemental body, the four elements and related natural phenomena, and the new `فصل في المركبات التي لها مزاج` at II:50, introducing *mizāj* and its connection with living powers.
