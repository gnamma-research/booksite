**English:** ‘Regarding the objection ...’: The objection arises by assuming that possession of a book and of a revealed law must be combined in a messenger, as is implied by the conjunctive ‘and’ (wa). There is also a third account under which the category ‘messenger’ is narrower than ‘prophet’: a prophet is anyone to whom revelation is given, whether or not commanded to convey it; a messenger is one to whom revelation is given and who is commanded to convey it. It is obvious why the preceding objection does not apply to this account. One must nevertheless recognize that the familiar claim that prophets outnumber messengers must rest on the account that messengers are a narrower class of prophets: it would be incompatible with the two being coextensive.
