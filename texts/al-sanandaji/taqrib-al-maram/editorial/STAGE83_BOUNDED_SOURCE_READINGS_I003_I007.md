**Illustrative bounded printed Arabic (I:3, Wasīm lower stream):**

> وكان المحصلون المترددون إلى والدنا الماجد راغبين في تحصيله عنده، ومنهم الأخ الأكبر الماهر الشيخ عبد القادر، وكان في أثناء تحصيله يراجع شرح المقاصد ويأخذ منه معاني دقيقة يكتبها حواشي على هذا المتن المتين بلفظ موجز متين، وبعد إتمام تحصيله جعلها شرحاً له.

**Aligned first-pass English:** The students who frequented our eminent father were eager to study it with him. Among them was my accomplished elder brother, Shaykh ʿAbd al-Qādir. While studying it, he consulted *Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid*, extracting subtle meanings and writing them as concise marginal annotations on the text. Once he finished his study, he developed those notes into a commentary.

The distinction between Sanandajī’s *sharḥ*, Taftāzānī’s embedded lemmas, Wasīm’s self-identified lower preface, and unsigned ruled/ḥāshiyah notes is binding. Ellipses in the full Stage 83 reading files mark **untranscribed printed material**, not physical damage to the copytext. Stage 82 historical summaries have been preserved in concealed apparatus instead of appearing in continuous reading.
