## Authorial distinction

The upper register mixes quoted propositions from al-Taftāzānī’s *Tahdhīb al-Kalām* with ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Sanandajī’s exposition. The lower register may contain objections by several different writers. None of the specific lower passages is here positively authenticated as Muḥammad Wasīm’s. The latter’s separately self-identified preface I:2–5 remains distinct.
