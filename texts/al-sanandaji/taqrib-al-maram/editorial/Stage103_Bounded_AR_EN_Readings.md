## 1. `I038.upper.01` — printed I:38, physical PDF 51, upper register

**Arabic — selected source phrases**

> […] لا تتوقف على الوضع والعلم ولذلك لا تختلف باختلاف اللغات […] (و) الأمر الثاني (الجزم مع التردد في الخصوصية) من أنواع الموجودات وأشخاصها، ولو لم يكن الوجود مشتركا معنويا بل كان له في كل معنى غير ما في الآخر لم يصح الجزم به مع التردد في خصوصيته. […] وأما على الأول فلأن التردد في الخصوصيات غير التردد في الوجودات، وأما على الثاني فلأن التردد في شيء يستلزم التردد فيما يختص به قطعا. […]

**English — translation of those selected phrases**

> […] [The relevant intelligible divisions] do not depend on linguistic convention and knowledge of it; thus they do not differ with differences of language. […] “Second”: one may affirm [existence] with certainty while remaining undecided about the particular determination among the kinds and individuals of existents. If existence were not common in meaning, but had a different meaning in each case, one could not affirm it with certainty while remaining undecided about its particular determination. […] On the first supposition, hesitation over particular determinations is distinct from hesitation over the several existences; on the second, hesitation over something entails hesitation over what pertains specifically to it. […]

**Source boundary:** I:38 upper begins within an argument already underway in I037.upper.02; only selected fresh p38 clauses are added. This I038.upper.01 selection stops before the existing I038.upper.02 beginning “(و) الثالث (تمام الحصر في الموجود والمعدوم)”. No previous upper Arabic/English has been replaced.

**Still unresolved:** The compact opening continuation from I:37 and the intervening explanation distinguishing several senses of uncertainty are only selectively quoted; the printed sentence preceding the second proof and some connectors remain to be independently rechecked.

## 2. `I039.lower.01` — printed I:39, physical PDF 52, lower register

**Arabic — selected source phrases**

> لا على التعيين، ثم الحصر أيضا لفظا يتوقف على اعتبار عموم المجاز المتوقف على الوضع واللغة كما سبق في التقسيم، والمقصود تمام الحصر عقلا من غير توقف على ما ذكر. […] (قوله لجواز الاتصاف بالوجود بمعنى آخر إلخ) وهذا لا يتوقف على اتحاد مفهوم العدم، إذ على تقدير تعدده كان عدم الحصر أظهر لجواز أن يكون متصفا بالعدم بمعنى آخر. […] (قوله بمعنى أن العقل يلاحظ الوجود دون الماهية إلخ) إشارة إلى أن المقصود من زيادة الوجود على الماهية هو تغايرهما بحسب المفهوم دون الهوية. […]

**English — translation of those selected phrases**

> “Without specifying which one”: a merely verbal exhaustiveness of the division would also depend on considering the generality of figurative application, which in turn depends on linguistic convention and language, as previously explained in the discussion of division. What is intended is an exhaustive division in reason, without depending upon those considerations. […] On his words “because it might be qualified by existence in another sense”: this does not depend on the concept of nonexistence being one; if that concept were multiple, the failure of an exhaustive division would be still clearer, since a thing might be qualified by nonexistence in another sense. […] On “meaning that the intellect attends to existence without quiddity”: this indicates that the intended addition of existence to quiddity is their difference in concept, not necessarily a difference in identity. […]

**Source boundary:** The printed p39 lower text starts «لا على التعيين», completing the conditional objection at the end of I038.lower.02, which is NOT retranslated. The present record then samples the printed glosses on alternative senses of existence and on conceptual distinction. Stop before the separate «(قوله أي عن الماهية إلخ)» gloss.

**Still unresolved:** The final connective in the opening explanation and some intervening short phrases are not fully established. Print-register divisions between the two legacy I039 lower IDs are editorial and require independent second proof. Do not supply any text from p38 again.

## 3. `I039.lower.02` — printed I:39, physical PDF 52, lower register

**Arabic — selected source phrases**

> (قوله أي عن الماهية إلخ) […] فصحة هذا السلب لا تفيد كون الوجود غير ماهية العنقاء، إذ معناه هو أن العنقاء ليس شيئا متصفا بالوجود. […] قلت: الوجود الذي يبحث عنه أنه هل هو نفس الماهية أو زائد عليها هو مبدأ انتزاع الوجود بالمعنى المصدري، لا المعنى المصدري الذي هو مأخذ اشتقاق الموجود. […] النزاع في الوجود المقابل للعدم وهو معنى الكون. […] أما أن يراد بالوجود المسلوب مبدأ الانتزاع فيلزم المصادرة، أو المعنى المصدري الانتزاعي فلا يفيد المدعى أعني مغايرة […]

**English — translation of those selected phrases**

> On his words “that is, from quiddity” […] The possibility of this denial does not establish that existence is other than the quiddity of the ʿanqāʾ; it means only that the ʿanqāʾ is not something qualified by existence. […] I say: the existence under discussion—whether it is identical with quiddity or additional to it—is the principle from which existence in the verbal-noun sense is abstracted, not that verbal-noun meaning itself from which “existent” is derived. […] The dispute concerns existence as opposed to nonexistence, namely being. […] If what is meant by the existence being denied is the principle of abstraction, the inference begs the question; if the meaning is the abstracted verbal-noun sense, it does not establish the proposed difference, namely the divergence of […]

This separately indexed record contains selected phrases from the subsequent exchange on the derived predicate (al-mushtaqq), the principle of its derivation, and the ontological or abstract noun senses of existence. The final sentence remains unfinished at the page turn to printed I:40; no p40 source is supplied.
