## I040.lower.01 · printed I:40, physical PDF 53
**Arabic (selected; ellipses mark omitted words and arguments)**
الماهية لبداهة الانتزاع فليتأمل جدا. (قوله لأن حمل الشيء على نفسه إلخ) المراد من الحمل هنا أيضا هو الحمل الاشتقاقي […] لا حمل المواطأة […] لأن إفادة هذا الحمل ممنوعة […] وأما عند القائلين بمغايرتهما فلعدم صحة هذا الحمل فضلا عن إفادته. […] وأن المراد من الحمل هو حمل الاشتقاق فلا نسلم أن قولنا […] موجود غير مفيد ولو على تقدير كون الوجود نفس الماهية. […] فإذا كان نفس الماهية فإنه حينئذ يكون معناه أن الوجود متصف بالكون المنتزع من وجود هو نفس الموضوع، وذلك ضروري غير مفيد وغير مستلزم للتسلسل. […]

**English (translation of selected clauses only)**
[Completion of the I:39 sentence:] “…of quiddity, since abstraction is self-evident; reflect carefully.” On his words “because predicating a thing of itself,” what is intended by predication here, too, is derivative predication […] not predication by strict identity. […] For it is denied that such a predication conveys information. […] For those who assert that [quiddity and existence] differ, this follows because such predication is not sound, quite apart from its being informative. […] Since what is meant is derivative predication, we do not concede that saying “[the subject] is existent” is uninformative, even supposing that existence is identical with quiddity. […] On this latter assumption, to say that existence is qualified by being, abstracted from an existence identical with the subject, is necessary and uninformative, but does not entail an infinite regress. […]

**Source and boundary:** Opening p40 lower continuation of the unfinished I039.lower.02 sentence, followed by selected argument on derivative (ishtiqaqi) versus identity (muwataʾa) predication; many intervening clauses omitted.
**Still open:** The illustrative subject in the example and intervening concession/reply are not fully transcribed; do not infer that the author of this lower gloss is identified.

**Start boundary:** I039.lower.02 ends «فلا يفيد المدعى أعني مغايرة» and I040.lower.01 opens «الماهية لبداهة الانتزاع فليتأمل جدا»; the p39 record is unchanged.

## I041.lower.01 · printed I:41, physical PDF 54
**Arabic (selected; ellipses mark omitted words and arguments)**
متغايرين بالمفهوم والاعتبار لكنهما في الخارج موجود له هوية واحدة، فلا قيام بينهما في الخارج، بل إنما هو بالاعتبار كما يأتي، فلا يوجب الإمكان المنافي للوجوب، إذ لا معنى لواجب الوجود سوى ما يمتنع زوال وجوده من ذاته نظرا إلى ذاته […] فإن الممكن المنافي للواجب ما يحتاج في ثبوت الوجود له إلى غير ذاته، والواجب ما لا يحتاج في ذلك إلى غير ذاته، بل يكون ذاته مقتضيا لوجوده. […]

**English (translation of selected clauses only)**
[Completion of the I:40 sentence:] “…different in concept and consideration, yet externally they are one existent with a single identity.” There is therefore no inherence of one in the other outside the mind; such inherence is instead by consideration, as will follow. Hence this does not entail the possibility opposed to necessity, for the Necessary Existent means that whose existence cannot cease through consideration of its own essence. […] The possible, as opposed to the necessary, needs something other than itself for its existence to be established; the necessary needs no such other, but its essence entails its existence. […]

**Source and boundary:** Source-checked first stretch of the lower register at I:41, continuing I:40 on the conceptual/extramental distinction; not the whole lower register.
**Still open:** Middle qualifications on dependence and reflective attribution of necessity remain only partially transcribed; full independent source proof open.

**Start boundary:** Direct continuation from I040.lower.02: «فإن الماهية والوجود وإن كانا / متغايرين بالمفهوم والاعتبار». No duplicate p40 source is entered.

## I041.lower.02 · printed I:41, physical PDF 54
**Arabic (selected; ellipses mark omitted words and arguments)**
(قوله وأما بالوجود فلا إلخ) أي وأما تقدمها بالوجود فليس بلازم، بل اللازم هو تقدمها بما هي عليه، إن كانت بالوجود فبالوجود أو بالماهية فبالماهية. […] (قوله كتقدم ماهية الممكن إلخ) فإن تلك الماهية من حيث هي متقدمة على الوجود من غير اعتبار الوجود أو العدم، وكتقدم الماهية على لوازمها المستندة إلى نفس الماهية من حيث هي، كالثلاثة للفردية والأربعة للزوجية. […] (قوله ولهم أن يقولوا إلخ) […] وحاصله أن الكلام فيما يكون علة لوجود أو موجود في الخارج وبديهة العقل […]

**English (translation of selected clauses only)**
On his words “but not [precedence] by existence,” etc.: its precedence in existence is not necessary; what is necessary is its precedence as what it is—if it is through existence, then through existence; if through quiddity, then through quiddity. […] On “as the quiddity of the possible precedes,” that quiddity, insofar as it is itself, is prior to existence without bringing either existence or nonexistence into the consideration. So too a quiddity precedes the concomitants grounded in the quiddity itself, as three precedes oddness and four precedes evenness. […] On “they may say,” etc.: […] the gist is that the discussion concerns what is a cause of existence or is existent outside the mind, and the intellect’s evident judgement […]

**Source and boundary:** Selected separately introduced glosses from latter portion of I:41 lower register; end of p41 continues onto I:42 and must not be completed by conjecture.
**Still open:** Some printed connective arguments and the words in the final appeal to intellectual self-evidence are only excerpted; p41-to-42 join requires source check.

**End boundary:** I041.lower.02 ends after «وبديهة العقل» (continuation into I042.lower.01 not supplied).
