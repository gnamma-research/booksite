### Layer attribution
- **Matn:** al-Taftāzānī's expressions commonly embedded in parentheses within the upper running commentary.
- **Sharḥ:** Sanandajī's explanation (upper register) when identified from layout and context.
- **Ḥāshiya:** smaller lower-register annotations; specific authorship may vary and is **unassigned** unless explicitly verified.
- **Modern translator/editor:** separate explanatory footnote with no claim of historical authorship.

Initial passages begin with Sanandajī’s actual preface and cover (a) doctrinal belief, (b) immediate apprehension of existence, (c) complete causality, (d) the reported separate-intellect account, and (e) wisdoms/purposes in certain divine acts. The excerpts are **not** a full translation of Sanandajī's arguments. Page references are printed volume/page and the user-supplied PDF page; original-page mapping near the opening is checked individually.

### Integration
Each commentary selection has a stable section ID and may be linked from its matched Tahdhīb passage. Do not present quoted lemmata embedded in the sharḥ as Sanandajī's independently authored assertions.
