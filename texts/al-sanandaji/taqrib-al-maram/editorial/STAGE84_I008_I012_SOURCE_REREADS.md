# Stage 84 · 12 bounded source checks on opening leaves I:8–12

**Authority:** original 1318 AH Būlāq facsimile; combined source PDF physical pp.21–25. The printed upper text is separated from the separately ruled and lower ḥawāshī. These are **selected legible passages**, not complete printed pages. All omitted Arabic and English must be checked against the included scan. No manuscript variants or definitive readings are claimed.

**Editorial correction:** on I:11 the comparison is the Companions’ knowledge **بالعمليات** (*of practical matters*), not **بالعقائد**; this matters because the sentence analogizes kalām and fiqh without conflating them.

## I008.upper.01 — printed I:8 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** كمباحث العلم والنظر والدليل لأن تحصيل العقائد بطريق النظر والاستدلال والرد على منكري حصول العلم مطلقا أو استفادته من النظر مطلقا أو في الإلهيات خاصة يتوقف على ذلك

**English (corresponding selected portion):** [These introductory investigations include] questions of knowledge, inquiry and proof, since acquiring doctrines through reflection and inference—and responding to those who deny the attainment of knowledge altogether, or deny that it is gained through reflection either in general or specifically in divine matters—depends upon them.

**Limit:** Upper opening follows the I:7 division; the brief concluding transition on I:8 is not transcribed.

## I008.upper.02 — printed I:8 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** (الكلام هو العلم بالعقائد) وهي الأحكام التي يتعلق الغرض بمجرد اعتقادها بالقلب

**English (corresponding selected portion):** “Kalām is knowledge of doctrines”: these are judgments whose intended object is simply their being believed in the heart.

**Limit:** Only the opening lemma and its immediate explanation are transcribed; the upper discussion continues and the different practical class is not fully entered.

## I008.ruled.01 — printed I:8 · ruled
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** (قال المصنف رحمه الله الكلام هو العلم إلخ) أي علم الكلام هو

**English (corresponding selected portion):** “The author—may God have mercy on him—said: ‘Kalām is knowledge,’ etc.” That is, the science of kalām is [the knowledge here intended].

**Limit:** The ruled author’s historical theories of the name kalām remain untranscribed, and no author is inferred for this note.

## I008.lower.01 — printed I:8 · lower
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** قوله خاصة والمراد من اختصاص علم الكلام هو الاختصاص الإضافي على ما صرح به المصنف في شرح المقاصد

**English (corresponding selected portion):** Concerning his expression “specifically”: the particularity intended for the science of kalām is a relative particularity, as the author explicitly states in Sharḥ al-Maqāṣid.

**Limit:** Only the initial marginal argument is transcribed; its continuation and the second lower block are substantial.

## I009.upper.01 — printed I:9 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** (الدينية) المنسوبة إلى دين نبينا محمد صلى الله عليه وعلى آله وسلم ⟦كلام مطبوع متروك هنا⟧ وسواء كانت من الدين في الواقع ككلام أهل الحق أم لا ككلام الفرق المبتدعة الضالة

**English (corresponding selected portion):** “Religious”: ascribed to the religion of our Prophet Muḥammad—may God bless him and his family and grant him peace. [An intervening printed qualification has not been transcribed.] Whether they belong to the religion in reality, as in the discourse of those the writer calls the people of truth, or do not, as in the discourse of groups he calls errant innovators.

**Limit:** Explicit middle omission; the attribution belongs to the historical discourse, not a modern editorial endorsement.

## I009.upper.02 — printed I:9 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** (عن الأدلة اليقينية) أي المكتسب

**English (corresponding selected portion):** “From certain proofs”—that is, [knowledge] acquired [through those proofs].

**Limit:** The prior objection about mistaken assent and its final qualification are not fully transcribed; I:10 continues the argument.

## I009.ruled.01 — printed I:9 · ruled
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** (قوله أي المكتسب) أشار بهذا التفسير رحمه الله إلى أن قوله عن الأدلة متعلق بالعلم باعتبار تضمنه معنى الاكتساب

**English (corresponding selected portion):** Concerning his words “that is, acquired”: by this explanation—may God have mercy upon him—he indicates that “from proofs” is connected with “knowledge,” insofar as it involves the notion of acquisition.

**Limit:** Initial ruled grammatical gloss only; remaining printed clause is open.

## I010.upper.01 — printed I:10 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** لأن الدليل اليقيني ما يفيد اليقين إلا أنهم قالوا المراد بالأدلة اليقينية المعتقدة بزعم المستدل

**English (corresponding selected portion):** For a certain proof is one that produces certainty. Yet they said that what is meant by “certain proofs” is proofs believed [to be certain] in the judgment of the one arguing.

**Limit:** The upper response before and after this sentence is not fully transcribed; “believed” is not to be mistaken for objective certitude.

## I011.upper.01 — printed I:11 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** علم الصحابة وإن لم يسم في ذلك الزمان بالكلام كما أن علمهم بالعمليات فقه وإن لم يسم به

**English (corresponding selected portion):** [The doctrinal] knowledge of the Companions [was present] even if it was not called kalām in that age, just as their knowledge of practical matters was jurisprudence even if it was not called by that name.

**Limit:** A sentence continuing from I:10; initial words depend on the preceding page. Note بالعمليات, not بالعقائد, in the second comparison.

## I011.upper.02 — printed I:11 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** (وموضوعه المعلوم من حيث يتعلق بذلك)

**English (corresponding selected portion):** “Its subject is the knowable insofar as it bears upon that [doctrinal concern].”

**Limit:** Only the short parenthesized lemma is entered; the following explanation continues on I:12.

## I012.upper.01 — printed I:12 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** أي بالعقائد الدينية لأنه يبحث فيه عن أحوال الصانع من القدم والوحدة وغيرهما وعن أحوال الجسم والعرض والحدوث والتركيب من الجواهر الفردة وقبول الفناء وغير ذلك

**English (corresponding selected portion):** That is, in relation to religious doctrines; for it investigates the states or attributes of the Maker, including beginninglessness and oneness, and also questions of body and accident, origination, composition from indivisible particles, susceptibility to annihilation, and other matters.

**Limit:** Selected contiguous opening of I:12; remainder of examples and terminological question still require complete transcription.

## I012.upper.02 — printed I:12 · upper
**Arabic (selected printed portion):** فالمراد بموضوعات هذه المسائل هو المعلوم المتناول للوجود والعدم وقد يقال المعلوم

**English (corresponding selected portion):** Thus what is intended as the subject of these investigations is the knowable, comprising existence and nonexistence. One may also say: the knowable ... [the following qualification has not been entered].

**Limit:** Opening phrase of the second upper argument only; must be joined to complete explanation of the science’s subject and include its ruled/lower glosses.
