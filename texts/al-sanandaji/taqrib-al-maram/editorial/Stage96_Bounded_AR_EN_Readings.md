### `I018.lower.02` — printed I:18 · lower register

**Arabic (source-checked selection):**

> … نُقل من الإمام أنه قال: لا نزاع في إفادة النظر الظن، وإنما النزاع في اليقين الكامل. […] (قوله لأن كون النظر مفيداً) أي لأن الحكم بأن النظر مفيد للعلم. […]

**English (translation of the selected Arabic):**

> … It is reported from the Imām that he said: “There is no dispute over inquiry yielding probable judgment; the dispute concerns complete certainty.” […] On his words “because inquiry produces [knowledge]”: that is, because of the judgment that inquiry produces knowledge. […]

**Scope:** Closing bounded phrases on the efficacy of inquiry and the dispute about probable judgment versus certitude. The surrounding lemmatic gloss crosses to I:19.

**Untranslated and unresolved:** The initial words of the printed lower block address intellectual movements, not this record; only the closing lemmatic passage is entered. The preceding argument and following continuation on I:19 are not double-entered.

### `I020.lower.01` — printed I:20 · lower register

**Arabic (source-checked selection):**

> … وقد يختار أن الحكم بكون النظر مفيداً نظري يثبت بنظر مخصوص من غير لزوم لدور أو تناقض، بأن يقال: المركب بقولنا «العالم متغير، وكل متغير حادث» نظر، كما هو معلوم، ثم إنه يفيد العلم بأن العالم حادث ضرورة. […]

**English (translation of the selected Arabic):**

> … One may instead choose to regard the judgment that inquiry is productive as theoretical, established through one particular inquiry without entailing circularity or contradiction. Thus one may say: the composite argument expressed by “The world undergoes change, and whatever undergoes change is originated” is an instance of inquiry, as is known; and it necessarily yields knowledge that the world is originated. […]

**Scope:** Bounded, contiguous lower-register passage at the beginning of printed I:20, including the particular syllogistic example.

**Untranslated and unresolved:** The qualifications immediately before and after the passage are omitted; the existence of one working instance is not a proof that every arrangement called inquiry succeeds.

### `I020.lower.02` — printed I:20 · lower register

**Arabic (source-checked selection):**

> … فالموقوف هو القضية المهملة التي موضوعها مفهوم النظر، والموقوف عليها هي القضية الشخصية التي موضوعها ذات النظر المخصوص، أي قولنا «العالم متغير» إلخ. […]

**English (translation of the selected Arabic):**

> … The proposition whose establishment depends upon another is the unquantified proposition whose subject is the concept of inquiry; what it depends upon is the singular proposition whose subject is this particular inquiry, that is, our statement “The world undergoes change,” and so forth. […]

**Scope:** Bounded middle-page distinction between al-qaḍiyya al-muhmala (unquantified proposition) and al-qaḍiyya al-shakhṣiyya (singular proposition).

**Untranslated and unresolved:** Subsequent discussion of the order of assent to premises and conclusion is not exhaustively transcribed; “unquantified” is not silently rewritten as “universal.”

### `I020.lower.03` — printed I:20 · lower register

**Arabic (source-checked selection):**

> … وذلك لأن معلومية الحكم بالحدوث على العالم إنما هو من جهة كون المحكوم عليه من أفراد المتغير مثلاً، ومجهوليته من جهة كونه من أفراد الأصغر، أعني العالم، فلا تناقض أصلاً. […] (قوله لا نسلّم أن الحقائق الإلهية إلخ) وسيأتي الكلام على ذلك إن شاء … […]

**English (translation of the selected Arabic):**

> … That the judgment of origination concerning the world is known is due to the subject’s falling under things that change; its being unknown is due to its being considered as an instance of the minor term, namely “the world.” There is therefore no contradiction whatsoever. […] On his words “We do not concede that the divine realities [cannot be conceived],” the discussion of this will come later, God willing … […]

**Scope:** Bounded final lower-register argument about describing the same subject under different terms and the concluding lemma on divine realities.

**Untranslated and unresolved:** Stage95 placed the teacher-authority discussion under I020.lower.03; the Būlāq I:20 lower register instead turns to the divine-realities lemma. The old text is kept as prior overview only, not displayed as translation. The final source line is truncated, and the bracketed “cannot be conceived” is context from the upper lemma rather than independent transcription of these final words.

### `I021.lower.01` — printed I:21 · lower register

**Arabic (source-checked selection):**

> (قوله لا يفيد إلا بعد العلم بصدقه إلخ) … قد يمنع ذلك بأنّا لا نجعل المعلم مستقلاً بإفادة المعرفة … بل نجعل … النظر … مقروناً بإرشاد من المعلم … ودفع الشبهة. […]

**English (translation of the selected Arabic):**

> On his words “It is of no benefit until his truthfulness is known,” and so forth: […] This may be denied, for we do not make the teacher independently responsible for imparting knowledge […] rather, we make […] inquiry […] accompanied by the teacher’s guidance […] and the dispelling of doubt. […]

**Scope:** Source-checked opening lower-page objection: teaching assists inquiry rather than independently generating knowledge by sheer authority.

**Untranslated and unresolved:** Intervening clauses explain the terms of guidance and the limitations of unaided minds; these are not silently reconstructed. The remaining lower commentary on instruction is not completely translated.

## Corrections to earlier working summaries

1. The former I:20 lower.03 overview placed the teacher-authority argument at the end of printed I:20. The actual I:20 lower register concludes by treating the subject under different logical respects, and then refers to a lemma on the divine realities. The teacher problem is taken up explicitly on printed I:21. The former summary and title are retained under `stage96_prior_working_overview` and `stage96_prior_working_title`; they are not published as literal translated clauses.
2. I:20 uses **القضية المهملة** (an *unquantified proposition*) contrasted with **القضية الشخصية** (a *singular proposition*). Do not silently conflate the former with a fully quantified universal proposition.
3. The p.18 gloss distinguishes probable judgment (*ẓann*) from complete certainty (*al-yaqīn al-kāmil*); the old general account of the circularity objection belongs to a longer argument and is not itself the literal p.18 reading.
4. Page divisions, register attributions and source image SHA-256s are recorded per touched record. Lower commentary authorship remains unassigned unless separately substantiated.
