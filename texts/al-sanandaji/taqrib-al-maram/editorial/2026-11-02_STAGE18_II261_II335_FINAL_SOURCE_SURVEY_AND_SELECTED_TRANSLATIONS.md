# Taqrīb al-Marām — Stage 18: final source section map and selected English
## Original volume II, printed 261–335 (main argument, conclusion, paratext and later imamate continuation)

**Editorial status:** This stage is a **source-navigation and selected-translation pass**, NOT a complete Arabic–English translation of all printed text. Original 632-image scan supplied by the user. Page locators use physical PDF number = printed II page + 295. The main running argument continues to printed II:314, a closing colophon occupies II:315, commendations follow II:316–317, a printed table of contents II:318–320, and substantial **post-index imamate material II:321–335**. This unusual ordering is faithfully reported as printed; it must not silently be represented as one uninterrupted line of Sanandajī running commentary.

### Source sections and original headings (visually checked)

- **II:261:** `فصل الثواب فضل من الله والعقاب عدل منه` — reward as divine favour and punishment as justice, followed by final-recompense questions.
- **II:264:** `فصل لا خلاف بين المسلمين في خلود من دخل الجنة ... وفي خلود الكافر في النار` — permanence of Paradise and Hell, distinct views of grave sinners.
- **II:270:** `فصل اتفقت الأمة على أنه تعالى يعفو ويغفر عن الصغائر` — forgiveness, minor and major sins, mercy, divine pardon and intercession.
- **II:275:** `فصل في التوبة` — repentance and its prerequisites; the subsequent argument includes commanding right and forbidding wrong.
- **II:282:** `فصل في حقيقة الإيمان` — faith as `taṣdīq`, verbal profession, deeds, legal signs and the various theological definitions.
- **II:297:** `فصل في الإسلام` — whether every believer is Muslim and conversely; contrasted semantic and doctrinal definitions.
- **II:301:** `مطلب الكلام على صحة الاستثناء في الإيمان` — questions concerning adding ‘God willing’ to the assertion of faith.
- **II:302:** `فصل الجمهور على صحة إيمان المقلد` — validity of the faith of one who relies on authority without a fully articulated rational demonstration; its practical and eschatological consequences.
- **II:314:** the final substantive discussion within that sequence.
- **II:315:** printed conclusion/colophon, with reference to a printing of **1319 AH**. It is not a new running theological chapter.
- **II:316–317:** endorsements/commendations and a poetic piece, separately attributed in the print; NOT Sanandajī's continuous `sharḥ`.
- **II:318–320:** the original printed **contents pages**. They give the precise chapter map of the preceding material, ending the list at the chapter on the muqallid's faith; they do **not** list the subsequently printed imamate division.
- **II:321:** despite the intervening closing apparatus, the printed numbering resumes with a real source heading `فصل في الإمامة`.
- **II:327:** another original `فصل الإمام الحق بعد رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم`, moving to the sequence of the first caliphs and competing accounts of succession.
- **II:335:** the post-index imamate argument ends with a closing prayer and editorial note. Its place in a critical stemma requires investigation rather than flattening the printed arrangement.

### Genuine structural correction and critical consequences

The old 615-position printed-page map was a correct **page-number index**, but a reader must not assume each numbered leaf is a normal uninterrupted `Taqrīb` source page. Specifically, II:315 closes the earlier treatise; II:316–317 are named encomia; II:318–320 form a table of contents; II:321–335 is a substantive imamate continuation after those leaves. Its physical position must be recorded independently of any eventual theory of authorial composition, supplementary setting or rebinding.

The post-index imamate argument is theological and legal in substance, but it follows the printed closing apparatus; a stemmatic hypothesis should compare other imprints rather than silently relabel it. Do not classify the endorsements as Sanandajī's commentary, or infer that every small lower-register sentence throughout the book is Wasīm's.
