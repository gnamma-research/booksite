# Volume I: all-register completion and release standard

Adopted 10 November 2026. This supersedes completion claims based on page cards, summaries, selected excerpts, or the mere presence of English.

## Scope

The primary witness is the combined image-only `taqribmaram(2).pdf`, 632 physical pages, SHA-256 `4addd660de68794f9d64e062e3b25a2782497b3342eb4580218c3e07e8d12134`. Volume I comprises 280 numbered leaves, including its title leaf; the body-page denominator is 279, I:2–280. Printed I:p corresponds to combined-PDF physical page p+13 (one-based). The printed text remains authoritative over inherited working translations.

Completion includes every visible textual register: embedded matn, principal sharh, long lower hashiya, separately ruled short notes, signatures and relevant paratext. A lower-only page is not an empty or completed principal-text page. A new passage must not acquire an author merely from its position below a rule. Only positively evidenced attributions are displayed as established.

## Distinct progress measures

1. **Working coverage:** some English exists. Historical context only; never the primary completion bar.
2. **All-register first pass:** every visible source passage has a connected English draft, with all registers and cross-page continuations represented. Local uncertain readings may remain explicitly marked. Unreadable or missing source text prevents gap-free closure and is counted separately.
3. **Second source comparison:** a subsequent visual comparison of the Arabic reading and English against the scan has been recorded. This is an editorial proof pass, not independent human certification.
4. **Release-ready base-print text:** the page has passed the source comparison, joins are closed, corrections are integrated, and no unresolved issue that could materially alter the translation remains.
5. **Independent scholarly review:** recorded separately from assistant source checking. Never inferred from a second pass by the same assistant.
6. **Independent-witness collation:** records actual compared editions/manuscripts and variants. A catalogue entry, downloaded duplicate, parallel work, or file-integrity check is not witness collation.

A complete draft may therefore exist without being release-ready. Conversely, the absence of independent scholarly review does not mean that no translation has been produced. Source damage, translation omissions, doubtful readings, proof status, and external review must not collapse into one misleading counter.

## Required records

Each passage has a stable ID, printed page, physical source page, register, attribution status, source reading or an exact image-span locator, English translation, cross-page joins, open reading issues, and proof history. Editorial explanations are separate from translated source text. Hypothetical restorations must never be silently printed as the witness's words.

Inherited drafts are preserved for provenance but clearly superseded where a new source-aligned reading replaces them. A cumulative reading edition must not present an old mistranslation and its correction as equally current alternatives.

## Publication gates

The existing website remains a working edition until the complete base-print translation passes its release gates. Review-branch material may be published only with its incomplete status and precise scope visible. Do not label Volume I complete, definitive, or manuscript-collated merely because all page positions have an entry.

The complete checked translation of the base print is a distinct deliverable from a multi-witness critical edition. Volume II work resumes after the former is completed; inaccessible manuscript leads need not block that base-print deliverable, but unresolved gaps in the base witness must remain explicit.
