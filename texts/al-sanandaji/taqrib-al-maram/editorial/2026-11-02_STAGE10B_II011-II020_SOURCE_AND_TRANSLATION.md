## Volume II:11–20 · indivisible atoms, contact, and attributes of the atom

**Date:** 2 November 2026. **Witness:** the same user-supplied combined two-volume scan. Printed II:11–20 corresponds to physical PDF images 306–315. The photographed pages were inspected. The passages listed here are *preliminary argument-oriented readings*, not translations of the entire Arabic text. The lower register—including Wasīm and other scholars—remains substantially untranscribed and untranslated.

**Important chapter boundary:** volume II, printed p.18 begins **فصل في أحكام الجزء** (the chapter on the properties of the indivisible part). This is a distinct step beyond general body-division arguments: the text asks whether life, knowledge, power and will can belong to a putatively indivisible constituent.

- **Printed page:** II:16; original scan PDF 311.
- **Short Arabic anchor:** «فإن الانفصال».

The upper explanation resists an easy inference from relational multiplicity to parts within a supposedly simple indivisible substance, whereas the lower discussion presses that inference.

- **Printed page:** II:19; original scan PDF 314.

If this situation required the upper atom to have two actual faces corresponding to the two lower atoms, the atomist's indivisibility would be threatened.
