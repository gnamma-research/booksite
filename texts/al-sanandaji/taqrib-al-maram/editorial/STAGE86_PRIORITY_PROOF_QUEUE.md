## Editorial acceptance rule

For every remaining record, first compare the printed facsimile; enter the full Arabic of its *own* register and join through page breaks; translate literally without compressing arguments; distinguish Taftāzānī lemmas, Sanandajī commentary, explicitly attributed Wasīm remarks and anonymous lower or ruled notes; resolve uncertain words in an apparatus; independently proof against the original page image. **No page may be marked complete until all upper, ruled and lower registers on that page pass.**

Only after the first 230 record gaps are corrected should the pass move to full-line audit of all records (including 79–280) and produce the actual definitive single-print critical reading edition. Extensive manuscript collation is optional and not required by the user for this initial edition.
