## 1. `I036.upper.01` — printed I:36, physical PDF 49, upper register

**Arabic — selected printed source text**

> الأمر يتوقف على بداهة أجزائه في نفس الأمر، لكن لا يتوقف العلم ببداهته مطلقا على العلم ببداهة أجزائه، أي كل واحد منها مفصلا. مثلا إذا علم أن هذا التصديق حاصل لمن لا يتصور منه كسب كالصبيان، علم أن كل واحد من أجزائه بديهي. […] كان ههنا مظنة أن يقال: إذا كان الوجود ضروري التصور كان غنيا عن التفسير، فدفع توهمه فقال […]

**English — aligned bounded translation**

> “…in reality”: the self-evidence of [the judgement] depends on the self-evidence of its parts in reality. But knowing that it is self-evident without qualification does not depend upon knowing separately that each of its parts is self-evident. For example, if one knows that this assent occurs in someone from whom acquired reasoning cannot be expected, such as children, one thereby knows that each of its parts is self-evident. […] The point here gives occasion to ask: if existence is necessarily [immediately] conceived, why would it need explanation? He forestalls this impression and says …

**Boundary:** The first word «الأمر» completes the preceding I035 upper expression «في نفس». This record ends before the already Arabic-bearing I036.upper.02 lemma «(والتعريف)». No I035 or I036.upper.02 translation is replaced.

**Unresolved:** A short connective after «بديهي» and the exact transition into the next lemma are not fully established. The source selection is not a whole-page or independently proofed transcription.

## 2. `I038.lower.01` — printed I:38, physical PDF 51, lower register

**Arabic — selected printed source text**

> (قوله بل كان له في كل معنى إلخ) إن كان مشتركا لفظا لا معنى. (قوله ضرورة أن الوجود إما نفس الخصوصيات إلخ) أي على تقدير أن يكون مشتركا لفظيا له معان مخصوصة متغايرة، بخلاف ما إذا كان مشتركا معنويا فإنه حينئذ لا يكون نفس الخصوصيات ولا مختصا بها بل عرضا عاما يصح الجزم به مع التردد فيها. […] حيث يقع تقسيم كل منهما إلى الواجب والممكن، وأيضا […] مع التردد في كونها واجبا أو ممكنا […] أجيب بأن مطلق الماهية والتشخص أيضا مفهوم كلي مشترك معنوي بين الكل فلا نقض.

**English — aligned bounded translation**

> On his words “rather, it would have [a separate meaning] in each case”: [that applies] if the term is shared verbally and not in meaning. On “necessarily, existence is either the particular determinations themselves …”: that is, on the supposition that its commonality is merely verbal, it has mutually different particular senses. But if it is shared in meaning, it is neither identical with those determinations nor confined to them; rather, it is a general attribute that can be affirmed with certainty while one remains undecided about the determinations. […] [The objection invokes the fact] that each [of the concepts under discussion] can be divided into necessary and contingent, and that one may remain undecided whether [an object] is necessary or contingent. […] The answer is that unqualified quiddity and individuation are themselves general concepts shared in meaning across all the cases. Thus this is no counterexample.

**Boundary:** Begins after the previous printed I037.lower discussion ending in «إلى معنى مشترك لا مطلقا»; continues through the lower text ending «فلا نقض», stopping before the next lemma «(قوله ما اتصف بالوجود)».

**Unresolved:** The argument introducing quiddity, individuation and the series of originated things has damaged/compact connectors and is given only as short source anchors; do not silently normalize its syntax or treat the English as exhaustive.

## 3. `I038.lower.02` — printed I:38, physical PDF 51, lower register

**Arabic — selected printed source text**

> (قوله ما اتصف بالوجود) أي بواحد من تلك المعاني بخصوصه. (قوله فأي مفهوم من معانيه) أي فأي معنى مخصوص، إلخ. إن قيل: لو أريد حينئذ من الموجود ما اتصف بالوجود بأحد المعاني […]

**English — aligned bounded translation**

> On “what is qualified by existence”: that is, by one of those senses in particular. On “then which concept among its meanings?”: that is, which particular meaning, and so forth. If it is objected: “If by ‘existent’ one then means what is qualified by existence in any one of its senses …” [the objection continues on the next printed page].

**Boundary:** Starts at the second distinct lemma on the I038 lower register «(قوله ما اتصف بالوجود)» and ends, still mid-objection, with «بأحد المعاني» on I038. Do not supply I039 continuation.

**Unresolved:** The final objection proceeds on I:39 and is not completed here. The pre-existing I039 lower record is Arabic-empty and must be addressed separately.
