### B. One bounded small-print marginal note — author unassigned

**Source:** printed II:222 / physical PDF 517. Begins (قوله واعترض إلخ), and ends before the separate lemma (قوله بوجوب البعثة). The individual gloss writer has NOT been independently identified; do not label it Wasīm merely by position in the lower register.

**Arabic, first-pass source reading (editorial punctuation):**

(قوله واعترض إلخ) منشأ الاعتراض هو اعتبار اجتماع الكتاب والشريعة في الرسول على ما يقتضيه التعبير بالواو الواصلة، فإذا غير إلى التعبير بأو الفاصلة اندفع الاعتراض. وفي قول ثالث اعتبار كون الرسول أخص من النبي أيضا، لكن بأن النبي من أوحي إليه سواء أمر بالتبليغ أو لا، والرسول من أوحي إليه وأمر بالتبليغ. وعدم ورود الاعتراض المذكور على هذا القول أظهر من أن يخفى. لكن ينبغي أن يعلم أن ما اشتهر من أن عدد الأنبياء أكثر من عدد الرسل يجب أن يكون مبنيا على القول بأن الرسول أخص من النبي؛ لأن ذلك ينافي تساويهما كما لا يخفى.

**Close English:**

‘Regarding the objection ...’: The objection arises by assuming that possession of a book and of a revealed law must be combined in a messenger, as is implied by the conjunctive ‘and’ (wa). If that is replaced by the disjunctive ‘or’ (aw), the objection falls away. There is also a third account under which the category ‘messenger’ is narrower than ‘prophet’: a prophet is anyone to whom revelation is given, whether or not commanded to convey it; a messenger is one to whom revelation is given and who is commanded to convey it. It is obvious why the preceding objection does not apply to this account. One must nevertheless recognize that the familiar claim that prophets outnumber messengers must rest on the account that messengers are a narrower class of prophets: it would be incompatible with the two being coextensive.

The principal logical distinction is between a **conjunction** of scripture AND law in a definition of the messenger and a **disjunction** allowing scripture OR abrogation of part of an earlier law. A further criterion makes a prophet anyone receiving revelation, and a messenger one additionally commanded to convey it. The alleged disparity between numbers of prophets and messengers has a different force under each definition.
