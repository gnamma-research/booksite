## Key source joins and doctrinal distinctions
- I:17–23: knowledge, conception/assent, the role of valid inquiry, the challenge of skeptical denials, and the difference between immediate first principles and inferential proofs.
- I:24–26: knowledge after inquiry by **custom** (`ʿāda`), **generation** (`tawlīd`), or productive **necessity**, with a reported fourth view of **rational entailment**. Do not infer divine lack of choice merely from logical entailment.
- I:27 is substantially **lower marginal-only**, with one closing main-text line; it debates the prerequisite of an unconditional duty, cause vs condition, and capacity.
- I:28–32: text, consensus, rational fear of harm, and when inquiry into a messenger's proof can be made binding without presupposing the very law being established. Upper and lower contexts run across page breaks.
- Never assign every lower gloss to Muḥammad Wasīm merely because the printed title advertises his ḥāshiya: other writers are also printed.
