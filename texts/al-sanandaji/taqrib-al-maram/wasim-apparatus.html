Source identification and editorial rule

The original title page reads مع حاشية المحاكمات للشيخ محمد وسيم الكردستاني وحواشي متفرقة لبعض الأفاضل — “with the Ḥāshiyat al-Muḥākamāt of Shaykh Muḥammad Wasīm al-Kurdistānī, and miscellaneous glosses by other distinguished scholars.” A note must be attributed to Wasīm through an explicit source siglum, independently ascribed passage, or corroborating witness; none of these can be replaced by the fact that the text is below the dividing rule.

One externally attributed quotation — reported II:117, exact scan locus unresolved

Record W.II.117.01. The passage is attributed to Wasīm in a published 2022 article citing volume II p.117. On inspecting the uploaded original, the corresponding printed II:117 (physical PDF 412) and adjacent leaves do NOT contain this wording. It remains a secondary-source quotation with its precise location in the uploaded witness UNVERIFIED; full-note boundaries, proofreading and variants are not certified.

ثم أقول لا يبعد أن يقال المراد من التغير المحال عليه الحاصل بذلك الاتصاف تأثره بحدوث ذلك الحادث فيه ولو بإحداث من ذاته، فإنه كما يمتنع تأثره بحدوث ذاته ولو من ذاته كذلك يمتنع تأثره بحدوث الوصف فيه ولو من ذاته، فليتأمل تأملا يليق بالمقام.
Then I say: One may reasonably hold that the change impossible for Him, arising from His being qualified by this [new] attribute, means His being affected by the occurrence of that originated thing within Him, even if its production is ascribed to His own essence. For just as it is impossible that He should be affected by the origination of His own essence, even if ascribed to Himself, so too it is impossible that He should be affected by the origination of an attribute within Him, even if ascribed to Himself. This calls for reflection befitting the question.
