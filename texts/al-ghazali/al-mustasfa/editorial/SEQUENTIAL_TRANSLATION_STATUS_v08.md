# al-Mustaṣfā — Sequential translation, working v0.8

**Coverage:** 16 OpenITI digitized page units (V00P000 and V01P003–017), 105 source-aligned paragraphs. This is a *partial* English translation, **not a translation of the complete treatise**.

## Technical editorial observations

- *ḥadd* is rendered “definition” only where the operation is one of conceptual delimitation; distinguish real definition (*ḥadd ḥaqīqī*), verbal explanation (*ḥadd lafẓī*), and descriptive delimitation (*rasm*).
- *mawjūd* is treated in the argument as an underived simple concept; it is not defined by another genus and difference.
- *aḥwāl* names the theological doctrine of states in the colourhood passage, rather than psychological moods.
- Distinguish conceptual composition (in the intellect) from composition of externally separate entities. Al-Ghazālī denies that the predicables imply multiple separate bodies in blackness.
- Preserve the difference between a merely shared expression (*ism mushtarak*) and synonymy.
- Definitions of the first intelligibles and demonstrations from first premises terminate the regress differently but in analogous ways.
