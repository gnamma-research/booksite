# al-Mustaṣfā · Gnamma Research working edition v0.6

**Coverage:** 111 independent bilingual *excerpt records* including overlaps. Not an unabridged translation and not a chapter-complete translation.

**Arabic:** full OpenITI digital witness in the repository's shared `arabic/full-arabic-pages.json` (381 digital page units). License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, source credited in `docs/SOURCE_AND_LICENSES.md`. It is a navigable transcription, **not a diplomatic or stemmatic critical edition**.

**New in v0.6:** eight newly translated units R15–R22 from OpenITI pages V01P007–008, including legally relevant *khiṭāb*, *sabab*, *ʿilla*, *dalālat al-iqtiḍāʾ*, *maʿqūl al-lafẓ*, *qiyās*, *ḥujjiyya* and the role of *naẓar*. R15 and R19 are complete short source paragraphs; the others are selected excerpts from longer passages.

**Secondary sources:** The translation apparatus uses material found in the user's project Library, including Wael B. Hallaq, *A History of Islamic Legal Theories*, and Felicitas Opwis, “Syllogistic Logic in Islamic Legal Theory,” in *Philosophy and Jurisprudence in the Islamic World*, together with the project's previously developed scholarship registers. Such works inform terminology; they are not treated as Arabic textual witnesses.

**Hammad:** Source comparator only. The 1987 dissertation scans are not published here. Hammad's printed pagination and OpenITI's pagination are distinct. The source comparator contains only selective dossiers; full line-by-line collation remains outstanding.

**Full-English completion:** Outstanding, requiring sequential translation, proofreading and technical review.
