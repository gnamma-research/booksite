## C. Specific textual and interpretive problems

1. **§4.649** — The phrase `وغير متناهية، بمعنى انقطاع اثارهم بالكلية` is semantically problematic against the following assertion of infinitely many possible effects. The first-pass translation follows the argument's direction but flags the exact Arabic. Do not silently insert `عدم` or create a variant without witness support.
2. **§4.650** — The proof for the eternity of the First Intellect and temporal motion assumes a particular analysis of agent, recipient, and conditions of origination. Preserve it as an argument under examination, not an uncontested conclusion.
3. **§4.651** — `أو آخر ان معها لا أصلها` appears corrupt or badly segmented. The source supplies a note on al-Jandī. Compare the original sharḥ and print; no conjectural emendation has been made.
4. **§§4.652–4.655** — The theologians' objection and both replies rely upon distinguishing perpetual causal concurrence from equal causal rank, and a past infinity from a future infinity. Keep each objection separately numbered.
5. **§4.656** — `البرازخ السافلة` in Illuminationist use should not be equated without qualification with the Akbarian imaginal *barzakh*.
6. **§4.660** — Motion–illumination–further-motion sequence addresses a regress/circularity challenge; verify the reference marked `(1)` to the Illuminationist original and the relevant commentary.
7. **§§4.662–4.665** — Fanārī's account of the `التجلي الحبي` uses **heat** as part of a metaphysical and historical cosmological explanation, not modern thermal physics. The descent through Names, spirits, imagination and sensory motion, and the technical pair `الجلاء والاستجلاء`, are maintained.
8. **§4.675** — Gold/ruby and fire analogies are the source's *premodern alchemical model*, not empirical claims endorsed by this edition; the phrase `وردة كالدهان` alludes to Qurʾān 55:37.
9. **§4.677** — `الرصاص والأسرب` needs historical metallurgical/lexical checking; avoid unsupported modern metal assignments.
10. **§4.680** — `دلالة لمية` and `دلالة انية` designate demonstrations from cause and effect respectively. The original `الحقيقة الغيبية الالية الأصلية` is textually delicate and the rendering provisional. Distinguish eternal, unmade receptivity of quiddities from existent receptivity.
11. **§4.682** — The “four or two” highest spheres is a deliberate alternative in the text and belongs with the earlier Qūnawī/Farghānī cosmological discussion. The circular motions are said to be both voluntary and necessitated *under different considerations*.
12. **§§4.683–4.685** — The planet/conjunction discourse is historical astral science and Name-correspondence, not a validated account of cosmic causation. Check the Saturn association `الجواد` in the base print.
13. **§§4.687–4.688** — **Crucial for comparison with Akbarian and post-Avicennan philosophies:** the Perfect Human is an effect/result `بصورته` but an agent/producer `بحقيقته ومعناه ومرتبته`. Do not collapse these different respects or infer independent divinity. Preserve all three `ويحتمل` readings.
14. **§§4.688–4.695** — Al-Ḥallāj's maternal paradox is interpreted by successive ontological meanings of “mother”: Nature and the universal Cloud/Greater Mother of the Book. It is a doctrinal allegory, not a genealogical assertion.
15. **§4.694** — The phrase `كنت كنزا مخفيا` is reported *within the source*. Do not assert independent hadith authenticity without investigation.
16. **§4.696** — Opposites, predications, and Divine transcendence are explicitly qualified by `لكن بالاعتبارات` (“but under differing considerations”). This restriction must survive all future comparative analysis.
17. **§4.697** — The critical phrase `يخلق عند التوجه ... لا بالتوجه` distinguishes God's creating *at* the occurrence of intermediary orientation from the orientation having autonomous creating power.
18. **§§4.698–4.700** — Breathing `نفخ` and calling `دعاء` are differentiated by Qurʾān 5:110 and 2:260. The meaning of `العنصر الأعظم` in §4.700 requires comparison against the quoted work.

### Source and quotation boundaries

- **§4.710:** a critique of ancient astronomers precedes the explicit introduction of **al-Jandī** with `وقال الجندي`.
- **§§4.711–4.719:** fourfold elemental differentiation, seven planetary heavens, each with its own governing divine Name. The speech closes `ثم كلامه` in the witness at §4.719, which may require print verification.
- **§4.720:** **al-Farghānī** is explicitly introduced. His quotation continues through §4.734, where `إلى هنا كلامه` closes it. The account distinguishes the unitive Cloud (`العماء`), the Breath (`النفس الرحماني`), the Great Element (`العنصر الأعظم`), and the joining/separating (`رتق/فتق`) of the heavens and earth.

- **§4.749:** **al-Farghānī** resumes with the gradation of the Breath of the Merciful; second degree begins §4.750 and continues beyond.

### Critical text and interpretation flags

1. **§4.708:** OpenITI reads `فقال بها عليها` concerning mountains. The form is obscure; English tentatively renders fastening or stabilising the mountains, without conjectural Arabic alteration.
2. **§4.709:** `الناشرات`, an angelic group, needs identification from the Arabic witnesses. Qāf, the earth-encircling serpent, dark ocean and rock belong to the source's visionary cosmography and should not be represented as verified geography.
3. **§4.710:** distinguish quoted words from Fanārī's framing and al-Jandī. The reported cosmology and criticism of the ancients should not be converted into modern science or attributed to a single writer without source comparison.
4. **§§4.719–4.720:** witness's `ثم كلامه` may be `تم كلامه`; check print. Al-Farghānī explicitly begins §4.720.
5. **§§4.723–4.725:** `الأئمة السبعة` refers in context to seven leading Names/principles, not a lineage of human imams. `طوعا أو كرها` is explained from different ontological considerations of voluntary reception and necessary subjection.
6. **§§4.732–4.733:** **critical parsing trap**: the Arabic marker `733 - 4` is embedded after Qurʾān 79:30 on the same physical line and continues after a `~~` line continuation. A heading-only parser omits these sections. R14e explicitly records two separate bilingual units.
7. **§§4.734–4.737:** distinguish time, as measured celestial motion, from the dispositions of the bodies measured; establish the categorical shift to `الأصل السادس عشر`. In §4.737 the Shaykh rejects **both independent efficacy of causes and God acting in partnership with them**, describing customary causes (`أسباب عادية`). Verify the anti-astronomer polemic in §4.736 independently.
8. **§§4.738–4.748:** compare the cited `عقلة المستوفز` passage; distinguish inner states, transmitted sayings, cosmological models and historical commentary. The eschatological hostile quotation about Jews in §4.747 is a historically cited report, translated for textual completeness **without endorsement**. Authenticity and variant wordings need separate hadith criticism.
9. **§4.748:** the chain minerals → truffle → date palm → animals → ape → human is a medieval graded hierarchy, **not by itself a Darwinian theory of biological evolution**.
10. **§§4.749–4.750:** keep `الأحدية` and `الواحدية` distinct within the stated first degree of `النفس الرحماني`; the second degree and its infinity of detail are still unfolding at §4.751.

### Major doctrinal and source divisions

- **§§4.751–4.754:** al-Farghānī's contrasting Presences of Being/Necessity and Knowledge/Possibility; the human comprehensive isthmus; divine 'hands' and 'fingers' understood in the text through graded relations. Preserve «كلتا يديه يمينا» and its qualifying considerations; do **not** convert divine hand-language to bodily anatomy.
- **§§4.755–4.759:** mineral, vegetal and animal degrees as generated kingdoms; standard medieval four-element physics, alchemical and angelological claims; al-Farghānī's speech closes «هذا كلامه» §4.759.
- **§§4.760–4.762:** Fanārī resumes («ثم نقول») and quotes al-Farghānī anew; human equilibrium as the sensible form of the two isthmuses; physical temperament, intelligible comprehensive rank, and divine Spirit are distinct.
- **§§4.763–4.775:** premises for Adam's vicegerency, four conditions of divine representation, and an **eighteen-item list in §4.771** ascribing latent blameworthy traits to the angels' reactions in Qurʾān 2:30. This is a distinctive and potentially controversial exegetical argument, **not** neutral Sunni theological consensus about angelic impeccability; source verification and commentary required. «تعلق أو تخلق» §4.768 remains two technical modes, not synonyms.
- **§§4.776–4.778:** the angels' acquisition of knowledge through Adam, Iblīs' arguments, and explicit close «هذا كله مقتبس من كلام الفرغاني» at §4.778. The author indicates his account is drawn from al-Farghānī; whether verbatim must be checked.
- **§§4.779–4.786:** the creative Command descends without spatial translation, through degrees of entrustment; explicit distinction between chronological **order of creation** and hierarchical **order of descent** in §§4.784–4.785. The Perfect Human completes a circle of ascent in life; after death another universal return is described.
- **§§4.787–4.790:** long commentary quotations distinguishing **miʿrāj al-tarkīb** (compositional descent) and **miʿrāj al-taḥlīl** (analytic ascent). Especially §4.789–4.790: one irreducible relation of attachment/recognition remains even when the knower no longer witnesses it; «باقية في نفس الأمر». The contemplative direction avoids **both** particular acts of negation and affirmation; this is not a thesis of creaturely essence becoming God. **Second Chapter** heading occurs after «تم كلامه» in §4.790.
- **§§4.791–4.800:** images of the realities and degrees; ontological distinction between Being absolutely considered and Being in relation or conjunction. §4.800 gives **two grammatical parses** (rafʿ and jarr) of «وحكمه», an important example of conceptual perspective and the need for Arabic alignment.
- **§§4.801–4.810:** active/spoken Book, Pen, Tablet, revelation and differentiated forms; world as `kitāb mubīn` in one consideration and Qurʾān as the unique Muḥammadan comprehensive Book in another. §4.803 reads «فعلى وقولي» (probably intended `فعلي وقولي`), but Arabic must remain unchanged pending collation.
- **§§4.811–4.820:** two considerations of Nature, four qualities and four leading Names, four angelic manifestations, and differentiation of Throne/Pen/Name al-Raḥmān from Footstool/Universal Soul/Name al-Raḥīm. §4.817 reads **«ألف ومائة واحد وعشرون»** observed stellar universals, provisionally **1121**, which demands comparison with printed source and astronomical catalogues.

### Required textual checks before release

1. The reports `خلق آدم على صورته` / `على صورة الرحمن` in §4.762 differ in isnād/wording and must be independently assessed, not treated as equally authenticated. The academic distinction between image/representation and bodily anthropomorphism remains essential.
2. §4.771's **exactly eighteen** alleged angelic deficiencies and §4.778's argument that Iblīs induced the reaction must be collated with al-Farghānī and compared to Sunnī kalām, not presented as established doctrine without attribution.
3. §4.773 contrasts `نفخت فيه من روحي` (Qurʾān 15:29) and `فنفخنا فيها من روحنا` (21:91); preserve both forms.
4. §4.774 distinguishes real ontological Names from **names of Names**, including verbal and numerical forms; the first vs second determination issue must not be inverted.
5. §4.787's Saʿd ibn Muʿādh report, and §4.788's womb/insufflation report, need source-specific checking rather than general apologetic paraphrase.
6. §4.790 `توجه هيولاني الوصف` means an orientation analogised to pure receptivity, **not** material substratum in the Divine Essence; «نسبة» remains though it may escape witnessing.
7. §4.796's `فك الفص المحمدي` should be identified precisely in Qūnawī's writings. The end of Muḥammadan knowing is **not** equated simply with the First Determination; preserve the exception for Muḥammad and perfected inheritors.

9. §4.803 textual `فعلى وقولي`, §4.807 doubled rhetorical examples, and §4.813 shifting anaphora/possible errors all require printed witness comparison.
10. §4.817's number 1121, `الكلمتين`, §4.818's five 'marriages' with only three here enumerated, and §4.820's basmala/Fātiḥa interpretive distinction need comparison against earlier commentary and witnesses.
