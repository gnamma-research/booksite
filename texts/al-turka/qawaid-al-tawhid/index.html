Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd

قواعد التوحيد

Abū Ḥāmid Turka’s original treatise and a separately attributed translation of Ṣāʾin al-Dīn Ibn Turka’s Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid. The commentary now opens at its authorial introduction, with Joseph E. B. Lumbard’s selections used as a checked comparator.

The introduction, main commentary on QT-001–QT-019 (pp.48–95), and closing discussion are translated from the available Arabic. The commentary on QT-020–QT-060 remains in progress. The commentary reader distinguishes the Nekounam and Āshtiyānī editions and their different pagination. QT-059’s reported omission remains noted and deferred.
