Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd

قواعد التوحيد

The original treatise of Abū Ḥāmid Turka al-Iṣfahānī, reconstructed from the authorial passages quoted in his descendant Ṣāʾin al-Dīn Ibn Turka's Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid. English translation and textual notes appear beside the Arabic.

This is not yet a complete translation or a critical edition based on manuscript collation.

Editorial status

Current stage: opening ontological arguments only. Twenty-four matn passages occurring on Āshtiyānī's printed pages 48–109 have been separated and translated. The ancestor's own prologue begins at p. 48, not at the beginning of Ibn Turka's commentary. The passages are consecutive as authorial lemmata, but separated by Ibn Turka's intervening commentary in the published volume.

Spelling and punctuation have been lightly standardized for reading. All variants shown are those reported in the published edition; they are not independent manuscript collations. See the editorial status file, witness register, Lumbard translation comparison and plain-text bilingual transcription.

Order of work: establish and translate the complete ancestral matn, collate accessible manuscript witnesses and earlier prints, and only after that undertake a separately identified translation of the sharḥ. No commentary paragraphs are presented as if they belonged to Abū Ḥāmid's treatise.

Source and page references

Base: Ṣāʾin al-Dīn ʿAlī Ibn Turka, Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid, ed. Sayyid Jalāl al-Dīn Āshtiyānī, Tehran, second printing 1360 SH / 1982. The published text is readable by printed page online; the present transcription is based on the digital representation, not on personal inspection of every photographic leaf.

Independent copies of the grandfather's Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd are listed in a manuscript catalogue. Their readings have not yet been inspected. The source list and comparison plan distinguish genuine witnesses from later explanatory works.
