A second public catalogue describes an [1898 Tehran copy held at the University of Washington](https://kutuphane.osmanlica.com/ar/search?lang%5B0%5D=ara&library_id%5B0%5D=58&page=38). Both are **bibliographic sightings**, not evidence that the actual lithographed pages or the implied manuscript lineages are independent. [Google Play Books lists a free 1898/234-page ebook](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=-Wku6DfHLa8C); access to the disputed page images or a downloadable file has **not** been established in this research environment.

**Avoid false precision:** it is not known on which numbered 1898 lithographic pages the two disputed phrases appear. The modern A edition's pp. 49–52 and pp. 262–264 **cannot** be substituted as 1898 page numbers.

## Standalone original: exact identification visually checked

On printed **p. 521**, bibliography item **301** identifies the standalone original:

- Abū al-Maḥāmid Ṣadr al-Dīn Muḥammad al-Turkah al-Iṣfahānī, ***Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd***, edited by **Majīd Hādīzādeh**, in ***Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid***, **vol. IV, pp. 37–72**.
- **Primary verification:** [al-Khizāna bibliography (2018), p. 521 item 301](https://bibliography.mk.iq/files/0080f66f58b21603.pdf), PDF image **zero-index page 124**. The Arabic scans display `ص ٣٧–٧٢` in right-to-left layout; do **not** interpret the right-to-left order as pages 72–73.
- [Al-Feker's freely offered collection](https://alfeker.net/b/3875/) contains **vols. I, II, III only**, so no volume-IV text has been read from that portal.

The **first priority is obtaining actual photographed pages 37–72** including the editor's critical notes. The claim that those pages necessarily preserve the missing QT-059 wording is still untested.

## Two unresolved passages: exact strings to compare

### SH-002 (grandson’s commentary)

The local A source, modern printed A pp. 49–52, says:

1. `القطع بحقيّة هذه المسألة` — certainty in **the truth** of the doctrine, at the beginning of the opponent's argument;
2. `إذ القطع ببطلان جميع الأحكام العقلية` — immediately preceding quoted clause about the *invalidity* of rational judgements;
3. `القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد` — then the **disputed negative**, in a statement whose inference appears logically inconsistent with (1).

The A and H2014 printed sources preserve the disputed `ببطلان`. One possible palaeographical/rhetorical explanation is **perseveration** of the preceding `ببطلان`, with a conjectural `بحقيّة` in the later clause; **no inspected independent witness establishes it**. The working Arabic and English retain the negative. Record 1898 print or earlier manuscript images **with at least 40 surrounding words**, and determine whether an editor has indicated any variant.

### QT-059 (ancestor’s original)

The A editor’s printed pp. 262–264 contain a **surviving complete paragraph** beginning:

> `وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ وعلوم أصحاب المجاهدة مأخوذة من المبدأ الفيّاض فليس بشيء`

and ending:

> `وأمّا الثاني فلما بيّنّاه في كتبنا المعمولة في صناعة الحكمة`

The A critical apparatus reports missing ancestral material in this vicinity. The exact original extent and locus cannot be inferred from the clean syntax of the surviving excerpt. **SH-059 is later commentary**, not a substitute ancestral folio. The high-priority **Hādīzādeh vol. IV original** may directly answer whether the longer text survives. The 1898 print can supply evidence of words quoted within the descendant's commentary, but it is **not automatically a standalone edition of the ancestor**.

## Acceptance gate for changing the published text

For any new image, log library and shelfmark, original printed page / folio, stable image URL, date, 40 surrounding words, source-layer attribution (ancestor vs descendant), and a SHA-256 of the image. Compare to the preserved A and H2014 strings. Only an **actual inspected alternative reading** warrants a new source-supported variant. Recovered QT-059 words, if any, must be quoted as ancestral *only if the witness truly transmits them as such*. Do not upload third-party scans into the public booksite without reproduction rights.

The edition already has 171 complete translated units. This report improves **witness accessibility**, not translation percentage or manuscript-certified completeness.
