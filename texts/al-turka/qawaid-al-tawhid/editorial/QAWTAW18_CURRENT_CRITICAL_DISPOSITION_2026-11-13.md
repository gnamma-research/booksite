## SH-002 — the problematic polar negative

**Source wording transmitted in both A and the printed H2014 quotation:** `القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد`. Earlier within the same discussion the case is stated as `القطع بحقيّة هذه المسألة`; immediately before the disputed wording stands `إذ القطع ببطلان جميع الأحكام العقلية`. The surrounding explanation says that a unity proposition, on the opponent's account, eliminates ordinary judgments of differentiation and distinction. Thus the inferential direction **supports the commentator discussing certainty in the doctrine's truth**, not certainty that it is false. That is a **reasoned contextual diagnosis**, not a third printed witness.

**Potential mechanism:** anticipation or perseveration of `ببطلان` from the immediately preceding `ببطلان جميع الأحكام العقلية`. `بحقيّة` is an intelligible conjecture but has **no inspected independent early attestation** at the locus. The English reading must preserve the literal received *invalidity* while a separate apparatus explains why the logic points the other way. Do not silently reverse the polarity, drop the negative, cite it as a certified emendation or put a disruptive `[sic]` into continuous reading. Without the early lithograph/manuscript or a genuine variant apparatus the **original lexical reading remains unresolved**, regardless of the persuasive semantic conjecture.

## QT-059 — original extant argument versus reported loss

The surviving original begins `وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ` and ends `وأمّا الثاني فلما بيّنّاه في كتبنا المعمولة في صناعة الحكمة`. Its argument distinguishes the senses as **preparatory conditions** (`معدّات`) in both discursive and contemplative routes from the effusive Principle (`المبدأ الفيّاض`) as the source that actually bestows knowledge. The extant passage and its translation are **not incomplete as printed**. The closing cross-reference to previous philosophical books is **not in itself proof of a textual lacuna**.

An earlier editorial report of additional ancestral material remains **unverified with respect to exact original words, extent and location**. The extant H2014 lemma reproduces the same surviving argument; its surrounding grandson's commentary (SH-059) cannot count as the missing ancestor's words. Correct release label: **“complete translation of the consulted extant printed QT-059; reported additional original text not independently recovered.”** Do not insert a fabricated reconstruction or an artificial placeholder page into the reading text. This is an archival source gap, not a missing English translation of the printed material.

## Source-acquisition correction: Hādīzādeh page range

The actual scanned bibliography in *al-Khizāna* (2018), printed p. 521, item 301, gives Majīd Hādīzādeh's `Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd` in *Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid*, **volume IV, pp. 37–73** (Arabic printed `ص ٣٧–٧٣`). Some former project notes said **37–72**, an inaccurate transcription of the catalogue page range. [Photographed bibliography, PDF p. 125](https://bibliography.mk.iq/files/0080f66f58b21603.pdf#page=125).

This verifies **bibliographic metadata only**, not an image or text of the edited original. The specific Hādīzādeh text pp. 37–73 was not accessible for collation. A digitized catalogue is never to be described as a recovered medieval witness.
