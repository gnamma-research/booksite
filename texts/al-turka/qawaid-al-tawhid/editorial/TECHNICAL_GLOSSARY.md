# Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd — technical lexicon and Avicennian–Akbarian bridge
*Gnamma Research · Matn-only translation apparatus · 2 November 2026*

This glossary describes **what the ancestor actually says in the currently translated original matn**, not what the descendant's commentary or later commentators would say in an expanded system. Each entry gives matn unit(s) and an editorial comparison; it does not purport to establish direct textual borrowing from a particular philosopher. Distinguish words in the **matn** from subsequent interpretive clarification in the **sharḥ**.

## Ontology and logical predication

| Arabic term (matn anchor) | Working English rendering | Inherited philosophical problem | Reconfigured function in Abū Ḥāmid's argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| **الوجود — wujūd** (QT-005, 006, 015, 023, 026) | existence / being | Is it common in meaning? How is being predicated of distinct existents? | One reality is necessary through itself, while differentiated existents are investigated qua determinations. A conceptual common predicate does not by itself prove this ontological conclusion; the author supplies more arguments. |
| **الوجودات الخاصة — wujūdāt khāṣṣa** (QT-005, 008–013, 023) | particular existences | Specific vs general being and instances of a common intelligible nature | Four interpretations of a 'particular existent' are tested at QT-023 to deny an intrinsic plurality of independent existential realities. |
| **الاشتراك المعنوي — ishtirāk maʿnawī** (QT-005, 013, 016, 020) | community of meaning | Shared concept versus merely homonymous use of one word | The starting point for arguing that existence cannot be only a common name for fundamentally unconnected realities. Does **not** mean existence is assembled out of existent things. |
| **الاشتراك اللفظي — ishtirāk lafẓī** (QT-013, 016, 020) | verbal homonymy / equivocal predication | One word with different unrelated meanings | A dilemma for treating the Necessary as an intrinsically different 'specific nature' while still describing it and possibles under being. |
| **التشكيك — tashkīk** (QT-009, 015, 016) | graded/analogical predication | Priority, posteriority, intensity and degrees under shared description | The ancestor **stages the opponent's defence** (QT-009), then challenges its capacity to establish distinct necessary existential nature without begging the question (QT-015). Not an endorsement of graded absolute existence. |
| **المفهوم / الحقيقة — mafhūm / ḥaqīqa** (QT-005, 013, 016, 020) | concept / reality | What intellect conceives versus what obtains objectively | The author repeatedly moves from shared semantic concept to a claimed extramental reality; the validity of that move is a central critical question. |
| **الماهية — māhiyya** (QT-013, 015, 022, 026) | quiddity | Difference between a thing's whatness and actuality | Real quiddities are contrasted with objective being itself; determinate natures qua determinate are interpreted with added relations/qualifications. Do not assume every occurrence of māhiyya is denied reality simpliciter. |
| **الوجود العيني / الكون العيني — wujūd ʿaynī / kawn ʿaynī** (QT-013, 020, 024–026) | objective/extramental existence / being | Existence outside thought versus a mental attribution | Their reality is treated as a unified objective existence; mental being, at QT-024, is said to be the same in essence but different in intellectual consideration. |
| **الموجودية بالفرض / بالعرض — mawjūdiyya bi-l-farḍ / bi-l-ʿaraḍ** (QT-024) | imputed existentness by supposition / derivative existentness | The status of things or relations called 'existent' because they are conceived or occur dependently | **Unresolved printed alternative**. The difference affects the metaphysical thesis; no editorial choice beyond the adopted provisional base should be asserted without witnesses. |

## Modality, individuation, and ontology of relations

| Arabic term (matn anchor) | Working English rendering | Inherited philosophical problem | Reconfigured function in Abū Ḥāmid's argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| **الواجب لذاته — al-wājib li-dhātihi** (QT-006, 007, 011, 015, 021, 023) | necessary through itself | Avicennian necessity vs possible beings | In the ancestor's demonstration intrinsic necessity is predicated of the unrestricted reality of existence itself rather than an additional separate existing species. |
| **الإمكان بالذات / بالغير — al-imkān bi-l-dhāt / bi-l-ghayr** (QT-011, 020, 023) | intrinsic/derivative possibility | Modal necessity and contingency of supposed individuals | If a nature has multiple conceptual individuals and only one is necessary, that necessity appears extrinsic to them and so cannot demonstrate intrinsic necessity of the underlying nature. |
| **لازم / ملزوم — lāzim / malzūm** (QT-008–011, 013, 015, 028) | concomitant / that of which the concomitant follows | Necessary entailment of a property by its underlying term | The opponent proposes a necessary nature which bears common existence as a concomitant. The ancestor challenges this independently necessary specific substrate and reworks the structure of necessity. Do not reverse the terms. |
| **التعيّن — taʿayyun** (QT-012, 016, 017, 023, 027) | determination / individuation | A universal or nature becoming a single thing | For the Necessary, individuation cannot depend on an externally added restricting characteristic. A general intelligible concept of determination need not comprehend the divine essence. |
| **الشخصية — shakhṣiyya** (QT-017) | sheer individuality / particularity | Individuation versus properties of an existing individual | The Necessary's essence-identical determination is **not merely sheer individuality**, which would threaten intellectuality. The author insists on a distinction. |
| **الحالّية / المحلّية — ḥālliyya / maḥalliyya** (QT-014) | inherence / being the locus of inherence | Different roles of accident and substratum | A reductio: allegedly indistinguishable items cannot be assigned opposed roles or necessity vs possibility without a differentiating ground. The grandson's gloss makes 'accident' and 'underlying subject' explicit. |
| **النسب / الإضافات / الاعتبارات — nisab / iḍāfāt / iʿtibārāt** (QT-016, 019, 023, 026–028) | relations / relational connections / considerations | Relations as conceptual predicates or extramental conditions | Plurality in the world is explained through determinations and relational aspects without multiplying absolute existence. The author distinguishes relations from physically opposite independent substances. |
| **الإطلاق / التقييد — iṭlāq / taqyīd** (QT-021, 026, 027) | unrestrictedness / restriction | What is not limited by a specific nature or differentia? | A necessary judgment grounded in unrestricted existence does not automatically introduce an additional limiting property. The Absolute is not one maximally broad genus. |

## Epistemology and names

| Arabic term (matn anchor) | Working English rendering | Inherited philosophical problem | Reconfigured function in Abū Ḥāmid's argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| **الحدس / النظر — ḥads / naẓar** (QT-004, 022, 025) | intellectual intuition / discursive inquiry | Intellectual apprehension of middle terms vs deliberative reasoning | The author claims both intuitive insight and reasoned debate; the grandson explicitly explains ḥads through philosophical psychology. It must not be flattened to undifferentiated mystical 'inspiration'. |
| **عاقلة / معقولة / عقل — ʿāqila / maʿqūla / ʿaql** (QT-018) | knower / intelligible / intellect | Avicennian identity of the Necessary with intelligence and its intelligible object | The ancestor applies all three to the necessary reality while distinguishing comprehension of its essence from knowing it through intelligible relations. |
| **حصول — ḥuṣūl** (QT-028) | presence / obtaining in knowledge | Peripatetic analysis of cognitive presence or representation | The author employs a deliberately philosophic idiom for self-knowledge of the Absolute. The grandson (p. 128) says the term is not intended in the ordinary Peripatetic sense of an acquired, other-dependent representation. |
| **الهوية المطلقة / الوحدة الحقيقية — al-huwiyya al-muṭlaqa / al-waḥda al-ḥaqīqiyya** (QT-027–028) | absolute identity / true unity | What is numerically one? Is unity a further attribute? | Unrestricted Reality is not an individual among peers; positive and negative judgments of it involve discriminated levels of consideration. |
| **النسبة العلمية — al-nisba al-ʿilmiyya** (QT-028) | relation of knowledge | The relation of knower and known without a second independent substance | The first concomitant of Absolute self-identity is self-presence/self-knowledge, through which the author then articulates ordering of further divine attributes. |
| **الموجبية / المفيضية — al-mūjibiyya / al-mufīḍiyya** (QT-028) | causal necessitation / bestowal, effusion | Necessitation of effects and emanative language | The ancestor treats these as consequent predicates of the Absolute rather than externally limiting additions to an independently defined possible nature. |

## What the inherited philosophical vocabulary does **not** settle

The central claim of a single extra-mental reality cannot be derived from shared conceptual predication alone (QT-005); nor is every possibility of gradation refuted by rejecting a circular argument for one specific model (QT-015). A responsible edition must keep distinct: (a) what the ancestor asserts, (b) positions introduced as **فلئن قيل**, (c) what the grandson explains under **أقول**, and (d) Āshtiyānī's and later Ṣadrian evaluations.

**A critical interpretive caution:** Āshtiyānī's marginal note at printed p. 86 says the older author and commentator handle *tashkīk ʿāmmī* but not adequately *tashkīk khāṣṣī*. This is a later philosophical objection in a modern edition, not a quotation from the ancient matn.

### Works that are NOT secretly incorporated as ancestor's matn

When the grandson cites Abū Ḥāmid's **al-Ḥikmah al-Rashīdiyya** or **al-Iʿtimād al-Kabīr**, those passages belong to separate books, even if they clarify *Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*. At p. 128 the grandson explicitly identifies the former as a separate Peripatetic-oriented work: its prose must not be inserted into the original matn.
