# Qawtaw 13 — 1315/1316 AH lithograph identity correction and source-access gate

**Dated:** 12 November 2026. **Scope:** critical-witness bibliography and reproduction strategy. This is a correction to the *identity and dating of catalogued printed sources*, **not** a new reading of the medieval text. No scan of either disputed passage was acquired, and neither crux is adjudicated by this addendum.

## Important correction to the Qawtaw 11–12 handoffs

The previously cited Kyoto/NII record **BB28267336** must **not** be represented as explicitly cataloguing a 1315 AH / 1898 lithograph. The accessible [CiNii record BB28267336](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB28267336) expressly gives **1316 [1899]** and **234 pages**, with the *Tamhīd* at **pp. 2–181**. Its stated holding is the Kyoto University Graduate School of Asian and African Area Studies, **accession 200037381740**. The record also lists later bound items, notably Qūnawī's *Miftāḥ al-fuṣūṣ / al-Nuṣūṣ* (pp. 182–216), a unity-of-being treatise (pp. 218–221), and *al-Ithnā ʿasharīyah* (pp. 222–228).

A second, specifically relevant bibliographical source, **Khān Bābā Mushār, *Fihrist-i kitābhā-yi chāpī-yi ʿArabī-yi Īrān*, p. 217**, [accessible transcription](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/13878/348?lang=ar), distinguishes descriptions under **two printed dates**:

| Mushār's date | Description in the catalogue | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| **1315 AH** | Tehran lithograph, **234 pp.**, Akbar Ṭālaqānī's calligraphy, undertaken by Shaykh Aḥmad Shīrāzī; bound with *Miftāḥ mafātīḥ al-fuṣūṣ*, a *Risālah fī waḥdat al-wujūd*, *Ithnā ʿasharīyah*, and *Iṣṭilāḥāt al-ʿurafāʾ*. | Its precise SH-002/QT-059 wording, or that Kyoto's year is definitively erroneous. |
| **1316 AH** | Tehran lithograph, **231 pp.**, catalogued with *al-Nuṣūṣ fī taḥqīq ṭawr al-makhṣūṣ* and *ʿAyniyyat al-wujūd* in one volume. | That the Kyoto 1316/234pp object is this same bibliographic state. |

**Unresolved inconsistency:** Kyoto labels its **234-page composite 1316**, but the *contents and extent described in its catalogue* more closely resemble Mushār's **1315/234-page composite** than Mushār's **1316/231-page composite**. This can reflect imprecise cataloguing, a reprint, re-bound copies, or other factors; none is verified without title/colophon photographs. The proper conclusion is **conflicting catalogue attribution, not the discovery of a critically independent third witness**. These two bibliographic descriptions cannot safely be normalized into a single 1315 or 1316 imprint.

Separately, the Turkish manuscripts/prints catalogue [YEK holding](https://portal.yek.gov.tr/works/search/full/15130?lang_id=en) identifies **Millet Kütüphanesi, Ali Emiri Arabi 01405/1, record 148198**, as a printed **1315 AH / 1898** *Tamhīd* holding (pp. 1–181 for the work). Google Play Books [advertises an 1898, 234-page free ebook](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=-Wku6DfHLa8C). Neither record supplies a folio that has been inspected here. The different pp. 1–181 versus pp. 2–181 are catalogued **extent conventions**, not proof of different textual content.

The independently edited **ancestor's original matn**, Majīd Hādīzādeh's *Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd* in *Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid*, **vol. IV, pp. 37–72**, remains the highest-value independent QT-059 target. The citation was checked against the [scan of *al-Khizāna* bibliography](https://bibliography.mk.iq/files/0080f66f58b21603.pdf), printed p. 521, item 301 (PDF zero-index page 124); its *actual edited volume-IV pages remain unseen*. Do not mistake a reference to those pages for a collation of their contents.

## Additional accessible *modern* edition: Nekounam

Muḥammad Riḍā Nekounam's edition, *al-Tamhīd fī Sharḥ Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd* (Tehran, **1393 Sh./2014**, 280 pp.), has a [publicly readable introduction/contents page](https://ayatollahnekounam.com/2018/07/16/التمهید-فی-شرح-قواعد-التوحید-الطلیعه-و/). The table of contents places the physiological-perception argument around **p. 49** and late discussion of study and purification around **pp. 257–270 in Nekounam's own pagination**. These are **not** the pp. 49 or 262–264 of the A base edition. The inspected webpage does **not** show the disputed SH-002 negative or the ancestral QT-059 paragraph. It is an acquisition aid and editorial-context witness, **not yet a third variant**. An edited reading matters only when a relevant page or its variant apparatus is actually inspected and its antecedents evaluated.

## Textual questions still open

**SH-002.** In A and the compared H2014 text, the later phrase is `القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد`. Earlier in the same objection: `القطع بحقيّة هذه المسألة`; immediately before the disputed phrase: `إذ القطع ببطلان جميع الأحكام العقلية`. Attraction or perseveration from the preceding `ببطلان` is conceivable, and `بحقيّة` is an editorial conjecture, **not a witnessed historical variant**. Keep both source Arabic layers and the literal English untouched.

**QT-059.** The ancestor's transmitted paragraph beginning `وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ` and ending `وأمّا الثاني فلما بيّنّاه في كتبنا المعمولة في صناعة الحكمة` remains translated as transmitted. The extent and locus of the reported older lacuna have **not** been established. The grandson's SH-059 explanatory material cannot be pasted into the ancestor as though recovered.

## Exact acquisition tasks

**Kyoto / NII, first:** obtain images of the beginning, imprint, **colophon**, all bibliographic dating marks, and the relevant first and late *Tamhīd* leaves from **NCID BB28267336, accession 200037381740**. Verify whether the *copy itself* says 1315 or 1316, whether the 234-page contents match Mushār's 1315 description, and locate `القطع بحقّية هذه المسألة`, `القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد`, and `وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ`. A catalogue date or OCR search is insufficient.

**Millet Library, second:** obtain the corresponding title/colophon/target pages for **Ali Emiri Arabi 01405/1 (record 148198)** and compare the **printing identity** with Kyoto before counting them as distinct textual evidence.

**Original and older manuscript, third:** obtain **Hādīzādeh vol. IV pp. 37–72 plus variant apparatus**, and **British Library Bijapur 128/2 (869–871 AH) relevant folios**, if accessible. The latter is a **commentary manuscript**; any ancestor text embedded within it must be attributed as quotation.

**Modern edition, fourth:** acquire the precise printed pages and editorial notes for **Nekounam p. 49 onward and p. 257 onward**, without conflating pagination with A or H2014.

A reusable archive/reproduction enquiry for Kyoto:

Please state folio/page numbers, permissions, and any reproduction charges. Images will be used for scholarly collation, not publicly redistributed without rights.

## Source grades and acceptance gate

**Verified catalogue text:** CiNii's 1316 [1899] date and 234pp; Mushār's contrasting 1315/1316 entries; Nekounam's introductory table of contents. **Verified *book-reference image* only:** the Hādīzādeh volume-IV citation in scanned bibliography. **Not verified by original-image inspection:** all actual relevant lithographic pages, variant readings, original Hādīzādeh pp. 37–72, Bijapur manuscript folios, Nekounam SH-002/QT-059 words, and reproduction rights.

For any new page, retain shelfmark, stable catalogue URL, source-page numbering, photographic image hash, at least 40 words of context, identification as ancestor vs descendant vs editor, and the provenance of each putative variant. Do not apply conjectural textual repairs automatically.
