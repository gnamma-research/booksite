**Unresolved QT-025 reading:** source p. 112 has **تكون طبيعته عن تلك الأكوان**; commentary p. 114 explains the conclusion with **عين طبيعتها**, suggesting a likely transcription/print problem. ## Bibliographical identification and caution
The bibliographer Āghā Buzurg al-Ṭihrānī, *al-Dharīʿa*, s.v. *Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*, identifies the ancestral author as Afḍal al-Dīn Abū Ḥāmid Muḥammad al-Iṣfahānī, descended from Ḥabīb Allāh and known as Turka, and notes that the later *Tamhīd* contains the original matn:
https://download.almohsinlibrary.com/OBooks/OB1677.pdf

**Do not confuse this text with** Abū al-Muʿīn al-Nasafī's separate *al-Tamhīd li-Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*, a Māturīdī kalām book, or its later commentaries. Similar titles are not sufficient evidence of identical authorship.

## Commentary's interpretive guidance (not copied into matn)
- *Sharḥ*, p. 48: the opening doxology points to unity of existence.
- *Sharḥ*, p. 49: the author tentatively construes two opposing classes as mutakallimūn and Peripatetic philosophers.
- *Sharḥ*, p. 54: ḥads as a counterpart to discursive fikr in speculative epistemology.
- *Sharḥ*, pp. 55–59: semantic commonality framed within an ontology of existence different from Peripatetic metaphysics.
- *Sharḥ*, p. 60: syllogistic reconstruction of the necessity proof, with additional independent proofs by the commentator.

When the complete matn is established, begin a **separate** sharḥ register (commentator's text, manuscripts, glosses, and English translation) and keep both layers aligned by QT identifiers.
