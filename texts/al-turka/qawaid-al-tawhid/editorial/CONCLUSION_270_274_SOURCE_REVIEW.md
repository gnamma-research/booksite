# Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid — closing commentary: direct source review (A pp. 270–274)

**Reviewed:** 7 November 2026. **Object:** SH-061-C, -D, -E, -F, -A, -B, in that chronological source order. **Evidence:** the live text of the 1982 Āshtiyānī print transcription, not independent manuscript photographs. This is an editorial comparison, not a new manuscript collation or a diplomatic edition.

## Textual issues requiring apparatus, not guesswork

1. **SH-061-C:** A p.271 reports **بلطيف تنبيه / بلطف تنبيه**, **يتوسّلون / يتوصّلون**, and **يميّزون / يتميّزون**. The present English tracks the base sense while identifying these as printed reports; they are not three collated manuscript branches.
2. **SH-061-D:** The printed base **يتبيّن الطريق حصولها** is grammatically strained and reports **يبيّن طريق**. Treat the English's syntactically coherent rendering as interpretative rather than as proof of a corrected original.
3. **SH-061-E:** The base includes **ما يميّز ... من البعض**; the reported alternatives **يتميّز** and **النقص** materially affect the last clause. Preserve this uncertainty instead of stating a definitive reconstruction.
4. **SH-061-F:** **يصيرى**, **والملوك**, and **الغير المنسّقة المنتظمة** are visibly unstable in the web transcription. The first may reflect **تصير**, and “travelling” in the current translation reads the second contextually as though **السلوك**, but this is a conjectural interpretation, **not a witness reading**. The source must be inspected in print photographs or an independent witness before emendation.
5. **SH-061-A:** **أقفاله / إقباله / أفعاله** is especially consequential: “keys to his locks”, “keys to his advance”, and “keys to his actions” entail different images. The base reading is retained with alternatives.
6. **SH-061-B:** The later editor's **الحمد لله الذي وفقني لتكميل هذه النسخة...** is a new colophon, not continued authorial theology. The dated Persian material is likewise modern editorial text.

## Argument structure — important for translation review

The closing inquiry distinguishes an immediately experienced cognition from the **criterion by which competing cognitive claims are discriminated**. The argument is not that unveiling can simply be replaced by syllogism. Rather, the acquired intellectual **malaka** can help a seeker evaluate what has been apprehended and communicate its truth. **SH-061-A then expressly limits the prior discussion to a seeker without a consummate guide**; when such a guide is available, a station-specific science and measure are supplied according to the person's preparedness. A translation omitting that qualification changes the theory of knowledge materially.
