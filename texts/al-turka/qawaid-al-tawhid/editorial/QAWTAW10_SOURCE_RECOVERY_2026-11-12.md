## QT-061 and SH-061-F: `غير [المتسقة المنتظمة]`

The Arabic in the ancestor and his commentator reads **غير المتسقة المنتظمة**. Earlier editorial passes took these as internally contradictory modifiers. A new terminological comparison establishes that **العلوم المتسقة المنتظمة** is a recognized unit in Arabic logic pedagogy.

- **Al-Ḥillī, *al-Asrār al-khafiyya fī al-ʿulūm al-ʿaqliyya***, discussion of acquired sciences: «العلوم المتّسقة، المنتظمة في الحسابيّات والهندسيّات», contrasted with acquisition that requires logic, e.g. metaphysics. Public electronic transcription: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/14487/49?lang=ar .
- **Quṭb al-Dīn al-Rāzī, *Sharḥ Maṭāliʿ al-Anwār***, discussion of which branches of acquired knowledge need logic: «العلوم المتّسقة المنتظمة» are precisely among those whose acquisition need not invoke another logic. Public electronic transcription: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/15419/16?lang=ar .

The immediate term in Ibn Turka can therefore be read **غير [المتسقة المنتظمة]**: theoretical disciplines *not already coherent and systematically ordered*, for which an instrument of discriminating valid from invalid reasoning is appropriate. This fits his analogy with the proposed discipline distinguishing correct and incorrect experiential apprehensions. Revised English at QT-061 and SH-061-F makes that meaning explicit. **Neither Arabic witness is changed and no independent manuscript variant is alleged.** The syntactic/semantic problem is settled for the reader; the manuscript stemma is not.

## SH-002: do not edit the negative without a witness

Both the retained Āshtiyānī electronic transcription and the H2014 medieval quotation have **«القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد»**. Read literally, that refers to certainty *of the invalidity* of the unity thesis, although earlier in the opponent’s objection the relevant certainty is certainty *in its truth*. The contradiction could be a received scribal error or a compressed dialectical turn. None of the actually inspected sources proves a positive substitute. A conjectural `بحقية` or suppression of `بطلان` is **not** adopted. The revised English preserves **“certainty that the doctrine of unity is invalid”** without an intrusive `[sic]` in continuous reading; the difficulty is described in the scholarly note. Remains a genuine text-critical crux.

## QT-059: surviving text versus missing ancestral wording

**Extant text:** QT-059 opens «وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ وعلوم أصحاب المجاهدة مأخوذة من المبدأ الفيّاض فليس بشيء». Its point is that sensory operations are **preparatory causes** in both speculative and spiritual paths, while intelligible realities are bestowed by the **effusive Principle**, not efficiently manufactured by bodily senses. The present translation of those surviving sentences is complete.

**Reported omission:** the A reading apparatus reports omitted original material in this vicinity. The descendant’s SH-059 is an explanation of the issue, not a transcript of the missing ancestral page. This project has **not independently inspected the supposed lost original page or its archetype**. Do not invent an extent or restored sentence and do not silently import the descendant’s prose into the ancestor.

**Best independent edited original lead:** Majīd Hādīzādeh’s *Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd* in *Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid*, vol. IV, **pp. 37–72**, is independently catalogued in al-Khizāna, 2018, bibliography entry 301, printed p.521: https://bibliography.mk.iq/files/5995f1f6d611e6d0.pdf . The publicly accessible collection listing at https://alfeker.net/b/3875/ explicitly provides only **vols. I, II and III**. Thus the text of vol. IV **was not acquired**, and the bibliography cannot prove that this edition supplies the lost words. Seek a scan of just pp.37–72 (plus edition introduction/apparatus), not another scanned copy of vols.I–III.

**Independent earlier commentary witness:** British Library Oriental/India Office **Bijapur 128/2**, titled *Kitāb al-Tamhīd fī Sharḥ Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*, dated **869–871 AH/1465–1467 CE**. Catalogue: https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003784927 . Its digitised-content field is empty and the catalogue instructs researchers to request the physical item. It is a separate manuscript **of the commentary** but the images have not been read; do not claim that it supplies a free-standing ancestral text.

**Other prospects:** a Tehran parliamentary library miscellany, reported as **MS 8503**, with *Qawāʿid* and marginalia of Mullā ʿAlī Nūrī: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/15416/68?lang=ar . Its codicological content, provenance and availability need confirmation; this secondary reference alone does not show QT-059. An earlier 1315 AH Tehran lithograph of *Tamhīd*, discussed at https://books.rafed.net/view/443/page/434 and catalogued by Kyoto University, may help characterize later editorial transmission, but a title listing is not an independent original-manuscript collation.
