# SH-058 — second-pass translation correction

**7 November 2026 · A, printed pp.260–262 · Arabic witness status: retained electronic transcription, no inspected manuscript images.**

**Issue:** the previous working English rendered **النوم والسهو** as “sleep and wakefulness.” In Arabic, **السهو** denotes inadvertence, distraction, or a lapse of attention, **not** wakefulness. The latter would require a different reading such as **السهر** (staying awake), for which there is presently no established manuscript evidence. The full phrase in the preserved source is **النوم والسهو وما يوجبهما من الجوع والشبع**.

**Corrected English (local context):** “The same applies to sleep and lapses of attention, and to the hunger and satiety that can give rise to them. Excess in any of these dulls and weakens the discursive faculty…”

This follows the electronic Arabic as it stands rather than introducing an unacknowledged emendation. It also preserves the causal relationship of hunger and satiety to the disturbances. The Arabic text is **not changed**.

**Remaining question:** inspect the printed page or independent Arabic witness to determine whether the electronic transcription has confused **السهو**, **السهر**, or another reading. Until that is done, report the issue as a possible source-level problem, not a definite scribal correction. The Arabic source fingerprint is preserved; this is an English translation correction only.
