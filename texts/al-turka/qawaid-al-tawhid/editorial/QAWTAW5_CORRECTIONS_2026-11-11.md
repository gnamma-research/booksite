## 7. SH-002 — A, printed pp. 49–52

## 8. SH-009 — A, printed pp. 68–71

## 9. SH-009 — A, printed pp. 68–71

## 10. SH-010 — A, printed pp. 71–72

**Reason:** Keep الماشي as a derivative predicate applied to subjects, rather than converting it into the act of walking.
