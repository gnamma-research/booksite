# Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid — concluding commentary source audit (A pp.270–274)

**7 November 2026 · six bilingual units, source-graded.** This note verifies the boundaries, principal technical vocabulary and known difficult readings of the six concluding commentary units in the `Tamhīd`. It does **not** certify that their textual forms are manuscript-established, or that the independently transmitted readings are exhaustive.

## Structural distinction indispensable for interpretation

The passage progresses: (1) the prima facie priority of methodological reasoning; (2) the objection from immediate experience; (3) counter-objection from graded receptivity; (4) the need for cultivated discrimination; (5) the distinct case of an accomplished spiritual guide; (6) conclusion. A reading that treats only SH-061-F as the final unqualified position omits SH-061-A and misstates Ibn Turka's conclusion.

## Provenance

Āshtiyānī's printed Arabic is accessed through [Ahlulbayt Library, book 17638](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/17638/470?lang=ar). An independently collated Arabic manuscript for these five pages has **not** been obtained. The source transcriptions include later editorial glosses and variant markers; the working edition removes the glosses but retains documented readings in notes. Source pages 271–274 are not included in the retained main-source JSON snapshot covering pp.48–270, and must not be counted as hash-verified in that earlier corpus QA.

**Result:** six conclusion units are present and broadly follow the source's argument and boundaries; no verified manuscript correction has been made. Their difficult readings remain open in the apparatus.
