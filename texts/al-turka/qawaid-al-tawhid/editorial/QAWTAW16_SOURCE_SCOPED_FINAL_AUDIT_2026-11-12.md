**Newly checked facsimile-type comparison (modern, not an independent old witness):** The **H2014 printed commentary, volume II, pp. 571–572** was inspected at the pages carrying the lemma beginning `وما قيل إن علوم النظار مأخوذة من الحواس` and ending `وأما الثاني فلما بيناه في كتبنا المعمولة في صناعة الحكمة`. It supports the **surviving already-translated QT-059**, not the alleged additional missing ancestral passage. It is not a manuscript and gives no grounds to infer the unprinted extent of a separate ancestral loss. The inherited [Qawtaw7 comparison](../qa/qawtaw7-witness-register.json) already registered the same modern source; this is **visual confirmation of the known working passage**, not a newly discovered variant or proof of a different archetype.

**Further public scan lead identified:** the **2005, 176-page ʿĀṣim al-Kayyālī edited printing** is presented on [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=369qDwAAQBAJ) and a [public page with a Google Drive preview](https://www.aswajabooks.com/2025/08/tamhidul-qowaid-as-shufiyyah.html). The advertised book file and excerpt were **not** readable at the exact SH-002 / QT-059 pages in this environment. It is another *modern editorial print*, not evidence of an independently recovered ancestor folio. Do not use catalogue snippets or OCR of an unrelated book as a collated variant.

**Limit of extraction/OCR:** Existing typeset text in the project's PDFs and modern H2014 comparator has already been transcribed. A new OCR pass over a re-typeset *copy of that same wording* can catch transcription errors, but **cannot create a missing ancestral folio** or establish the stemma. Image inspection of the relevant available H2014 print was more reliable than its malformed PDF text layer. No original independent historical page showing the crux has become accessible.

## 3. Substantive standing of the two high-priority cruxes

**SH-002 — later polar-negative phrase.** A and H2014 transmit `القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد`; the earlier objection says `القطع بحقية هذه المسألة` and its immediately previous wording repeats `ببطلان` regarding rational judgments. A scribal attraction of the latter is possible, but **no independently inspected source establishes `بحقيّة`** at the disputed point. Arabic and literal English are retained; conjecture belongs only in notes.

**QT-059 — ancestral original.** The full surviving quoted argument says that the senses provide **preparatory conditions** for both inferential and contemplative knowledge, while true knowledge is bestowed by the effusive Principle. Its ending refers to other philosophical writings for the second claim. A prior editor reports additional ancestral material omitted from a transmission; exact missing words, extent and locus cannot be established from present evidence. Do not add the grandson's surrounding SH-059 explanation as though it were the missing original.

**SH-058 — an example of a later source-graded resolution:** The A-source transcription reads `النوم والسهو` (sleep and lapses of attention); the modern **H2014 II:560** printed comparison supports `النوم والسهر` (sleep and wakefulness) and `الحركة الانتقالية`. The published preferred English therefore uses *sleep and wakefulness*, while the A-source `السهو` and the source divergence remain explicitly preserved in the apparatus. The earlier automatic SH-058 rewriter, which would reinsert the old inattention choice, has been **retired**; it must not overwrite this printed comparison.

**Additional transparency example:** The opening `الحمد لله رب العالمين` in QT-001 is a **disclosed editorial adoption from a catalogue incipit**, while the A commentary prints `الحمد لوليّه`. This is a documented editorial choice with apparatus, **not a manuscript-established unanimous original**. Other variant treatments must be read with their respective notes.

## 4. Final validation procedure

The independent, executable [Qawtaw16 acceptance script](../qa/qawtaw16_final_acceptance.py) enforces:
1. Exact 61 + 110 canonical unit inventory, no duplicate IDs, no missing source or English, no placeholder blocks and no broken original–commentary links.
2. **Every one of 171 Arabic and English source hashes** agrees with the generated clause-ledger entries; all **5,520** Arabic punctuation segments and exact status histograms agree; old flagged entries are not erased to make results look cleaner.
3. SH-002 negative is **not** conjecturally replaced; QT-059 is extant with an **explicit** source note; SH-058 prior correction is retained.
4. Arabic technical dictionary entries, sourced reference cards, historical witnesses and the **12/1/1 selected** decision register agree with canonical files.
5. Five PDF bytes, SHA-256, sizes, actual page counts, searchable text, and internal links of study/reference profiles agree with the regenerated manifest.
6. The Qawtaw5 review's accidentally inserted Hindi word in one QT-002 note is corrected to the English **illness**, and the complete ledger is regenerated rather than leaving contradictory derivative records.
7. Real-browser checks (when the Qawtaw16 workflow succeeds) exercise bilingual/layer switching, search, focus/continuous reading, notes/bookmarks, source cards, navigation, mobile widths and absence of JavaScript errors.

A successful CI report is to be preserved at [Qawtaw16 acceptance JSON](../qa/qawtaw16-final-acceptance.json). Earlier reports are immutable snapshots, not ground-truth file hashes for a later release. The older Qawtaw11 five-PDF hashes can differ from current binaries owing to later automated publication; the **actual current manifest must match the actual current files**. The regenerated manifest is dated Qawtaw16 to remove misleading Qawtaw5 version stamping.

## 5. Release disposition and future boundary

**Release as a complete translation of the consulted extant electronic source and a qualified scholarly study/reference edition.** Nothing else needs translating for that explicitly bounded source sequence. Readers should have uncluttered continuous text; all editorial issue records, reviews, history and variant arguments are in the reference PDF, comparison page and separate scholarly apparatus.

**Do not call it a definitive manuscript-collated critical edition.** That claim would require source images of the independent Hādīzādeh matn and early *Tamhīd* witnesses, resolution of SH-002 and QT-059, provenance assessment of other flagged readings, and independent specialist review. The user has explicitly chosen **not** to dispatch library enquiries; no email or standing acquisition work has been initiated.
