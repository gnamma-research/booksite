## What the new material covers

The opening objections and replies concerning spiritual cognition; the common meaning and reality of existence; five additional arguments for necessity; unrestrictedness and qualifying conditions; the later Peripatetic alternative; graded predication; the objection that the Necessary would exist twice; intrinsic determination; determination by opposition versus determination by comprehensiveness; aspectual and comprehensive knowledge of the divine; and the distinction between non-oppositional otherness and contrariety.

The distinction between speakers is retained. Arguments attributed to an opponent are not presented as Ibn Turka's final position. The medical discussion remains historical source material, not contemporary medical guidance.

## Lumbard comparison

Joseph E. B. Lumbard's published selection was checked where it overlaps **SH-001–006**, corresponding to his pp.468–479. The earlier introductory comparison remains in place. The Arabic controls the translation. In particular, the direction of the effect-to-cause inference, the negative in **ليس مقصورا على اللفظ**, and the positive definition of possibility are preserved. Defective Arabic readings are explicitly flagged and contextual interpretations bracketed where necessary.

SH-007–019 lie beyond the supplied Lumbard selection and are independently translated from A. His modern English paragraphs are not reproduced as this edition's translation.

Hash identity checks establish agreement with the reviewed electronic source, not independent manuscript authenticity. The source remains a printed-edition digital transcription; historical variants and interpretative corrections remain distinguishable.
