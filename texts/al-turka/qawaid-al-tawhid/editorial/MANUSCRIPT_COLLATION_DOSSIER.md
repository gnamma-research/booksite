# Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd — manuscript collation and lacuna-recovery dossier

**Gnamma Research — 3 November 2026 · Research dossier 1 · Evidence-limited**

**Authorial work:** Afḍal al-Dīn / Abū Ḥāmid Muḥammad Turka al-Iṣfahānī, *Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd* (قواعد التوحيد). **Separate later work:** his descendant Ṣāʾin al-Dīn ʿAlī Ibn Turka's *Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid* commentary. Do **not** confuse with Nasafī's *al-Tamhīd li-Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd*, or with juristic *Tamhīd* works.

## Editorial verdict

**The public manuscript catalogues have now been directly verified; the manuscript folios themselves have not been obtained or compared.** Hence no claim is made to have completed collation, to have read the missing page, or to have established a stemma. The present Gnamma edition remains a **61-unit bilingual, lacunose, provisional reading text**. The recorded substantial defect at QT-059 (base printed pp.262–264) remains unresolved.

The significant new discovery is a **separately published edition of the ancestor's original matn**, not merely another printed commentary, edited by **Majīd Hādīzādeh**, *Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid*, **vol. IV, pp. 37–72**. The exact page range is legible in the journal PDF page visually inspected (viewer page 124, journal p.521); digital right-to-left extraction has rendered it confusingly as “72–73” in one result. **This volume's text is not available for direct comparison in the current working environment.**

## II. A catalogue-excerpt collation at the boundaries — evidence grade C (not direct manuscript)

### Incipit: QT-001; Āshtiyānī p.48

**A (digital Āshtiyānī source base; variant preserved in apparatus, not followed in reader's opening):**

> الحمد لوليّه، والصلاة على نبيّه محمد وآله. وبعد، فإن تقرير مسألة التوحيد على النحو الذي ذهب إليه العارفون ...

**F (Fahares/FANKHĀ work-level catalogued incipit, in published Persian orthography):**

> الحمدلله رب العالمین و الصلوة علی نبیه محمد و آله اجمعین فان تقریر مسئلة التوحید علی النحو الذى ذهب اليه العارفون ...

**Differences documented in the excerpts:**

1. **A: الحمد لوليّه** vs **F: الحمد لله رب العالمين**: substantive **different opening**, **not** simple orthography.
2. **A: وآله** vs **F: وآله أجمعين**: an additional qualifying word in catalogue excerpt.
3. **A: وبعد** vs **F: not represented**: the F extract may be abridged/regularized; **no conclusion that the manuscripts omit وبعد**.
4. **F** preserves **فإن تقرير مسألة التوحيد...** as a shared text anchor, independently of its different doxology. The catalogue transcription contains Persian print-orthographic features and should **not** be adopted as a normalized critical Arabic base.

**Additional external bibliographical confirmation of A-style incipit**: Āghā Buzurg al-Ṭihrānī, *al-Dharīʿa*, 17, entry 952, quotes **الحمد لوليه والصلاة على نبيه وآله**. [Digitized bibliography](https://download.almohsinlibrary.com/OBooks/OB1677.pdf). This establishes **two published reports of competing incipits**, not a manuscript stemma.

**Editorial decision (reading edition):** Adopt **«الحمد لله رب العالمين»** as the conventional first phrase at the project's request; leave the remainder of the prologue under A's reading. Retain A's **«الحمد لوليّه»** and other catalogued differences in the critical apparatus. This is a transparent editorial choice, **not** an established stemmatic reading; no assumption about Basmala is warranted. The substantive task remains QT-059 and full independent collation, not the doxology.

### Explicit: QT-061; Āshtiyānī p.270

**A ending:** «وهذا آخر ما أردنا أن نورده في هذه الرسالة، والحمد لله رب العالمين، والصلاة والسلام على نبيّه محمّد المصطفى وآله الطاهرين.»

**F catalogue explicit:** «لا یحتمله هذا المختصر و هذا آخر ما اردنا ان نورده فی هذه الرساله و الحمدلله رب العالمین و الصلوة و السلام علی نبیه محمد المصطفی و آله الطاهرین».

The **shared authorial closing formula** corroborates work identity and the ending of the quoted series in the commentary. The additional F lead-in **لا يحتمله هذا المختصر** appears already in Gnamma QT-061 before the closing words. **This does not establish the completeness of the intervening work** or that all three manuscript explicits have actually been independently read.

## III. Separate original matn printing: Hādīzādeh (new highest-priority comparator)

**P₁** = Abū al-Maḥāmid Ṣadr al-Dīn Muḥammad al-Turka al-Iṣfahānī, **Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd**, ed. **Majīd Hādīzādeh**, within *Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid*, **IV:37–72**, published by Hastī-namā, 1386 Sh/2007 (bibliography's imprint year is occasionally inconsistent; inspect title page).

**Evidence:** Ḥaydar Kāẓim al-Jubūrī, “Dalīl al-nuṣūṣ wa-l-ijāzāt al-muḥaqqaqa fī al-mawsūʿāt wa-l-kutub,” *al-Khizāna* **2/4** (Muḥarram 1440/October 2018), printed **p.521, entry 301**. [Journal PDF](https://bibliography.mk.iq/files/0080f66f58b21603.pdf), PDF viewer **page index 124**. The original page *visually* reads Arabic **ص ٣٧–٧٢**; some digitized text yields **ص ٧٢–٧٣** due to direction/recognition. Also corroborated by the alternate OCR PDF at https://bibliography.mk.iq/files/5995f1f6d611e6d0.pdf; identify actual edition, ISBN, and editorial preface before full use. A book listing identifies the volume as **في الحكمة والكلام**: [Majīd Hādīzādeh book list](https://mahdroo.ir/shop/category/book/author/484480-sl5s).

**Why this matters:** P₁ is a possible **continuous original matn** witness (approximately 36 printed pages), and might disclose QT-059's missing section, absent from the *Tamhīd* quotations. **Its exact underlying manuscripts and editorial method are unknown until the printed volume is read.** No claims about its QT-059 contents, independence from A, or its readings can be made now.

**Status:** bibliographically identified, **pages not obtained or examined**. The publicly located *alfeker.net* PDF set contains only volumes I–III, **not IV**, so it cannot serve for QT-059.

## IV. Main transmission problem: QT-059

**Location:** Āshtiyānī printed pp.262–264, original lemma QT-059. The editor reports omission of material exceeding one printed page in the ancestor's text. **Do not silently insert an estimated clause, the grandson's explanation, or modern Persian editor remarks.**

**Recovered known left boundary** (the extant surviving original begins):

> وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ وعلوم أصحاب المجاهدة مأخوذة من المبدأ الفيّاض فليس بشيء، فإنّ الحواسّ هي المعدّات الأُوَل في الطريقين، ومفيض المعارف والعلوم هو المبدأ الفيّاض بالذات.

**Recovered extant rightmost terminal clause** (do not assert exact lacuna placement without printed-image comparison):

> وأمّا الثاني فلما بيّنّاه في كتبنا المعمولة في صناعة الحكمة.

**Exact procedure before any reconstruction:**
1. Photograph or scan A printed pages **262–264**, with all Persian footnotes, and independently transcribe the editorial lacuna statement *verbatim*. Do not conflate the report with a directly seen dropped manuscript line.
2. Obtain **P₁ IV:37–72** and search for exact anchors **الحواسّ هي المعدّات الأوَل** / **مفيض المعارف والعلوم** / **وأمّا الثاني**. Check whether Hādīzādeh presents continuous original words, explicitly indicates an omission, or reproduces A.
3. For M₁/M₂/M₃ digitizations, locate these anchors in the entire original treatise; compare **at least ten lines before and twenty lines after each boundary**, logging folio/page, column, lines, and photographs. Don't invent a folio number.
4. If at least one independent witness actually supplies missing text, transcribe it diplomatically and collate the complete context against A and P₁ before inserting it between explicit brackets. Translate only after choosing a documented reading and logging variants.
5. If **all** accessible copies share the loss, classify as **unrecoverable lacuna** and state that clearly; reconstructing a plausible paragraph from metaphysics is not a critical text.
