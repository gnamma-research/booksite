# Qawtaw 11 — early printed witness and remaining archetypal cruxes

**Research date:** 12 November 2026. **Scholarly state:** the available bilingual reading edition is complete (61 ancestor units, 110 commentary units, 5,520 original punctuation-derived comparison segments). Two distinct *historical textual* questions remain open; neither is resolved by source catalogues alone. This update does **not** invent or silently amend medieval Arabic.

Google Play Books advertises the same title, author, **1898**, **234 pages**, and a **free ebook**, [item `-Wku6DfHLa8C`](https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=-Wku6DfHLa8C).

**Exact claim:** an earlier, independently datable printed *copy* is now located bibliographically and free access is advertised. **Not yet demonstrated:** its precise SH-002 or QT-059 words, that it descends from a distinct manuscript exemplar, or that the Google Play ebook reproduces the same physical exemplar as Kyoto. ## Outstanding question A — SH-002 negative

**SH-002 A, printed pp. 49–52** is the grandson's commentary on the opponent's physiological objection to contemplative certainty.

Two clauses in the **same extant base**:

1. **Earlier positive:** `و بيانه، ان القطع بحقيّة هذه المسألة يدل ...` — the objection holds that *certainty in the truth* of this unity doctrine indicates disordered cognitive instruments.
2. **Later negative:** `و بيان ذلك ان القطع ببطلان مسألة التوحيد قطع ببطلان جميع اقسام اليقينيّات` — literally *certainty in the invalidity of the unity doctrine entails certainty in the invalidity of every class of certain cognition*.

The immediately preceding quotation is `إذ القطع ببطلان جميع الأحكام العقلية`. **Conjecture, not an attested variant:** if the later text originally had **`القطع بحقيّة مسألة التوحيد`** (“certainty in the *truth* of the unity doctrine”), the objection would become coherent. A copyist's **perseveration or attraction** of `ببطلان` from the immediately preceding phrase is a plausible causal mechanism. It is **not a manuscript reading** and no independent witness inspected here supplies `بحقيّة` at that point.

**Historical distribution:** retained A electronic reading and the medieval quotation incorporated in modern H2014 both have **`ببطلان`** at this point. They are not proof of independent archetypal agreement without their source genealogy. To establish an alternative, inspect the **1898 lithograph's opening SH-002 commentary** and preferably a dated original commentary codex (British Library Bijapur 128/2, 869–871 AH). A third modern edited printing (e.g., Nekounam, 2014, or Ramaḍānī, 2003/2018) is helpful only insofar as its editor's underlying manuscript readings and variant apparatus can be traced. Neither edition's SH-002 passage was retrieved in the present effort.

**Current editorial disposition:** retain actual `ببطلان` in both Arabic layers and its literal English reading. The `بحقيّة` possibility is a **clearly rejected-as-unattested conjectural option** in the apparatus, not printed as received text. The literary argument can be explained without pretending an emendation has been verified.

## Outstanding question B — QT-059 lost ancestor's words

**QT-059's extant opening:**

> `وما قيل إنّ علوم النظّار مأخوذة من الحواسّ وعلوم أصحاب المجاهدة مأخوذة من المبدأ الفيّاض فليس بشيء، فإنّ الحواسّ هي المعدّات الأُوَل في الطريقين، ومفيض المعارف والعلوم هو المبدأ الفيّاض بالذات.`

It argues that senses and embodiment prepare the soul in **both** inferential inquiry and spiritual discipline; the First Principle bestows actual intelligible understanding. The current extant paragraph closes **`وأمّا الثاني فلما بيّنّاه في كتبنا المعمولة في صناعة الحكمة`**. The prior A edition's textual apparatus reports a substantial loss of the ancestor's transmitted text. The surviving prose itself is syntactically complete. Therefore **neither the exact locus nor the extent of that historical omission can be certified merely by present unit boundaries.** The descendant's **SH-059** is an explanatory commentary, not proof of the precise words missing from the ancestor.

**Independent *original-text* lead:** Majīd Hādīzādeh, editor of Abū al-Maḥāmid Ṣadr al-Dīn Turka's `Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd`, printed in [*Nuṣūṣ wa-Rasāʾil min Turāth Iṣfahān al-ʿIlmī al-Khālid*](https://m.imamhussain.org/lib/15), **vol. IV, pp. 37–72**, bibliography item 2327. This volume is titled **`fī al-ḥikma wa-l-kalām`** (“philosophy and theology”) in the [publisher/retailer catalogue](https://mahdroo.ir/shop/category/book/author/484480-sl5s). The accessible [al-Feker collection](https://alfeker.net/b/3875/) holds only **vols. I–III**. The required printed **vol. IV leaves 37–72 were not obtained or read** and cannot be said to preserve the missing text.

**Early embedded-text lead:** the **1898 Tehran lithograph**, p. 2–181 for the *Tamhīd*, may quote parts of the ancestor not present in the later A transcription. Check actual `قال` clauses around the **QT-059 sensory / effusive Principle** discussion; even a longer quotation would be *embedded testimony*, not automatic proof of independent full ancestral original.

**Other manuscript leads:** [British Library, Bijapur 128/2](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003784927), original *Tamhīd* commentary manuscript dated 869–871 AH (no digitized content in available catalogue); [Sepahsālār, Tehran MS 2930/9](https://kias.sakura.ne.jp/ibnarabi/index.php/Sain_al-Din_Turka), a reported commentary copy needing physical-image access; [Tehran Parliamentary MS 8503](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/15416/68?lang=ar) needs a confirmed catalogue record and verification whether it contains the ancestor's **standalone matn** or only a commentary excerpt. Do not confuse any of these with the independently edited original Hādīzādeh source.

**Current editorial disposition:** retain the surviving ancestor wording and mark the textual lacuna as *reported, extent unverified, not reconstructed*. No words may be supplied without showing the witness, edition reference, exact incipit/explicit and comparison with QT-059.

## Witness acceptance and editorial safeguards

For any newly acquired image/PDF: (1) capture exact library shelfmark, scan identifier, date, stable URL, folio/page range and image SHA-256; (2) inspect pixels rather than relying on OCR; (3) copy the *actual* Arabic including unclear readings marked `[غير مقروء]`; (4) identify whether the evidence is ancestor matn, quotation, grandson commentary or **modern editor's commentary**; (5) compare at least 40 surrounding words, not a detached `ببطلان`; (6) only then propose an updated critical reading with its exact provenance.
