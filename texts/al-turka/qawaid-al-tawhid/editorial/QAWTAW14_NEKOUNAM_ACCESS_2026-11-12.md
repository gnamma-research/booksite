## What is actually accessible now: Nekounam's edition

An editor/publisher-hosted [online excerpt of Muḥammad Riḍā Nekounam's 2014 (1393 Sh.) edited *Tamhīd*](https://ayatollahnekounam.com/2018/07/16/التمهید-فی-شرح-قواعد-التوحید-الطلیعه-و/) is openly readable. It gives bibliographic particulars **Tehran: Ṣubḥ-i Fardā, 1393 Sh.; 280 printed pages; ISBN 978-600-7347-97-3**, its table of contents, the editor's introduction, and the **actual beginning of the edited Arabic *Tamhīd*, printed pp. 19–42**. This is a textual excerpt of a later edition, not a photographed independent medieval manuscript.

**Methodological advance:** Nekounam explicitly says in his *Muqaddimat al-Muṣaḥḥiḥ* (printed p. 15) that his edition was prepared by comparison with multiple other copies and repeated teaching in Qum: `بذلت الجهد في تصحيحها ... مع مقابلتها بنسخ أخرى وتدريسها كرارا`. He also says he sought a conceptually sound text, modernized spelling/punctuation and supplied explanatory annotations. These are **the editor's self-description**; no list, date, shelfmark, actual comparison register or relevant variants have yet been independently inspected here. Therefore his occasional wording **must not automatically be ranked as the strongest original manuscript reading**.

## Another possible access route, not an inspected witness

The [mktba.net listing](https://mktba.net/library.php?id=9533) advertises a **6 MB downloadable book** titled *al-Tamhīd fī Sharḥ Qawāʿid al-Tawḥīd* with Nekounam's commentary, dated as uploaded **12 March 2019**. The inspected publicly readable HTML displays a download label but **no usable PDF/document URL** was obtained through this research environment; the PDF's bytes, pages, license, and the actual disputed Arabic **were not fetched or examined**. The entry is evidence of potential access only. Do not publish a speculative direct URL or claim to have collated it.

A separate, already known [online bibliography in *al-Dharīʿa*](https://books.rafed.net/view/443/page/434) explicitly dates a Tehran printed *Tamhīd* to **1315 AH**. This confirms that a 1315 date was historically circulated, but it **does not resolve** Qawtaw13's discrepancy with the Kyoto/NII catalogue reading **1316 AH [1899]** for a 234-page copy. Only the actual imprint/colophon of each object can resolve that issue.

## Result: one important *source-method* improvement, not a restored passage

Nekounam's **explicit claim of comparing several copies** justifies prioritizing retrieval of his **actual target printed pages and relevant critical notes**, but also requiring provenance: since his editorial preface stresses conceptual intelligibility, orthographic modernization and clarification, a coherent reading, if found, could represent editorial intervention rather than an early manuscript variant. It would be wrong to treat the hypothetical `بحقّية` substitution in SH-002 as attested until the passage itself is seen and independently supported.

### Acceptance gate

Do not claim a new SH-002 variant, QT-059 recovered original, new manuscript collation, or finished *critical* edition unless an actual source page or genuine independent Arabic text containing the disputed phrase has been acquired and inspected.
