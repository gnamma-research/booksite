43 of 57 groups now have a proposed resolution at working-reading level. Fourteen remain open in whole or part. These are issue-group counts, not an accuracy score. The earlier register’s 218 punctuation segments remain preserved as the baseline.

No live website or canonical Qawtaw5 text was changed in this pass. This package contains the collations and proposed amendments. A source-supported working reading is not the same as an established manuscript reading.

Evidence and its limits

The comparator is the medieval quotation layer in Kamāl al-Ḥaydarī’s two-volume Sharḥ Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid fī ʿilm al-ʿirfān al-naẓarī, reported by Muḥammad al-Rabīʿī al-Baghdādī (Baghdad: Muʾassasat al-Imām al-Jawād lil-fikr wa-l-thaqāfa, 1436/2014). PDF page images were visually inspected.

The reporter describes consulting earlier commentaries and comparing available copies (I:9–10), but this pass does not establish the independence of its readings from earlier printings. The modern notes and bracketed explanations have not been inserted as medieval authorial text. No independent manuscript has been collated.

Most consequential findings

QT-056: II:539 supplies the missing exception إلّا, yielding “not permissible except for”. SH-058: II:560 supports “wakefulness” and “transitional movement”, rather than the electronic “inattention” and “receptive movement”. SH-040: II:305 reads “prohibition” opposite “command”. SH-048: II:425 reads “unseen intellection” in the objection, distinct from the concrete, differentiated apprehension later described in the reply.

SH-054: II:511 supplies a full missing comparison between apprehension through the higher faculty and through intellect. Several electronic repetitions are absent in print, but SH-049’s repetition remains in II:446 and should not be deleted on analogy.

Integration rule

The proposed excerpt is not automatically a whole-block replacement. Make exact lemma-level changes in a separate working text; preserve the raw electronic source and reported alternatives. Regenerate English alignment, readers and PDFs only after reconciliation.
