# Tajrīd al-iʿtiqād — editorial status

**Status:** Working aligned reader; **not** a critical manuscript-collated edition.

## Textual base and coverage

- 837 stable reading references, representing the 836-unit numbering sequence with §1 divided into §1a and §1b.
- Arabic control text uses Ṭūsī's matn transmitted within al-Ḥillī's *Kashf al-murād* (Ḥasan Ḥasanzāda Āmulī edition). Selected readings have been compared with the standalone al-Jalālī edition; no comprehensive manuscript collation is claimed.
- English is an editorial working translation. Technical terms and grammatical decisions remain subject to review.
- The reader carries **182 extended explanatory notes** and **149 shorter notes**. The notes are **selected and editorially developed, drawing on al-Ḥillī**; they are not a complete translation of al-Ḥillī's *Kashf al-murād*.
- **38** technical glossary entries. Arabic-right/English-left reading and English-only options; downloadable PDF and editable Word editions.

## Integrity and next scholarly gates

1. Collate all matn lemmata and significant variants against the designated edition and manuscript witnesses; distinguish source variants from editorial choices.
2. Verify every extended explanatory note against the exact al-Ḥillī locus and preserve commentator voice, objections and replies.
3. Audit technical terms and doctrinal nuance, especially al-wujūd, al-thubūt, shayʾiyya, wujūb, quwwa, qudra, ḍarūrī, and causal terms.
4. Perform full visual accessibility review, iOS Safari / VoiceOver QA, and download-source checks before public release.
5. Keep a release checklist and preserve an exact versioned text-data and translation pair.
