# Tajrīd al-iʿtiqād — reader editorial status

**Working bilingual scholarly translation and reading apparatus. Not manuscript-collated or certified definitive.**

The reader contains **837 stable editorial reference units** (the numbered sequence §1–836, with the preface divided into §1a and §1b), **182 expanded notes**, **149 shorter bilingual notes**, and **38 glossary entries**, drawn from the existing translation work.

Available reading configurations: Arabic–English together, Arabic only, or English only; single/two-column layout; search; continuous reading; notes, glossary, cross-references, themes, and a browser-local personal Reading Desk. The Download panel provides PDF/DOCX bilingual and English editions. Browser-local annotations are not synchronized or stored on a server.

The explanatory footnotes are **editorial notes informed by al-Ḥillī and other scholarly sources**, not 182 verbatim passages authored by al-Ḥillī. When the text directly quotes al-Ḥillī or another commentator, preserve the source attribution. A complete full-scale *Kashf al-murād* apparatus is an outstanding separate translation programme.

The corresponding research workspace is [The Tajrīd Debates](../../../research/tajrid-comparative/). In particular it contains selections from direct commentaries and parallel Akbarī/Illuminationist primary sources whose source status must not be confused with the *Tajrīd* matn.
