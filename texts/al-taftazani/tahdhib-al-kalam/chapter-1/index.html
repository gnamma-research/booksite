    Tahdhīb al-Kalām · Chapter I

    Introduction and the foundations of knowledge

    الباب الأول في المقدمة

    A consecutively readable English translation of the opening argument, with the corresponding Arabic working text, Sanandajī's explanations, and individually attributed source notes. No missing explanatory steps have been supplied as if they were al-Taftāzānī’s own words.

    Source basis: 1912 Cairo Tahdhīb, chapter I; and Taqrīb al-Marām, volume I, printed pp. 7–33 (2012 reproduced scan, PDF pp. 20–46). English continuity is established in this working translation. Editorial Arabic joins and the 1587 manuscript variants are not yet certified.

    The final distinction between what depends on the authority of transmitted proof and what must be established by independent reason concludes this chapter. Continue to Chapter II in the complete working reader.
