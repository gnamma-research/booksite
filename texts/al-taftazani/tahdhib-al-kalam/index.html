    Tahdhīb al-Kalām

    تهذيب الكلام

    Saʿd al-Dīn al-Taftāzānī

    An explanatory English reading of al-Taftāzānī’s compressed theological text, with Arabic on the right and the English translation on the left. Notes handle definitions, competing doctrines, and selected explanations from Sanandajī; gaps remain visible rather than being silently paraphrased.

    Editorial status: Revision 16, 81 provisional source-ordered units from the earlier translation; full continuous six-chapter collation remains incomplete. The source-graded text is publicly readable as work in progress; it is not presented as a finished or manuscript-critical edition. Selected explanatory notes distinguish Sanandajī's voice from al-Taftāzānī's matn and the additional marginal glosses.

    Citation policy: cite the indicated primary printed pages and independent witnesses, not the working OCR locator as if it were a verified printed page.
