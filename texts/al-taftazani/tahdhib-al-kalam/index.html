    Tahdhīb al-Kalām

    تهذيب الكلام

    Saʿd al-Dīn al-Taftāzānī

    Arabic and English readings of al-Taftāzānī’s theological text, with explanatory footnotes on difficult arguments and a separately switchable translation of al-Sanandajī’s commentary.

    Reading edition: Arabic–English passages and commentary translations. The entire 1912 printing is also accessible in a page-aligned working reader. The definitive textual collation remains in progress.

    Read Chapter I · Read the full 1912 kalām draft by printed page

    Primary witness: Cairo, Maṭbaʿat al-Saʿāda, 1330 AH / 1912. Original printed text, explanatory commentary and later marginalia remain separate. Sources and textual apparatus.
