# Tahdhīb al-Kalām — editorial status
**30 October 2026 · Public scholarly working translation, not a definitive critical edition.**

## Intended finished work
A continuous, independent Arabic–English edition of al-Taftāzānī's six kalām chapters, with Arabic to the right and clean English to the left; selective checked vocalisation; page/folio provenance; and numbered explanatory footnotes. The prose translation should be understandable without expanding al-Taftāzānī's actual wording by silently adding the commentator's ideas. Interpretive expansion belongs in clearly attributed footnotes.

- Links to a separate reader for ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Sanandajī.

## Critical-edition release gate
Every original-text interval must have (a) clear diplomatic Arabic, (b) independently checked source page/folio, (c) recorded variants rather than invented consensus, (d) faithful continuous translation, (e) commentary attribution and relevant explanatory notes, (f) zero unmatched intervals, and (g) proofed Arabic and English book outputs. **This release has not passed this gate.**

## Language principles
Translate key distinctions accurately: ʿilm / taṣdīq, thubūt / wujūd, shayʾ / maʿdūm, imkān / ḥudūth, ʿilla fāʿiliyya / ʿilla muʿidda, ḥikma / gharaḍ, and kasb / ījād. Do not infer an author's endorsement from the doctrines he introduces as reports. Footnotes cite the correct speaker and printed page.

## Revision 19 — primary commentary facsimile audit (30 October 2026)

**The attached source was actually used, page by page.** The two-volume scanned `Taqrīb al-Marām` (2012 reproduction of an earlier print) has its Chapter I on printed vol. I:7–33, physical PDF pp. 20–46. These 27 pages were inspected visually. The following ranges supply primary evidence:

|---|---:|---:|---|
| Definition of kalām and its subject | I:7–13 | 20–26 | Distinguish the short matn lemma from Sanandajī’s explanation; confirm that `al-maʿlūm` encompasses existents and nonexistents |
| Definitions of `ʿilm` | I:14–17 | 27–30 | Retain three distinct proposed definitions; do not conflate `taṣawwur` with firm assent |
| Movement of rational inquiry | I:17–20 | 30–33 | Add a missing unit I.02a, explicitly flagged as editorially rejoined from distributed printed lemmata |

| Custom, generation and necessity | I:24–26 | 37–39 | Directly identify the rival causal accounts and preserve their attribution |
| Obligatory inquiry and transmitted evidence | I:26–31 | 39–44 | Check the scriptural, consensus, and prerequisite arguments and the Muʿtazilī objection |
| Circularity and the reply | I:30–31 | 43–44 | Replace the unfinished I.06 translation with the verified contrast of knowing a duty and the duty itself |
| Proof and epistemic priority | I:32–33 | 45–46 | Tie the proof definition and its rational/transmitted distinction to the scanned commentary |

**Certification boundary:** this is a direct **commentary-image collation** of a complete chapter-span, *not* an independent line-by-line critical collation of the Tahdhīb matn. The opening Arabic reading still needs to be compared with the independent 1587 witness and the 1912 matn itself before a definitive edition can be certified.
