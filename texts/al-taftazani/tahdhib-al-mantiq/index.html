    Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq

    تهذيب المنطق

    Saʿd al-Dīn al-Taftāzānī

    A continuous, bilingual rendering of the logic half of al-Taftāzānī’s Tahdhīb, arranged in nineteen editorial sections. Arabic appears beside the English translation, with scholarly explanatory and textual footnotes.

    Editorial status: Complete continuous working logic translation: nineteen sections. Critically verifying modal inferences and manuscript readings remains necessary. The continuous logic text is readable as work in progress; it is not presented as a finished or manuscript-critical edition. Notes identify technical terminology and source uncertainties; commentary ascriptions remain distinct from al-Taftāzānī’s matn.

    Citation policy: cite the indicated primary printed pages and independent witnesses, not the working OCR locator as if it were a verified printed page. Footnotes identify their own authorship where known. This reader will retain stable passage IDs as critical collation advances. Editorial status & source witnesses.
