# Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq — Editorial record

*Translation coverage:* all nineteen editorial divisions of the short logic matn are represented in sequence and are independently readable. This is not yet a diplomatic or critical edition of the Arabic.

*Arabic source:* the user's 21-page PDF `متن التهذيب في المنطق 2.pdf` (edited by Ḥusayn b. Abī Bakr b. Muḥammad Saʿīd); compared with the earlier five-page matn transcription in the al-Yazdī commentary tradition. The material is a continuous translation of the logic half only, not the separate theological portion.

*English translation:* inherited bilingual Revision 6, checked for all L01–L19 and with the fourth-figure wording at L16 corrected: the Arabic does not infer from two negative premises. Footnotes distinguish textual, logical, and translator observations.

*Release gate for critical status:* Arabic word-by-word proofing against the 1587 al-Azhar manuscript, direct examination of al-Yazdī and other logical commentary on the modal rules, collation of variant readings and exact printed-page references. The Logic book is accordingly labelled a complete **working translation**, not a definitive critical text.

*Companion text:* [Tahdhīb al-Kalām](../tahdhib-al-kalam/), on the same site, remains a separate theological translation.
