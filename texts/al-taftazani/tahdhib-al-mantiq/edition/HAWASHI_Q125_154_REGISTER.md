# Qum pp.125–154 · Later **Ḥawāshī al-ḥāshiya** · 30-position handoff
**Source and translation-register date:** 7 November 2026. **Status: NOT a complete translation, NOT manuscript-collated.**

## Textual boundary (non-negotiable)
- Mullā ʿAbd Allāh al-Yazdī's **principal** `Ḥāshiya` ends with authorial concluding prayer on **Qum printed p.123**, followed by a separate printed completion notice.
- **Q125** contains only `حواشي الحاشية`. **Q126** is blank in the searchable text.
- **Q127–154**: later **glosses upon the gloss**, with textual units often signed **Mīrzā Muḥammad ʿAlī**, **Shaykh ʿAbd al-Raḥīm**, and named older authors quoted or paraphrased (**Jalāl al-Dīn al-Dawwānī**, **al-Taqrīb**, **al-Jurjānī**, etc.). This section is **not al-Yazdī's original speech**; the book-level catalogue’s author field is misleading if used as a per-passage attribution.
- Later references on Qum p.132 to **Muḥammad Ḥusayn al-Ṭabāṭabāʾī** and modern Muḥammad al-Karmī prove that some material in these printed annotations is very late; its presence cannot establish the autograph text of sixteenth-century Yazdī.

## Delivered in this 30-position pass
- `hawashi-q125-139-data.js` and `hawashi-q140-154-data.js`: **30 unique linked Qum printed positions**, **28 substantive pages with a short original Arabic lemma and a selected English rendering / analytical précis**, **one section heading** and **one empty page**.
- `hawashi-q125-154.html`: **separate compact companion reader** with 30 selectable page positions, previous/next navigation, original-source deep links, per-page named-voice register and a conspicuous statement that none of the 28 substantive pages has had its complete contents translated.
- The **principal Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq reader** still has only **Matn** and **Matn + sharḥ**. The later gloss-on-gloss is reached through **Menu → References & personal desk → Later glosses**. This is a separate reading artifact, **not** a third main-text display mode.
- English first-pass selected translations/analytical prose: **3449 words**. Source Arabic for the selected lemma excerpts: **326 words**. These are NOT full-page word totals.
- **0 of 28 substantive pages fully translated**. **0 of 30 positions manuscript-certified**. In other words this is an **audited working page register and annotated translation selection**, not 30 complete translated pages. Do not use completed-page percentages for the original Ḥāshiya from this pass.

## Critical priorities
1. **Separate each numbered note by author**, not merely by printed page. One page can carry several signatures and quotations. Names in `voices` are **candidate/identified voices present on the page**, not evidence that they personally authored every phrase in the translated excerpt. High-resolution printed photographs are necessary to establish the exact start/end of each attributed note.
2. **Collate the original main Ḥāshiya separately** against Qum, Gujarat and Tehran prints and independent manuscripts. Do not use late glosses as independent witnesses to al-Yazdī's own text when they simply quote or reinterpret him.
3. **Resolve late interpolation and source history**, especially the modern al-Mīzān excerpt on Q132, and the `al-Taqrīb` excerpts in several pages. Quotations should have separate authorship and edition provenance.
4. For the first-pass secondary critical translation, proceed from **full Q127** clause-by-clause, keeping the numbered note and its signature together. Replace individual short source lemmas only after every sentence has been transcribed, translated and checked; don't count a page as complete until its full assigned note prose is covered.
5. Study topics include: form of the basmala, nominal praise, the senses of **beginning**, *ḥamd* and voluntary beauty, divine proper-name theory, *hidāya*, literal versus figurative semantic transfer, *risāla* vs *nubuwwa*, *āl* vs *ahl*, truth/correspondence, grammar of explicit/implicit governing terms, and demonstratives referring to physical script versus mental organized discourse.
