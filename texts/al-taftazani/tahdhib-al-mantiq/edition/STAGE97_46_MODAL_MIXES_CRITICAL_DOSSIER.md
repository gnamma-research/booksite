# Tahdhīb Stage 97 — all 46 exceptional fourth-figure modal mixes: complete source-typed register

**2 November 2026.** **Project:** Taftāzānī, *Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq*, Cairo Maṭbaʿat al-Saʿāda 1330/1912, L11/L14–L16. This is an analytical and editorial completion of the **enumeration and source-typed conclusions** for the three exceptional fourth-figure moods, **not** an independent necessity-semantic proof of 46 historical entailments and **not** an independent Taftāzānī manuscript collation.

## 1. Source hierarchy and distinctive evidentiary scope

1. **Taftāzānī's Cairo matn** governs the bilingual Arabic and English and is **unchanged** here. It gives a compressed fourth-figure account in L16, original p.12. It does not list 46 modern rows or impose our sigla.
2. **Al-Kātibī, *al-Risālah al-Shamsiyyah***, §§95–104, in Tony Street's *Najm al-Dīn al-Kātibī’s al-Risālah al-Shamsiyyah* (NYU, 2024), original text pp.64–69, commentary §104 pp.252–254, **directly reports 12 sixth, 22 seventh and 12 eighth modal mixes** and the source's reductions.
3. **Tony Street's Appendix Tables 1, 3, 4**, pp.305–307, give actual typed conversion/figure-II/figure-III results. **Street explicitly says** on p.303 that the mixed-modal inference tables he adopts come from **Ibn Mubārakshāh's Shamsiyya commentary** (manuscript fols. 96a–103a), rather than two problematic versions of al-Taḥtānī's tables. Those cells are *later commentarial interpretation*, not direct Taftāzānī words, and are marked as such.
4. **Özpilavcı–Öztop's edition (2019)** of *Sharḥ al-Mawāḍiʿ al-Mushkila*, Arabic p.89, describes six expanded conditions: especially mode 6's special negative **minor** plus major drawn from the six negative-convertible classes, mode 7's special negative **major**, and mode 8's special negative **minor** plus appropriately typed affirmative major. Authorship of this early commentary is probably al-Kātibī but not certain.
5. **Modern model tests** in Stages 93,94,96,97 prove only explicitly specified **temporal consequences**. They do not model Aristotelian modal necessity, metaphysical possible worlds, actual/potential subject realms, or all original quantity interpretations.

Key editions: Street 2024 PDF <https://dhjhkxawhe8q4.cloudfront.net/library-of-arabic-literature-wp/assets/20240530034338/9781479827510_WEB-1.pdf>; Özpilavcı–Öztop 2019 <https://isamveri.org/pdfdrg/D04349/2019_2/2019_2_OZPILAVCIF_OZTOPA.pdf>.

## 2. Source-derived proposition nomenclature

**Modal CLASS (not present quality)**: `L` necessary (ضرورية مطلقة), `A` perpetual (دائمة مطلقة), `LD1` general conditional (مشروطة عامة), `AD1` general conventional/customary (عرفية عامة), `LD2` special conditional (مشروطة خاصة), `AD2` special conventional/customary (عرفية خاصة). These six have convertible *universal NEGATIVE* counterparts; a currently affirmative premise may belong to one of them.

The *special particular negative* itself has only two qualifying source types, `O(LD2)` or `O(AD2)` (O is negative-particular **quantity/quality**, separate from modal type). Both **convert to `O(AD2)`**, by the source rule in Street Table 1 p.305. The other five actual classes allowed for mood VII are `X1` general absolute (مطلقة عامة), `LT2` temporal (وقتية), `LX2` spread (منتشرة), `X2` non-perpetual existential (وجودية لا دائمة), and `X~L` nonnecessary existential (وجودية لا ضرورية).

**Result symbols:** `A` = perpetual; `AD1` = general customary; `AD2` = special customary; `X2` = nonperpetual existential; `XD2` = **non-perpetual absolute continuing** (الحيْنية المطلقة اللادائمة), NOT `AD2` and NOT an unqualified English “special customary.” Source key: Street Tables pp.303–304.

## 3. Exact source-typed derivations

### Mood VI: AOO, 12 mixes

- Figure-IV major = **universal affirmative** `A(P,M)` (one of six modal classes), minor = **special particular negative** `O(M,S)` (LD2/AD2).
- Simple conversion of minor: `O(M,S; LD2/AD2)` → `O(S,M; AD2)`.
- Reduction: second-figure **Baroco** with `A(P,M)`; take type from Street Table 3 **row AD2, columns six major classes**. The 12 outcomes split into **four perpetual `A`** and **eight general-customary `AD1`**.
- **Quantity/quality remain O** (particular negative). The symbol `A` as a result is the MODAL TYPE “perpetual”; it does not mean affirmative A-quantity/quality. The source's conditions are not vacuously met by an arbitrary simple O(M,S).

| Affirmative major class | Source-typed O conclusion | Either special negative minor |
|---|---|---|
| L | A (perpetual) | LD2 or AD2 |
| A | A (perpetual) | LD2 or AD2 |
| LD1 | AD1 (general customary) | LD2 or AD2 |
| AD1 | AD1 (general customary) | LD2 or AD2 |
| LD2 | AD1 (general customary) | LD2 or AD2 |
| AD2 | AD1 (general customary) | LD2 or AD2 |

### Mood VII: OAO, 22 mixes

- Figure-IV major = **special particular negative** `O(P,M)` (LD2/AD2); minor = **universal affirmative** `A(M,S)` from one of the eleven actual classes.
- Simple conversion of major: `O(P,M; LD2/AD2)` → `O(M,P; AD2)`.
- Reduction: third-figure **Bocardo**, reading Street Table 4 **column AD2, row of the chosen actual minor**. The results divide into **twelve O(XD2)** for the six descriptionally constrained actual minor classes and **ten O(X2)** for the five others. These two result types are not equivalent.

| Affirmative minor class | Source-typed O conclusion | Either special negative major |
|---|---|---|
| L | XD2 (nonperpetual absolute continuing) | LD2 or AD2 |
| A | XD2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| LD1 | XD2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| AD1 | XD2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| LD2 | XD2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| AD2 | XD2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| X1 | X2 (nonperpetual existential) | LD2 or AD2 |
| LT2 | X2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| LX2 | X2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| X2 | X2 | LD2 or AD2 |
| X~L | X2 | LD2 or AD2 |

### Mood VIII: IEO, 12 mixes

- Figure-IV **major I(P,M)**, one of six convertible-negative modal types; **minor E(M,S)**, one of two special negatives. This negative is **universal**; do not repeat Stage93's deliberately weaker particular-only experiment.
- Reorder as first figure: the particular I-major becomes the first-figure minor and E-special becomes its major. The result is a *special negative particular* `O(P,S; LD2/AD2)` under the corresponding descriptional/modal restrictions.
- Special-negative conversion gives `O(S,P; AD2)` **for all 12 typed combinations**. Contrast this conclusion with mode VI's `A`/`AD1` and VII's `XD2`/`X2`.
- Stage96 2-individual/2-time shadow: **6,561** profiles; **128** complete-premise profiles, zero countermodels; weakening major's subject-description connection, nonpermanence, or E's universality produces countermodels (**112**, **581**, **240**, respectively). This validates that *temporal model only*, not all necessity claims.
