# Tahdhīb Stage 103 — independent witness candidates and the three textual families
**9 November 2026 · critical witness census (catalogue records verified, manuscripts not yet image-collated)**

## Scope and the decisive source distinction
Do not collapse three physically and historically different textual strata:

1. **T**: Saʿd al-Dīn al-Taftāzānī's original *Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq wa-l-Kalām* (matn).
2. **Y**: Mullā ʿAbd Allāh al-Yazdī's *Ḥāshiya/Sharḥ* of the **logic** section; Qum's printed principal source occupies pp.7–123. Its reported completion in Najaf in 967/1560 is a claim in the tradition, not an autograph independently photographed by this project.
3. **H**: later *ḥawāshī al-ḥāshiya*, printed after Q125 and carrying many separately named, often much later speakers. They may quote or criticise Y but are not additional autograph leaves of Y.

The 400-page Qum print contains all these strata. A printed-page count is not a count of authorial Yazdī pages, and not a count of wholly translated glosses. Catalogue labels such as `شرح التهذيب` versus `حاشية على شرح التهذيب` require incipit/explicit verification, not identity assumptions.

**Important exclusion:** The al-Azhar 995-AH and Princeton Garrett 4139Y copies described in Stage100 are candidates for **Taftāzānī's T matn**, not automatic independent Y witnesses; they cannot settle the wording of al-Yazdī's Y commentary. Moreover the later ḥawāshī H pages may contain material postdating Y by centuries; none is an authorial Y witness just because of the binding.

## Reconciliation of two Arabic commentary traditions
The [Persian manuscript study](https://www.molaabdellah.ir/index.php/2maghalat/280-1356) says that some authorities report **two** Arabic Yazdī glosses: the printed school text and another reportedly held at the Riḍawiyya, and additionally a separate treatment of the **four-figure rule**. A record called `Ḥāshiya ʿalā Sharḥ al-Tahdhīb` need not name the printed Y-text itself; ascertain the lemma sequence and incipit first. This is a **bibliographical hypothesis**, not a proven two-recension stemma.

## Responsible collational workflow
For each photographed witness, record: holding library; verified shelfmark; catalogue name; copied title; physical extent; paper/hand/ink; exact incipit/explicit; date and whether from actual colophon; first/last authorial unit; gloss-vs-matn column layout; page/folio/side/line and photograph citation. Apply an **identity test** to `قوله الحمد لله` (opening), the Q37 differentia continuation, the fourth-figure discussion around Q95–101, Q108's `حكم عليه كل كل`, Q114's `بالبرهان`, Q117's `لذواتها`, and the close equivalent to Q123. Collate *like with like*: Taftāzānī T vs T, Yazdī Y vs Y, and each identified later commentator H vs its own witnesses.

Witness confidence codes:
- **C0 catalogue lead**: title or author alone; no confirmed incipit;
- **C1 identity corroborated in a catalogue**: sufficiently distinctive start/end or contents, still no photos;
- **C2 photographed and identity-confirmed**: exact folio map and authorial boundary available;
- **C3 independently collated**: recorded variants with page/line/image and defensible witness relationship.

As of this stage, **Y-I is at most C1; Y-A at most C1 for the final fragment; Y-BL candidates C0; Y-P an independent *print*, not C3**. **No new C2 or C3 status is claimed**. None of these metadata records licenses emending the existing Arabic working text.
