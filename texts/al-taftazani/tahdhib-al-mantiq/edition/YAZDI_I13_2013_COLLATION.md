### Witness identification
- **Q**: Qum, Muʾassasat al-Nashr al-Islāmī, *al-Ḥāshiya ʿalā Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq*, searchable pages [14](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/14?lang=ar), [15](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/15?lang=ar), [16](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/16?lang=ar), [17](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/17?lang=ar), [18](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/18?lang=ar), [19](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/19?lang=ar). The 400-page volume also contains later **هامش** texts; these are not part of al-Yazdī’s own commentary.
- **I13**: *Sharḥ al-Tahdhīb ʿalā Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq*, al-Taftāzānī's matn and al-Yazdī's commentary with separate later annotations by Abū al-Qāsim Muḥammad Ilyās b. ʿAbd Allāh al-Ghadwī al-Gujarātī; reviewed/corrected by Muftī Muḥammad Kalīm al-Dīn al-Katakī and Muftī Abū Bakr b. Muṣṭafā al-Faṭanī. First ed., Idārat al-Ṣiddīq, Dābhel, Gujarat (India), **1434/2013**. [Shamela searchable PDF, 198 PDF pages](https://shamela.org/gen/042fb96f81e9c1897008a735c3383ee5_gen.pdf), bibliographic preliminaries and main text.
- I13 printed **page numbers** below refer to the pagination reported in the Indian edition’s contents and body, **not** to the Qum volume or the PDF viewer index. Its editorial material is **not** a third historic commentary by al-Yazdī. The I13 text has not been demonstrated stemmatically independent of Q, and a reading repeated in both is not an independent manuscript confirmation.
- **Method limitation:** both compared sources here were searchable/digital **printed transcriptions**. Attempted PDF screenshot inspection of the Shamela host returned an access block. Therefore exact character-level variants are flagged **provisional pending comparison with page images**. No manuscript collation has been done in this batch.

**Apparatus convention:** compare Q and I13 **only within the main ḥāshiya**. Named marginal notices in Q (e.g. its p.17 discussion of whether formal logic can secure the soundness of premises) and I13 (distinguished in print by footnote rule and annotator sigla such as `عن`, `سل`, `مس`, `عب`) remain external unless later scholarly commentary is explicitly enabled under its own attribution. The annotated transcriptions of the 2013 source preserve extra contextual notes, but that is not proof that al-Yazdī wrote those notes.

### Connected translation batch: Q pp.15–19 / I13 pp.30–39
The new bilingual passages **YZ-15.04–15.06, YZ-16.03, YZ-17.02, YZ-18.03–18.04, YZ-19.02–19.04** supply ten additional Arabic/English main-commentary selections:
1. Early philosophers' three-constituent account of a proposition (Q15).
2. Explicit division and implied two-way classification (Q15).
3. The self-evidence of this division (Q15–16).
4. Why `معقول` is chosen over the equivocal `معلوم` (Q16–17).
5. Reasoning to contradictory cosmological outcomes (Q17–18).
6. The definition of logic deduced from the three-premise argument (Q18), including the Q/I13 reading difference.
7. The third question raised by the introduction: the subject of logic (Q18).
8. Defining account as *known conception capable of yielding previously unknown conception* (Q18–19).
9. What logic investigates in definitions and proofs, namely their arrangement (Q19).
10. The explanation of `معرّف` and `حجّة` (Q19).

This is a **connected topical sequence**, but not a complete continuous translation of Q pp.15–19; paragraphs not translated remain missing and are not editorially supplied.
