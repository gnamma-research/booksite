Critical points already identified

  • Authorship: the work is a ḥāshiya keyed to short lemmata. Distinguish Taftāzānī’s words, al-Yazdī’s discussion, and later writers’ marginal notes before assigning textual authority.
  • Q p.114: al-Yazdī criticises بالبرهان as an addition found in some copies, but also explains how it could be construed defensibly. Both sides belong in the apparatus; independent readings remain unverified.
  • Q pp.105–107: a definition of induction or analogy must distinguish the method/operation from the complete inferential ḥujja. The causal premise of analogy points to jurisprudential argumentation.
  • Q p.110: the five arts are classified by the epistemic or imaginative force of their matter, not merely by modern logical validity.

Continuous main-ḥāshiya translation

One consecutive translated section is available: Qum printed pages 22–29, the main explanation of signification and kinds of expressions (logic L.03). It replaces eleven overlapping short selections within the reader, without changing the historical count of 177 selected excerpts. This is a transcription of the source's digitally searchable main شرح, not a manuscript-certified edition. Other writers' هامش material is excluded.

Open textual problem: Qum page 24 has the searchable reading ولا لازم ذهني in an argument requiring an implied concomitant. The passage is preserved, flagged and not conjecturally emended. Read the eight-page coverage and critical notes.

Release standard for a definitive edition

The main ḥāshiya must be transcribed continuously, each paragraph translated and lemma-aligned, with photographic proofing, a documented independent manuscript comparison, recorded variants, an explicit emendation policy, consistent technical terminology, and checked source-linked navigation. None of the unfinished divisions is marked certified.
