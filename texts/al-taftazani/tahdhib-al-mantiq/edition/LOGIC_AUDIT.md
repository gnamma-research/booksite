## Formal test method

Every technical inference should receive: **Arabic clause → literal English → historical logical reconstruction → explicit example → counterexample to any overbroad reading → commentary/variant locator**.

For example, the unqualified rule “every universal affirmative converts to a universal affirmative” fails in traditional Aristotelian quantity conditions; a source-grounded table should distinguish *A→I*, *E→E*, and exceptions induced by existential or modal assumptions. Modern formal validity cannot be substituted directly for the text's own modal taxonomy.
