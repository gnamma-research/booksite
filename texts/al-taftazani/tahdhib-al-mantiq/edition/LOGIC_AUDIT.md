# Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq — Round 2 logical and textual audit

**2 November 2026.** Source is the continuous nineteen-unit bilingual working translation in the existing Gnamma reader. The 19 divisions are editorial; they do not mean nineteen pages of an original witness.

**Status:** 19/19 units have an English draft. Full Arabic line-by-line print verification, independent manuscript collation, and formal reconstruction of difficult inference rules have **not** yet been certified. This is an audit programme, not a record of completed verification.

## Formal test method

Every technical inference should receive: **Arabic clause → literal English → historical logical reconstruction → explicit example → counterexample to any overbroad reading → commentary/variant locator**.

For example, the unqualified rule “every universal affirmative converts to a universal affirmative” fails in traditional Aristotelian quantity conditions; a source-grounded table should distinguish *A→I*, *E→E*, and exceptions induced by existential or modal assumptions. Modern formal validity cannot be substituted directly for the text's own modal taxonomy.

## Witness priorities

- Controlling printed logic text in the Cairo 1912 copy (different pagination and endings from the online commentary-contained printing).
- Independent al-Azhar 995 AH manuscript; inspect extent and folios before attributing variants.
- Separate logic text in the al-Yazdī *Sharḥ al-Tahdhīb* tradition, with gloss voices distinguished.
- Selected parallels from Avicenna, later Shamsiyya commentators and traditional manṭiq sources, used for interpretation **not** as surrogate Tahdhīb witnesses.

## Release gates

1. Diplomatic Arabic line completeness confirmed for L01–L19 against a *defined* print witness.
2. Syllogistic and modal reasoning individually tested for L10–L17; every alleged rule formally stated and checked.
3. Independent reading/witness divergences recorded by actual photograph folio or print page.
4. English syntax and full logical taxonomy proofread by a second distinct pass.
5. Generate printed Arabic, English, facing bilingual and annotated editions from one corpus; retain editorial apparatus rather than manufacturing evidence.
