> **Stage 92 Abharī–Kātibī source and six-rule comparison (2 November 2026):** A 2019 critical edition by Özpilavcı–Öztop of *Sharḥ al-Mawāḍiʿ al-Mushkila*, probably by al-Kātibī (near-contemporary ms Süleymaniye Fazıl Ahmed Paşa 1612, fols.3b–39a, codex copied c.691 AH), attributes the three extra fourth-figure moods to al-Kātibī's teacher **Athīr al-Dīn al-Abharī**. It expands *Shamsiyya*'s five modal restrictions into **six**, distinguishing a mode-6 specially convertible negative **minor** from its **major of a class whose negative counterpart converts**. This resolves the apparent textual contradiction without an emendation. See [Stage92 critical genealogy dossier](STAGE92_ABHARI_KATIBI_SIX_MODAL_CONDITIONS.md). ## Formal test method

Every technical inference should receive: **Arabic clause → literal English → historical logical reconstruction → explicit example → counterexample to any overbroad reading → commentary/variant locator**.

For example, the unqualified rule “every universal affirmative converts to a universal affirmative” fails in traditional Aristotelian quantity conditions; a source-grounded table should distinguish *A→I*, *E→E*, and exceptions induced by existential or modal assumptions. Modern formal validity cannot be substituted directly for the text's own modal taxonomy.
