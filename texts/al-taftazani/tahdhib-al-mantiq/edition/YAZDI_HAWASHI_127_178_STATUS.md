# Later Ḥawāshī on al-Yazdī's Ḥāshiya: Qum pp.127–178
**Editorial status:** 7 November 2026 · first-pass, page-sampled, source-linked bilingual reader · **not a complete-page translation** and **not a definitive text**.

## What has been completed
A companion reader, [`hawashi-companion.html`](hawashi-companion.html), now presents **52 consecutive printed-page positions in Qum**: page 127, 128, …, 178, inclusive. Each has one specifically quoted, source-linked Arabic passage and an English translation of **that passage**, followed by a distinct, openly labelled **editorial analysis of the wider page**. The analysis is not represented as translated Arabic or proof that the remainder of the page has been translated. The editorial files contain **52 excerpt records**, **zero certified complete-page transcriptions or translations**, **zero collation-certified manuscript readings**, and **zero fully verified single-author attributions for whole printed pages**.

The page positions, signatures and themes were checked in Qum's searchable printed text. Every record has an original source link of the form `https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/PAGE?lang=ar`. Pages **141** and **163**, initially unavailable, were subsequently recovered; they have individual source records. No page in the requested 127–178 range is knowingly omitted.

## Why this is a separate book layer, not more al-Yazdī
The principal `شرح`/ḥāshiya by **Mullā ʿAbd Allāh al-Yazdī** has a completion notice on printed [Qum page 123](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/123?lang=ar). Page [125](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/125?lang=ar) is explicitly headed `حواشي الحاشية` (later glosses **on** al-Yazdī's gloss). The later pages contain **separately attributed notes**, notably to Mīrzā Muḥammad ʿAlī and Shaykh ʿAbd al-Raḥīm, plus quoted discussion from `al-Taqrīb`, al-Raḍī, al-Dawwānī and others. A bibliographic record crediting the volume's principal author as Yazdī does **not** make him author of these later notes.

The source print often puts several **numbered marginalia with different sigla** on the same page. Consequently the per-page `attribution` field records *named voices on the source page*, not verified authorship of every supplied excerpt. The annotation signature adjacent to each excerpt must be checked against photographed pages before definitive attribution. Never silently combine those discussions with the historical main ḥāshiya or the original Taftāzānī matn.

## Actual complete critical-translation requirement for this 52-page block

The current first pass establishes source-page coverage and translates one sample of actual Arabic prose on each page; it does **not** establish the text of whole notes. For the user's larger complete-edition goal, each page still requires:
1. **Photographic page-by-page layout segmentation**, identifying original authors, their sigla, note numbers, quoted works, source lemmata and continuations crossing page boundaries. One page may contain more than one separately authored voice.
2. **Continuous transcription of every later marginal note**, in the correct note order, not simply page order where the print columns interrupt one another.
3. **Full Arabic–English translation of each individual note**, including its internal quoted sources, and a separate clearly attributed place for modern editorial introductions.
4. **Critical collation of textual witnesses for each distinct author's gloss** where available. A Qum print and a Gujarat print are not automatically independent; unrelated marginalia cannot serve as variants of al-Yazdī's own main text.
5. **Source-bound technical glossary and subject index**, including *taṣdīq*, *nisba ḥukmiyya*, *nisba taqyīdiyya*, *tawfīq*, *ikhtiṣāṣ*, *ḥamd*, *mafhūm*, *malaka* and introduction/discipline distinction.
6. **Line-level two-way reader navigation** between a Taftāzānī lemma, Yazdī’s comment and an explicitly attributed later gloss, not by introducing another always-visible main reader button.

Do not convert the status “52 pages sampled” to “52 pages fully translated” unless the above page-by-page transcription and translation are demonstrably complete. This rule also applies to future requests to process 50–60 pages per stage: report separately source pages **examined**, pages **transcribed in full**, pages **translated in full**, and pages **critically certified**. What was achieved here is **52 / 0 / 0 / 0** in those four categories, plus 52 individual selected passages translated.

## Current main al-Yazdī translation, separate from the companion
The principal main ḥāshiya from Qum p.22–123 has **100 consecutive bilingual passage/printed-page blocks over 97 distinct print positions** (with gaps where the printed page contains only other marginalia or headings). It is currently a searchable-print working text, not an image-collated definitive edition. Qum pp.7–19 have only selected translations, so the full main commentary **is not yet continuously translated from start to finish**. The latter source gap should remain the scholarly priority before certifying either tradition.
