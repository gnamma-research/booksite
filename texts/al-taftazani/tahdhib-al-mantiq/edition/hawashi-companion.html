What this reader contains. Fifty-two distinct Qum printed-page positions (127–178), each represented by a translated selection and an independent editorial analysis. The printed pages include many additional passages that have not been translated here. This does not claim 52 complete-page translations. Page and author boundaries are provisional until printed images and independent manuscripts are collated.

These are later glosses on the commentary by Mullā ʿAbd Allāh al-Yazdī, not additional writing by al-Yazdī himself. Multiple signatures may occur within one printed page. Each card therefore describes the voices found on the page; it does not certify that all its contents have a single author. Main commentary's critical register and the two-state Matn / Matn + sharḥ reader are kept separate.
