## Scope and method
New bilingual page-aligned sources:
- `yazdi-continuous-q48-51-data.js` (**four Qum main-شرح page positions**, L.08–L.09)
- `yazdi-continuous-q54-58-data.js` (**five Qum main-شرح page positions**, L.10)

Both are based on the searchable [Qum print](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/48?lang=ar). Textual spaces and punctuation are normalized, modern annotation calls omitted, original lemma quotation preserved, and no wording under **هامش** is silently attributed to al-Yazdī.

- Qum **p.52** supplies a punctuation mark only, not a main-commentary passage.
- Qum **p.53** prints only `المقصد الثاني في التصديقات`, an original subject heading, not al-Yazdī commentary.
- Qum **p.55** has only `المعنى لا يتوقف معرفته على معرفة الخبر والقضية فلا دور` as the main شرح, completing the `وهذا` of p.54. The very long note thereafter includes al-Sharīf al-Jurjānī and discussions of attributive and assertoric relations and is in the printed **هامش**, associated with Mīrzā Muḥammad ʿAlī. It cannot be claimed as main ḥāshiya translated.
- Qum **p.58** ends mid-explanation with `أو من كليهما`; Qum **p.59** is the next main-text page and remains outside this translated batch.
- No photographs of Qum, Gujarat, Tehran or independent manuscripts were collated in this batch. The page numbers and attributions are direct from the searchable edition.

## Editorial questions requiring apparatus, not normalisation

**Q48–49 · What does “natural universal exists” mean?** Al-Yazdī distinguishes (i) universality as a logical predicate, which obtains intellectually; (ii) the nature considered as the subject of that predicate; and (iii) their intellectual composite. He separately reports a disagreement: the **majority of philosophers** affirm the natural universal's external existence *through* individuals; **al-Taftāzānī's favoured position** is that only individuals exist externally, and the phrase “the nature exists” is shorthand for their existence. Al-Yazdī appends **`وفيه تأمل`** and refers to `Ḥawāshī al-Tajrīd`. Treat this as a reserved/qualified assessment, not unconditional adoption by all parties. It directly concerns whether *human nature qua human* is instantiated or numerically one external bearer.

**Q50 · `al-muʿarrif` versus `al-muʿarraf`.** The same undiacritized letters `المعرف` can mean the *defining expression* or the *defined object*. English renders the intended roles transparently. Al-Yazdī distinguishes (a) common predicative extension (neither wider nor narrower) and (b) greater intelligibility to the learner. A subject can be grasped incidentally through an overly narrow idea, but that does not validate the narrower term as a scientific definition.

**Q50–51 · Two axes of definition.** Essential versus accidental differentiating content yields **ḥadd** versus **rasm**; with or without proximate genus yields **complete** versus **incomplete**. Al-Yazdī explicitly reports *earlier logicians* admitting wider and even accidentally narrower incomplete accounts, while he attributes a stricter criterion to al-Taftāzānī. Distinguish these voices. A lexical clarification does not produce an unknown essence from known ingredients; it disambiguates a familiar concept.

**Q54–55 · Mental relation versus verbal utterance.** The term `قول` applies to conceptual and spoken composition. The identification of truth with `المطابقة للواقع` must not be falsely described as a lengthy proof by al-Yazdī: in the **main** commentary the p.54 clause continues only with a brief anti-circularity qualification on p.55. A long, philosophically sophisticated discussion under *hāmish* is not al-Yazdī; including it under his name would reproduce precisely the editorial confusion the critical edition is supposed to prevent.

**Q56 · What is the copula?** Al-Yazdī says the real `رابطة` is the **judgmental relation**, while a pronounced word is named copula by metonymy (the signifier takes the name of the signified). He reports al-Fārābī's historical explanation of the borrowed **`هو` / `هي`** non-temporal connector by contrast with Persian **`است`** and Greek **`استين`**. Do not conflate this second-intentional predicative relationship with an independent real categorial relation inhering in a subject. It is a linguistic-logical account and needs further interpretation in any Thomistic/Akbarī comparison.

**Q57–58 · Why a subject must exist for an affirmative.** The three subjects are **actually externally existent** (`خارجية`), **supposed possibly external** (`حقيقية`), and **mentally posited** (`ذهنية`). The example of `شريك الباري ممتنع` belongs to mental supposition denying extramental possibility, not positive predication of an external divine partner. These are classical distinctions involving existential import and varying reference domains. The claim that indefinite entails particular must be evaluated under source-specific assumptions about subjects and empty terms.

Expected visible main sharḥ cards in the full continuous reader: **142** (177 − 71 + 36). This does not mean 142 completely translated pages, nor that any of the remaining logic units has full continuous al-Yazdī commentary.

**Do not call this definitive.** Qum source photographs, Gujarat 2013 line comparisons, Tehran variants and at least one identifiable independent manuscript remain uncollated. The edition has working source-linked translation, separate main/marginal attribution and a growing critical dossier—not certification.
