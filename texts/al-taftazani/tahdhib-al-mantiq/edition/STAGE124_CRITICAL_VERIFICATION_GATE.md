## Scope
The eventual complete Volume I is three distinguishable historical layers: T, al-Taftāzānī's original logic *matn*; Y, al-Yazdī's principal commentary ending at Qum printed p.123; and H, the later mixed-author ḥawāshī, covering Qum printed pp.127–395. Later modern editorial quotations constitute a fourth, separately attributed and rights-sensitive layer. Speaker attribution must be preserved at clause level.
