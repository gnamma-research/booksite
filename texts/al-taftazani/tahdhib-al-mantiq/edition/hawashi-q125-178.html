Glosses on the gloss

A separately attributed reading of the fifty-four Qum printed positions beginning immediately after Mullā ʿAbd Allāh al-Yazdī’s principal commentary.

This is NOT a complete translation of the later glosses. Each substantive printed page contains a short Arabic source lemma and a selected translation with a condensed account of its argument. The remaining paragraphs have not been fully translated. Authors change within pages and are not all al-Yazdī.

Textual boundary, provenance, and how to read the notes

Qum p.123 contains the close of al-Yazdī’s main commentary and a separately printed completion notice. Page 125 begins حواشي الحاشية, literally “glosses on the gloss”; page 126 supplies no text. The body starts on page 127. These subsequent notes contain material signed by Mīrzā Muḥammad ʿAlī, ʿAbd al-Raḥīm, and others, together with historical quotations and some much later sources. The online book-level catalogue labels al-Yazdī as author of the volume, but that is not authorship evidence for every passage of this apparatus.

The source-site text is searchable print; manuscript and photographed print collation are not completed. Exact attributions require per-note signatures and image checks. The English text below is a selective first-pass translation and analytic synopsis, not an edition of every Arabic sentence. Source links point to the specific Qum printed page.
