## Specific witness disagreements and required corrections

**K02.** The photographed H text appears to use **متصفًا بالنطق** (characterized by rational speech), whereas the derivative comparator supplies **مستلزمًا للنطق** (entailing rational speech). They are not the same clause; keep both pending final photographic letter-level audit.

**D06.** The H-1405 and H-1433 photographed printings read the second disjunction approximately **زوجًا أو كسرًا** (‘even or a fraction’), while the Shamela digitisation instead offers **لا زوجًا ولا فردًا** (‘neither even nor odd’). The Stage118 English was based on the latter and **must not stand as the translation of the photographed H source without a variant note**. The print example is now translated separately, retaining the other reading as a distinct witness.

**D03 and K07.** Restore the quantifier **دائمًا** where earlier English condensed it away. These are examples of nested propositional forms, not deductions the editor has independently proved valid.
