# Stage 78 — Cairo logic collation and printed-witness separation

**Date:** 2 November 2026  
**Work:** Saʿd al-Dīn al-Taftāzānī, *Tahdhīb al-Manṭiq wa-l-Kalām*, first (logic) section  
**Controlling witness:** Cairo, Maṭbaʿat al-Saʿāda, 1330/1912, original numbered pp. **3–14**.  
**Comparison text:** Matn printed within *Sharḥ al-Tahdhīb*, online book pages **11–15** at [Arabic Dawate Islami](https://www.arabicdawateislami.net/bookslibrary/3507/page/11).

This is a **direct printed-witness comparison**, **not** a manuscript collation. The nineteen editorial divisions now have page-span boundaries visually checked against Cairo. This does not by itself prove that every word of every clause, every iʿrāb, or every modal inference has received a second independent review.

## A. Mandatory retraction of an erroneous Stage 77 claim

In Stage 77, L16 was said to have been restored to the wording of the Cairo 1912 print by adding **«الكلية»** twice:

> «... مع الموجبة الكلية الموجبتين، ومع السالبة الكلية السالبتين ...»

A direct enlargement of **Cairo p. 12** actually reads:

> «مع الموجبة الموجبتين، ومع السالبة السالبتين بالضرورة»

The **longer wording is genuinely present** in the distinct commentary-containing [printed source, p. 14](https://www.arabicdawateislami.net/bookslibrary/3507/page/14); it must **not** be imported into a Cairo transcription. Cairo itself clearly specifies **«وكلية الكبرى»** just before this enumeration, so the logical condition of a universal major premise is not lost by respecting its shorter wording.

**Action taken:** corrected both logic datasets; annotated the former Stage 77 claim as *retracted*; corrected continuous text, PDF-source data and the automated regression assertion; and added the alternative printing as a distinct source-reading rather than a conjecture. Any edition citing the Stage 77 “restoration” as Cairo wording is superseded by Stage 78.

## B. Newly confirmed L13 Arabic repair

The same type of witness conflation had affected L13, *On Contradiction*.

| Locus | Cairo 1912 | Matn printed with the commentary |
|---|---|---|
| p. 9 / online p. 13, opening | «التناقض اختلاف قضيتين» | «التناقض اختلاف القضيتين» |
| p. 10 / online p. 13, ordering | «ولا بد من الاختلاف في الكيف والكم والجهة» | «ولا بد من الاختلاف في الكم والكيف والجهة» |

The Cairo expression **«قضيتين»** and ordering **«الكيف والكم والجهة»** are now the adopted *Cairo-base* readings. Both are short-form / ordering distinctions, not evidence of authorship or manuscript priority.

### Consequence for translation

The previously brief English wording “the contradictory of a necessary proposition is generally possible” could wrongly be taken as possibility of the proposition of the **same** quality. The corrected English expressly states **“a generally possible proposition of opposite quality.”** For *necessity of P* the contradiction requires possibility of **not-P**, while contradiction of a compound modal must negate the appropriate components with attention to the quantified individual; a modern schematic negation is an **explanatory formalization**, not Taftāzānī’s complete historical modal semantics.

## E. What the logical demonstration actually does — and does not — establish

### Syllogistic figures (non-modal skeleton)

Use **S** for the minor term, **P** for the major term, and **M** for the middle term.

| Figure | Major premise | Minor premise |
|---|---|---|
| First | M–P | S–M |
| Second | P–M | S–M |
| Third | M–P | M–S |
| Fourth | P–M | M–S |

Cairo L16 explicitly requires **«كلية الكبرى»** for its first-figure account. The exhaustive finite-model tests on 512 three-term assignments check the *non-modal* categorical skeleton and term-existence requirements, but they do **not** certify the exact traditional modal implications, which invoke actualization, permanence, subject description and possibility in a richer historical technical system.

### Important modal distinctions not to collapse

In Cairo L11, **necessary while the subject exists**, **necessary while it has a description**, **permanent while the subject exists**, **permanent while it has the description**, **necessary at a fixed time**, **necessary at an unspecified time**, **actually holds**, and **not necessarily its contrary** are distinct simple forms. Adding absence of permanence or absence of necessity produces specific compounded forms. A contemporary Boolean truth table is insufficient to reconstruct every modal conversion rule in L14–L16.

The next proper review should provide each Arabic rule, a literal English gloss, its traditional commentary interpretation, a proposed formal semantics *labelled as reconstruction*, proofs and countermodels under explicit presuppositions, and the status of each competing print/manuscript reading.

## F. Kalām and manuscript gates remain unchanged

The calām corpus preserves **1,044** bilingual clauses across Cairo pp. **15–127**, with **108 of 113** pages having recorded full single-print checks. Five printed pages **39, 77, 80, 100 and 124** still contain unresolved localized readings. Stage 78 has not invented a witness to settle them and has **not** promoted the certification count.

Independent manuscript witnesses have been catalogued (al-Azhar 995 AH, and Princeton Garrett 4139Y, 1126 AH); full page-by-page collation of them was **not** completed here. Their availability and exact contents must be established before assigning positive variants.
