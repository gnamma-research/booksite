**Reading notes:** In the first example `ناطق` is the technical differentiating characteristic ‘rational/articulate’, not merely audible speech. The examples illustrate the *composition* of propositions; their presentation does not certify the inferential validity of every nested formula. Connected 7/9 is particularly susceptible to overreading as an entailment: the source presents it as a **formal construction**. Preserve the Arabic rather than silently repair it into a modern tautology.
