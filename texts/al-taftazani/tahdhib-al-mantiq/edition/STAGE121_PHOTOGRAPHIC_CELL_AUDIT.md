## 2. Twenty more original Q294 directional core glyphs

The historical [Q293 source legend](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/12170/293?lang=ar), signed **ʿAbd al-Raḥīm**, explains that the table compares the **obtaining/truth conditions of entire propositions**, not the predicability of a term over another term. Hence `مط` indicates unrestricted inclusion and the directional marks require explicit translation:
- **`حم`**: the **lower** proposition is more general than the upper.
- **`فم`**: the **upper** proposition is more general than the lower.
