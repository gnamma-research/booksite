## Q259 arithmetic separate from translation status

The inherited English faithfully reports the *source's* numerical totals, including `450` types, `120` permutations and `5,400` claimed product. Modern arithmetic shows **450×120=54,000**. The translation is faithful to the visible numerals precisely because it does **not** substitute the modern computed product into the historical reading. Source itself may contain a calculation or copy/edition error; no independent archetypal reading has been established.
