Textual witnesses and editorial method

The reading text comes from an openly accessible online Arabic transcription of Qūnawī's al-Nuṣūṣ; the al-Iznīqī extracts are checked against Balaban and Çalışkan's 2023 printed edition. Neither is yet a critical transcription compared against the early manuscript witnesses. No unexamined manuscript folios are represented as translated.

For Qūnawī, compare Arabic printed edition PDF and the manuscript survey. For al-Iznīqī, see the 2023 Arabic edition. A large manuscript PDF catalogued as al-Iznīqī's commentary has been identified but not inspected; its attribution must be checked before publication.
