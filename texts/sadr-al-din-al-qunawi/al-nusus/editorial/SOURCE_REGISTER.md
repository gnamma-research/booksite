## Primary evidence / finding aids
- Qūnawī online transcription with printed-page labels beginning at p. 236: [forum text](https://alshrefalm7sy.ahlamontada.com/t3169-متن-كتاب-النصوص-في-تحقيق-الطور-المخصوص-للإمام-العارف-بالله-صدر-الدين-القونوي). A public-domain medieval work, but the web transcription is **not proof of editorial accuracy**.
- Qūnawī, *al-Nuṣūṣ* (Ibrāhīm Muḥammad Yāsīn Arabic printed edition, 155 PDF pages): [PDF](https://sufism.ir/library-files/ghonyavi/ar/alnosus-fi-tahqiqe-toorel-makhsous-gonyavi-ar.pdf). PDF page 45 visibly corresponds to the mirror/manifestation discussion. A separate authoritative manuscript should be collated before treating this as a critical edition.
- Early copies: [Hüseyin Çelebi 477, fols. 65a–89b](https://ibnarabisociety.org/sadr-al-din-al-qunawi-al-nusus-hulya-kucuk/), [Şehid Ali Paşa 1351, fols. 213b–229b, dated 690 AH](https://www.islam-anatolia.ac.uk/data/documents/TK_Sul_Sehid_AP_1351%3Ba11?hl=Iran&hlu=). Confirm the catalogue descriptions and manuscript dates before manuscript-level claims.
- al-Iznīqī: Muḥammet Seyyit Balaban and Abdulhamid Çalışkan, critically edited short Arabic treatise, *İhya* 9/2 (2023), pp. 814–856; Arabic authorial text printed pp. 831–850 and following; [PDF](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3074106); [article](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ihya/article/1280654). The modern article's CC BY-NC-ND licensing concerns scholarly editorial material. Only limited primary medieval excerpts and new English translations are published here, with explicit attribution; no systematic reuse of the edition’s modern apparatus.
- Another manuscript scan **catalogued** as `Sharḥ al-Nuṣūṣ al-Qūnawī li-Quṭb al-Dīn al-Iznīqī`: [direct PDF](https://sufism.ir/library-files/ghonyavi/ar/sharho-nosoos-azniqi-ar.pdf), approximately 16 MB. The public PDF is identified but **not** opened as full manuscript pages in this stage; no attribution has yet been established from its colophon. This is **not** the same document as the securely attributed short Risāla.
- al-Khūyī manuscript catalogue [Princeton Garrett 4037Y](https://dpul.princeton.edu/Garrettcollections/catalog/r494vk24f); M. N. Bardakçı's [2014 manuscript study](https://isamveri.org/pdfdrg/D226920/2014/2014_BARDAKCIMN.pdf). The study reports the author's name as al-Khūyī in Ali Emiri Arabi 980 despite catalogue attribution to al-Iznīqī. This remains a genuine attribution problem.
- Al-Shīrāzī/al-Tabrīzī: [Bursa Hüseyin Çelebi 479 catalogue](https://portal.yek.gov.tr/works/detail/31549); Ragıp Paşa 1469 and other notices from the [Qūnawī commentary survey](https://ibnarabisociety.org/sadr-al-din-al-qunawi-al-nusus-hulya-kucuk/). Check al-Sayrūzī variant separately before asserting identity.

## Text-critical cautions opened this stage
1. QUN-NU-001-B: electronic text repeats `في نفسه` in an unusual sequence. The site preserves the reading and flags it; compare independent witnesses.
2. QUN-NU-020-A: `بل هي لنفسها ثابتة مثبتة لا مثبتة` is ambiguous without verified vocalisation or collation; the English is provisional. Do not build metaphysical argument on this exact phrase yet.
3. QUN-NU-008-C: the source assigns the flash's non-continuance to `حكم جمعية الحقيقة الإنسانية`; it also affirms enduring post-disclosure effects. There is no primary proof here that the flash is simply permanent from a timeless viewpoint. Keep the reception/duration/effects distinct.
4. Distinguish `aʿyān thābita`, `māhiyya`, `taʿayyun`, and `nisba` from discrete additional entities; check each author's specific usage.
5. QUN-NU-001-A: negative predication under essential absoluteness must not be confused with the denial of divine names at the level of determination.
6. Hadiths and spiritual sayings cited in the base texts need independent isnād/source checking; editorial inclusion is not authentication.
7. *Zubdat* and *Asrār* authorial attributions are **unresolved**. Do not automatically conflate authors because editions and catalogues share overlapping titles.

## Further source comparison — added this stage
- QUN-NU-003-B, the statement that judgement depends on the state of judge and judged, has been translated in full in the bilingual record. The publicly accessible Arabic transcription and the printed Ibrāhīm Muḥammad Yāsīn edition (printed p. 43, PDF index 38) show significant differences in the opening and in the relation of judgement to the time of judging. **No preferred authorial lemma is imposed without manuscript collation.**
- QUN-NU-003-C, the statement that knowledge follows existence, has been translated in full and checked against the separate printed edition (printed p. 44, PDF index 39). The final sentence has a materially different wording in the two accessible witnesses. **The English corresponding to the online reading is marked provisional.**
- QUN-NU-004-A and 004-B now translate Qūnawī's explicit distinction between special, name-conditioned stations and the most comprehensive station. This primary passage should be the baseline for the planned study of how metaphysical terminology shifts with perspective.
- QUN-NU-005-A gives a source-located full translation of the passage on the unseen of divine Identity, the most comprehensive determination, and principiality under the essential relation of knowledge.
- **Current bilingual working records: 18** (Qūnawī 16; al-Iznīqī 2). No complete medieval book has been translated and no actual manuscript leaf has yet been collated.

## Primary translation priorities (not merely thematic notes)
**1. Complete al-Iznīqī.** Finish printed 831–850+ in Arabic–English paired units: preface; introductory discussions of `ʿilm` and `maʿrifa`; all four chapters; Qurʾān and hadith citations; Persian poetry; conclusion. The existing Stage 2 English draft translated seven consecutive rational arguments on pp. 835–836 plus selected later passages, but the Arabic transcript and many paragraphs are missing. No coverage of a page is awarded merely because one sentence was read.
**2. Establish a critical Qūnawī base.** Finish the full continuous matn, reading and translating each text; align each on *incipit* and statement-level anchors; reconcile 21/22 numbering against witnesses. Collate opening text, the mirror/lightning passage, and the long One-Wujūd text as the initial critical checkpoints.
**3. Acquire al-Khūyī's Ayasofya 4805.** First fols. 1a–13b: 22 questions; then the commentary incipit/explicit, especially fol. 29b, 35a, 95a, 98b–106a reported in secondary study. Check Ali Emiri 980 and Princeton 4037Y in parallel.
**4. Acquire *Asrār al-surūr*.** Prioritise Bursa Hüseyin Çelebi 479, then Ragıp Paşa 1469. Transcribe complete folios with quotations clearly separated from commentarial analysis; compare incipits and colophons before determining author.
**5. Import the already-running Mahāʾimī reader's validated units** without retranslating them; build the cross-work alignment table from Qūnawī incipits rather than arbitrary numbers.

## Website implementation
Reader: `texts/sadr-al-din-al-qunawi/al-nusus/`, JSON source `data/parallel.json`, styles and script under the same directory. Supports Arabic / English / both, 7-work corpus shelf, paragraph selection, cross-work links, search, editorial note toggle, themes, reader bookmarks, copyable source anchors, direct JSON export and responsive mobile layout.
