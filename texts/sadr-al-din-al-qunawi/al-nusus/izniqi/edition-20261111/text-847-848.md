## 847 · Authorities on the inseparability of the path and the religious law

### Arabic

وعضّوا عليها بالنواجذ، وإياكم ومحدثات الأمور فإن كل محدث بدعة، وكل بدعة ضلالة». قال الإمام النوري: «من رأيته يدعي مع الله حالة ويخرج هو من حد العلم الشرعي فلا تقربن منه». وقال الجنيدي رئيس الطائفة: «من لم يحفظ القرآن ولم يكتب الحديث لا يقتدى به في هذا الأمر، لأن علمنا مقيد بالكتاب والسنة». وقال قطب الإسلام، شيخ الشيوخ، مجد الدين البغدادي في تحفة البررة: إن أبا بكر الدقاق (الزقاق) كان في تيه بني إسرائيل فوقع في قلبه أن علم الحقيقة يخالف علم الشريعة، فإذا بشخص تحت شجرة أم غيلان فصاح: «يا أبا بكر! كل حقيقة يخالف الشرع فهو كفر»، وذكر أنه لا يتصور انقطاع العبودية التي هي اللب، ولا المتابعة التي هي الصورة، لا في الدنيا ولا في الأخرى فلا يخالف الشريعة أحد إلا من رد الله تعالى فأورده في المهالك بئس الورد المورود كما كان حال المستدرحين كبلعم وبرصيص نعوذ بالله من الحور بعد الكور، وقال مقتدى العرب والعجم، أبو نصر عبد الله بن علي السراج الطوسي في كتاب اللمع في علم التصوف في باب ذكر الصوفية وطبقاتهم:
«إن طبقات الصوفية اتفقوا مع الفقهاء وأصحاب الحديث في معتقداتهم، وشاركوهم بالقبول والموافقة في جميع علومهم، ولم يخالفوهم في علومهم ورسومهم، ومن لم يبلغ من الصوفية مراتب الفقهاء رجعوا إليهم فيما أشكل من أحكام الشرع، فإذا اجتمعوا في شيء فهم في جملتهم، فإذا اختلفوا فالصوفية يأخذ بالأحسن والأولى احتياطا في الدين».
وذكر أيضا في باب الوجد من ذلك الكتاب، وحكى عن سهل بن عبد الله التستري، أنه قال: «كل وجد لا يشهد له الكتاب والسنة فهو باطل»، وذكر في مرصاد العباد في بيان صفة الشيخ أنه ينبغي أن يكون الشيخ على مذهب أهل السنة والجماعة وأن يكون عالما بالأحكام الشرعية، وقال الإمام القشيري: «إن المشايخ مجمعون على تعظيم الشريعة، متفقون بسلوك طريق الرياضة، مقيمون على متابعة السنة غير مخلين بشيء من آداب الديانة»، وقال من مشايخ سلسلتنا العارف الأجل شهاب الدين السهروردي:
«ينبغي أن يكون ما يفعله المريد في الظاهر ويخطره في سره موافقا لظاهر الشرع»، ثم قال: «رأيت جمعا من طوائف السالكين استولت عليهم الخيالات الفاسدة بسبب شوم ترك الجمعات والجماعات وقعدوا في دهليز الزندقة»، وقال صاحب التعرف: «مهما كان مقام الباطن أصفى وأطهر كان مقام الظاهر أكثر آدابا وأشد حرمة للشريعة، ومن لم يقدم علم الشريعة ثم شرع في علم هذه الطائفة، فهو مفوت عن نفسه دين الإسلام وخالع ربقته عنه نعوذ بالله».

### English

—and hold fast to it with your back teeth. Beware of newly introduced matters: every such innovation is a novelty in religion, and every religious novelty is error.”

Imam al-Nūrī said: “When you see someone claiming a state with God while departing from the bounds of knowledge of the religious law, do not approach him.” Al-Junaydī, chief of the community, said: “Someone who has not memorised the Qurʾān and recorded the hadith is not to be followed in this affair, for our knowledge is bound by the Book and the Sunnah.”

The Pole of Islam and master of masters, Majd al-Dīn al-Baghdādī, says in *Tuḥfat al-barara* that Abū Bakr al-Daqqāq (al-Zaqqāq) was in the wilderness of the Israelites when the thought occurred to him that knowledge of reality contradicts knowledge of the religious law. Suddenly a person beneath an acacia tree called out: “Abū Bakr! Every purported reality that contradicts the religious law is unbelief.” He also states that neither servanthood, which is the inner kernel, nor following the prophetic example, which is its form, can conceivably cease, either in this world or the next. No one contradicts the religious law except someone whom God has turned away and brought to destruction—an evil destination indeed—as happened to those gradually led to ruin, such as Balʿam and Barṣīṣā. We seek God's protection against unravelling after consolidation.

The exemplar of Arabs and non-Arabs, Abū Naṣr ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAlī al-Sarrāj al-Ṭūsī, says in *The Book of Flashes concerning the Science of Sufism*, in the chapter mentioning the Sufis and their classes:

“The different classes of Sufis agree with the jurists and the specialists in hadith in their beliefs. They share in accepting and assenting to all their sciences, and do not contradict them in their sciences and established practices. Those Sufis who have not attained the jurists' rank refer to them concerning difficult rulings of the law. When the jurists agree upon something, the Sufis are among those who agree; when they differ, the Sufis take what is better and more appropriate, out of caution in religion.”

In the chapter on ecstasy in that same book, he reports that Sahl b. ʿAbd Allāh al-Tustarī said: “Every ecstasy to which the Book and the Sunnah do not bear witness is false.” In *Mirṣād al-ʿibād*, when describing the qualities of the spiritual master, it is stated that the master should follow the doctrine of the people of the Sunnah and the community and know the rulings of the religious law. Imam al-Qushayrī says: “The masters are unanimous in venerating the religious law, agree upon travelling the path of discipline, remain steadfast in following the Sunnah, and neglect none of the proprieties of religion.”

The eminent knower Shihāb al-Dīn al-Suhrawardī, one of the masters in our chain, said: “What the disciple does outwardly and entertains inwardly should conform to the outward religious law.” Then he said: “I have seen groups of wayfarers overcome by corrupt imaginings because of the ill consequences of abandoning Friday and congregational prayers; they have taken their seats in the vestibule of heresy.”

The author of *al-Taʿarruf* says: “The clearer and purer the inward station, the more abundant are the outward proprieties and the stronger the reverence for the religious law. Whoever enters the knowledge of this community without first acquiring knowledge of the law deprives himself of the religion of Islam and throws its bond off his neck. We seek refuge in God.”

### Editorial notes

1. The opening continues the prophetic report begun on p. 846. The reported authorities and claims of unanimity are translated as the author presents them, not independently authenticated here.
2. The printed text gives Abū Bakr al-Daqqāq with al-Zaqqāq parenthetically. Both forms are retained rather than silently resolving the identity.
3. The printed main reading المستدرحين is defective; the translation follows the contextually clear sense of المستدرجين, also reported as a variant in the edition. This is a disclosed reading correction, not an independent manuscript finding.
4. The title Tuḥfat al-barara and the references to Balʿam and Barṣīṣā belong to the source. Their historical identifications and the exact locations of all quotations remain separate checks.

## 848 · Annihilation, servanthood, and the sense of ecstatic utterances

### Arabic

وقال صاحب اصطلاحات الصوفية: «فإن من لم يصن حاله وطريقته بالشريعة فسد حاله وآلت طريقته هوسا وهوى ووسوسة، ومن لم يتوصل بالطريقة إلى الحقيقة، ولم يحفظها بها فسدت حقيقته، وآلت إلى الزندقة والإلحاد»، وقال قطب الأقطاب عبد القادر الجيلي رضي الله عنه في بيان مقامات العارف:
«ثم يفنون عن الفناء بالفناء حتى لا يشهدون فناء ولا بقاء فيكلأهم الله عز وجل كلاءة الطفل الوليد ولا يصح هذا الوصف لأحد إلا بعد ملازمة آداب العبودية والاستقامة على القيام بأوامر الشريعة، فكل جمع بلا تفرقة زندقة وكل تفرقة بلا جمع تعطيل، وقال في وصف القطب لا مسلك في الحقيقة إلا وله مأخذ مكين، ولا مقام في النهاية إلا وله فيه قدم راسخ، ولا سر في عالمي الغيب والشهادة إلا وله إليه مطالعة، ولا مظهر لوجود إلا وله فيه مشاركة، ولا يغيب مشهوده، ولا وجود أتم من وجوده، ولا شهود أظهر من شهوده، ولا اقتفاء للشرع أشد من اقتفائه، وقال رضي الله عنه: كل حقيقة لم يشهد لها الشريعة فهي زندقة».
فالحاصل من كلمات هؤلاء السادات والأعلام الهداة: أن كل عمل واعتقاد مخالف لظاهر الشرع رد، ومن الظاهر المكشوف بلا ريب أن الوجود منقسم إلى واجب وممكن، وأن انقلاب الحقائق ممتنع باتفاق العلماء من الإسلاميين والحكماء، فأنى يكون العبد إلها، العبد عبد، والرب رب، يعني ينقلب الوصف ولا يزول كما قال علي: «من أين التراب ورب الأرباب»، وقال: يا ليتني لم تلدني أمي، وقال خاتم الرسل عليه السلام: «يا ليت رب محمد لم يخلق محمدا» إشارة إلى امتناع الانقلاب وعدم إحاطة العلم على ما في غيب الغيب من الشؤونات الذاتية، فافهم عن فطانة، فغاية ما يمكن للعبد أن يكون فناء في فناء، ومحوا في محو، وطمسا في طمس، فحينئذ يكون كالكرة في صولجان الفارس وكقطعة لوح على بحر تابعة لأمواجه وكالميت في يد الغسال يقلبه كيف يشاء ويجري فيه ما أراد الله تعالى محفوظا على شريعة حبيبه عليه السلام لطفا من الله تعالى، فحصل الصيانة والعصمة من غير تكلف ومشقة، وقال الله تعالى: ﴿كذلك لنصرف عنه السوء والفحشاء إنه من عبادنا المخلصين﴾ [يوسف: 24]، وهو محبوب الرب ومراده ومرباه في حجر قربه، وهو المتخلق والمتحقق، فحينئذ يختار العبد باختيار الله ومشيئته، ويرضى برضاه عز وجل، ولا يرى لغيره وجودا ولا فعلا، ولا يشاهد ما سواه هذا، ويلزم منه أن لا يكون لغير الله وجود إمكاني ولا فعل كسبي بحسب نفس الأمر، لأنه خرم ما عليه إجماع أهل السنة، بل هذا العبد غلب عليه الوجود الوجوبي، وكان في عينه وشهوده كل الأشياء هالكا، والحال أن كل وجود إمكاني هالك في حد ذاته لافتقار إلى قيوم موجد، فبهذا الاعتبار قال بعض المشايخ: لا وجود لغير الله، وقال بعض: «أنا الحق»، وقال بعض: «ليس في جبتي سوى الله»، وغير ذلك كل هذا رمز إلى ما أشرت، فلا عجب، لا سيما في مقام السكر والاستغراق، ألا يرى أن المحب المجازي، ربما يعتري عليه حال يتعطل مشاعره عن إدراكاتها، ولا يرى غير محبوبه، بل ربما يتكلمه حاسبا

### English

The author of *The Technical Expressions of the Sufis* says: “Whoever does not safeguard his state and path through the religious law corrupts his state, and his path turns into frenzy, caprice, and whispering suggestions. Whoever does not reach reality through the path and safeguard it through that path corrupts his reality, which turns into heresy and deviation.”

The Pole of poles, ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Jīlī—may God be pleased with him—says in his account of the knower's stations:

“Then, through annihilation, they pass away from annihilation, until they witness neither annihilation nor subsistence. God guards them as a newborn child is guarded. This description applies to no one before he has adhered to the proprieties of servanthood and remained upright in fulfilling the commands of the religious law. Every gathering without differentiation is heresy, and every differentiation without gathering is divestment.” In describing the Pole he says: “There is no path within reality in which he lacks a firmly established means of access, no ultimate station in which he lacks a firmly planted foot, and no secret in the unseen and visible worlds into which he has no insight. There is no manifestation of existence in which he has no share. What he witnesses never becomes absent; no existence is more complete than his, no witnessing more manifest than his, and no adherence to the law stricter than his.” He also says: “Every purported reality to which the religious law does not bear witness is heresy.”

The conclusion of these words of the noble masters and guiding authorities is that every deed and belief contrary to the outward religious law is rejected. It is indisputably evident that existence is divided into necessary and possible, and that the transformation of realities into their opposites is impossible by the agreement of the Islamic scholars and the philosophers. How, then, could the servant become a god? The servant is a servant and the Lord is Lord—“that is, the qualification changes but does not disappear” [so the printed text; see the textual note]. As ʿAlī said: “How far apart are dust and the Lord of lords!” He also said: “Would that my mother had not borne me!” The Seal of the messengers—peace be upon him—is reported as saying: “Would that Muḥammad's Lord had not created Muḥammad!” These are taken as allusions to the impossibility of such a transformation and to knowledge's inability to encompass the essential affairs within the unseen of the unseen. Understand discerningly.

The utmost possible for the servant is annihilation within annihilation, effacement within effacement, obliteration within obliteration. He then becomes like a ball under a horseman's polo stick, a piece of wood upon a sea following its waves, or a corpse in a washer's hands, turned as the washer wills. What God wills takes place in him, while divine kindness safeguards his adherence to the law of God's beloved—peace be upon him. Safeguarding and protection from error thus arise without contrivance or hardship. God says: “Thus We averted evil and indecency from him; he is among Our chosen servants” [Q 12:24]. He is beloved and desired by the Lord, nurtured in the embrace of His nearness, and endowed with the corresponding character and realisation. The servant then chooses through God's choice and will, is pleased through God's good pleasure, sees neither existence nor action belonging to another, and witnesses nothing apart from Him.

A consequence drawn from this would be that nothing other than God possesses possible existence or acquired action in reality; yet that would breach what the people of the Sunnah agree upon [the printed connective is problematic; see the note]. Rather, necessary existence has so prevailed over this servant that, in his sight and witnessing, all things have perished. In fact every possible existence, considered in itself, is perishing because it depends upon a sustaining giver of existence. Under this consideration some masters said, “There is no existence for anything other than God”; some said, “I am the Real”; some, “Within my robe there is nothing but God”; and others spoke similarly. All this alludes to what I have indicated. There is no cause for astonishment, especially in the station of intoxication and absorption. Do you not see that even a lover in the figurative sense may be overtaken by a state in which his senses cease their ordinary apprehensions, so that he sees nothing but his beloved? ### Editorial notes

1. Two printed readings require manuscript review. The main text has يعني ينقلب الوصف ولا يزول, literally “the qualification changes but does not disappear”, after affirming the impossibility of transformation. The English retains this awkward wording instead of silently adding a negation. A reading such as لا ينقلب is conjecturally possible but not adopted as established.
2. The main text next prints ويلزم منه أن لا يكون … لأنه خرم … بل … . Read as an unqualified assertion of entailment, this clashes with the immediate objection and correction. The English presents the consequence as one being rejected; an omitted لا before يلزم is another possible explanation, not a verified manuscript reading. The Arabic preserves the printed words.
3. The sayings attributed to ʿAlī and especially the saying attributed to the Prophet about his own creation are not authenticated by inclusion here. They are translated solely as quotations in this treatise.
4. The distinction between ontological dependence and a recipient’s perception is explicit. The final sentence continues on p. 849. “Acquired action” translates the technical term fiʿl kasbī.
