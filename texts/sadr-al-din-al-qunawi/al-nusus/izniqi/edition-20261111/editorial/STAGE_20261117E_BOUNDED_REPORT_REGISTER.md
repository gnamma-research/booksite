## Significant distinct new source checks

- Ibn Taymiyya explicitly rejects the alleged Prophetic formula `رب زدني فيك تحيرا` (occurs twice in the treatise): <https://ebntaymiah.midad.com/masail/4839/هل-صح-عن-النبي-قوله-زدني>.
- The statement `العجز عن درك الإدراك إدراك`, three occurrences ascribed to Abū Bakr, is rejected as reliably preserved from him by Ibn Taymiyya: <https://dorar.net/h/0hsYUzTT?sims=1>.
- The paired maxim `من عرف نفسه … عرف ربه` has no authenticated Prophetic isnād according to multiple evaluators, yet Iznīqī's use at p837 does not explicitly claim it to be a hadith there: <https://dorar.net/h/ftd1uGQE>.
- al-Qushayrī, *Risāla*, chapter on al-Junayd: source match for `من لم يحفظ القرآن ولم يكتب الحديث`: <https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/1087/28/>. The chapters on *maʿrifa* and *tawḥīd* preserve parallel sayings of Sahl, Dhū l-Nūn, and al-Shiblī: <https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/1087/161/> and <https://islamweb.org/ar/library/content/1087/159/>.
- Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 486 supports `لا أحصي ثناء عليك` but not automatically adjacent additions: <https://sunnah.com/muslim/4/252>. Muslim Introduction 6 supports the warning about unfamiliar reports, though the expanded wording in Iznīqī is not exactly matched: <https://sunnah.com/muslim/introduction>.
- Bukhārī 6502 supports a core of the divine proximity quotation, but not all its added body-parts and causative clauses: <https://sunnah.com/bukhari/81/91>. Bukhārī 4547 supports the ʿĀʾisha warning against interpreters of ambiguous verses: <https://sunnah.com/bukhari/65>.
- The Ibn ʿAbbās tri-partite guidance report of p850 has an earlier al-Ḥārith reference and Ibn Ḥajar's ḥasan grade: <https://dorar.net/h/6PvSe08I>.
- Abū Dāwūd 2532 contains the p852 three-foundations report; Yazīd b. Abī Nashaba's weakness and a separate *ḥasan li-ghayrih* judgement both remain recorded: <https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/BookToc/ViewMatnPage?bookId=3&mainId=92457>.
- The closing maxim in p852 has a weak earlier *mawqūf* source from ʿUmar, not a verified sound *marfūʿ* version: <https://dorar.net/h/JjGjSeER?osoul=1>.
- The p848 “would that Muḥammad…” saying circulates in later literature but has no verified sound Prophetic isnād here.
