- Printed p848 (image https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3074106#page=35): `يعني ينقلب الوصف ولا يزول` (A) and `ويلزم منه أن لا يكون لغير الله وجود إمكاني ولا فعل كسبي ... لأنه خرم` (B) remain printed, unexplained cruxes. - 2023 manuscript sigla: Râgıb Paşa 692 **ر**, Nuruosmaniye 2398 **ن**, Nuruosmaniye 2888 [edition descriptive slip 2388] **ع**, Hacı Mahmud Efendi 4223 **م**. Independent study identifies **Nuruosmaniye 2398/1**, calling it 17 folios where edited description says 14: https://katalog.marmara.edu.tr/veriler/yordambt/cokluortam/5E/T0041886.pdf. This is a **catalogue/extent discrepancy, not an established variant**.
- Süleymaniye Library accepts remote digital-copy applications, as described at https://suleymaniye.yek.gov.tr/Home/Index_?n_id=2. Request text is prepared in the local bundle, **NOT SENT**.
- Hadith p852 `ثلاث من أصل الإيمان`: Abū Dāwūd 2532 is identifiable. Al-Mundhirī notes Yazīd b. Abī Nashaba, practically unknown; al-Albānī judges weak; al-Arnaʾūṭ's composite hasan li-ghayrih is a separate overall grade: https://dorar.net/h/ihdAAQQ4/osoul and https://dorar.net/h/ERbdaki3/osoul.

- p848 `يا ليت رب محمد لم يخلق محمدا` is attested within Rūmī's *Fīhi mā fīhi*, p272 Arabic translation, but this **does not authenticate an isnād**: https://najafdesertlibrary.com/book/فيه-ما-فيه/v/1/p/272.
