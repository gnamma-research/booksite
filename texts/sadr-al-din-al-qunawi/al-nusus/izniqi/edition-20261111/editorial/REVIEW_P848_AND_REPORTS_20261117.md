## Printed p. 848: two unresolved readings
Primary proof: published image on https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/3074106#page=35 (printed p848, PDF index 34).
CRUX A. The printed text reads: يعني ينقلب الوصف ولا يزول, soon after saying أن انقلاب الحقائق ممتنع. This creates a logical tension. An inserted negative لا ينقلب الوصف is a conjecture, NOT an independently witnessed manuscript reading. Keep the literal printed Arabic and inherited translation unchanged.
CRUX B. The printed text reads: ويلزم منه أن لا يكون لغير الله وجود إمكاني ولا فعل كسبي بحسب نفس الأمر، لأنه خرم ما عليه إجماع أهل السنة. The affirmative consequence appears to be rejected by the following لأنه خرم. The possible emendation ولا يلزم منه is a conjecture ONLY. No independent source folio was obtained. Preserve the existing contextual English construal but label it non-diplomatic. The printed image governs over garbled PDF text extraction such as حرم for خرم.
Both cruxes are UNRESOLVED. Neither has been verified against any original manuscript.

4. p848, each reality must be witnessed by the law: located in Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani, Futuh al-ghayb, maqala 40: https://www.islamarchive.cc/ketab_content/103000/p-201. Textual parallel, not independent manuscript collation.
