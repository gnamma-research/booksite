# Al-Iznīqī: complete Arabic–English reading edition

Release: IZN-READING-20261111. This supersedes the interpretive reconstruction of 9 November 2026 without deleting that historical archive.

## What is complete

All medieval running text on printed pages 831–852 of Balaban and Çalışkan, Ihya 9/2 (2023): invocation, introduction, four chapters, both concluding chapters, extended quotations, and every Arabic and Persian poem. Arabic transcription and English translation are aligned by source page. The 22 pages contain 12,379 English translation words, 7,968 Arabic words and 78 separate editorial notes. No running-text passage is intentionally replaced by a summary or left untranslated.

## What this does not claim

This is a complete reading translation from one printed critical edition, not a newly constituted manuscript-collated Arabic edition. No manuscript was independently collated. Difficult readings remain explicitly flagged, notably two clauses on p. 848. Quotations are translated as attributed in the treatise; not every hadith and historical saying has been independently authenticated. The modern editors' introduction, numbered apparatus and bibliography are excluded. The modern bibliography begins below the medieval ending on p. 852.

## Canonical recovery

The eleven text-*.md files listed in manifest.json collectively preserve the complete current Arabic, English and all editorial notes. The manifest records exact byte counts, SHA-256 and Git blob hashes. Run `python rebuild.py --output /tmp/izniqi` to verify them and create a single cumulative Markdown file and structured JSON. Do not count the superseded Nov9 reconstruction or the separate excerpt dataset as additional new translation coverage.

The reader at ../index.html offers Arabic, English, both, search, notes, source links, bookmarks, and a Full cumulative handoff button that exports the entire current text and notes. The previous reconstruction remains at ../../editorial/IZNIQI_EN_CUMULATIVE_DRAFT_20261109.md. It is not the current reading text. No inherited material in that archive has been deleted.

## Significant corrections

The opening retains al-Qāshānī's subtle individual trace. Al-Shiblī's 'God's wild creatures' is no longer replaced by a different saying. The actual reports on p. 833, the complete sequence of spiritual kingdoms, and every poem are restored. The less-preferred affirmative interpretation of essential knowledge on p. 842 is preserved. The argument against treating created existence as a mere mirage on p. 846 is translated fully. The conclusion expressly allows assent to vary in strength while distinguishing quantitative increase. The bibliography boundary on p. 852 is corrected.

## Next critical work, not translation gaps

Collate original manuscripts, resolve declared textual cruxes, compare quoted works in their own editions, and authenticate transmitted reports. Preserve the present reading and alternatives rather than silently replacing them. Completion here is running-text coverage, not a claim of infallibility or settled textual criticism.
