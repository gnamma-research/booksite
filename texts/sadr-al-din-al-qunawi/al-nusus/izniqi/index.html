Source, scope, and editorial limits

The source is the 2023 Arabic edition in İhya 9/2 by Muhammet Seyyit Balaban and Abdulhamid Çalışkan. The modern introduction, numbered footnotes and bibliography are excluded. The bibliography begins below the medieval ending on printed p. 852. The Arabic is lightly normalized and aligned with English by source page; a sentence may continue on the next page.

Quotations attributed to the Prophet and earlier authorities are translated as transmitted here. Their inclusion does not certify their authenticity. In particular, the printed readings on p. 848 remain marked as cruxes. The earlier interpretive reconstruction is retained as an archive, not used as the reading text.
