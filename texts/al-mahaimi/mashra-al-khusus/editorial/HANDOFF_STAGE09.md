## Editorial discoveries

1. APP-01: `توجب` inside the commentary versus `يوجب` in 2008 appended Qūnawī; independent online Qūnawī and Jāmī's *Naqd al-Nuṣūṣ* instead have `بموجب`. This changes the causal construal, but a preferred reading is not established without manuscript checks.
2. APP-05: `الظهور في الوجود` (commentary quotation) vs `الظهور للوجود` (appended matn); Jāmī, *Ashiʿʿat al-Lamaʿāt* supports the latter quotation, as does al-Mahāʾimī's explanation.
3. APP-09: Printed transcription of al-Mahāʾimī p.232 ends with `فإذا ظهرت آثار الوجود بشرط`; keep the lacuna, no invented completion.
4. A separately published Qūnawī edition with Eshkūrī's notes and Nawāʾī's editing contains printed variant sigla; samples checked but no systematic collation.
5. Āshtiyānī's Qom 1421 edition, 335 pages (ISBN 964424804X), is bibliographically confirmed only; actual text not accessed.
