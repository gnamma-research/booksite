## Concordance problem: 20 / 21 / 22

The 2008 commentary's *online segmentation* distinguishes 22 units with headings **النص الأول ... النص الثاني والعشرون**. Other descriptions of Qūnawī's original give 20, while some manuscript catalogues report 21. Three plausible explanations must be **tested**, not presumed: (a) division or merger of a long text; (b) main texts plus appended sections; (c) divergent enumeration already within manuscript witnesses. There is currently **no responsibly completed one-to-one mapping**. ### A preliminary, explicitly *nonconcordant*, high-value anchor

M-22's Qūnawī statement distinguishes the **appearing effects** of *aʿyān thābita* from the **nonappearing fixed entities themselves**. Its next assertion says that some reports of manifestation are true *bi-lisān baʿḍ al-marātib wa-l-adhwāq al-nisbiyya*, according to a particular degree or relative taste. Al-Mahāʾimī glosses this as station-limited validity rather than an arbitrary false report. Source: [online unit 22](https://www.ibnalarabi.com/qunawi/khusus.php?id=32), section beginning «اعلم أن أعظم الشبه والحجب». **Printed 2008 page not assigned here.** This unit has been *examined for the research register*, not counted among the consecutively translated pp.11–72.

Do not infer from “fixed entities themselves never appear” the stronger claim that a subset of all possibilities is intrinsically incapable of yielding **any manifestation**. Qūnawī here differentiates the *entity itself* from its *effects*. Establish the modal issue independently.

### Verification route for completing the concordance

1. Transcribe the **first and last 12–20 authorial words** of every one of the 22 units, stripping Mahāʾimī's commentary and modern footnotes.
2. Collate Qūnawī's independent text witness, each of the 20-unit and 21-unit textual divisions, and other independent commentators' lemmata. Record the manuscript **folio or original printed page**, not only a website section number.
3. Only then assign correspondence as 1:1, split, merger, plus appendix, or nonmatching; retain incipits even where numbers differ.
4. Compare **Āshtiyānī 335pp** with **al-Mazīdī 272pp** and verify whether apparent differences reflect format or substantial textual variants. No variants are established by comparing lengths alone.

## Other independently attributed commentaries to consult

- *Asrār al-surūr* attributed to al-Musannif al-Shīrāzī, a bibliographical lead requiring edition/manuscript confirmation.
- *Zubdat al-taḥqīq* attributed to Ismāʿīl Ḥaqqī/Izniqī in descriptions of early Qūnawī commentary tradition (names and editions require exact verification).
- Chittick, *The Texts: The Keys to the Fuṣūṣ* is an English comparator for Qūnawī's **own Nuṣūṣ**, not for al-Mahāʾimī's commentary; it should never be inserted as al-Mahāʾimī translation.

**Research safety:** The first two works have verifiable printed editions and digital text; other titles are **catalogue hypotheses** awaiting author/title witnesses. A catalogue entry alone is not a critically established attribution.
