The printed commentary p.232 ends its last explanatory sentence:

> بشرط الأعيان، فإذا ظهرت آثار الوجود بشرط.

It then prints, in a separate bold line:

> آخره والحمد لله رب العالمين

**Conclusion:** the interruption is already present in the printed 2008 edition. It is not merely a truncation introduced by the project's online extraction. The scan contains the end of that printed commentary followed by the separate Qūnawī work; it provides no missing complement to the interrupted sentence. The cause of the printed interruption remains undetermined. It could arise at an earlier stage of textual transmission or editing; this scan alone cannot determine which. No conjectural continuation should be supplied as authorial text.

## Readings that must remain distinct

The embedded Qūnawī lemma on printed p.232 reads **أن الظهور في الوجود**, "that manifestation is in existence." Al-Mahāʾimī's explanation later on the same page reads **علمت أن الظهور للوجود**, "you know that manifestation belongs to existence." The separate appendix p.267 has **للوجود** in Qūnawī's text. A translation must retain the difference rather than silently normalizing all three to the same English phrase.

Printed p.230 has **توجب** in the opening final lemma. The previous project's apparatus records **يوجب** in appendix p.267 and **بموجب** in other transcriptions. Treat these as recorded readings of their respective sources; neither the new scan nor their doctrinal plausibility establishes a manuscript-based preferred reading.

## Editorial status

This advances the project from electronic-transcription-only knowledge to direct comparison with a scan of its printed copy-text. It does not establish a critical text against historical manuscripts. The Āshtiyānī edition and Leiden Or.5675 remain independent comparison targets; no new manuscript folio has been inspected in this work. The ending should be labelled **"The 2008 printed commentary breaks off here"**, while retaining the printed closing formula separately.

The page-specific English revisions for pp.230–232 remove material displaced from adjacent pages, restore the final text's opening to p.230, preserve the printed variants, and mark the interrupted ending without completing it.
