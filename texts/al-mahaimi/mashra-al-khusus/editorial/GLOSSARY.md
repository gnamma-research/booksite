# GNAMMA | Akbarī technical glossary — al-Mahāʾimī, stage 3
**Status:** context-sensitive working lexicon. “Attested” means that a term or its explicit construction occurs in the **consulted working text**, *Mashraʿ*, ed. al-Mazīdī, pp.11–72, unless a different source is named. It does not imply an exhaustive count. **Prospective** entries are not retroactively ascribed to pp.11–72. Exact lexical usage and authorial ownership (cited earlier authority vs al-Mahāʾimī) matter.

## A. Metaphysical and logical operations: never translate as one undifferentiated “aspect”

| Arabic | Transliteration | Principal translation; important alternative | Status/context | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| الوجود | *al-wujūd* | being, existence; sometimes the unrestricted reality of Being | Attested pp.11,14–19,29,36–38 | The abstract predicative *concept*, independent existence, dependent created existence, and the divine Reality cannot be swapped without local argument |
| وجود الحقّ | *wujūd al-Ḥaqq* | existence/Being of the Real | Attested, e.g. pp.14,18–19,36–37 | Check whether identity, divine self-existence, causal dependence, or phenomenological exclusivity is asserted |
| الوجود المطلق | *al-wujūd al-muṭlaq* | unrestricted Being | **Attested p.39**, and central in p.47; again in commentary proper, M-01 | “Unrestrictedness” is not necessarily an added positive restriction (*bi-sharṭ al-iṭlāq*) |
| الماهيّة / الماهيات | *māhiyya / māhiyyāt* | quiddity/ies; what-it-is | Attested p.19 | Distinguish a possible essence from independently existent substance and from an *ʿayn thābita* |
| العين / الأعيان | *ʿayn / aʿyān* | entity, determination, individual, “fixed entity” where *thābita* is explicit | Attested p.11 and cited passages later | Never translate every *ʿayn* as a *manifest corporeal object* or automatically as a fixed entity |
| الأعيان الثابتة | *al-aʿyān al-thābita* | fixed entities | Examined independently in M-22, now additionally attested in translated pp.61, 77–78; not attested in the *initial* pp.11–38 | Their effects' appearing is not identical with their own appearing; separate ontological from modal claims |
| الإيجاد | *al-ījād* | bringing into existence, existentiation | Attested p.19 and p.29 | A productive/causal relation; not ipso facto the divine Essence. Mahāʾimī objects to al-Rāzī's inference from it to proximity to the Essence at p.19 |
| الموجود | *al-mawjūd* | existent, what exists | Attested | May refer to a real individual or be used under a qualifying relation to God; *mawjūd* ≠ *wujūd* |
| الموجِد / الموجَد | *al-mūjid / al-mūjad* | cause who brings into being / what has been brought into being | Problematic unvowelled transmitted wording p.15 | Explicitly distinguish agent and product; no silent vocalization where grammatically unsecure |
| اعتبار | *iʿtibār* | consideration; an intentional way of regarding a subject | **Attested p.16** in cited Ghazālī; pp.36 elsewhere | A cognitive or philosophical consideration may track an objective foundation; not thereby an extramental object or an arbitrary fiction |
| حيثيّة / من حيث | *ḥaythiyya / min ḥayth* | respect, aspect, insofar as | Attested construction **p.29** (“insofar as existence / insofar as bringing into being”) | Specifies the scope of the predication; does not automatically posit an additional ontic constituent |
| جهة | *jiha* | respect, orientation, aspect, direction | Attested pp.36–37 | May be metaphorical or relational; must not spatialize divine transcendence |
| نسبة / إضافة | *nisba / iḍāfa* | relation, attribution | Attested p.19, and M-22 online | A relation can be objectively grounded and still not be identical to its term, substance, or productive cause |
| تعيّن | *taʿayyun* | determination; becoming determinate | **Attested pp.47,50**, as determinations and explicitly the first determination; developed further in M-01 and M-22 | Must identify whether logical restriction, principial determination, rank of self-disclosure, name, or actual manifested individuation |
| تجلّي | *tajallī* | self-disclosure, disclosure | Attested p.11 and pp.28ff, and online M-20 | Distinguish divine act/reality, its effect in a locus, and the knower's unveiling of the effect |
| مظهر / مظاهر | *maẓhar / maẓāhir* | locus/loci of manifestation | Attested pp.11–12, 37 | The mirror does not become the source of light merely because light is seen in it |
| ظهور / بطون | *ẓuhūr / buṭūn* | manifestation / hiddenness | Attested pp.11,37–38, M-22 | Subject can differ: the Real becomes outward; the fixed entities remain intrinsically unmanifested according to M-22 |
| استعداد / قابلية | *istiʿdād / qābiliyya* | preparedness / receptivity | Attested p.11 and in testimonies p.28; M-20 | A limit in reception, not necessarily limitation of the divine Essence; objective status of preparedness requires argument |

## C. Terms now established by consecutive pp.60–72, and still-open terms

| Arabic | Transliteration | Working translation | Page-specific register and caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| الأسماء الذاتية / الفعلية | *al-asmāʾ al-dhātiyya / al-fiʿliyya* | essential / act-related names | **p.60** distinguishes names indicating nontransitive meanings (e.g. life, knowledge) from names indicating activity beyond the subject (e.g. creation, sustenance); alternative definitions are reported and must remain alternatives |
| الأحدية / الواحدية | *aḥadiyya / wāḥidiyya* | nondifferentiated oneness / all-inclusiveness of differentiated Names | **pp.61–62**: the status of undifferentiated *shuʾūn* differs from distinct fixed entities at the level of knowledge; do not assign independent external existence to every rank |
| التعين الأول / الثاني | *al-taʿayyun al-awwal / al-thānī* | first / second determination | **p.62**: a principial hierarchy of disclosure and knowledge, not an assertion of two separately created things; quoted phrase around *intifāʾ/intiqāʾ al-taʿaddud* textually uncertain |
| الوجود الخارجي / الوجود العقلي / الوجود المطلق | *al-wujūd al-khārijī / al-ʿaqlī / al-muṭlaq* | extramental / intelligible / unrestricted existence | **p.64** explicitly distinguishes the *natural subjects* of three kinds of determinations. The claim is about the determination’s respective context, not that these are three equal substances |
| سراية | *sarāya / sarayān* | pervasion, diffusion, permeation | **pp.63–64** directly rejects *ḥulūl* and physical migration; described by sunlight manifesting in glass. Never translate as material indwelling |
| الحلول | *ḥulūl* | indwelling | **p.63** expressly rejected in the sense of one thing entering another or something confined in space |
| صفاء / استعداد | *ṣafāʾ / istiʿdād* | purity / receptivity | **pp.65–70**: relevant to station-specific ability to receive disclosure, not a limitation of the unrestricted Reality |
| الإنسان الكامل | *al-insān al-kāmil* | Perfect Human | **pp.65–67**: full development of bodily, intellectual and spiritual capacities, not their abolition; counts as a concrete philosophical-psychological argument for comprehensiveness |
| الحقيقة المحمدية | *al-ḥaqīqa al-Muḥammadiyya* | Muhammadan Reality | **pp.68–69**: operative comprehensive name/universal mediation contrasted expressly with the Prophet as created servant; avoid claims of independent divinity |
| الشهود / الجمعية | *al-shuhūd / al-jamʿiyya* | witnessing / integral comprehensiveness | **p.67**: reciprocal seeing of unity in plurality and plurality in unity is a more expansive station than exclusive attention to one alone |
| الحقيقة / الماهية / الهوية | *al-ḥaqīqa / al-māhiyya / al-huwiyya* | reality, quiddity, identity | **p.71**: explicitly differentiated definitions; not three automatic English synonyms for “essence” |
| الإطلاق الذاتي | *al-iṭlāq al-dhātī* | essential unrestrictedness | **p.72, M-01**: no qualified predication (even unity or necessity) under **that respect**; does not mean the Real is simply non-necessary under other respects |

**Still requiring systematic source evidence:** explicit *tajalliyāt dhātiyya barqiyya* (lightning-like essential self-disclosures), the Muhammadan *lā-maqām* (station of no station), whether there are fixed possibilities unable to produce any effects, the full taxonomy of nonformal apprehension, and the precise meanings of *Non-Being* relative to divine possibilities. These are research questions, not retroactive doctrines of pp.11–72.

## Contrast pairs for the definitive edition

- *Wujūd* in first-intentional ontology vs the mental *concept* “existence”; neither entails the other without premise.
- *Min ḥayth al-wujūd* vs *min ḥayth al-ījād*: **p.29**, explicit qualification by al-Nahrajūrī as transmitted by al-Mahāʾimī.
- **One underlying reality** vs **many true attributions** (pp.16,32); these do not eliminate differentiation within scriptural discourse.
- **Phenomenological non-seeing** of creatures (pp.16,19–23) vs **ontological lack of independent existence** (pp.19,25,36–37); neither alone proves literal identity of Creator and creature.
- **Validity in a station** vs **unqualified validity** (M-22 online), with the author's claimed hierarchy itself subject to investigation.
