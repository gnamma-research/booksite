# Stage 09 addendum — independent text/reception evidence (9 November 2026)

**Status:** source transcription and quotation comparison, *no manuscript collation*; variants not silently adopted in the base text.

## APP-01: direction of causation

2008 embedded Qūnawī quotation in al-Mahāʾimī (p.230): `التعددات الواقعة في الوجود الواحد توجب آثار الأعيان الثابتة فيه`.
2008 separately reproduced Qūnawī text (p.267): `التعددات الواقعة في الوجود الواحد يوجب آثار الأعيان الثابتة فيه`.
Separate Qūnawī transcription and Jāmī, *Naqd al-Nuṣūṣ*, printed p.47: `التعددات الواقعة في الوجود الواحد بموجب آثار الأعيان الثابتة فيه`.

The third reading, **بموجب**, reverses the likely causal construal: multiplicity occurs by virtue of the effects of the fixed entities rather than itself producing those effects. Al-Mahāʾimī's own gloss on p.231 says `هذه التعددات إنما هي آثار الأعيان الثابتة`, which supports this sense. The third reading is a *supported alternative*, not a manuscript-established emendation.

Sources: https://www.ibnalarabi.com/qunawi/nusus.php?id=22 ; https://ablibrary.net/book_content/3772/178 .

## APP-05: in existence or belonging to existence

2008 embedded lemma (p.232): `علمت أن الظهور في الوجود`.
2008 original appendix (p.267): `علمت أن الظهور للوجود`.
Al-Mahāʾimī's own gloss p.232 and Jāmī, *Ashiʿʿat al-Lamaʿāt*, printed p.129, both employ `الظهور للوجود`. Jāmī quotes Qūnawī by name and also states `وأن البطون صفة ذاتية للأعيان`. This independently supports the *sense*, not a particular manuscript exemplar.

Source: https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/17500/129?lang=ar .

### Explanation and editorial consequences

**APP-01 — agreement of the verb.** Embedded reading (p. 230–231): «توجب آثار الأعيان الثابتة فيه». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «يوجب آثار الأعيان الثابتة فيه». Different agreement (feminine vs masculine form); the plural subject is التعددات. No emendation made.

**APP-02 — non-manifestation.** Embedded reading (p. 231): «ولم تظهر أبدا». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «ولم تظهر هي ، ولا تظهر أبدا». Independent text adds the explicit pronoun and distinguishes past and future non-manifestation.

**APP-03 — intrinsic incapacity.** Embedded reading (p. 231): «لأنها ذاتها لا تقتضي الظهور». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «لأنها لذاتها لا تقتضي الظهور». The second specifies ‘in virtue of their own essence’ with a preposition.

**APP-04 — relative validity.** Embedded reading (p. 231): «إنما يثبت صحتها بالنسبة». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «إنما تثبت صحتها بالنسبة». Agreement differs; contextual reference is the validity of statements from restricted ranks.

**APP-05 — locus of manifestation.** Embedded reading (p. 232): «علمت أن الظهور في الوجود». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «علمت أن الظهور للوجود». Material distinction: “manifestation is in existence” vs “manifestation belongs to existence.” The latter accords with the commentary’s explicit gloss but must be checked against Āshtiyānī and manuscripts.

**APP-06 — alternation wording.** Embedded reading (p. 232): «بين الظهور ، وبطون غلبة ومغلوبية». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «بين ظهور وبطون لغلبة ومغلوبية». Definiteness and preposition in account of manifestation and hiddenness.

**APP-07 — origin of relations.** Embedded reading (p. 232): «صور أحوال وأحكام تنشأ بين المراتب». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «صور أحوال وأحكام تنشئ بين المراتب». Two printed forms of the verb; assess syntactic fit against a scan before vocalizing.

**APP-08 — concealment.** Embedded reading (p. 232): «ويخفى بعضها بعضا». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «ويخفي بعضها بعضا». Printed form differs by ya/hamza/vowel convention; the intended causative relation must be checked.

**APP-09 — ending.** Embedded reading (p. 232): «فإذا ظهرت آثار الوجود بشرط . آخره والحمد لله رب العالمين». Separate Qūnawī (p. 267): «تمّ الكتاب والحمد لله وحده وصلّى الله». The commentary sentence ends after an uncompleted prepositional construction; the standalone Qūnawī original ends normally. The latter cannot supply the missing Mahāʾimī commentary.

1. Qūnawī lemma should carry a segment ID, a verse/page reference to **both** the embedded and independently reproduced locations, and a note explaining whether wording is normalized or diplomatic.
2. Al-Mahāʾimī commentary remains a separate authorial layer. When the commentary quotes the lemma, both are accessible without conflating the speakers.
3. Āshtiyānī readings belong in a distinct column **only after the text is consulted**. The word “variant” above refers to an internal printed divergence, NOT to a collated manuscript variant.
4. The p.232 truncated clause should be displayed exactly and tagged `witness_ends_mid_sentence`; do not add a conjectural completion.

### English translation of p. 267 (checked passage only)

**Qūnawī, final text (source-checked translation of printed p. 267).**

Know that the greatest doubts and veils arise when the multiplicities occurring in the one existence produce effects belonging to the fixed entities therein. One then imagines that the entities themselves have appeared in existence and through existence. Yet what appears in existence is their effects; **they themselves neither appeared nor will ever appear**, because their very nature does not demand manifestation.

When a realized knower reports otherwise, or attributes existence and manifestation to them, this report is in the idiom of certain ranks and relative tastes: it is valid only in relation to one determinate station or several stations, not according to the experiential knowledge of the station of perfection.

The definitive statement, whose ruling cannot be superseded, is what we have stated; so too every statement in this book. This is the unqualified truth about the matter. What is said elsewhere may be true unqualifiedly, as this is, or may be true only relatively and in connection with some station, as already indicated.

When what I have explained in this text becomes clear, you will know that manifestation belongs **to existence**, but under the condition of multiplicity together with the effects of the entities within it. Hiddenness is an intrinsic attribute of the entities, and likewise of existence insofar as its unity is conceived. The matter alternates between manifestation and hiddenness through predominance and subordination: whatever diminishes from the manifest falls within the hidden, and conversely. Relations and attributions are forms of states and judgments arising among ranks: some make others manifest and hide others according to the predominance and subordination already indicated.
