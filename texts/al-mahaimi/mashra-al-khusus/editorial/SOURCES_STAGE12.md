# Sources and acquisition record

## Consulted modern edition

ʿAlāʾ al-Dīn ʿAlī al-Mahāʾimī, *Mashraʿ al-khuṣūṣ ilā maʿānī al-nuṣūṣ*, prepared by Aḥmad Farīd al-Mazīdī (Beirut: Dār al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyya, 2008). The commentary runs from printed p.11 to p.232. Qūnawī’s separate *al-Nuṣūṣ* is printed on pp.235–267. The editor’s modern footnotes are distinguished from both medieval works.

The original full electronic export is an acquisition intermediate; the reader publishes the edited medieval body with its provenance and correction record.
