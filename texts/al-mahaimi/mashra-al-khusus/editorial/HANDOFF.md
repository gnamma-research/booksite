## Known primary-text issues to retain

1. Modern printed editor footnotes are not al-Mahāʾimī; p76 and p86 footnotes are excluded from medieval translation.
2. The 2008 text is an **online searchable transcription**; the physical 2008 printed edition and independent Āshtiyānī edition have not been collated. Suspicious words are flagged rather than corrected without a witness.
3. The “20, 21, 22” chapter-count conflict remains open; five incipits have now been pinned to printed pages 72, 80, 83, 86, 88.
4. Qūnawī’s bracketed statements and al-Mahāʾimī’s prose are distinguishable by quotation labels, although some alignment chunks contain both—further segmentation is an editorial revision priority.
5. Main metaphysical distinctions: absolute/qualified unrestrictedness; first vs second determination; intrinsic knowledge vs relational *taʿalluq*; real multiplicity of external quiddities vs one existence considered in itself; first effusion vs conditioned names; existence of fixed entities in knowledge vs manifest external existence; temporal appearance vs principial/epistemic order; universal knowing vs its variable manifestation.
