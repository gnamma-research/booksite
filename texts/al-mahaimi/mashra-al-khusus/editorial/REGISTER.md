# GNAMMA | Register of Metaphysical Perspectives and Technical Terms

**Stage 3; 9 November 2026.** 68 proposition-level records from the first-pass translated *Mashraʿ* pp.11–78 plus **explicitly nonconsecutive, uncounted research excerpts** from *Khuṣūṣ al-niʿam* and *Mashraʿ* Nuṣūṣ units 20 and 22. Each comparison is flagged as an inspected passage or a **navigation lead**, never as a manuscript-collated reading.

## Editorial method

Every record separates: speaker; text and location; ontology; controlling *iʿtibār*; mode of knowing; state of the knower; lexical meaning; truth qualification; parallels; and open problems. The **speaker field** prevents al-Mahāʾimī's quotation of Qushayrī/Ghazālī from being misrepresented as his own uninterrupted prose.

## MP-001 — Levels of intellectual apprehension, from discursive thought to unveiling

- **Source:** Mashraʿ; p.12 / MSH-001. [Source text](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/9637/12?lang=ar). **Speaker:** al-Mahāʾimī.
- **Ontological level/object:** Levels of intellectual apprehension, from discursive thought to unveiling.
- **Aspect (*iʿtibār/ḥaythiyya*):** Difference between processes for acquiring evidence and attained direct vision.
- **Epistemic register:** Discursive inference, ḥads, kashf, ʿayn al-yaqīn.
- **Knower/station:** Seeker with purified intellect, not every ordinary rational subject.
- **Key terms:** naẓar; ḥads; kashf; ʿayn al-yaqīn.
- **Force/status:** Authorial epistemological programme; not a blanket proof of the falsehood of logic. **Evidence:** `first_pass`.
- **Comparators:** Turka SH-INTRO-003/004 and SH-004 (repository navigation leads, not checked in this stage).
- **Unresolved question / error risk:** Some readings of faculty names are textually uncertain; his account of al-Ghazālī and al-Rāzī needs source checks.

## Derived propositions and falsification checks

1. **A consideration is not automatically an ontological component.** The iʿtibār of many/one at MSH-005, p.16 requires an argument concerning its real foundation.
2. **Cognitive effacement and causal dependence differ.** MSH-011, p.23 and MSH-025, p.37 supply a countercheck against treating loss of perceptual differentiation as metaphysical annihilation of created realities.
3. **First and second differentiations may both be true at their proper rank.** MSH-011 p.23 gives a return to obligations not construed as falling away from God.
4. **Divine names can share a referent while differing in meaning.** Khuṣūṣ 189–190 should be tested against the same author’s treatment of objective name-distinctions in *Mashraʿ* M-01–M-22.
5. **Fixed entities and their effects are different topics.** M-22 asserts the appearance of effects and the nonappearance of the fixed entities themselves. The stronger issue of intrinsically unmanifestable *possibilities* is **not proven by this passage**.
6. **The author’s own hierarchy needs testing.** The claim in M-22 that some speech is relative while another cannot be superseded is a position to reconstruct and assess, not a license to harmonize all mutually inconsistent propositions.
7. **Do not pre-translate as Guénon.** Terms “Beyond-Being”, “Non-Being” and “Universal Possibility” are external comparators, not currently imported glosses for *wujūd, ʿadam,* or *aʿyān*.

## Deferred critical questions

- Does a modal claim about the possibility of a manifestation follow from the *nonappearance* of the fixed entity itself? If not, what would constitute a textual test?
- Can a statement be more comprehensive **without** discarding ontological distinctions the previous station understood correctly?
- Is there an objective criterion for a station-unconditioned claim, apart from the speaker’s self-certification?
- Does the same word *wujūd* denote divine unconditioned reality, a creature’s dependent realizedness, or the knower’s “finding” in the cited source? **Check the clause, speaker, and level.**
- How might Qūnawī’s absolute-Essence discourse and brief *barqī* disclosures be described temporally without imposing the temporal duration of the bodily experience upon the disclosed Reality? **No answer established in this stage.**

## Methodological synthesis for the next critical pass

1. **Do not infer numerical identity from predicative unity** (pp.102, 122): direct and derivative predication of the Real are already distinct logical operations, and both differ from the determination of one intelligible effusion (pp.128–130).
2. **Do not infer an abolished ontology from the loss of awareness** (pp.108–109): al-Mahāʾimī explicitly denies that the human faculties cease to exist; p.137 expressly distinguishes the transient flash from its enduring cognitive results.
3. **Do not infer independence from conditional efficacy** (pp.117–119, 128–129): *sharṭ* and *sabab* have distinct causal roles, and intermediate ranks are not treated as independent origins of the first effusion.
4. **Do not infer arbitrariness from station-qualified truth** (pp.89, 116, 135–136): the partial report can be right relative to a name or station while less adequate than the perfected integration.
5. **Do not import Guénon or Schuon as a taxonomy** until the meanings of unrestrictedness, first determination, fixed entities and manifested existence have been established directly. The current file does not equate Qūnawī’s *iṭlāq* with a particular Guénonian “Beyond-Being” tier or place *sat–cit–ānanda* into the Akbarian hierarchy.

**Revision threshold:** all source readings are first-pass; no manuscript critical status, independent quotation authentication, or complete cross-author doctrinal concordance is claimed.
