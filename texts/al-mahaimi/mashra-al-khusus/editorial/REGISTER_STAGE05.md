# Al-Mahāʾimī — Stage 05: additional perspectives register

Source witness: al-Mazīdī 2008 online page transcription; manuscript variants not checked. Each proposition is ascribed according to its embedded authorial and quoted layers.

| Page | Layer | Claim | Ontological/epistemic register | Qualification and issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 139–140 | Qūnawī, explained by al-Mahāʾimī | Perfect responsiveness is above simple obedience and answered supplication | Station of the Perfect Human; willing, action | Its scope is specifically relative to the first universal will. Transmitted reports involving Abū Ṭālib not verified. |
| 141 | al-Mahāʾimī | The Perfect Human reunites what has become distinguished through cosmic manifestation | Comprehensive human determination; *jalāʾ/istijlāʾ* | The Perfect Human is “intrinsically intended” under a stated consideration of divine will; divine self-sufficiency explicitly retained. |
| 142 | al-Mahāʾimī | The human being is necessary through God and possible in himself; *fanāʾ* and *baqāʾ* give distinct presentations | Existential dependence; spiritual realization | Necessary *through another* is not necessary *through self*. |
| 143 | al-Mahāʾimī | “He effects what he wills” applies through the Lord, not independently | Ontological agency / spiritual station | Explicit rejection of divine becoming-servant and servant becoming-Lord. |
| 144 | Qūnawī, explained by al-Mahāʾimī | The promise of response pertains to correct conception of the addressee | Station-conditioned invocation | Do not collapse divine will's realization with every prayer by every agent. |
| 145–146 | Qūnawī and al-Mahāʾimī | Knowledge increases insofar as the differentiating distance of knower and known is overcome | Modes of cognition; analogy of image/mirror; unveiling | “Union” is explicated by the presentation of a form in knowledge, not automatically substantial fusion. |
| 147 | al-Mahāʾimī | Objects are united and necessary *as present in divine knowledge*, but plural and possible as distinct realities | First determination; divine knowledge / creaturely possibilities | Distinguish objective plurality from the standpoint of knowing. |
| 148 | Qūnawī and al-Mahāʾimī | The first determination contains potential distinctions; a second consideration establishes plurality of known objects | *Taʿayyun awwal*, conceptual plurality, object-differentiation | Does not automatically entail extramental existentiation or temporal succession. |
| 149 | Qūnawī and al-Mahāʾimī | Unity appears plural through the differing capacities of loci; difference in knowledge is one of rank, not time | Receptivities, relations, knowledge | Distinguish formal universality, metaphysical unity, and the glass/mirror analogies. |
