# Complete bilingual working edition

**Edition date: 11 November 2026 · Stage 13.**

The continuous reader contains the full available Arabic and working English for al-Mahāʾimī’s commentary on Qūnawī, followed by Qūnawī’s separate original text as printed in the same 2008 volume.

| Component | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Al-Mahāʾimī commentary | Printed pp.11–232: 222 pages, represented by 221 entries because pp.18–19 remain one reading unit |
| Qūnawī original appendix | Printed pp.235–267: 33 pages, each with Arabic and a new page-bound English translation |
| Canonical reader | 254 entries; one current version of each reading unit |
| Earlier overlapping Qūnawī material | Preserved separately as 54 historical draft/excerpt entries |
| Commentary translation review against available Arabic | 222/222 pages; source uncertainties remain marked |
| Documented commentary print-image comparison | 98/222 pages; seven previously omitted Stage12 records recovered |
| Qūnawī appendix print-image inspection | 33/33 recorded in Stage12 |
| Independent manuscript collation | None claimed |

The full 2008 electronic transcription and a scan of the printing have been acquired. The scan itself is missing printed pp.113–114; their electronic text is included. Its final commentary page confirms that the unfinished sentence on p.232 is unfinished in the printing, so the edition leaves it unreconstructed.

Substantive review has addressed omitted text, page-boundary drift, reversals of meaning, speaker attribution, and modern footnotes mistakenly incorporated into the medieval body. The [current review ledger](../data/coverage-stage13.json) and [page review index](review-stage13/review-index.json) give the exact completed review coverage. Stage13 completed the remaining 57 commentary-page reviews and applied 85 English passage corrections, 30 editorial notes and three heading corrections. The first passage of p.43 still includes an explicitly marked synopsis of an unstable source argument; it is not certified as a full literal reconstruction. Several other readings remain flagged. The separate [textual notes](TEXTUAL_NOTES.md) explain consequential decisions and unresolved readings.

## Scholarly scope

“Complete” describes the available running text and its English translation. It does not certify a manuscript-critical edition, settle every difficult printed reading, or authenticate each quoted report. The commentary, quoted Qūnawī lemmata, separately printed original, and modern apparatus remain distinct. An internal parallel in the same modern volume is not counted as another manuscript witness.

The [source record](SOURCES_STAGE12.md), [terminology glossary](GLOSSARY.md), and earlier research apparatus remain available. The next independent textual task, if pursued, is comparison with actual manuscript images or another commentary edition, especially at the unfinished ending. That research is distinct from the completed bilingual reader and downloads.
