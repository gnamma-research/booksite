The crucial editorial principle is to tag **(A) words of al-Mahāʾimī**, **(B) lemmata from Qūnawī**, **(C) cited words of earlier authorities**, and **(D) the modern editor's footnotes**. Modern footnotes are **absent from the bilingual authorial text**, not silently reattributed.

## Method for an edition deserving “critical”

1. Secure high-quality image of M08, A21 and at least independent manuscript witness(es); record provenance, dates, folios, lacunae, and interventions.
2. Transcribe each witness separately, retaining spelling and lineation; do not back-project web punctuation onto manuscripts.
3. Enter every lemma of Qūnawī into a separate quotation layer; tag attributed earlier Sufi, kalām, Qurʾānic, ḥadīth, and modern editor layers.
4. Collate *actual readings*: record `M08: X | A21: Y | ms-siglum: Z` **only after checking each image**. Until then preserve `suspect / suggested / unverified` states.
5. Reconcile the independent 20/21/22 divisions by incipit and explicit rather than section numbers alone.
6. Re-translate passages affected by readings; only then promote any completed units to critically checked status.

**Not yet done:** manuscript collation, reconstruction of Qūnawī's 20/21 division, verification of quotations in the cited originals, and verification of editorial printing errors against physical facsimile. These remain explicit deficits, not hidden omissions.
