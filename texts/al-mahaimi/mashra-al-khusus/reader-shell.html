SOURCES & EDITORIAL METHOD

About this working edition

Al-Mahāʾimī’s commentary and Qūnawī’s separately printed original are kept as separate works. The 2008 Beirut edition supplies the printed page references. Editorial headings and notes belong to this reading edition; they are separate from the medieval texts.

Reading the text and its source layers

Commentary includes Qūnawī’s quoted lemmata within al-Mahāʾimī’s explanation. Original displays the separately reproduced Qūnawī text in printed-page order. The main reading corpus keeps one current version of each passage. Earlier overlapping drafts and the separate forum transcription are preserved in the historical source archive; their numbering and textual divisions are not automatically interchangeable with the printed appendix.

Cross-references identify related passages. A lexical correspondence within the same modern edition does not count as an independent manuscript witness. The English is a working translation reviewed against the Arabic, with exact review coverage recorded in the accompanying files.

Textual witnesses and unresolved readings

The source register distinguishes the al-Mazīdī edition (Beirut, 2008), the Āshtiyānī edition (Qom, 1421 AH), Leiden manuscript Or. 5675, and later quotations of Qūnawī. A catalogued witness is not counted as collated until its text has been examined.

The acquired print itself leaves the final commentary sentence on p.232 unfinished. Printed pp.113–114 are absent from the scan but available in the electronic text. The readings توجب / يوجب / بموجب and الظهور في الوجود / الظهور للوجود require the distinctions documented in the apparatus. Do not silently combine a commentary reading with a different wording in Qūnawī’s original.
